Tiri kuhaya Emir Beganović seHead of Infrastructure yeOmarchy! Anotanga Mbudzi 1 paOmacom Foundation semushandi wechitatu wenguva yakazara, achibatana naKrzysztof Wilczyński paKernel naoutfoxxed paShell.

Heano mabatiro anoita Emir anozvitsanangura rwendo rwake kuenda kuOmarchy:

Rudo rwangu rweLinux runodzokera kumashure makore anopfuura makumi maviri. Ndakava mugadziri weUbuntu ndichiri teenager uye ndaive ndakadzika munharaunda yemuno. Basa rangu randitora nomunyika nhatu, ndichishanda pakuvimbika nemashandiro mumastartup nemascaleup uye ndichitungamira zvikwata. Ndakapedza makore anoda kusvika manomwe mudhipatimendi reSRE reBooking.com, ndichitungamira basa pabuild systems neCI infrastructure. Achangobva, ndakavaka Isorun, pandanga ndichishanda pama microVM, masnapshot, isolation, scheduling, necompute inoraswa. Ikozvino ndiri kuunza ruzivo irworwo kurudo rwangu rwekutanga.

Chakanyatsobata pfungwa dzangu nezvaEmir chaive TryOmarchy. Akatora desktop yeOmarchy, akaiisa mubrowser (kwete kuitazve, asi chinhu chaicho), uye akaiita kuti ibhutsu mumasekonzi paCPU infrastructure yakajairika paCloudflare. Takatanga kutaura nezvekuyedza kudiki ikoko kunosetsa, uye hurukuro yakakurumidza kuwedzera kumalaptop, magadzirirwo matsva, maOEM, nezvivakwa zvatichada kana Omarchy ichimhanya kwese kwatinoda. Takaona kuti taigovana chiono chimwe.

Ndizvo zviri kuuya kuzotungamira Emir.

Zvivakwa zveOmarchy zvinoreva zvakawanda kupfuura kumhanya maseva. Ndizvo zvatinoshandisa kuvaka, kuedza, nekutumira Omarchy, uye kuchengeta system ichishanda zvakavimbika sezvo tichiwedzera kune hardware yakawanda nemagadzirirwo akawanda. Kuita kuti kuburitsa kuvimbike, kuita kuti kukundikana kudzokororwe, uye kupa munhu wese ari papurojekiti nheyo yakasimba kuti vakurumidze kufamba.

Uye pane zvakawanda zvekuongorora! Omarchy inofanira kushanda zvakanaka pamuchina uri patafura yako, asi hapana chikonzero inofanira kumira ipapo. TryOmarchy yakatipa kuona kwezvinoitika kana kutanga komputa yeLinux kwava nyore sekuvhura peji reweb, kuisa kuravira kweLinux pakudzvanya kumwe. Tichaona kuti zvinotiendesa kupi.

Ndatenda kumunhu wese ari kupa mari basa iri, tinogona kupa vanhu vakaita saEmir nguva yekupinda zvizere muOmarchy. Vatsigiri vedu vanoita kuti zvikoneke kushandura kuedza kukuru kuita basa rinogara, nevanhu venguva yakazara vakatsaurirwa kuita kuti system yese inake. Tinotenda nerutsigiro rwavo.
