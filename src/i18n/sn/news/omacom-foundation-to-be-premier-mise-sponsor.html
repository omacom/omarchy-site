Rutsigiro rwechitatu rwabuda! Omacom Foundation iri kuva premier sponsor yemise, uye nekudaro yajdx, akapedza makore achivaka chishandiso chinoita kuti kubata mavhezheni akasiyana eRuby, Node, Go kufadze kubata, uye chinopa agent tooling yako muchannel yekusimudzira inogona kuenderana nemwero wekutumira.

Kana wakashandisa Omarchy, wakashandisa mise, ungave wakaona kana kwete. Ndiyo inobata ma-runtimes emitauro, saka mise use -g ruby inokupa Ruby pasina mutsetse mumwe wePATH archaeology. Asi chikamu chandinoita hanya nacho izvozvi ma-agent.

Yese major coding-agent CLI muOmarchy inotumirwa se lazy-loading mise stub mu~/.local/bin/. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - dzose dzakagara ipapo, dzisingakubhadharise chinhu kusvika kekutanga waanyora imwe. Ipapo inozviisa uye wave kushanda. Hapana package yekuvhima, hapana version yekunamira, hapana toolchain yekufunga nezvayo. omarchy-mise-install <package> inoputira chero imwe CLI nenzira imwe chete, uye omarchy update inochengeta yese yekupedzisira iri nyowani.

Iyo ndiyo nzira inomhanya. MuThe Age of Agents, mukaha pakati pekunzwa nezvechishandiso nekuchimhanya unofanira kuva masekonzi mana, uye mise inoendesa izvozvo. Hapana aifanira kurongedza chinhu chedistro kutanga.

Kufarira kunoenda mativi ose, zvakare: traffic yemise yakwira 24% kubvira Omarchy yatanga! Vanhu vazhinji vari kusangana nechishandiso ichi kekutanga kuburikidza nesu, ndizvo chaizvo chinhu chinofanira kucherechedzwa nerutsigiro panzvimbo yekuchitora sezvinhu.

Zvakare, ndatenda yakakosha kuna Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, naPeter Steinberger nekuita kuti izvi zvikoneke. Dai pasina Oligarchy, hatingakwanise kupa mari vagadziri veopen-source seizvi!
