Omarchy yakura kuva nharaunda yepasi rose, ine nhengo ~35,000 paDiscord, uye ikozvino yava nguva tatanga kusangana takatarisana. Saka tiri kuvhura Omarchy Meetups nekarenda yakagovaniswa yemisangano pasi rose.

Izvi zviitiko zvinomhanyiswa nenharaunda. Haudi mvumo kuronga chimwe, hapana ane guta, uye chero munhu anogona kuronga chimwe kupi zvako. Taurai nezveOmarchy, Linux, open source, programming, customization, kana chero hacker culture iri pedyo inoita kuti vanhu vafarire kuita makomputa anonakidza zvakare.

Chengetai yakasununguka kumunhu wese, zivisa chero rutsigiro, uye musashandura zita reOmarchy kuita bhizimisi rezviitiko kana sales pitch. Kupfuura izvozvo, itai sevakuru vane tsika (nekushamisika kwemwana), uye munakidzwe zvikuru.

Uyai nelaptop yenyu. Ratidzai magadzirirwo enyu. Chinjana matheme nemaplugin. Batsirai mumwe munhu mukuisa kwavo kwekutanga. Chinangwa ndechekuunza vanhu vane hanya nemakomputa ega, anorongeka, akanaka pamwe chete muimba imwe.

Unogona kuwana musangano uri kuuya kana kutumira wako kuburikidza nekarenda yepasi rose yeOmarchy paLuma. Ngatiite izvi kufamba kwepasi rose!
