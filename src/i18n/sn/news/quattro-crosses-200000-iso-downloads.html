Kudhawunilodha kunobva kunyika nematunhu 215. Idzo nzvimbo dzakawanda kupfuura nhengo dzeUN! Inopfuura hafu yekudhawunilodha inobva kunze kwenyika shanu dzepamusoro. Uye pamunhu, maNordics ari kutungamira: Iceland, Norway, neDenmark dzose dzinodhona Omarchy zvakapetwa kaviri mwero weAmerica. MaViking akunda! (Asi US ichiri 28% yekudhawunilodha muhuwandu chaihwo.)
