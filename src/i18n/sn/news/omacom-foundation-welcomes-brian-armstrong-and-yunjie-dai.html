Brian Armstrong ndiye cofounder uye CEO weCoinbase, iyo yakazvarwa ichishandisa Ruby on Rails, uye yakakura kuita nzvimbo yepamusoro yecrypto nevashandisi vanopfuura mamiriyoni zana. Brian akanga achiri kutanga kuramba zvematongerwo enyika zvisina maturo kubasa paakazivisa Coinbase sekambani inotungamirwa nechinangwa muna 2020. Akatsoropodzwa zvikuru nechinzvimbo ichocho panguva iyoyo, asi kubva ipapo akapembedzwa zvizere (murume chaiye weNight's Watch!).

Uye nezvecrypto, ndakachinja pfungwa makore apfuura. NaBrian muchikepe, tichaita kuti Omarchy ive nzvimbo yakanaka yecrypto. Nekuti tinogona kugadzirisa zvese!

Yunjie Dai muzvinabhizimisi wemitambo kubva kuChina anomhanya TapTap, imwe yemapuratifomu makuru enyika ekuwana nekuparadzira mitambo. Isingatori revenue share kubva kuvagadziri. Asi ndichamurega azvizivise, nekuti tsamba yaakatumira yaive yakanaka:

Makore 21 apfuura, muna 2005, ndakanga ndapedza koreji pandakaona vhidhiyo yako uchivaka blog neRuby on Rails neTextMate paMac OS X mumaminitsi 15. Ndakatenga white MacBook mushure mezvo, ndakasiya Windows, ndakatenga TextMate, uye ndakatanga kudzidza Ruby. Yakachinja zvikuru mafaro andaiita kukodha.

Ndiri muzvinabhizimisi wemitambo kubva kuChina, uye mhuri yangu neni ikozvino tinogara muUS. Tinovaka mitambo, uye tinomhanyawo TapTap… Kusiyana neApp Store kana Google Play, hatingatori revenue share kubva kuvagadziri, tichiri kushanda pachiyero chine revo uye nepurofiti. Pfungwa iyi inonzwa pedyo nezvandagara ndichiyemura nezve37signals: gara wakazvimirira, wana chekurarama nacho, usatora rendi nekuti unogona, uye vaka chimwe chinhu chinobatsira zvekuti vanhu vanosarudza kugara.
