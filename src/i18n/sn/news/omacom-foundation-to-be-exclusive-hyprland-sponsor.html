Ndeipi nzira iri nani yekutanga kushandisa pfuma yatakangoounganidzira kuOmacom Foundation pane kumwana weLinux akapenga kwazvo muPoland: Vaxry!

Semuvambi weHyprland, ane mutoro weimwe yetekinoroji nhatu dzepakati muOmarchy (Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), uye ndinofara kuzivisa rutsigiro rwega rwunoita kuti atarise zvakazara kuivandudza pasina kuda kufunga nezvekuvandudza kwekutengesa kana kutsvaga mari.

Tiri kusaina kwenguva refu naVaxry kutsigira ramangwana renguva refu reOmarchy. Chibvumirano chedu cherutsigiro rwega chakagadzirirwa makore matatu, nesarudzo yemamwe maviri.

Chibvumirano chinobvumirawo chirongwa cheHyprland cheHyprperks kuti chibudiswe mahara, kumunhu wese, sezvo kunyoresa kwakabhadharwa kuchapera. Asi Hyprland icharamba ichigamuchira zvipo zvega kuti vanhu vatenge mukuvandudza kweprojekiti uye kutsigira Vaxry nechikwata.

Ndatenda zvikuru ku37signals (mhoro ini! 😂), Butterfly, neFramework nekubatsira Hyprland kusvika pano. Ndinofara kuti Omacom Foundation inogona kutora kubva muna Gumiguru 10, uye kupa mumwe wevagadziri vakanakisa munguva itsva yeLinux mukana wekupinda zvizere.

(* Chibvumirano ichi hachisanganisi rubatsiro rwakananga mukumuwanira Vaxry musikana, asi ini pachangu ndichavepo sechirevo chemibvunzo panyaya iyi.)
