Tiri kuhaya outfoxxed, muvambi weQuickshell, seHead of Omarchy Shell! Ari kubatana neOmacom Foundation nguva yakazara uye kuva nhengo yeOmarchy Core. Munhu akavaka toolkit yakaita kuti Quattro igoneke ari kuuya muchikepe kutibatsira kuvhura chinhu chose kuenda kumwedzi.

Izvi zvinotsiva rutsigiro rwemakore matatu rweQuickshell rwatakazivisa muna Nyamavhuvhu. Panzvimbo perutsigiro irworwo, outfoxxed achava mushandi wenguva yakazara weOmacom Foundation, achitungamira basa redu paOmarchy Shell. Tiri kuisa muvambi pakati pezvatiri kuvaka.

Shell yakakosha kuagentic OS yatiri kugadzira. Ibhara, mamenu, zviziviso, mawidget, nekudyidzana kudiki kwese kunoita kuti komputa inzwe se yako. Quickshell inotipa zvidhinha zvekuvaka kuumba ruzivo irworwo muQML. Sanganisa izvo nema-agent akanaka kwazvo pakunyora QML, uye kamwe kamwe kuchinja desktop yako chinhu chaunogona kukumbira, kuedza, nekugovana.

Uchapupu huri mumaplugin! Patakazivisa rutsigiro, nharaunda yakanga yatogadzira anopfuura chiuru muvhiki rekutanga reQuattro. Ikozvino katalog ine pedyo nezviuru zvitatu zvemapulgin. Mitambo yaunogona kutamba kubva mubhara, mapanel anotevera ma-agent ako ekodha, maridhiyo emimhanzi, uye kunyange katsi inofamba-famba mubhara rako yakamirira kupuruzirwa. Vanhu vari kutora system yaimbonzwa yakamira uye vachiita yavo. Ndiyo komputa inochinjika ichiuya kuhupenyu.

Kuva nemuvambi weQuickshell muchikepe zvinoreva kuti tinogona kushanda pastack yese pamwe chete. Ita kuti shell imhanye uye ivimbike. Ita kuti zvive nyore kuvaka plugin yakanaka. Vandudza nheyo kana munyori weplugin asangana nemuganho. Shandura zvatiri kudzidza kubva muzviuru zvekuedza kuita ruzivo ruri nani kumunhu wese anobhutsu Omarchy. Uye ona kuti chinhu chose chakachengeteka.

Uye nekubatana neOmarchy Core, outfoxxed achabatsira kuisa gwara redistro sese. Shell iri pakati peruzivo zvekuti haigoni kuva pfungwa yekupedzisira. Ndipo pane runako rwakawanda, fun, nemikana yeagentic OS zvinova chimwe chinhu chaunogona kuona nekushandisa.

outfoxxed mushandi wechipiri wenguva yakazara weOmacom Foundation, achitevera Krzysztof Wilczyński, Head wedu weOmarchy Kernel. Nevanhu vanotungamira Kernel neShell vari muchikepe, tiri kuisa mari munheyo dzeruzivo rwese. Unogona kusangana nevashandi vefoundation pasaiti.

Ticharamba tichihaya nekukoka sezvo tinowana vanhu vakakodzera kuburikidza nebasa ravo munharaunda. (Ndapota musazadze inbox yangu nemaCV!)

Ndizvo zvinotiitisa rutsigiro rwefoundation: kupa vagadziri veopen-source vakasarudzika nguva yekupinda zvizere. Ndatenda kumunhu wese ari kupa mari basa iroro, tiri kushandura zvishuvo zvedu zvakawanda kuita mabasa enguva yakazara.

Mauya muchikepe, outfoxxed! Ngatiite kuti komputa iyi ichinjike zvakanyanya.
