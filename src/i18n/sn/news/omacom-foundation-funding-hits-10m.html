Mari yeOmacom Foundation yasvika ku10 million USD neFounding Patrons vaviri vatsva vari kubatana nechinangwa: Drew Houston, cofounder uye co-CEO weDropbox, naPeter Steinberger, muvambi weOpenClaw.

SevaFounding Patrons vasere vekutanga, Drew naPeter mumwe nemumwe ari kupa 1 million USD. Izvo zvinopa foundation vatsigiri gumi neMAMIRIYONI GUMI EUSD kufambisa chinangwa chedu chikuru chekuita kuti Linux padesktop iitike nenzira yakakura.

Zvinotapira kuva naDrew naPeter muchikepe nekuti ndanga ndiri fan mukuru wevose vari vaviri nezvavakaita. Ndanga ndiri mutengi anofarira weDropbox kwemakore anoda kusvika makumi maviri, uye iri pakati pemabatiro andinoita kurarama neno backup, no cry asi ndichigara ndichichinja makomputa.

Ndosaka Omarchy ichitumirwa nekubatanidzwa kwakanaka kweDropbox kubva mubhokisi. Ingoenda kuInstall > Service > Dropbox, uye uchava nekubatanidzwa kwefile manager nebespoke menu panel yezvinodzora.

Uye OpenClaw yakapa roadmap yemabatiro anofanira kuitwa neOmarchy kukura kwayo kunoputika. Unobata sei PR backlogs dzinoerekana dzaenda parabolic? Ko chengetedzo yakatenderedza maplugin? Misangano muzita reprojekiti inofanira kubatwa sei? Ndatova nemufaro wekushandisa zano neruzivo rwaPeter pamisoro yakawanda yeiyi.

Kupfuura izvozvo, vose vari vaviri vanomiririra rudzi rwechishuvo chetekinoroji chandinoda chakatenderedza Omarchy. Drew akashandura kuda kwake kufambisa mafaira pakati pemakomputa kuita Dropbox. Peter akashandura purojekiti yevhiki kuita OpenClaw, uye mukuita akayeuchidza munhu wese kuti chido chei chiripo chemakomputa anonzwa ega, anorongeka, uye anonakidza.

Ndiwo mweya uri seri kweOmacom Foundation zvakare. Tiri pano kupa mari zvivakwa, open-source projects, nevagadziri vanodiwa kuita kuti Linux desktop ive imba inofadza yechizvarwa chinotevera chevashandisi vemakomputa.

Mauya, Drew naPeter. Founding Patrons gumi. Mamiriyoni gumi eUSD. Chiporofita changoswedera neimwe 2 million USD!
