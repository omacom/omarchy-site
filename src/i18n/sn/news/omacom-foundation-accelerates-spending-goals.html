Uyu ndiwo mukana mukuru weLinux. Desktop yave kutorwa nenzira isati yamboitika mumakumi emakore. Ma-agent aita kuti operating system ichinjike. Hardware haisati yambova nani. Uye makwikwi haasati amboda kutsamwisa vashandisi vawo. Hatisi kuzorega nguva ino ichipfuura!

Saka Omacom Foundation iri kukurumidza zvinangwa zvayo zvekushandisa. Zvese zvakaunganidzwa gore rino zvichashandiswa mumakore matatu anotevera. Kwete kuiswa muendowment. Kwete kudonhedzwa kwemakore gumi. Kushandiswa!

Ne13 million USD yatovimbiswa, izvo zvinoreva mabhajeti egore anopfuura 4 million USD a2027, 2028, na2029. Nezvakawanda sezvo mari ichipinda kubva kuvatsigiri vemarudzi ese. Founding, Distinguished, Corporate, nemunhu wese akabatana nechinangwa nhasi.

Mari iyo inoenda kumaopen-source projects akavakirwa paOmarchy. Kumavanyori vanoita kuti inake. Kumavagadziri vanoita kuti imhanye, ienderane, uye ivimbike. Kumakwikwi, magrant, nezvivakwa. Kune zvese zvinodiwa kuzadzisa chiporofita cheLinux paDesktop.

Mari haisi iyo pfungwa. Chinangwa ndicho. Asi zvinobatsira kuva nezvose!
