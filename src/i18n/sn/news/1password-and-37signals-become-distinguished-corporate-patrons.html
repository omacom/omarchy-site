Omacom Foundation yava neDistinguished Corporate Patrons vaviri vekutanga! 1Password ne37signals imwe neimwe iri kupa 100,000 USD pagore kwemakore matatu anotevera, izvo zvinotora mari yefoundation kusvika ku12.6 million USD.

1Password yave chikamu cheOmarchy kubvira zuva rekutanga (uye chikamu cheOmakub zvisati zvaitika!). Nekuti yakachengeta zvitupa zvangu kwemakore mazhinji ndisati ndambofunga kuchinjira kuLinux.

SeDropbox, chimwe chezvishandiso zvakakosha zvinogonesa nzira yeno backup, no cry. Pese pandinogadzira muchina mutsva (izvo zvinoitika katatu pavhiki mazuva ano!), chinhu chekutanga chandinoita Install > Service > 1Password. Ipapo kugadzira ma-agent angu, kupinda muBasecamp neHEY, nezvimwe zvese zviri nyore. Asi zvakachengeteka, zvakavimbika.

37signals yave nzvimbo yekurera Omarchy kubvira isati yava nezita. Tatova tachinjira timu yese yetekinoroji kuOmarchy, uye tanga tichibhadhara mabhiri emarudzi ese munzira inoenda kuQuattro (nezvisati zvaitika). Ikozvino rutsigiro rwakarongeka, kuburikidza nefoundation, nematemu mamwe chete nemunhu wese.

Zvakakodzera kucherechedzawo kuti mari yemakambani nemari yevanhu zvose zvinotenga chinhu chimwe: kucherechedzwa nekuva chikamu chechinangwa. Hapana quid pro quo pano.

Omarchy inogara ichiisa sarudzo dzayo pane zvinoita kuti ive yakanakisa malleable OS munyika. Uye izvo zvinotsanangurwa zvakanyanya ne“by DHH” byline paunoisa system. Isati yava system inoshamisa kumunhu wese, inofanira kutanga yava system inoshamisa kwandiri, pachangu. Isa mask yako wega kutanga nezvose!
