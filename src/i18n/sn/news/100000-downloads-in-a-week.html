Zviuru zana zvakadhawunilodha ISO yeOmarchy muvhiki rapfuura! Vanhu zviuru zana vakasarudza kuti vhiki rino ndiro ravachaedza Linux (kana kudzokera kwairi). Iboka remabhosi!

Panyaya yebandwidth, takakanda petabyte imwe mumazuva manomwe mamwe chete. Chiuru cheterabytes cheLinux muvhiki pakati pemaISO nepackage updates!

Izvi zvingadai zvakanyatsodonhedza Omarchy mune imwe nguva. Kuzvitumira tichimhanyira mamirror kana matorrents (uye kufunga nezvekuda imwe mortgage paimba kuti tibhadhare hosting bill). Asi hatina kuda.

Izvo nekuti Cloudflare iri pamberi pezvose: maISO, mapackage eOmarchy, neArch mirror yedu. Zvose zviri paR2 uye seri kweCDN yavo. Hapana chimwe chazvo chakambodikitira. Uye ndicho chikonzero nei vanhu zviuru zana vaine kudhawunilodha kunokurumidza, kunofinha, kusingana chiitiko kubva kupi zvako munyika muvhiki rapfuura.

Ndatenda kumunhu wese paCloudflare, uye kuna Matthew Prince zvikuru. Matthew ariwo mumwe weFounding Patrons, zvinoreva kuti ari kutsigira chirongwa ichi nezvivakwa nemari yake. Muchas gracias, señor!
