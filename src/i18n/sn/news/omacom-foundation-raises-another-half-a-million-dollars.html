Omacom Foundation yakaunza imwe hafu yemiriyoni yeUSD! Pakati pematokeni matsva, chivimbiso chemakambani chemakore matatu, nezviuru zveUSD kubva kuopen patronage, iwo 510,000 USD akavimbiswa kuLinux yakanaka, inonakidza & ine ma-agent. Izvo zvinounza zvese zvedu zvakavimbiswa nezvipo kusvika ku15.5 million USD!

Kutanga, tiri kugamuchira OpenRouter seDistinguished Corporate Patron ne150,000 USD mumatokeni. Kana mugove iwoyo wapera, tinogona kuvandudza hurongwa nekubvumirana. Matokeni aya achasimbisa ma-agent anovaka, anoedza, uye anochengetedza Omarchy. Patinogona kuisa ma-agent kubasa kugadzirisa mabug, kuongorora mipiro, nekuvandudza distro, tinokurumidza kuita kuti komputa yemunhu wese inake. Mamwe mafuta echinangwa!

Tichayeredzawo mamiriyoni eUSD mumatokeni akapihwa kuburikidza nepuratifomu yakanaka yeOpenRouter. Kuti tigovere matokeni akakodzera kuzvikwata zvakakodzera kubva kumamodheru akakodzera.

Ipapo pane Four Technologies, ichibatana seDistinguished Corporate Patron nechivimbiso che100,000 USD pagore kwemakore matatu. Iwo 300,000 USD kutsigira mapurojekiti enharaunda anovimbwa neOmarchy, zvivakwa zvinoichengeta ichimhanya, nebasa rekuunza Linux yakanaka kuvanhu vakawanda. Kana makambani azvipira kwemakore zvinotipa chivimbo chekuita zvimwe chetezvo.

Heano matauriro anoita timu yeFour Technologies:

Tiri kambani yefintech inotungamirwa neAI, uye makambani akaita seFour Technologies anongowana kuvaka mabatiro aanoita nekuda kweopen-source infrastructure yakavakwa nevamwe vanhu kutanga. Kutsigira Omacom Foundation ndiko kuisa mari seri kwaizvozvo. Tinofunga kuti danho rinotevera reAI hausi umwe app pamusoro peoperating system, iAI yakavakwa zvakananga muoperating system, uye ndiyo bet iri kuitwa neOmarchy. Taida kuva chikamu chenharaunda iri kuita bet iyo, kwete kungobatsirwa nayo gare gare.

Uye kubvira takavhurira patronage kumunhu wese, tagamuchira 60,000 USD kubva kuvanoda kusvika 500 donors kuburikidza neopen patronage. Iyoyo mhinduro inoshamisa kubva kuvanhu vanoda kubatsira kuita kuti izvi ziitike. Mupiro wese unowedzera pane zvatingaite pamwe chete.

Matokeni ekuvaka nawo. Mari yekutsigira mapurojekiti atinovimba nawo. Vanhu nemakambani anoda kuisa chimwe chinhu seri kwekutenda kwavo muLinux padesktop. Ndiwo mabatiro atinoenderera mberi!

Ndatenda kuOpenRouter, Four Technologies, nemunhu wese ava patron. Kana uchida kubatana navo, nyora david@omarchy.org ye founding ne distinguished patronage, uye ipa zvakananga kuopen patronage.
