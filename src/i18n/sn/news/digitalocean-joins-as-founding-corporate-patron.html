DigitalOcean iri kubatana neOmacom Foundation seFounding Corporate Patron, ichipa 1 million USD pagore kwemakore matatu! Uku kuzvipira kwe3 million USD mukuvandudza, kuchengetedza, nekuparadzira Omarchy. Vhoti inoshamisa yekuvimba muOS yakanaka, inonakidza & ine ma-agent yatiri kuvaka.

Ndakashandisa DigitalOcean Droplets kweanopfuura makore gumi. Inzira yakanaka yekuisa muchina weLinux painternet uye kutanga kuvaka chimwe chinhu. Tinoashandisa kumhanya contributors.rubyonrails.org, saiti inopemberera zviuru zvevanhu vakabatsira kuita kuti Rails ive zvayiri. Ichi chivakwa chandavimba nacho kwemakore, saka kuva nekambani iri seri kwayo ichibatana nesu zvinonzwa zvakanaka.

Uye ndiyo patron yakakwana yeagentic OS. DigitalOcean yatova neone-click Droplets dzeHermes nedzeOpenClaw. Unogona kuva neagent kumba padesktop yako yeOmarchy uye imwe ichishanda siku nesikati paDroplet.

Asi chikamu cherutsigiro ichi chakanyatsodzosera kunyemwerera ndechekukurumidza kwazvakaungana. Ndakasvika kuna Paddy Srinivasan, CEO weDigitalOcean, paX neChitatu. Takataura parunhare husiku humwe chete. Ndakatumira chikumbiro neMugovera. NeSvondo, takanga tapedza zvese.

Uku ndiko kumhanya kwekubatana kunogoneka neX, ma-agent, nechido chisingaperi! Kana vanhu vaona mukana mumwe chete uye vachida kuita kuti chimwe chinhu chiitike, hapana chikonzero chekupedza mwedzi tichisvika pahongu. Tinogona kufamba nemwero wemikana iri pamberi pedu.

DigitalOcean inobatana neMeta Superintelligence Labs seFounding Corporate Patron, pamwe chete nevanhu vese nemakambani atova akatsigira chinangwa ichi. Chivimbiso chavo chinounza rutsigiro rwedu rwese kusvika ku18.5 million USD. Imwe nzvimbo yekudyara muvanhu, mapurojekiti, nezvivakwa zvinoita kuti Omarchy inake kumunhu wese.

Sekuhaya vanhu vanoshamisa vakawanda! Tichangounza Krzysztof Wilczyński seHead of Omarchy Kernel naoutfoxxed, muvambi weQuickshell, seHead of Omarchy Shell. Kupa vanhu vakadai mukana wekushanda nguva yakazara kuita kuti Omarchy imhanye, ivimbike, uye ive nesimba zvinogoneka chete nevatsigiri vakaita seDigitalOcean. Rutsigiro rwavo runoshandura zvishuvo zvedu kuita mabasa chaiwo evanhu vanogona kuzvizadzisa.

Ndatenda, Paddy nemunhu wese paDigitalOcean. Ngatiise rutsigiro urwu kubasa uye tiite kuti ramangwana rakanaka, rinonakidza, rine ma-agent reLinux riitike!
