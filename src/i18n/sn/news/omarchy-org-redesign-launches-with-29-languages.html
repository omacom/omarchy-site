Website yeLinux distribution yakanaka, inonakidza inofanira kunaka uye kunakidza zvakare. Saka tapa Omarchy.org kugadziridzwa kwakakwana, uye tiri kuiburitsa mumitauro 29 (nekuti kana wangounganidza ~2m USD mumatokeni, sei kwete?). Mashiripiti madiki echinyakare, zvakawanda zvekuongorora, nekugamuchira kukuru kupasi.

Iyi iwebsite inoda kuti utambe nekomputa yako! Mapixels, mavara, nemaanimation anotakura mweya weOmarchy wakananga mubrowser. Unogona kunzwa nzvimbo usati wadhaunilodha ISO imwe. Ndiko kubatana kwangu pachangu kumakore ekukura munyika yepamhepo isati yave internet yeASCII art, Amiga demoscene, nerunako rune simba rwema90s. Ruvara rwakawanda, polish shoma. Mapixels akawanda, magradients manyoro mashoma.

Ikozvino pane nzvimbo yekuratidza maeditor, materminal, ma-agent, mitambo, Windows VM, nehardware zvinoita kuti Omarchy ibatsire mazuva ese. Ongorora matheme enharaunda, tsvaga plugin, ona maworkstation chaiwo, kana tsvaga musangano pedyo newe. Sangana nevanhu vari kuivaka nevatsigiri vari kuipa mari. Pakupedzisira tava nemukova wepamberi une nzvimbo yakakwana yezvese zviri kuitika mukati.
