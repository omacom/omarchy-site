Update, Gunyana 9, 2026: Rutsigiro urwu rwatsiviwa neoutfoxxed achibatana neOmacom Foundation nguva yakazara seHead of Omarchy Shell uye nhengo yeOmarchy Core.

Mari yakawanda mukati, mari yakawanda kunze! Rutsigiro rwedu rwechipiri rukuru kubva kuOmacom Foundation rwuri kuenda kuna outfoxxed nebasa rake rakanaka paQuickshell. Yava chikamu chekuburitsa pachena kwevhiki chete, asi zvatova pachena kuti ndicho chidimbu chaishaikwa, chakakosha kuti tinyatsozadzisa The Malleable Computer.

Uchapupu huri mumaplugin! Anopfuura chiuru chemapulgin akagadzirwa muvhiki iyo Quattro yabuda. Uku kupupura kunoshamisa kwekuchinjika kweQuickshell, uye kuti ma-agent anoda kunyora QML code.

Rutsigiro urwu runopa mubairo outfoxxed nemupiro wake wakanaka kuOmarchy, uye runobatsira kusimbisa kubatana kuhurongwa hwedu hwenguva refu hweThe Age of Agents (usazvitumira paBluesky, vachapenga!). Rwakarongwa kwemakore matatu kubva pamukova.

Ndatenda yakakosha kuna Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, naPeter Steinberger nekuita kuti izvi zvikoneke. Dai pasina Oligarchy, hatingakwanise kupa mari vagadziri veopen-source seizvi!
