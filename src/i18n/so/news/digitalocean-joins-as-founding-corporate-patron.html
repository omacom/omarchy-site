DigitalOcean waxay ku biirtay Aasaaska Omacom iyadoo ah Taageere Shirkadeed Aasaasi ah, iyadoo ku biirinaysa 1 million USD sannadkii muddo saddex sannadood ah! Taasi waa ballanqaad 3 million USD ah oo loogu talagalay horumarinta, dayactirka, iyo faafinta Omarchy. Waa vote of confidence cajiib ah oo OS-ka quruxda badan, xiisaha leh & agentic ah ee aan dhisayno.

Waxaan isticmaalay DigitalOcean Droplets in ka badan decade. Waa hab wonderfully straightforward ah oo Linux machine internet-ka la saaro laguna bilaabo dhismaha wax. Waxaan u isticmaalnaa inaan ku maamulno contributors.rubyonrails.org, bogga sharfaya kumanaanka qof ee ka caawiyay Rails inay noqoto waxa ay tahay. Tani waa infrastructure aan ku tiirsanaa sannado, markaa in shirkadda ka dambaysa ay nagu soo biirto waxay dareemaysaa si gaar ah fiican.

Waxayna yihiin taageeraha perfect ah ee agentic OS. DigitalOcean horay waxay u bixisaa one-click Droplets loogu talagalay Hermes iyo OpenClaw. Waxaad heli kartaa agent gurigiisa ku jira desktop-kaaga Omarchy iyo mid kale oo around the clock ka shaqeeya Droplet.

Laakiin qaybta patronage-kan ee dhab ahaan iga qoslisay waa sida dhakhso ah ee dhammaantood isu yimaadeen. Waxaan la xiriiray Paddy Srinivasan, CEO-ga DigitalOcean, X Arbacadii. Waan wacnay isla habeenkaas. Proposal ayaan diray Sabtida. Axadda, wax walba waan finalize-garaynay.

Taasi waa speed-ka collaboration ee suurtagal ah X, agents, iyo boundless ambition! Marka dadku arkaan isla fursadda oo ay rabaan inay wax dhacaan, ma jiro sabab lagu qaato bilooyin lagu gaaro haa. Waxaan ku dhaqaaqi karnaa pace-ka possibilities-ka hortayada yaal.

DigitalOcean waxay ku biirtay Meta Superintelligence Labs iyadoo Taageere Shirkadeed Aasaasi ah, oo ay la socdaan shakhsiyaadka iyo shirkadaha horay u soo istaagay inay maalgeliyaan mission-kan. Ballanqaadkoodu wuxuu keenayaa total backing-keena ku dhawaad 18.5 million USD. Meel badan oo lagu maalgeliyo dadka, mashaariicda, iyo infrastructure-ka ka dhiga Omarchy mid qof kasta u wanaagsan.

Sida shaqaaleysiinta dad cajiib ah oo badan! Waxaan hadda keenay Krzysztof Wilczyński oo ah Head of Omarchy Kernel iyo outfoxxed, abuuraha Quickshell, oo ah Head of Omarchy Shell. Siinta dadka noocan ah fursad ay full time uga shaqeeyaan Omarchy mid dhaqso badan, la isku halayn karo, oo awood badan ka dhigidda waxay suurtagal ku tahay taageerayaal sida DigitalOcean. Taageeradoodu waxay ambitions-keena u rogtaa actual jobs dadka samayn kara.

Mahadsanid, Paddy iyo qof kasta oo DigitalOcean ah. Aan taageeradan shaqo gelino oo aan sameyno mustaqbalka Linux ee quruxda badan, xiisaha leh, agentic ah!
