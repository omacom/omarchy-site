Waxaan shaqaaleysiinaynaa Emir Beganović oo ah Head of Infrastructure ee Omarchy! Wuxuu bilaabayaa Nofeembar 1 isagoo ah Aasaaska Omacom shaqaaleysiigii full-time ahaa ee saddexaad, isagoo ku biiraya Krzysztof Wilczyński on Kernel iyo outfoxxed on Shell.

Halkan waa sida Emir u sharraxayo safarkiisa gaar ah ee Omarchy:

Jacaylkayga Linux wuxuu dib u noqdaa in ka badan labaatan sannadood. Waxaan noqday Ubuntu developer anigoo weli teenager ah waxaana si qoto dheer ugu jiray local community-ga. Career-kaygu waxay i martay saddex waddan, anigoo ka shaqaynaya reliability iyo performance startups iyo scaleups iyo leading teams. Waxaan ku qaatay ku dhawaad toddobo sannadood Booking.com SRE department-keeda, anigoo leading work ku sameynaya build systems iyo CI infrastructure. Ugu dambayn, waxaan dhisay Isorun, halkaas oo aan ka shaqaynayay microVMs, snapshots, isolation, scheduling, iyo disposable compute. Hadda waxaan experience-gaas dib ugu celinayaa jacaylkaygii koowaad.

Waxa dhab ahaan attention-kayga qabtay Emir waa TryOmarchy. Wuxuu qaaday desktop-ka Omarchy, browser ku riday (ma aha reimplementation, ee waa real thing), wuxuuna ka dhigay booting seconds gudahii ordinary CPU infrastructure Cloudflare. Waxaan bilaabnay inaan ka hadalno silly experiment-gaas, conversation-kuna si dhakhso ah ayuu ugu fiday laptops, architectures cusub, OEMs, iyo infrastructure-ka aan u baahan doonno haddii Omarchy ay ku ordi doonto meel kasta oo aan rabno. Waxaan ogaanay inaan wadaagno isla vision-ka.

Taas waa waxa Emir loo keenayo inuu hoggaamiyo.

Infrastructure Omarchy macnaheedu waa wax ka badan running servers. Waa waxa aan u isticmaalno inaan ku dhisno, tijaabino, oo dirno Omarchy, iyo inaan nidaamka si reliably ah u shaqeyno iyadoo aan ku fidino hardware badan iyo architectures badan. Waa in releases laga dhigo dependable, failures laga dhigo reproducible, iyo in qof kasta oo mashruuca ku jira la siiyo solid foundation si ay dhakhso u dhaqaaqaan.

Waxaa jira wax badan oo la explore-gareeyo! Omarchy waa inay si qurux badan ugu shaqaysaa mashiinka miiskaaga saaran, laakiin ma jiro sabab ay halkaas ku joojiso. TryOmarchy waxay na siisay glimpse waxa dhaca marka starting Linux computer noqdo mid fudud sida opening web page, iyadoo taste Linux hal click u jirta. Waxaan arki doonnaa halka ay horseedayso.

Mahad qof kasta oo shaqadan maalgeliya, waxaan siin karnaa dadka sida Emir waqti ay all in ugu galaan Omarchy. Taageerayaashayadu waxay suurtogeliyaan in ambitious experiments loo rogo lasting work, iyadoo full-time people dedicated u ah inay nidaamka oo dhan ka dhigaan mid wanaagsan. Waad ku mahadsan tihiin patronage-kooda.

Soo dhawee, Emir! Aan Omarchy meel kasta ka orodsiinno.
