Omarchy waxay u kortay si dhakhso ah oo kumanaan qof ay mar qudha imanayaan, badankooduna waxay ka imanayaan macOS ama Windows. Taasi waa rakibid first ah oo badan, “where do I even start” badan, iyo su'aalo badan oo ka faa'iidaysta jawaab dhab ah oo ka timaadda qof used u ah caawinta kuwa kale.

Soo gala Omarchy Rangers: Koox official ah oo cusub oo loo dejiyay inay ka caawiso dadka inay wadada helaan.

Saddex qof, saddex continents, taas oo roughly ujeedadu tahay. Community-ga Omarchy ma haysto office hours time zone kaliya, mana aha inay haystaan dadka caawinaya. Discord-ka mar walba waa buzzing.

Sababtoo ah ku dhawaad qofna Linux kaligiis uma wareego. Badana way joogaan sababtoo ah qof ayaa toddobaadkii koowaad ka dhigay welcoming. Qaar markaas waxay favor-ka celiyaan iyagoo isla sidaas u sameeya qofka xiga. Sidaas ayay waxan oo dhan ku koraan.
