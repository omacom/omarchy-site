Aasaaska Omacom waxay soo ururisay half million USD oo kale! Inta u dhaxaysa fresh supply tokens ah, three-year corporate pledge, iyo kumanaan dollar ah oo open patronage ah, taasi waa roughly 510,000 USD oo dheeraad ah oo loo ballan qaaday Linux qurux badan, xiiso leh & agentic ah. Taas waxay total pledges iyo donations keentaa ku dhawaad 15.5 million USD!

Marka hore, waxaan soo dhaweynaynaa OpenRouter oo ah Taageere Shirkadeed oo Sharaf leh iyadoo 150,000 USD oo tokens ah. Marka allotment-gaas la kharash gareeyo, waxaan renew gareyn karnaa arrangement-ka by mutual agreement. Tokens-kan waxay power-garayn doonaan agents-ka dhisa, tijaabiya, oo maintain-gareeya Omarchy. Inta badan ee aan agents shaqo gelin karno fixing bugs, reviewing contributions, iyo improving distro, in that faster ayaan kombuyuutarka qof kasta ka dhigi karnaa mid wanaagsan. Fuel badan mission-ka!

Sidoo kale waxaan flow gareyn doonnaa millions dollars oo tokens ah ee la donate-gareeyay iyadoo excellent OpenRouter platform la marayo. Si aan right tokens ugu apportion-garayno right teams from the right models.

Markaas waxaa jira Four Technologies, oo ku biirtay sida Taageere Shirkadeed oo Sharaf leh iyadoo pledge 100,000 USD sannadkii muddo saddex sannadood ah. Taasi waa 300,000 USD si loo taageero community projects Omarchy ku tiirsan tahay, infrastructure-ka ku haya running, iyo shaqada keenista beautiful Linux dad badan. In shirkaduhu commit gareeyaan sannado waxay na siisaa confidence inaan isla sidaas sameyno.

Halkan waa sida Four Technologies team-ku u dhigayo:

Waxaan nahay AI-forward fintech company, shirkadaha sida Four Technologies waxay kaliya u dhisaan sida aan u dhisno sababtoo ah open-source infrastructure dad kale horay u dhiseen. Taageerista Aasaaska Omacom waa sida aan lacag behind dhigno. Waxaan u malaynaynaa in next stage AI ma aha app kale layered operating system dusheed, ee waa AI built directly operating system-ka, taana waa bet Omarchy samaynayso. Waxaan rabnay inaan qayb ka noqono community-ga bet-gaas samaynaya, ma aha kaliya benefiting later.

Tan iyo opening patronage qof kasta, waxaan helnay roughly 60,000 USD oo ka timid ku dhawaad 500 donors iyadoo open patronage la marayo. Taasi waa incredible response dadka raba inay ka caawiyaan in tani dhacdo. Contribution kasta waxay ku dartaa waxa aan wada samayn karno.

Tokens lagu dhiso. Lacag lagu taageero projects aan ku tiirsan nahay. Dad iyo shirkado diyaar u ah inay wax behind dhigaan belief-kooda Linux on the desktop. Sidan ayaan ku sii soconnaa!
