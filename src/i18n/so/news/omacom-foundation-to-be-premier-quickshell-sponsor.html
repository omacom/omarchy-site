Update, Sebtembar 9, 2026: Sponsorship-kan waxaa supersede-gareeyay outfoxxed oo ku biirtay Aasaaska Omacom full time sida Head of Omarchy Shell iyo xubin Omarchy Core ah.

Lacag badan soo gashay, lacag badan baxday! Sponsorship-kayagii waynaa ee labaad ee Aasaaska Omacom waxay tagaysaa outfoxxed shaqadiisa fiican ee Quickshell. Waxay public release ka mid ahayd toddobaad oo keliya, laakiin horay crystal clear waa inuu ahaa missing, pivotal piece si aan truly u gaarno The Malleable Computer.

Caddeyntu waxay ku jirtaa plugins-ka! In ka badan kun plugins ayaa la abuuray toddobaadkii Quattro soo baxday. Taasi waa marag absolutely astounding ah oo technical flexibility-ga Quickshell, iyo inta agents jecel yihiin inay qoraan QML code.

Sponsorship-kan wuxuu thus abaalmarinayaa outfoxxed outstanding contribution-kiisa Omarchy, wuxuuna caawiyaa cement connection-ka long-term plans-keena loogu talagalay The Age of Agents (don't post that on Bluesky, way waalan doonaan!). Waa termed for saddex sannadood out the gate.

Mahad gaar ah Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, iyo Peter Steinberger oo tani suurtogeliyay. Haddii aan Oligarchy jirin, ma awoodno inaan open-source developers sidan u maalgelino!
