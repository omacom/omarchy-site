Waa waqtigii lagu riyoodo wayn. Omarchy Quattro waxay dadka siisay fursad ay ku experience-gareeyaan waxa malleable computer-ka mustaqbalka u eeg yahay, wayna jecel yihiin (a lot!). Hadda waxay dareemaysaa moral obligation in mustaqbalkan si ballaaran loo heli karo oo fundamentally loo beddelo sida dadku ula xiriiraan kombuyuutarradooda markii ugu horreysay wax u eg forever.

Si taas loo sameeyo, waxaan incorporate-garaynayaa Aasaaska Omacom si loo hubiyo in mission-kan si buuxda loo maalgeliyo, durable yahay, oo diyaar u yahay accelerate.

Nonprofit foundation-kan waxay hayn doontaa trademarks-ka, waxay maalgelin doontaa infrastructure-ka, waxay horumarin doontaa shaqada, waxayna taageeri doontaa mashaariicda open-source-ka ah iyo developers-ka Omarchy ku tiirsan tahay.
