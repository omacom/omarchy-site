Aasaaska Omacom waxay hadda dhaaftay 13 million USD! Afartii Taageerayaal Sharaf leh ee ugu horreeyay waxay ballan qaadeen 100,000 USD mid kasta mission-ka:

Ryan R. Hughes halkan introduction uma baahna. Waa xubin Omarchy Core ah, garabkaygii distro tan iyo maalintii koowaad, iyo partner Oodle ah. Hadda wuxuu aasaaska ku taageerayaa lacagtiisa gaar ah sidoo kale. Taasi waa rumaysad dhab ah!

Ed Huang waa aasaasaha iyo CTO-ga PingCAP, shirkadda ka dambaysa TiDB, database-ka SQL ee distributed ah. Wuxuu toban sannadood ku qaatay isagoo caddeynaya in infrastructure open-source ah oo dhab ah ay sidoo kale noqon karto business dhab ah.

Adrien Treccani wuxuu aasaasay Metaco, platform-ka Swiss-ka ah ee digital-asset custody ee Ripple iibsatay. Waa crypto pioneer kale oo ku biiraya mission-ka Omarchy looga dhigayo meel fiican oo crypto ah.

Max Schoening waa Head of Product ee Notion, ka horna wuxuu maamulay design-ka Heroku wuxuuna ahaa VP of Design ee GitHub. Wuxuu qaabeeyay qalab badan oo developers jecel yihiin inta dadka badankiisu waligood ogaan doonaan.

Waxaa la socda laban-iyo-tobankii Taageerayaal Aasaasi iyo labadayadii Taageerayaal Shirkadeed oo Sharaf leh, taasi waa siddeed-iyo-toban taageere iyo 13 million USD oo loo ballan qaaday wax ka yar laba toddobaad gudahii.

Soo dhaweeya, afartiina oo dhan. Mission-ku wuu koraa!
