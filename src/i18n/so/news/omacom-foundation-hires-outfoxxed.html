Waxaan shaqaaleysiinaynaa outfoxxed, abuuraha Quickshell, oo ah Head of Omarchy Shell! Wuxuu ku biirayaa Aasaaska Omacom full time wuxuuna noqonayaa xubin Omarchy Core ah. Qofkii dhisay toolkit-kii Quattro suurtogeliyay ayaa soo galaya si uu naga caawiyo inaan waxaas oo dhan dayaxa u dirno.

Tani waxay supersede-garaynaysaa sponsorship-kii Quickshell ee saddexda sannadood ahaa ee aan Agoosto ku dhawaaqnay. Halkii sponsorship-gaas, outfoxxed wuxuu noqon doonaa shaqaale full-time ah oo Aasaaska Omacom ah, isagoo hoggaaminaya shaqadayada Omarchy Shell. Waxaan creator-ka dhigaynaa xarunta waxa aan dhisayno.

Shell waa pivotal agentic OS-ka aan samaynayno. Waa bar-ka, menus-ka, notifications-ka, widgets-ka, iyo little interactions oo dhan ee kombuyuutarka ka dhiga mid kuu gaar ah. Quickshell waxay na siisaa building blocks si aan experience-gaas QML ugu qaabayno. Ku dar agents incredibly good QML qorista, si lama filaan ah beddelista desktop-kaagu waxay noqotaa wax aad codsan karto, tijaabin karto, oo wadaagi karto.

Caddeyntu waxay ku jirtaa plugins-ka! Markii aan sponsorship-ka ku dhawaaqnay, community-gu horay waxay u abuurteen in ka badan kun Quattro toddobaadkii koowaad. Hadda catalogue-ku waxay ku dhowdahay saddex kun oo plugins ah. Games aad ka ciyaari karto bar-ka, panels track-gareeya coding agents-kaaga, music players, iyo xataa cat ku wareegaya bar-kaaga oo sugaya in la petted. Dadku waxay qaadanayaan nidaam used u ahaa fixed waxayna ka dhigayaan mid iyaga u gaar ah. Taasi waa malleable computer-ka oo noolaanaya.

In creator-ka Quickshell on board yahay macnaheedu waa inaan stack-ka oo dhan wada ka shaqayn karno. Ka dhig shell-ka mid dhaqso badan oo la isku halayn karo. Ka dhig mid fudud in plugin fiican la dhiso. Horumari foundations-ka marka plugin author la kulmo limitation. U rog waxa aan ka baranayno kumanaan experiments experience wanaagsan qof kasta oo Omarchy boot-gareeya. Hubi in waxaas oo dhan ay safe iyo secure yihiin.

Ku biirista Omarchy Core, outfoxxed wuxuu ka caawin doonaa dejinta jihada distro oo dhan. Shell aad ayay ugu central tahay experience-ka si ay afterthought u noqoto. Waa halka badan oo beauty-ga, fun-ka, iyo possibilities-ka agentic OS noqdaan wax aad dhab ahaan arki karto oo isticmaali karto.

outfoxxed waa shaqaaleysiigii full-time ahaa ee labaad ee Aasaaska Omacom, iyadoo la socota Krzysztof Wilczyński, Head of Omarchy Kernel-keena. Iyadoo dadka hoggaaminaya Kernel iyo Shell labadaba on board yihiin, waxaan maalgelinaynaa foundations-ka experience-ka oo dhan. Waxaad la kulmi kartaa staff-ka aasaaska bogga.

Waxaan sii wadi doonnaa inaan shaqaaleysiino invitation iyadoo aan helno dadka saxda ah iyadoo shaqadooda community-ga la marayo. (Fadlan inbox-kayga CVs ha ku buuxin!)

Tani waa waxa backing-ka aasaaska na ogolaado inaan sameyno: siin exceptional open-source developers waqti ay all in u galaan. Mahad qof kasta oo shaqadaas maalgeliya, waxaan ambitions-keena badan u rogaynaa full-time jobs.

Soo dhawee, outfoxxed! Aan kombuyuutarkan ka dhigno mid xataa malleable badan.
