Aasaaska Omacom way sii kobcaysaa! Labo Taageerayaal Aasaasi ah oo kale ayaa mission-keena ku biirtay iyadoo 1 million USD mid kasta, iyagoo khasnadayada gaarsiinaya 12 million USD: Brian Armstrong iyo Yunjie Dai.

Brian Armstrong waa aasaasaha iyo CEO-ga Coinbase, oo ku dhalatay iyadoo isticmaalaysa Ruby on Rails, waxayna kortay booska ugu sarreeya crypto iyadoo in ka badan boqol million oo users ah. Brian sidoo kale wuu horeeyay inuu maya ku yiraahdo nonsense-ka siyaasadda shaqada markuu ku dhawaaqay Coinbase inuu yahay shirkad mission-focused ah 2020. Wuxuu qaaday heat badan stance-gaas waqtigaas, laakiin tan iyo markaas si buuxda waa vindicated (a true man of the Night's Watch!).

Crypto, well, fikirkayga waan beddelay sannado ka hor. Iyadoo Brian nala joogo, waxaan Omarchy ka dhigi doonnaa meel fiican oo crypto ah. Sababtoo ah wax kasta waan hagaajin karnaa!

Yunjie Dai waa games entrepreneur Shiinaha ka yimid oo maamula TapTap, mid ka mid ah platforms-ka ugu wayn dalka ee game discovery iyo distribution. Mid aan revenue share ka qaadan developers. Laakiin aan isaga is soo bandhigo, sababtoo ah letter uu soo diray waa wonderful:

21 sannadood ka hor, 2005, waxaan hadda ka qalin jabiyay jaamacadda markii aan daawaday fiidiyahaaga oo blog ku dhisaya Ruby on Rails iyo TextMate Mac OS X qiyaastii 15 daqiiqo gudahii. Waxaan iibsaday MacBook cad wax yar ka dib, Windows ayaan ka tagay, TextMate ayaan iibsaday, waxaana bilaabay barashada Ruby. Waxay si wayn u beddeshay inta aan ku raaxaystay coding-ka.

Waxaan ahay games entrepreneur Shiinaha ka yimid, qoyskaygana hadda US ayaan ku nool nahay. Waxaan dhisnaa games, sidoo kale waxaan maamulnaa TapTap… Si ka duwan App Store ama Google Play, revenue share developers kama qaadno, iyadoo aan weli ku shaqayno scale macno leh iyo faa'iido. Dareenku wuxuu ku dhow yahay waxa aan mar walba ku majeertay 37signals: independent ahaada, kasbada noloshiina, kiro ha qaadanina sababtoo ah waad kartaan, oo dhisa wax tar leh oo dadku doortaan inay joogaan.

20 sannadood ka hor, Apple waxay samaysay software-kii ugu fiicnaa adduunka. Maanta, dareenka hamigaas wuxuu u muuqdaa mid largely gone ah. Waxaan u malaynayaa in badan oo naga mid ah ay sugayaan operating system mar kale xiiso dareema - modern, opinionated, iyo truly made for developers. Hadda, iyadoo AI software-ka increasingly malleable ka dhigaysa, openness matters more than ever: nidaamka laftiisu waa inuu noqdaa wax aan fahmi karno, beddeli karno, oo ka dhigan karno mid annaga u gaar ah. Omarchy waxay dareemaysaa inay noqon karto taas.

Markii Steve Jobs Mac dhisayay, wuxuu kooxdiisa u arkayay burcad-badeed - waxay xataa calan burcad ka dul lulayaan building-kooda. “Better to be a pirate than join the navy.” Omarchy waxay i siisaa in yar oo dareen la mid ah. Maybe it's time for the pirates again. 🏴‍☠️🏴‍☠️🏴‍☠️
