Waa habkee ka fiican in lagu bilaabo kharash-garaynta khasnad aan hadda u soo ururinnay Aasaaska Omacom marka loo eego Linux kid-ka ugu cracked Poland: Vaxry!

Sida abuuraha Hyprland, wuxuu mas'uul ka yahay mid ka mid ah saddexda core technologies ee Omarchy (Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), aadna waan ugu faraxsanahay inaan ku dhawaaqo exclusive sponsorship u ogolaata inuu si buuxda diiradda u saaro horumarinteeda iyadoon loo baahnayn inuu ka fikiro commercial development ama fundraising.

Waxaan la saxiixanaynaa long term Vaxry si aan u taageerno long term future-ka Omarchy. Exclusive sponsorship deal-keenu waa set for saddex sannadood, oo leh option laba kale ah.

Deal-ku sidoo kale waxay ogolaataa Hyprland Hyprperks program-kii jira in free loo sii daayo, qof kasta, maadaama paid subscriptions dhammaan doonaan. Laakiin Hyprland wali waxay aqbali doontaa personal donations si individuals buy into development-ka mashruuca iyo in la taageero Vaxry iyo kooxda.

Mahad badan 37signals (hello self! 😂), Butterfly, iyo Framework ka caawinta Hyprland inay halkan gaarto. Aad ayaan ugu faraxsanahay in Aasaaska Omacom ay la wareegi karto Oktoobar 10, oo ay siiso mid ka mid ah developers-ka ugu fiican new Linux moment fursad all-in ku gala.
