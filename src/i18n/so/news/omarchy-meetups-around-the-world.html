Omarchy waxay u kortay bulsho global ah, iyadoo ~35,000 xubnood ku leh Discord-ka, hadda waa waqtigii aan fool ka fool kulanno. Markaas waxaan bilownaa Omarchy Meetups iyadoo kalandar la wadaago loogu talagalay kulammada adduunka oo dhan.

Kuwani waa events ay bulshadu maamusho. Permission uma baahnid inaad mid qabanqaabiso, qofna magaalo gaar ah ma laha, qof kasta meel kasta mid ku qaban karaa. Ka hadal Omarchy, Linux, open source, programming, customization, ama hacker culture-ka ku dhow ee dadka ka farxiya in kombuyuutarrada mar kale fun laga dhigo.

Qof kasta u fur, sponsorship kasta shaaci, hana ka dhigin magaca Omarchy ganacsi events ama sales pitch. Waxaa dheer, u dhaqan sida dad civilized ah (oo leh child-like wonder), oo waqti fiican ku qaado.

Keen laptop-kaaga. Muuji setup-kaaga. Isweydaarso themes iyo plugins. Ka caawi qof rakibidiisii koowaad. Ujeedadu waa in dadka danaynaya kombuyuutarrada personal, programmable, beautiful ah la isu keeno hal qol.

Waxaad heli kartaa meetup soo socda ama soo gudbin kartaa mid kuu gaar ah iyadoo kalandarka global ah ee Omarchy ee Luma la marayo. Aan tani dhaqdhaqaaq worldwide ah ka dhigno!
