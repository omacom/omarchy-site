Tani waa fursadda wayn ee Linux. Desktop-ku waa up for grabs hab aanu ahayn decades. Agents-ku waxay operating system-ka ka dhigeen malleable. Hardware-ku waligiis ma fiicnaan. Competition-kuna waligiis ma eagerayn inay annoy-gareeyaan users-kooda. Moment-kan ma dayn doonno inuu dhaafo!

Markaas Aasaaska Omacom waxay accelerate-garaynaysaa spending goals-keeda. Wax kasta oo sannadkan la soo ururiyo waxaa la kharash gareyn doonaa saddexda soo socda. Ma aha endowment la dhigay. Ma aha decade la dhibciyay. La kharash gareeyay!

Iyadoo 13 million USD horay loo ballan qaaday, taas macnaheedu waa yearly budgets ka badan 4 million USD 2027, 2028, iyo 2029. Iyo wax badan marka lacag ka timaaddo taageerayaal nooc kasta ah. Aasaasi, Sharaf leh, Shirkadeed, iyo qof kasta oo mission-ka maanta ku biiray.

Lacagtaas waxay tagtaa mashaariicda open-source-ka ah ee Omarchy lagu dhisay. Fannaaniinta ka dhiga mid qurux badan. Developers-ka ka dhiga mid dhaqso badan, compatible ah, oo secure ah. Competitions, grants, iyo infrastructure. Wax kasta oo loo baahan yahay si loo fuliyo Linux on the Desktop prophecy-ga.

Lacagtu ma aha ujeedadu. Mission-ku waa. Laakiin waxay hubaal caawisaa in labadaba la haysto!
