Website-ka loogu talagalay Linux distribution qurux badan oo xiiso leh waa inuu isna qurux badan yahay oo xiiso leh. Markaas waxaan Omarchy.org siinnay redesign complete ah, waxaana ku bilownaa 29 luuqadood (sababtoo ah marka aad hadda ~2m USD oo tokens ah soo ururisay, maxaa diidaya?). Sixir retro ah oo yar, wax badan oo la sahamin karo, iyo soo dhawayn adduunka oo aad u wayn.

Kani waa website raba inaad kombuyuutarkaaga la ciyaarto! Pixels-ka, midabada, iyo animations-ku waxay spirit-ka Omarchy si toos ah browser-ka u keenaan. Waxaad dareemi kartaa meesha ka hor intaadan download single ISO. Sidoo kale waa xiriirkayga personal ah ee formative years pre-internet online world ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, iyo vibrant aesthetic 90s. Midab badan, polish yar. Pixels badan, soft gradients yar.

Hadda waxaa jira meel lagu muujiyo editors-ka, terminals-ka, agents-ka, games-ka, Windows VM-ka, iyo hardware-ka ka dhiga Omarchy mid useful every day. Dhex mar themes-ka bulshada, hel plugin, arag workstations dhab ah, ama discover meetup kuu dhow. La kulma dadka dhisaya iyo patrons maalgeliya. Ugu dambayn waxaan leenahay albaab hore oo meel ku filan wax kasta oo gudaha ka dhacaya.

Albaabkaas hore hadda wuxuu ku hadlaa luuqaddaada. Carabi, Bangali, Katalan, Danish, Ingiriisi, Filipino, Finnish, Faransiis, Giriig, Hindi, Hungarian, Icelandic, Irish, Talyaani, Jabbaan, Kuuriyaan, Lithuanian, Polish, Boortuqiis, Shiinaha La Fududeeyay, Sinhala, Isbaanish, Swedish, Tamil, Thai, Turkish, Urdu, Uzbek, iyo Fiitnaam. Bogga ugu weyn iyo every news story waa la turjumay. Buugga wali waa English; kan xiga ayaan tackle gareyn doonnaa. Kuwani waa initial translations, sixitaanka dadka native very welcome through site repository-ga.
