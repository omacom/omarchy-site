Maalgelinta Aasaaska Omacom waxay hadda gaartay 10 million USD iyadoo labo Taageerayaal Aasaasi ah oo cusub ay mission-ka ku biireen: Drew Houston, cofounder iyo co-CEO ee Dropbox, iyo Peter Steinberger, abuuraha OpenClaw.

Sida siddeedii Taageerayaal Aasaasi ee asalka ahaa, Drew iyo Peter mid kasta wuxuu ku biirinayaa 1 million USD. Taas waxay aasaaska siinaysaa toban taageere iyo TEN MILLION DOLLARS oo nadiif ah si ay u kaxayso mission-keena audacious ah ee Linux on the desktop looga dhigo mid aad u wayn.

Waxay extra sweet tahay in Drew iyo Peter on board yihiin sababtoo ah anigu waxaan ahaa fan wayn oo labadooda iyo accomplishments-kooda ah. Waxaan ahaa customer enthusiastic ah oo Dropbox ah ku dhawaad laba decades, waxayna central u tahay sida aan u noolaan karo iyadoo no backup, no cry haddana constantly computers beddelaya.

Taas daraaddeed Omarchy waxay la socotaa Dropbox integration fiican isla markaaba. Kaliya tag Install > Service > Dropbox, waxaadna heli doontaa file manager integration iyo bespoke menu panel controls-ka.

OpenClaw-na waxay essentially bixisay roadmap-ka sida Omarchy ula tacaali lahayd explosive growth-keeda. Sidee ula tacaashaa PR backlogs si lama filaan ah parabolic u noqda? Ka waran amniga plugins ku wareegsan? Sidee meetups magaca mashruuca loo maamulaa? Horay waxaan ugu raaxaystay inaan Peter advice-kiisa iyo experience-kiisa ka faa'iidaysto dhowr topics.

Waxaa dheer, labaduba waxay matalaan exactly nooca technical ambition ee aan Omarchy ku wareejin rabo. Drew wuxuu personal itch oo ku saabsan files u wareejinta computers u rogay Dropbox. Peter wuxuu weekend project u rogay OpenClaw, process-kana wuxuu qof kasta xasuusiyay inta appetite ee loo qabo computers dareema personal, programmable, iyo fun.

Taasi waa spirit-ka ka dambeeya Aasaaska Omacom sidoo kale. Waxaan halkan u joognaa inaan maalgelino infrastructure-ka, mashaariicda open-source-ka ah, iyo developers-ka loo baahan yahay si Linux desktop looga dhigo delightful home generation-ka soo socda ee computer users.

Soo dhaweeya, Drew iyo Peter. Toban Taageerayaal Aasaasi ah. Toban million oo dollar. Prophecy-gu waxay hadda 2 million USD u dhawaatay!
