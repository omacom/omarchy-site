Update, September 9, 2026: outfoxxed a ɔkaa Omacom Foundation ho full time sɛ Head of Omarchy Shell ne Omarchy Core muni asi saa sponsorship yi anan.

Sika foforɔ aba, sika foforɔ kɔ! Yɛn sponsorship kɛseɛ a ɛtɔ so mmienu fi Omacom Foundation mu rekɔ outfoxxed nkyɛn ma nʼadwuma nwanwa wɔ Quickshell so. Wɔadi dapɛn wɔ public release mu, nanso ada adi pefee sɛ ɛno ne ade a na ayera, a ɛho hia, na yɛatumi anya The Malleable Computer ankasa.

Adanseɛ wɔ plugins mu! Plugins apem ne akyi na wɔayɛ wɔ dapɛn a Quattro aba mu. Ɛno yɛ adanseɛ nwanwa paa ma Quickshell technical flexibility, ne sɛnea agents pɛ QML code kyerɛw.

Enti saa sponsorship yi tua outfoxxed ka ma nʼmmoa nwanwa a ɔde maa Omarchy, na ɛboa ma nkitahodi a ɛkɔ yɛn long-term plans ma The Age of Agents no yɛ den (mma mfa nntɔ Bluesky so, wɔbɛbɔ dam!). Wɔayɛ no mfeɛ mmiɛnsa fi mfiase.

Medaase titiriw ma Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, ne Peter Steinberger ma eyi aba mu. Sɛ Oligarchy nni hɔ a, anka yɛrentumi mfa sika mma open-source developers sɛ yeinom!
