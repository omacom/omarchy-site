Omarchy nyɛ Linux distribution a ɛyɛ fɛ, ɛyɛ anika, na ɛwɔ agents ma wɔn a wɔde bɛyɛ adwuma nko. Wɔsi no saa ara nso! Ɛwɔ ahoɔfɛ, anika, ne tokens pii. Nokorɛm, Omarchy Quattro no, agents na wɔasi no mpo. Efisɛ wɔyɛ biribi sɛ bash, QML, ne Linux issues siesie ho adwuma yie kɛse.

Sɛ yɛbɛkɔ so ama Omarchy ayɛ yie seesei a yɛatwe users ne cocreators mpempem ɔha no, ehia sɛ yɛma tokens no kɔ so. Eyi ne sɛnea yɛbɛkɔ ntɛm, yɛasiesie, na yɛade mmoa nwanwa a aba no nyinaa aba. Yɛwɔ backlog a ɛyɛ PRs 1,600 ne akyi fi dapɛn kakra akyi a yɛyii adi! Obiara rentumi mfa mu a agentic mmoa nni mu.

Enti mʼani agye sɛ merebɔ dawuru sɛ yɛanya tokens motherload fi labs kɛse no mu dodoɔ ama mission yi! Deɛ ɛdi kan ne Meta Superintelligence Labs a wɔreba sɛ Founding Token Patron wɔ 1.5 million USD mu tokens! Wɔn Meta Muse Spark model foforɔ no reyɛ numbers pa wɔ boɔ a ɛyɛ nwanwa so. Enti 1.5 million USD yɛ tokens a ɛnni ano! (Yɛbɛbɔ mmɔden ama wɔasa nanso!)

Nanso yɛn ani agye sɛ yɛregye frontier labs foforɔ mmienu a wɔdi kan no, Anthropic ne OpenAI, sɛ Distinguished Token Patrons wɔ 150,000 USD mu token donations biara. Eyi bɛyɛ mfasoɔ kɛse bere a wɔde security clearance a ehia na wɔayi guardrails a yɛhia ma security nhwehwɛmu ne fixes no aka ho.

Na ma open-weight models, mʼani agye sɛ merebɔ dawuru sɛ Fireworks yɛ Distinguished Token Patron nso wɔ 150,000 USD mu tokens a wɔahyɛ ho bɔ mu. Mayɛ Kimi K models no user daa titiriw, na titiriw bere a ɛreyɛ adwuma wɔ fast mode mu. Yɛde saa tokens ntɛm yi bɛyɛ adwuma pa nso!

Abom, eyi yɛ 1.95 million USD foforɔ a wɔahyɛ ho bɔ ama Omacom Foundation. Ɛno de yɛn pledges nyinaa kɔ 14.95 million USD. Number a ɛyɛ crazy sɛ wobɛkyerɛw ama distro a early detractors buu no sɛ dotfiles kuw (a sɛ yɛka nokorɛ no na ɛnyɛ mfomso koraa saa berɛ no, nanso yɛkɔɔ so siesiee biribiara!).

Sɛ tokens yi wɔ hɔ a, yɛbɛtumi asiesie biribiara. Na ɛno ne deɛ yɛbɛyɛ!
