Berɛ aso sɛ yɛnsusuw kɛse. Omarchy Quattro ama nnipa anya kwan ahunu sɛnea daakye malleable kɔmputa teɛ, na wɔpɛ (kɛse!). Seesei ɛte sɛ asɛdeɛ a ɛyɛ kronkron sɛ yɛbɛma daakye yi atrɛ ama obiara na yɛasakra sɛnea nnipa ne wɔn kɔmputa di nkitaho kane wɔ berɛ a ɛte sɛ afebɔɔ mu.

Sɛ yɛbɛyɛ saa pɛpɛɛpɛ a, mereyi Omacom Foundation asi na mahwɛ sɛ mission yi nya sika a ɛyɛ ma, ɛtena hɔ, na asiesie sɛ ɛbɛkɔ ntɛm.

Nonprofit foundation yi bɛkura trademarks no, de sika ama infrastructure, ahyɛ adwuma no ho nkuran, na aboa open-source dwumadie ne developers a Omarchy gyina soɔ no.
