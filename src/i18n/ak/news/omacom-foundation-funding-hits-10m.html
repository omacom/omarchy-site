Omacom Foundation sika aka 10 million USD wɔ Founding Patrons foforɔ mmienu a wɔaka mission no ho mu: Drew Houston, cofounder ne co-CEO wɔ Dropbox, ne Peter Steinberger, bɔfoɔ a ɔbɔɔ OpenClaw.

Sɛ Founding Patrons awotwe a ɛdi kan no, Drew ne Peter biara de 1 million USD rema. Ɛno ma foundation no patrons du ne 10 million USD a ɛyɛ fɛ ma yɛn mission a ɛyɛ hu a ɛyɛ sɛ yɛbɛma Linux wɔ desktop so aba mu kɛse.

Ɛyɛ dɛ kɛse sɛ Drew ne Peter ka ho efisɛ mʼani agye wɔn mmienu ne wɔn nkonimdie ho ankasa. Mayɛ Dropbox customer a mʼani agye ho mfeɛ aduonu bɛyɛ, na ɛyɛ mfinimfini ma sɛnea metumi atena no backup, no cry nanso masakra kɔmputa daa.

Ɛno nti Omarchy de Dropbox integration pa mena firi adaka no mu. Kɔ Install > Service > Dropbox nko ara, na wobɛnya file manager integration ne bespoke menu panel ma controls.

Na OpenClaw de roadmap ama sɛnea Omarchy ɛsɛ sɛ ɛdi nʼnyin a ɛyɛ hu no ho. Wobɛyɛ dɛn ahwɛ PR backlogs a ɛkɔ parabolic prɛko? Na security a ɛtwa plugins ho? Ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ dɛn hwɛ meetups a ɛwɔ project no din mu so? Manya anigye sɛ mede Peter afotuo ne osuahu ayɛ adwuma wɔ saa nsɛm yi mu binom so dada.

Deɛ ɛboro saa, wɔn mmienu gyina technical ambition a mepɛ atwa Omarchy ho no ankasa mu. Drew danee personal itch fa files a wɔtu fi kɔmputa baako kɔ foforɔ so no kɔɔ Dropbox mu. Peter danee weekend project kɔɔ OpenClaw mu, na wɔ saa kwan so no ɔkaee obiara sɛ akɔnnɔ dɔɔso ma kɔmputa a ɛte nka sɛ personal, programmable, na ɛyɛ anika.

Ɛno ne sunsum a ɛda Omacom Foundation akyi nso. Yɛwɔ ha na yɛde sika ama infrastructure, open-source dwumadie, ne developers a ehia na Linux desktop ayɛ fie anika ma computer users awoɔ ntoatoaso foforɔ no.

Akwaaba, Drew ne Peter. Founding Patrons du. 10 million USD. Nkɔmhyɛ no abɛn 2 million USD bio!
