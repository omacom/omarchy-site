Omarchy si so dua sɛ kɔmputa ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ sɛnea ɛyɛ productive. Saa nnyinasoɔ no, theme makers ne Omarchy artists foforɔ na wɔaso fi da a ɛdi kan. Wɔsi palettes a ɛyɛ ma, wɔ wɔn ankasa berɛ mu, kwa, efisɛ wɔpɛ sɛ wɔn mfiri no te sɛ biribi.

Enti yɛreyi Omarchy AIR adi - Artists in Residence - na yɛaboa wɔn mu pa no. Asram nsia tenabea ma artists na wɔayɛ adwuma kwa wɔ tɛmas, plugins, ne deɛ aka a wogye di sɛ Omarchy hia next so. Roadmap nni hɔ, tickets nni hɔ. Nea ɛhiã ne sɛ wɔbɛma adebɔ akɔ so wɔ Omacom Foundation mmoa mu.

Tenabea biara de 2,500 USD ba bosom biara ma asram nsia no nyinaa, token account sɛnea agents no rennyae, ne nkaeɛ a ɛtena hɔ daa sɛ AIR alumni sɛ asram nsia no wie.

Nkongua mmiɛnsa na ɛwɔ hɔ prɛko, na wɔafa mmienu ama season a ɛdi kan yi.

HANCORE wɔ tɛmas mmienu a ɛka Omarchy ho - Solitude ne Last Horizon - na aduonu foforɔ wowɔ extras page no so, ɛboro deɛ obiara de aboa: Batou, Oxo Carbon, Velvet Night, Rose of Dune, Shades of Jade, na ɛkɔ so. Ɔyɛ mechanical engineer a ɔbɛyɛɛ aesthetic tumi a ɛkyerɛ kwan wɔ kuo yi mu, ɔte Omarchy Core so, na ɔboa hwɛ plugin ecosystem no so.

OldJobobo na ɔyɛ tɛmas mmiɛnsa a ɛka Omarchy ho no - Lumon, Miasma, ne Retro 82 - a du foforɔ ka ho wɔ extras page no so, fi Batman so kɔ City-783 so kɔ Sakura Mochi so. Ɔyɛ adwuma wɔ palettes a ɛyɛ ma mu sen colour schemes baako pɛ, ɛno nti ne tɛmas no ka bom wɔ terminal, Neovim, btop, ne shell no mu sen sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ wɔ screenshot mu nko ara.

Nkongua a ɛtwa to no yɛ invitation so. Dwumadie no hyɛ ase October.

Mepɛ Vitruvius ahwehwɛdeɛ mmiɛnsa a ɛyɛ ma architecture pa: firmitas, utilitas, venustas. Ɛsɛ sɛ egyina, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ mfasoɔ, na ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ. Ɛnyɛ mmiɛnsa mu mmienu. Yɛde sika bɛhyɛ ne nyinaa mu. Yɛbɛtumi asiesie biribiara.
