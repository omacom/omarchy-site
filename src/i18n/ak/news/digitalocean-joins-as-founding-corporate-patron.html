DigitalOcean reka Omacom Foundation ho sɛ Founding Corporate Patron, a ɔde 1 million USD afe rema ma mfeɛ mmiɛnsa! Ɛno yɛ 3 million USD bɔhyɛ ma Omarchy dwumadie, ne hwɛ, ne trɛw. Gyidie aba nwanwa ma OS fɛ, anika, na ɛwɔ agents a yɛresi!

Mede DigitalOcean Droplets ayɛ adwuma mfeɛ du ne akyi. Ɛyɛ kwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw pa ma wode Linux mfiri hyɛ internet so na wokɔ so si biribi. Yɛde yɛ contributors.rubyonrails.org, site a ɛhyɛ nnipa mpempem a wɔaboa ama Rails ayɛ deɛ ɛyɛ no animuonyam. Eyi ne infrastructure a mede agyina mfeɛ pii, enti sɛ company a ɛda akyi no ka yɛn ho a, ɛte nka yie titiriw.

Na wɔyɛ patron pa ma agentic OS. DigitalOcean de one-click Droplets ma Hermes ne OpenClaw dada. Wobɛtumi anya agent wɔ fie wɔ wo Omarchy desktop so ne foforɔ a ɔyɛ adwuma da ne anadwo wɔ Droplet so.

Nanso patronage yi fa a ɛmaa mesereee paa ne sɛnea ne nyinaa baeɛ ntɛm. Mekɔɔ Paddy Srinivasan, DigitalOcean CEO nkyɛn wɔ X Wukuda. Yɛbɔɔ nkɔmmɔ anadwo no ara. Memanee proposal Memeneda. Dapaa no, yɛawiee ne nyinaa.

Ɛno ne collaboration ahoɔhare a ebetumi aba wɔ X, agents, ne ambition a enni ano mu! Sɛ nnipa hu opportunity koro na wɔpɛ sɛ wɔma biribi ba mu a, nti biara nni hɔ sɛ wɔbɛsɛe asram pii ansa na wɔaka yiw. Yɛbɛtumi akɔ ntɛm sɛnea possibilities a ɛwɔ yɛn anim no teɛ.

DigitalOcean aka Meta Superintelligence Labs ho sɛ Founding Corporate Patron, nkyɛn individuals ne companies a wɔasɔre aboa mission yi dada. Wɔn bɔhyɛ de yɛn backing nyinaa kɔ bɛyɛ 18.5 million USD. Kwan foforɔ ma yɛde sika hyɛ nnipa, projects, ne infrastructure a ɛma Omarchy yɛ yie ma obiara mu.
