Omarchy nyin a ɛyɛ hu no hia nkuo a wɔahyehyɛ, na yɛbɛhyɛ ase de Omarchy Core Kuo no aka ho. Kuo yi bɛkyɛ asɛdeɛ a ɛyɛ sɛ wɔkyerɛ yɛn distro kwan, hwɛ sɛ yɛkɔ so, na boa contributors foforɔ.

Momma menkyerɛ Core Kuo a ɛdi kan no:

Ryan Hughes ayɛ me wingman wɔ Omarchy so fi da a ɛdi kan. Yɛko tiaa 3am emergency AUR outages abom wɔ nna a ɛdi kan mu, na yɛyɛɛ pɔ sɛ yɛbɛsi yɛn ankasa package repository. Ryan mmoa no fa distro no layer biara mu: installation, packaging, hardware support, gaming, theming, CLI tooling, ne Quattro seesei. Ɔboae ma yɛkɔɔ Quickshell so, plugin system no, ne infrastructure dodoɔ a ɛdanee Omarchy fii Linux nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔahyehyɛ yie mu kɔɔ operating system ankasa mu. Sɛ ɔnreyɛ Omarchy so adwuma a, Ryan yɛ partner wɔ Oodle.

Tobi Lütke ne obi a ɔyɛ nipa baako a wayɛ me core nkuo mmienu no nyinaa mu! Sɛ Rails Core Team a ɛdi kan a wɔhyɛɛ ase 2004 no muni no, ɔnyɛɛ mmoa kɛseɛ maa framework no nko, na mmom ɔkyerɛwee Liquid, Delayed Job, ne Active Merchant. Saa adwuma no danee kɔɔ company ketewa bi a wɔfrɛ no Shopify mu, woate wɔn ho asɛm? Seesei ɔyɛ contributor a ɔyɛ pii ma Omarchy a projects sɛ try, Omasnap, Wi-Fi QR codes, ne Quake-style agent console ka ho. Ɔyɛ me co-driver nso wɔ #11 Shopify Racing LMP2 car a ɛtu mmirika wɔ IMSA mu.

Bjarne Øverli yɛ CTO wɔ Unite AS ne bɔfoɔ a ɔbɔɔ Aether, visual theme builder a yɛde mena wɔ Omarchy mu, ne cliamp, yɛn TUI-based music player. Waboa adane yɛn theming system afi configuration files anoano mu akɔ biribi a ɛyɛ systematic kɛse mu, a semantic colors ne generated themes wɔ Neovim, VS Code, btop, ne stack no nkaeɛ mu ka ho. Ɔyɛ tɛmas sɛ Ethereal, Hackerman, ne Lupine nso ho adwuma. Bjarne wɔ programmer rigor ne visual taste a ɛntaa nni a ɛfata Omarchy pɛpɛɛpɛ no.

HANCORE yɛ mechanical engineer a ɔde akwan nwanwa so bɛyɛɛ aesthetic tumi a ɛkyerɛ kwan wɔ Omarchy kuo mu no mu biako. Wayɛ tɛmas dodoɔ a ɛyɛ hu, Last Horizon ne Solitude ka ho, a ɛka Omarchy ho default. Seesei ɔboa hwɛ plugin ecosystem a ɛtwa Quattro ho no so. Omarchy asi so dua daa sɛ kɔmputa ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛyɛ mfasoɔ, na HANCORE de saa nnyinasoɔ no akɔ akyiri asen obiara.

Spencer Bull yɛ Distinguished Engineer wɔ Dell a ɔyɛ adwuma wɔ Linux client enablement, AI, ne platform software so. Wayɛ yɛn bridge akɔ hardware foforɔ realiti a ɛyɛ hu mu, aboa ma Omarchy ayɛ adwuma yie wɔ Panther Lake ne XPS mfiri foforɔ so ansa na Linux lag a ɛyɛ fam no aba. Kernel issues, drivers, displays, Wi-Fi, speakers, power management: Spencer yɛ adwuma wɔ layer a software hyia atoms, na saa nimdeɛ no a ɛwɔ Core Kuo no mu no ho hia kɛse.

Wɔnum yi ayɛ adwuma no dada. Eyi ma ɛyɛ official. Ka me ho a, ɛyɛ Core Kuo a ɛdi kan a ɛyɛ nwanwa. Nanso ɛnrenyɛ kuo baako. Twɛn nkabom fa Omarchy Rangers ho!

Abom yɛbɛsi Omarchy ama ayɛ Linux distribution a ɛdi kan ma nnipa a wɔn ani agye agents berɛ so, malleable kɔmputa, ne computing a ɛyɛ fɛ, modern, na ɛwɔ adwene ho.

Kɔ infinity ne akyi!
