Omacom Foundation anya ne Distinguished Corporate Patrons mmienu a ɛdi kan! 1Password ne 37signals biara de 100,000 USD afe rema ma mfeɛ mmiɛnsa a ɛdi hɔ, a ɛde foundation no sika kɔ 12.6 million USD.

1Password aka Omarchy ho fi da a ɛdi kan (na Omakub ho ansa!). Efisɛ akura me credentials mu yie mfeɛ pii ansa na Linux so kɔ no ayɛ twinkle mpo wɔ mʼani mu.

Sɛ Dropbox, ɛyɛ tools a ɛho hia no mu biako ma no backup, no cry kwan no. Bere biara a mesiesie mfiri foforɔ (a ɛyɛ bɛyɛ mprɛnsa dapɛn nna yi!) no, deɛ ɛdi kan a meyɛ ne Install > Service > 1Password. Afei me agents nhyehyɛeɛ, Basecamp ne HEY mu kɔ, ne deɛ aka nyinaa yɛ mmerɛw. Nanso ɛyɛ safe pɛpɛɛpɛ, secure pɛpɛɛpɛ.

37signals ayɛ incubation station ama Omarchy fi ansa na anya din. Yɛasakra technical team no nyinaa akɔ Omarchy so dada, na yɛatua bills ahorow wɔ kwan a ɛkɔ Quattro so (ne ansa). Seesei mmoa no yɛ formal, denam foundation so, wɔ mmara koro so sɛ obiara.

Ɛsɛ sɛ yɛhyɛ no nso sɛ corporate sika ne individual sika nyinaa tɔ ade koro: nkaeɛ sɛ woka mission no ho. Quid pro quo nni ha.

Omarchy de ne gyinae nyinaa bɛgyina deɛ ɛma malleable OS pa a ɛsen biribiara wɔ wiase so daa. Na wɔakyerɛkyerɛ no mu kɛse wɔ “by DHH” byline no mu bere a wode system no hyɛ mu. Ansa na eyi bɛyɛ system nwanwa ama obiara, ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ system nwanwa ma me ankasa kane. Fa wo ankasa mask hyɛ kane na deɛ aka nyinaa!
