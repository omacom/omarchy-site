Mfeɛ 21 a atwam, wɔ 2005, na mafi college aba a mahwɛ wo video a wode Ruby on Rails ne TextMate sii blog wɔ Mac OS X so bɛyɛ sima 15 mu. Metɔɔ white MacBook ntɛm akyi, megyaa Windows, metɔɔ TextMate, na mehyɛɛ ase sua Ruby. Ɛsakraa sɛnea mʼani gye coding ho kɛse.

Meyɛ games entrepreneur fi China, na me ne mʼabusua te U.S. seesei. Yɛsi games, na yɛhwɛ TapTap nso so… Yɛnte App Store anaa Google Play, yɛmfa revenue share mfi developers nkyɛn, nanso yɛyɛ adwuma wɔ scale a ɛwɔ mfasoɔ ne profitably so.
