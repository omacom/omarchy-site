Omacom Foundation anya half a million dollars foforɔ! Tokens foforɔ, three-year corporate pledge, ne thousands of dollars fi open patronage ntam no, ɛno yɛ bɛyɛ 510,000 USD foforɔ a wɔahyɛ ho bɔ ama Linux a ɛyɛ fɛ, ɛyɛ anika, na ɛwɔ agents. Ɛno de yɛn pledges ne donations nyinaa kɔ bɛyɛ 15.5 million USD!

Deɛ ɛdi kan, yɛregye OpenRouter sɛ Distinguished Corporate Patron wɔ 150,000 USD mu tokens mu. Sɛ allotment no sa a, yɛbɛtumi ayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no foforɔ denam nhyehyɛeɛ a obiara pene so. Saa tokens yi bɛma agents a wɔsi, sɔ hwɛ, na wɔhwɛ Omarchy so no ahoɔden. Dodow a yɛbɛtumi de agents ayɛ adwuma asiesie bugs, ahwɛ contributions mu, na ama distro no ayɛ yie no, dodow no ara na yɛbɛtumi ama obiara kɔmputa ayɛ yie ntɛm. Fuel foforɔ ma mission no!

Yɛbɛma millions of dollars wɔ tokens mu a wɔde aboa no akɔfa OpenRouter platform pa no so. Sɛnea yɛbɛtumi de tokens a ɛfata ama teams a ɛfata fi models a ɛfata mu.

Afei Four Technologies wɔ hɔ, a wɔreka ho sɛ Distinguished Corporate Patron wɔ pledge a ɛyɛ 100,000 USD afe ma mfeɛ mmiɛnsa mu. Ɛno yɛ 300,000 USD ma community projects a Omarchy gyina soɔ, infrastructure a ɛma ɛkɔ so, ne adwuma a ɛyɛ sɛ wɔde Linux fɛ brɛ nnipa foforɔ. Sɛ companies hyɛ bɔ ma mfeɛ a, ɛma yɛn gyidie sɛ yɛnyɛ saa ara.

Sɛnea Four Technologies team no kaeɛ ni:

Yɛyɛ AI-forward fintech company, na companies sɛ Four Technologies tumi si sɛnea yɛsi efisɛ open-source infrastructure a afoforɔ sii kane. Sɛ yɛboa Omacom Foundation a, ɛno ne sɛnea yɛde sika hyɛ saa mu. Yɛgye di sɛ AI stage a ɛdi hɔ nyɛ app foforɔ a wɔde ato operating system so, na mmom ɛyɛ AI a wɔasi atɔ operating system no mu tẽẽ, na ɛno ne bet a Omarchy reyi. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛka community a ɛreyi saa bet no ho, ɛnyɛ sɛ yɛbɛnya mfasoɔ fi mu akyi.

Na fi bere a yɛbuee patronage maa obiara no, yɛanya bɛyɛ 60,000 USD fi donors bɛyɛ 500 nkyɛn denam open patronage so. Ɛno yɛ mmuaeɛ nwanwa fi nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔboa ma eyi ba mu. Mmoa biara de ka deɛ yɛbɛtumi ayɛ abom ho.

Tokens a yɛde bɛsi. Sika a yɛde bɛboa projects a yɛgyina soɔ no. Nnipa ne companies a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔde biribi hyɛ wɔn gyidie a wɔwɔ wɔ Linux wɔ desktop so no ase. Eyi ne sɛnea yɛkɔ so!

Medaase ma OpenRouter, Four Technologies, ne obiara a wabɛyɛ patron. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ woka wɔn ho a, kyerɛw david@omarchy.org ma founding ne distinguished patronage, na donate tẽẽ ma open patronage.
