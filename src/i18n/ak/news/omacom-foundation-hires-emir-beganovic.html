Yɛrefa Emir Beganović sɛ Head of Infrastructure ma Omarchy! Ɔhyɛ ase November 1 sɛ Omacom Foundation full-time hire a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa mu, a ɔreka Krzysztof Wilczyński wɔ Kernel so ne outfoxxed wɔ Shell so ho.

Sɛnea Emir kyerɛ nʼankasa akwan a ɛkɔɔ Omarchy so ni:

Mʼɔdɔ a mewɔ ma Linux no kɔ akyiri sen mfeɛ aduonu. Meyɛɛ Ubuntu developer bere a meyɛ teenager na mede me ho hyɛɛ local community mu kɛse. Mʼadwuma afa me aman mmiɛnsa mu, a meyɛɛ adwuma wɔ reliability ne performance so wɔ startups ne scaleups mu na mehwɛɛ teams so. Medii bɛyɛ mfeɛ nson wɔ Booking.com SRE department mu, a mehwɛɛ adwuma wɔ build systems ne CI infrastructure so. Nkyɛeɛ, meyɛɛ Isorun, beaeɛ a mayɛ adwuma wɔ microVMs, snapshots, isolation, scheduling, ne disposable compute so. Seesei mede saa osuahu no resan aba me dɔ a ɛdi kan ho.

Deɛ ɛkyeree mʼani paa fa Emir ho ne TryOmarchy. Ɔfaa Omarchy desktop no, de hyɛɛ browser mu (ɛnyɛ reimplementation, na mmom ade ankasa no), na ɔmaa ɛbɔɔ wɔ sekɔne kakra mu wɔ ordinary CPU infrastructure so wɔ Cloudflare. Yɛhyɛɛ ase kasa faa saa experiment nkwasea no ho, na nkɔmmɔ no trɛe ntɛm kɔɔ laptops, architectures foforɔ, OEMs, ne infrastructure a yɛbɛhia sɛ Omarchy bɛyɛ adwuma baabiara a yɛpɛ so. Yɛhuu sɛ yɛwɔ anisoadehunu koro.

Ɛno ne deɛ Emir reba na ɔahwɛ so.

Infrastructure ma Omarchy kyerɛ pii sen servers a wɔyɛ adwuma so. Ɛno ne deɛ yɛde si, sɔ hwɛ, na yɛde Omarchy kɔma, na yɛma system no kɔ so yɛ adwuma yie bere a yɛretrɛ kɔ hardware foforɔ ne architectures foforɔ so. Ɛyɛ releases dependable, ɛma failures yɛ reproducible, na ɛma obiara a ɔka project no ho fapem a ɛyɛ den sɛnea wobetumi akɔ ntɛm.

Na biribi pii wɔ hɔ a yɛnhwehwɛ! Ɛsɛ sɛ Omarchy yɛ fɛ wɔ mfiri a ɛda wo pon so no so, nanso nti biara nni hɔ sɛ ɛbɛgyina hɔ. TryOmarchy maa yɛhuu deɛ ɛba bere a Linux kɔmputa a wobɛhyɛ ase no yɛɛ mmerɛw sɛ web page a wobue, a ɛde Linux ka kakra ba click baako mu. Yɛbɛhwɛ beaeɛ a ɛkɔ.

Medaase ma obiara a ɔde sika ma saa adwuma yi, yɛbɛtumi ama nnipa sɛ Emir berɛ ma wɔde ne nyinaa akɔ Omarchy mu. Yɛn patrons ma ɛbɛtumi aba sɛ experiments a ɛyɛ ambitious dane adwuma a ɛtena hɔ, a full-time nnipa de wɔn ho ama ma system no nyinaa ayɛ yie. Yɛda wɔn patronage ase.

Akwaaba, Emir! Momma yɛmma Omarchy nyɛ adwuma baabiara.
