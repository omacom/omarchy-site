Kwan bɛn na ɛyɛ sɛ yɛde sika a yɛnyae maa Omacom Foundation no bi bɛhyɛ ase asɛe sen sɛ yɛde bɛma Linux aberanteɛ a ɔyɛ cracked paa wɔ Poland: Vaxry!

Sɛ Hyprland bɔfoɔ no, ɔhwɛ core technologies mmiɛnsa a ɛwɔ Omarchy mu no mu biako so (Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), na mʼani agye sɛ merebɔ exclusive sponsorship dawuru a ɛma ɔde nʼadwene nyinaa si so na ɔnyɛ no nkɔ anim a ɔnnwen commercial development anaa fundraising ho.

Yɛne Vaxry reyɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ma bere tenten na yɛaboa Omarchy daakye tenten. Yɛn exclusive sponsorship deal no yɛ mfeɛ mmiɛnsa, a option wɔ hɔ ma mmiɛnu foforɔ.

Deal no ma kwan nso ma Hyprland Hyprperks program a ɛwɔ hɔ dada no aba kwa, ama obiara, efisɛ paid subscriptions no bɛba awieeɛ. Nanso Hyprland bɛgye personal donations atom na individuals atumi de wɔn ho ahyɛ project no dwumadie mu na wɔaboa Vaxry ne team no.

Medaase pii ma 37signals (hello self! 😂), Butterfly, ne Framework ma wɔboaa Hyprland duu ha. Mʼani agye sɛ Omacom Foundation betumi agye so fi October 10, na ama developer pa baako wɔ Linux berɛ foforɔ yi mu kwan ma ɔde ne nyinaa akɔ mu.

(* Saa deal yi mfa mmoa tẽẽ a wɔde bɛma Vaxry anya girlfriend nka, nanso mʼankasa mɛwɔ hɔ sɛ reference ma nsɛmmisa biara fa saa ho.)
