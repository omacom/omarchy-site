Website a ɛyɛ ma Linux distribution fɛ, anika no ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ na ɛyɛ anika nso. Enti yɛama Omarchy.org redesign a ɛyɛ ma, na yɛreyi no adi wɔ kasa 29 mu (efisɛ bere a woanya bɛyɛ 2 million USD wɔ tokens mu no, adɛn nti na ɛnyɛ?). Retro magic kakra, biribi pii a wobɛhwehwɛ, ne akwaaba kɛseɛ ma wiase.

Eyi yɛ website a ɛpɛ sɛ wodi agorɔ wɔ wo kɔmputa so! Pixels, kɔla, ne animations no de Omarchy sunsum kɔ browser mu tẽẽ. Wobɛtumi ate beaeɛ no nka ansa na woatwe ISO baako mpo. Ɛyɛ me nkitahodi ankasa nso ma saa mfeɛ a ɛyɛeɛ wɔ pre-internet online wiase a ɛyɛ ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, ne 90s aesthetic a ɛyɛ hyerɛn no mu. Kɔla pii, polish kakra. Pixels pii, soft gradients kakra.

Seesei kwan wɔ hɔ ma yɛkyerɛ editors, terminals, agents, games, Windows VM, ne hardware a ɛma Omarchy yɛ mfasoɔ da biara. Hwɛ community themes mu, hwehwɛ plugin, hwɛ workstations ankasa, anaa hu nhyiamu a ɛbɛn wo. Hyia nnipa a wɔresi ne patrons a wɔde sika ma. Yɛnya front door a ɛwɔ kwan a ɛdɔɔso ma biribiara a ɛrekɔ so wɔ mu seesei.

Na saa front door no seesei ka wo kasa.
