Sponsorship a ɛtɔ so mmiɛnsa aba! Omacom Foundation reyɛ premier sponsor ma mise, na ɛno mu jdx, a wadi mfeɛ pii asi tool a ɛma Ruby, Node, Go versions ahorow hwɛ yɛ anika, na ɛma wo agent tooling nyinaa upgrade channel a ebetumi ne shipping ahoɔhare akɔ.

Sɛ woadi Omarchy adi a, woadi mise adi, sɛ wuhuu anaa wonhuu. Ɛno ne deɛ ɛhwɛ language runtimes so, enti mise use -g ruby ma wo Ruby a PATH archaeology line baako mpo nni mu. Nanso fa a mʼani gye ho paa seesei ne agents no.

Coding-agent CLI kɛseɛ biara wɔ Omarchy mu mena sɛ lazy-loading mise stub wɔ ~/.local/bin/ mu. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - ne nyinaa da hɔ, ɛmfa wo hwee kosi bere a ɛdi kan a wokyerɛw biako ankasa. Afei ɛde ne ho hyɛ mu na woreyɛ adwuma. Package nni hɔ a wobɛhwehwɛ, version nni hɔ a wobɛpin, toolchain nni hɔ a wobɛdwene ho. omarchy-mise-install <package> kyekyere CLI foforɔ biara saa ara, na omarchy update ma emu biara yɛ foforɔ.

Ɛno ne fast track. Wɔ The Age of Agents mu, gap a ɛda tool ho nte ne tool no yɛ ntam no ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ bɛyɛ sekɔne nan, na mise de saa ba. Obiara nhia sɛ ɔpackage biribi ma distro kane.

Anigye no kɔ akwan mmienu so nso: traffic a ɛkɔ mise so akɔ soro 24% fi bere a Omarchy baeɛ! Nnipa pii rehyia saa tool yi kane denam yɛn so, ɛno pɛpɛɛpɛ ne ade a sponsorship ɛsɛ sɛ ɛhu na ɛmma ɛnyɛ fam.

Bio, medaase titiriw ma Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, ne Peter Steinberger ma eyi aba mu. Sɛ Oligarchy nni hɔ a, anka yɛrentumi mfa sika mma open-source developers sɛ yeinom!
