Third sponsorship out the door! Omacom Foundation efuuka premier sponsor wa mise, era bwe kityo wa jdx, eyamaze emyaka ng'azimba tool efuula mu kasirise okuddukanya versions ez'enjawulo eza Ruby, Node, Go essanyu okuddukanya, era ewa agent tooling yo yonna mu upgrade channel esobola okukwatagana ne sipiidi ya shipping.

Bw'oba okozesezza Omarchy, okozesezza mise, oba teweelaba. Kye kuddukanya language runtimes, kale mise use -g ruby ekuwa Ruby nga tewali single line ya PATH archaeology. Naye ekitundu kye mfaako nnyo kati ba agents.

Buli major coding-agent CLI mu Omarchy esindika nga lazy-loading mise stub mu ~/.local/bin/. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - zonna zitudde eyo, tezikusasuza kintu okutuusa omulundi ogusooka lw'okubaako emu. Olwo yeeteeka yokka era okola. Tewali package ya kunoonya, tewali version ya kupinning, tewali toolchain ya kulowoozaako. omarchy-mise-install <package> ezinga CLI endala yonna mu ngeri ye emu, era omarchy update ekuuma yonna nga current.

Eyo ye fast track. Mu Age ya Agents, gap wakati w'okuwulira ku tool n'okugiddukanya yeetaaga okuba sekondi nnya, era mise ewa ekyo. Tewali eyali yeetaaga okupackage ekintu ku distro okusooka.

Interest edduka mu directions zombi: traffic ku mise esusse 24% okuva Omarchy lwe yatongola! Abantu bangi basisinkana tool eno omulundi ogusooka ng'ayita mu ffe, ekyo ddala ekika ky'ekintu sponsorship erina okumanya mu kifo ky'okukitwala nga granted.

Nate, webale nyo mu ngeri ey'enjawulo eri Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, ne Peter Steinberger olw'okufuula kino okusoboka. Oligarchy singa yali tegiriwo, tetwandisobodde okuwagira open-source developers nga bano!
