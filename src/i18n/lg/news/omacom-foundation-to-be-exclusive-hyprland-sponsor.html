Nga omutonzi wa Hyprland, avunaanyizibwa ku emu ku core technologies essatu mu Omarchy (Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), era nsanyukidde okulangirira exclusive sponsorship emukkiriza okussa essira mu bujjuvu ku kugikulaakulanya nga tewali bwetaavu bw'okulowooza ku commercial development oba fundraising.

Deal era ekkiriza Hyprland's Hyprperks program eyaliwo okubeera released for free, eri buli omu, nga paid subscriptions zijja kukoma. Naye Hyprland ekyagenda mu maaso n'okukkiriza personal donations okukkiriza individuals okugula mu development ya pulojekiti n'okuwagira Vaxry ne team.
