Omacom Foundation erina Distinguished Corporate Patrons baayo abasooka babiri! 1Password ne 37signals buli emu ewaayo 100,000 USD omwaka okumala emyaka esatu egiddako, ekitwala ensimbi za foundation ku 12.6 million USD.

1Password ebadde kitundu kya Omarchy okuva ku lunaku olusooka (era kitundu kya Omakub nga tetunnaba ekyo!). Kubanga ekuumye credentials zange safe okumala emyaka mingi nga switching okudda ku Linux tennaba na kuba twinkle mu liiso lyange.

Nga Dropbox, emu ku essential tools oku enable no backup, no cry method. Buli lwe nteekateeka machine empya (ekyo kumpi emirundi esatu mu week mu nnaku zino!), ekintu ekisooka kye nkola kye Install > Service > 1Password. Oluvannyuma okuteekateeka ba agents bange, okuyingira mu Basecamp ne HEY, na buli kintu ekirala kyangu nyo. Naye perfectly safe, perfectly secure.

37signals ebadde incubation station ya Omarchy okuva nga tennaba na linnya. Dda twakyusa technical team yonna okudda ku Omarchy, era tubadde tusasula bills za ngeri zonna ku kkubo okutuuka ku Quattro (era nga tetunnaba). Kati support ye formal, ng'ayita mu foundation, ku terms ze zimu nga buli omu omulala.

Kikulu okumanya, nti corporate money ne individual money zonna zigula ekintu kyekimu: recognition olw'okubeera kitundu kya mission. Tewali quid pro quo wano.

Omarchy agenda bulijjo okusinziira decisions zaayo ku ekyo ekikola malleable OS esinga obulungi mu nsi. Era ekyo kyannyonnyoddwa nnyo ne “by DHH” byline bw'oteeka system.
