Omarchy ekulidde okufuuka global community, ne members ~35,000 ku the Discord, era kati kiseera twatandika okusisinkana face to face. Kale tutandika Omarchy Meetups ne shared calendar ya gatherings mu nsi yonna.

Zino community-run events. Toweetaaga lukusa okuteekateeka emu, tewali alina kibuga, era omuntu yenna asobola okukola emu wonna. Yogera ku Omarchy, Linux, open source, programming, customization, oba adjacent hacker culture yonna efuula abantu okunyumirwa okufuula kompyuta ennyumira nate.

Gikuume nga ggule eri buli omu, yogera ku sponsorships zonna, era tofuula linnya lya Omarchy events business oba sales pitch. Okusukka ekyo, beera nga civilized adults (ne child-like wonder), era onyumirwe nnyo.

Leeta laptop yo. Laga setup yo. Wanyisa themes ne plugins. Yamba omuntu ng'ayita mu installation ye esooka. Ekigendererwa kwe kutuusa abantu abafaayo ku personal, programmable, beautiful computers awamu mu kisenge kyekimu.

Osobola okunoonya meetup ejja oba okusindika eyo ng'oyita mu global Omarchy calendar ku Luma. Tukole kino worldwide movement!
