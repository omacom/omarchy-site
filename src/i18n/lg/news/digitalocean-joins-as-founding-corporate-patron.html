DigitalOcean yegatta ku Omacom Foundation nga Founding Corporate Patron, ng'ewaayo 1 million USD omwaka okumala emyaka esatu! Eyo 3 million USD commitment ku development, maintenance, n'okusaasaanya Omarchy. Vote ya confidence ya kitalo ki mu beautiful, fun & agentic OS gye tuzimba!

Nkozesizza DigitalOcean Droplets okusukka decade. Ye wonderfully straightforward way ey'okuteeka Linux machine ku internet era ogende mu maaso n'okuzimba ekintu. Tuzikozesa okuddukanya contributors.rubyonrails.org, omukutu ogujaguza enkumi z'abantu abaayambye okufuula Rails ky'eri. Eno infrastructure gye nesigamye okumala emyaka, kale company eri emabega waayo okwegatta naffe kiwulirwa bulungi mu ngeri ey'enjawulo.

Era be perfect patron ya agentic OS. DigitalOcean dda ewaayo one-click Droplets za Hermes ne OpenClaw. Osobola okubeera ne agent ewaka ku Omarchy desktop yo n'omulala akola around the clock ku Droplet.

Naye ekitundu kya patronage eno ekyansekesa ddala y'engeri gye byonna byagattibwa mangu. Natuukira ku Paddy Srinivasan, CEO wa DigitalOcean, ku X ku Wednesday. Twalina call ekiro kyekimu. Twasindika proposal ku Saturday. Ku Sunday, twali tumaze byonna.

Eyo ye sipiidi ya collaboration esoboka ne X, ba agents, ne boundless ambition! Abantu bwe balaba omukisa gwegumu era baagala okufuula ekintu okubaawo, tewali nsonga ya kumala myezi nga tutuuka ku yes. Tusobola okutambula ku sipiidi ya possibilities eziri mu maaso gaffe.

DigitalOcean yegatta ku Meta Superintelligence Labs nga Founding Corporate Patron, wamu na individuals ne companies zonna dda eziyimiridde okuwagira mission eno. Pledge yaabwe ereeta total backing yaffe ku kumpi 18.5 million USD. Ekifo ekinene eky'okuteekamu ensimbi mu bantu, pulojekiti, ne infrastructure ezifuula Omarchy ennungi eri buli omu.

Nga okupangisa abantu ab'ekitalo bangi! Tuleese Krzysztof Wilczyński nga Head of Omarchy Kernel ne outfoxxed, omutonzi wa Quickshell, nga Head of Omarchy Shell. Okuwa abantu nga bano omukisa okukola full time ku kufuula Omarchy eyangu, eyesigika, era esobola ebisingawo kisoboka yokka ne patrons nga DigitalOcean. Obuwagizi bwabwe bukyusa ambitions zaffe ne bufuuka actual jobs eri abantu abasobola okuzituukiriza.

Webale nyo, Paddy na buli omu ku DigitalOcean. Tuteke obuwagizi buno ku mulimu era tufuule beautiful, fun, agentic future ya Linux okubaawo!
