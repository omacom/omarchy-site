Omarchy ekakatiriza nti kompyuta erina okuba ennungi nga bw'eri productive. Premise eyo ebadde etwalibwa theme makers n'abayimbi ba Omarchy abalala okuva ku lunaku olusooka. Nga bazimba whole palettes, ku kiseera kyabwe, for free, kubanga baagala machine yaabwe erabike ng'ekintu.

Kale tutandika Omarchy AIR - Artists in Residence - okuwagira abasinga ku bo. Six-month residency ya artists okukola nga bwe baagala ku themes, plugins, n'ebirala byonna bye balowooza Omarchy by'yeetaaga. Tewali roadmap, tewali tickets. Ekigendererwa kwe kukkiriza creativity okukulukuta n'obuwagizi bwa Omacom Foundation.

Buli residency ejja ne 2,500 USD omwezi okumala emyezi mukaaga egyo, token account ba agents baleme okuyimirira, ne permanent recognition nga AIR alumni omyezi mukaaga bwe giwedde.

Waliwo entebe ezisukka ssatu ezisangibwa omulundi gumu, era bbiri ku zo kati zitwaliddwa ku season eno esooka.

HANCORE erina themes bbiri ezikola ne Omarchy - Solitude ne Last Horizon - n'endala makumi abiri ku extras page, okusinga omuntu omulala yenna ky'awaddeyo: Batou, Oxo Carbon, Velvet Night, Rose of Dune, Shades of Jade, era egenda mu maaso. Muwumbi wa mechanical engineer eyafuuka emu ku defining aesthetic forces mu community eno, atuula ku Omarchy Core, era ayamba okulabirira plugin ecosystem.

OldJobobo avunaanyizibwa ku ssatu ku themes ezikola ne Omarchy - Lumon, Miasma, ne Retro 82 - wamu n'endala kkumi ku extras page, okuva ku Batman okutuuka ku City-783 okutuuka ku Sakura Mochi. Akola mu whole palettes mu kifo ky'okola one-off colour schemes, ekyo kye kifuula themes ze okukwatagana okuyita mu terminal, Neovim, btop, ne shell mu kifo ky'okulabika bulungi mu screenshot yokka.

Entebe esembayo ya invitation. Program etandika mu October.

Nnyagala three requirements za Vitruvius eza good architecture: firmitas, utilitas, venustas. Erina okuyimirira, erina okuba useful, era erina okuba beautiful. Si bbiri ku ssatu. Tujja kuteeka ensimbi mu byonna. Tusobola okukola ku buli kimu.
