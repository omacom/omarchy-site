Explosive growth ya Omarchy yeetaaga structured teams nga response, era tugenda kutandika nga tukoza Omarchy Core Team. Group eno ejja kugabana responsibility ya kuvuga direction ya distro yaffe, okulaba nti tugenda mu maaso n'okusindika, n'okuyamba contributors abalala.

Mundeke mbategeeze Core Team esooka:

Ryan Hughes abadde wingman wange ku Omarchy okuva ku lunaku olusooka. Twalwana 3am emergency AUR outages awamu mu nnaku ezasooka, era twateesa okuzimba package repository yaffe. Contributions za Ryan zitambula mu buli layer ya distro: installation, packaging, hardware support, gaming, theming, CLI tooling, era kati Quattro. Yayamba okuvuga okukyuka okudda ku Quickshell, plugin system, ne infrastructure ennyingi eyakyusa Omarchy okuva ku carefully assembled Linux setup okudda ku proper operating system. Bw'aba takola hacking ku Omarchy, Ryan ye partner ku Oodle.

Bjarne Øverli ye CTO wa Unite AS n'omutonzi wa Aether, visual theme builder gye tusindika mu Omarchy, ne cliamp, TUI-based music player waffe. Ayambye okukyusa theming system yaffe okuva ku collection ya configuration files okudda ku kintu ekisinga systematic, ne semantic colors ne generated themes okuyita mu Neovim, VS Code, btop, ne stack yonna esigadde. Era avunaanyizibwa ku themes nga Ethereal, Hackerman, ne Lupine. Bjarne alina rare combination eyo eya programmer rigor ne visual taste ekwatagana ne Omarchy perfectly.

HANCORE muwumbi wa mechanical engineer mu ngeri yonna eyatuuka okufuuka emu ku defining aesthetic forces mu Omarchy community. Avuddeyo absurd number ya themes, omuli Last Horizon ne Solitude, esindika by default mu Omarchy. Kati ayamba okulabirira plugin ecosystem okwetooloola Quattro. Omarchy bulijjo ekakatirizza nti kompyuta erina okuba ennungi nga bw'eri useful, era HANCORE asindise premise eyo ewala okusinga kumpi omuntu yenna.

Spencer Bull ye Distinguished Engineer ku Dell akola ku Linux client enablement, AI, ne platform software. Abadde bridge yaffe mu messy reality ya brand-new hardware, ng'ayamba Omarchy okukola obulungi ku Panther Lake ne latest XPS machines nga usual Linux lag tennaba kutandika. Kernel issues, drivers, displays, Wi-Fi, speakers, power management: Spencer akolera mu layer software mwe esisinkana atoms, era okubeera ne expertise eyo munda mu Core Team kwa muwendo munene.
