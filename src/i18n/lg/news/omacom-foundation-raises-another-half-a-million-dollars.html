Omacom Foundation ekuŋŋaanyizza half a million USD endala! Wakati wa fresh supply ya tokens, three-year corporate pledge, ne thousands za dollars okuva mu open patronage, eyo kumpi 510,000 USD endala ezisuubiziddwa ku Linux ennungi, ennyumira & agentic. Ekyo kitwala total yaffe eya pledges ne donations ku kumpi 15.5 million USD!

Okusooka, tuyingiza OpenRouter nga Distinguished Corporate Patron ne 150,000 USD mu tokens. Allotment eyo bw'emala, tusobola okuzza arrangement nga mutual agreement. Tokens zino zijja kufuula ba agents abazimba, abegeza, n'abalabirira Omarchy. Gye tukoma okuteeka ba agents ku mulimu okukola ku bugs, okukebera contributions, n'okulongoosa distro, gye tukoma okufuula kompyuta ya buli omu ennungi mangu. Fuel endala ku mission!

Tugenda n'okutambuza millions za dollars mu tokens ezawaddeyo ng'ayita mu excellent OpenRouter platform. Kale tusobola okugabanya tokens entuufu ku teams entuufu okuva ku models entuufu.

Oluvannyuma waliwo Four Technologies, yegatta nga Distinguished Corporate Patron ne pledge ya 100,000 USD omwaka okumala emyaka esatu. Eyo 300,000 USD okuwagira community projects Omarchy mwe yesigamye, infrastructure egikuumira nga edduka, n'omulimu gw'okuleeta beautiful Linux eri abantu bangi. Companies bw zeewaayo okumala emyaka kituwa confidence okukola kye kimu.

Wano team ya Four Technologies we bigamba:

Tuli AI-forward fintech company, era companies nga Four Technologies zisobola yokka okuzimba nga bwe tukola kubanga open-source infrastructure abantu abalala baazimba okusooka. Okuwagira Omacom Foundation y'engeri gye tuteekamu money emabega w'ekyo. Tulowooza next stage ya AI si app endala layered waggulu wa operating system, ye AI ezimbiddwa butereevu mu operating system, era eyo y'bet Omarchy gy'ekola. Twagala okubeera kitundu kya community ekola bet eyo, si kufunamu benefit oluvannyuma yokka.

Era okuva lwe twaggula patronage eri buli omu, tufunye kumpi 60,000 USD okuva eri donors kumpi 500 ng'ayita mu open patronage. Eyo response ya kitalo okuva eri abantu abaagala okuyamba okufuula kino okubaawo. Buli contribution eyongera ku kye tusobola okukola awamu.

Tokens ez'okuzimba. Money ey'okuwagira pulojekiti mwe twesigamye. Abantu ne companies abeetegefu okuteekawo ekintu emabega w'okukkiriza kwabwe mu Linux ku desktop. Eno y'engeri gye tugenda mu maaso!

Webale nyo eri OpenRouter, Four Technologies, na buli omu eyafuuka patron. Bw'oba oyagala okwegatta nabo, wandika david@omarchy.org ku founding ne distinguished patronage, era waayo butereevu ku open patronage.
