Omarchy si beautiful, fun & agentic Linux distribution yokka eri abo abagenda okugikozesa. Era ezimbiddwa ekyo! Ne beauty, fun, ne tokens nnyingi nnyo. Mu butuufu, Omarchy Quattro kumpi yonna ezimbiddwa ba agents. Kubanga bali bulungi nnyo ku bintu nga bash, QML, n'okuzzaamu ensobi za Linux.

Okugenda mu maaso n'okufuula Omarchy ennungi kati nga tusikirizza users ne cocreators enkumi n'enkumi, tweetaaga okukuuma tokens nga zikulukuta. Eno y'engeri gye twanguyizaamu, tulongoosaamu, era tuwa ku contributions zonna ez'ekitalo ezibadde ziyiika. Dda tulina backlog ya PRs 1,600+ okuva mu weeks ntono zokka nga tumaze okutongola! Tewali agenda kuyita mu eyo nga talina agentic assistance.

Kale, nsanyukidde okulangirira nti tufunye motherload ya tokens okuva mu major labs ezisinga ku mission eno! Essooka ye Meta Superintelligence Labs ejja nga Founding Token Patron ne 1.5m USD mu tokens! Meta Muse Spark model yaabwe empya eteekawo numbers ennene ku price ya kitalo. Kale 1.5m USD ye basically unlimited tokens! (Tujja kugezaako okuzimalawo!)

Naye era tusanyukidde okuyingiza bbiri ku frontier labs endala ezikulembera, Anthropic ne OpenAI, nga Distinguished Token Patrons ne 150,000 USD mu token donations buli emu. Zino zijja kuba handy nnyo nga zigattiddwako security clearance eyeetaagibwa okuggyawo guardrails ze tweetaaga ku security research ne fixes.

Era ku open-weight models, nsanyukidde nnyo okulangirira Fireworks nga Distinguished Token Patron ne 150,000 USD mu tokens ezisuubiziddwa. Mbadde frequent user wa Kimi K models mu ngeri ey'enjawulo, era mu ngeri ey'enjawulo nga edduka ku fast mode. Tujja kuteeka speedy tokens zino ku mukozi omulungi nnyo!

Awamu, eno 1.95 million USD endala ezisuubiziddwa eri Omacom Foundation. Ekyo olwo kiteeka total pledges zaffe ku 14.95 million USD. Namba ya nnaku ki okukuba ku distro early detractors ze baagaana nga bunch ya dotfiles (ekyo mu bwenkanya tekyali kikyaamu ddala mu kiseera ekyo, naye olwo twagenda mu maaso nga tukola ku buli kimu!).

Ne tokens zino zonna, tusobola okukola ku buli kimu. Era ekyo kye tugenda okukola!
