Tupangisa Emir Beganović nga Head of Infrastructure ya Omarchy! Atandika November 1 nga Omacom Foundation hire eyokusatu eya full-time, ng'egatta Krzysztof Wilczyński ku Kernel ne outfoxxed ku Shell.

Wano Emir we annyonnyola olugendo lwe ye ku Omarchy:

Ok wagala kwange kwa Linux kudda emabega okusukka emyaka makumi abiri. Nafuuka Ubuntu developer nga nkyali teenager era nnali nywevu mu community ya wano. Career yange entambuzza mu countries ssatu, ng'akola ku reliability ne performance mu startups ne scaleups era ng'akulembera teams. Namala emyaka kumpi musanvu mu SRE department ya Booking.com, ng'akulembera omulimu ku build systems ne CI infrastructure. Obusembayo, nazimba Isorun, gye mbadde nkola ku microVMs, snapshots, isolation, scheduling, ne disposable compute. Kati nleeta experience eyo nzigya ku first love yange.

Ekyankwata nnyo ku Emir yali TryOmarchy. Yatwala Omarchy desktop, nagiteeka mu browser (si reimplementation, naye ekintu kyennyini), era nagifuna nga esimbibwa mu sekondi ku ordinary CPU infrastructure ku Cloudflare. Twatandika okwogera ku silly experiment eyo, era emboozi yagaziwa mangu ku laptops, architectures empya, OEMs, ne infrastructure gye tulina okwetaaga Omarchy bw'egenda okuddukana buli wamu gye twagala. Twategeera nti twalina vision emu.

Ekyo Emir ky'ajja okukulembera.

Infrastructure ya Omarchy kitegeeza okusinga nnyo okuddukanya servers. Kye tukozesa okuzimba, okugeza, n'okusindika Omarchy, era n'okukuuma system nga ekola reliably nga twegaza ku hardware endala ne architectures endala. Kifuula releases dependable, kifuula failures reproducible, era kiwa buli omu omulala ku pulojekiti foundation enywevu basobole okutambula mangu.

Era waliwo bingi eby'okunoonyereza! Omarchy erina okukola bulungi ku machine eri ku desk yo, naye tewali nsonga erina kukoma eyo. TryOmarchy yatwwaako glimpse ya ekibaawo okutandika kompyuta ya Linux bwe kifuuka kyangu nga okuggulawo web page, nga kiteeka taste ya Linux nga click emu ewala. Tugenda kulaba gye kitutwala.

Webale nyo eri buli omu awagira omulimu guno, tusobola okuwa abantu nga Emir ekiseera okwesigama ddala ku Omarchy. Ba patrons baffe bafuula kisoboka okukyusa ambitious experiments ne zifuuka lasting work, ne full-time people abeetegereza okufuula system yonna ennungi. Tubebaza patronage yaabwe.

Oyingiziddwa, Emir! Tukole Omarchy eddukane buli wamu.
