Tupangisa outfoxxed, omutonzi wa Quickshell, nga Head of Omarchy Shell! Yegatta ku Omacom Foundation full time era afuuka member wa Omarchy Core. Omuntu eyazimba toolkit eyafuula Quattro okusoboka ajja okutuyamba okutwala ekintu kyonna ku mwezi.

Kino kikyusa three-year Quickshell sponsorship gye twalangirira mu August. Mu kifo kya sponsorship eyo, outfoxxed ajja kubeera mukozi wa Omacom Foundation owa full-time, ng'akulembera omulimu gwaffe ku Omarchy Shell. Tuteeka omutonzi butereevu wakati w'ekyo kye tuzimba.

Shell kikulu nnyo ku agentic OS gye tukola. Ye bar, menus, notifications, widgets, n'obukwatagana obutono obufuula kompyuta okuwulirwa nga eyo. Quickshell etuwa building blocks ez'okukola experience eyo mu QML. Gatta ekyo ne ba agents abalungi nnyo mu kuwandiika QML, era amangu okukyusa desktop yo kifuuka ekintu ky'osobola okusaba, okugezaako, n'okugaba.

Obujulizi buli mu plugins! Bwe twalangirira sponsorship, community yali emaze okutondawo olukumi mu week esooka eya Quattro. Kati catalogue erina kumpi plugins enkumi ssatu. Games z'osobola okuzannya butereevu okuva ku bar, panels ezilondoola coding agents zo, music players, era ne paka atambulatambula ku bar yo ng'alindirira okukwatibwako. Abantu batwala system eyawulirwanga nga fixed era bagifuula eyabwe. Eyo ye malleable computer efuuka entuufu.

Okubeera n'omutonzi wa Quickshell kuwanguddwa kitegeeza nti tusobola okukola ku stack yonna awamu. Fuula shell eyangu era eyesigika. Fuula kyangu okuzimba plugin ennungi. Longoosa foundations plugin author bw'asanga limitation. Kyusa kye tuyiga okuva mu thousands za experiments ne kifuuka experience ennungi eri buli omu asimba Omarchy. Era laba nti ekintu kyonna nnungi era secure.

Era ng'ayegatta ku Omarchy Core, outfoxxed ajja kuyamba okuteekawo direction ya distro yonna. Shell mukulu nnyo ku experience okubeera afterthought. We waliwo beauty ennyingi, fun, ne possibilities za agentic OS bifuuka ekintu ky'osobola ddala okulaba n'okukozesa.

outfoxxed ye hire ya Omacom Foundation eyokubiri eya full-time, ng'agoberera Krzysztof Wilczyński, Head waffe wa Omarchy Kernel. Ne abantu abakulembera Kernel ne Shell bombi bawanguddwa, tuteeka ensimbi mu foundations za experience yonna. Osobola okusisinkana staff ya foundation ku mukutu.

Tugenda kugenda mu maaso n'okupangisa nga tuyitibwa nga tuwulira abantu abatuufu ng'ayita mu mulimu gwabwe mu community. (Tukirireko tojjuza inbox yange na CVs!)

Kino kye backing ya foundation ky'etukkiriza okukola: wa exceptional open-source developers ekiseera okwesigama ddala. Webale nyo eri buli omu awagira omulimu ogwo, tukyusa ambitions zaffe endala ne zifuuka full-time jobs.

Oyingiziddwa, outfoxxed! Tukole kompyuta eno malleable nnyo.
