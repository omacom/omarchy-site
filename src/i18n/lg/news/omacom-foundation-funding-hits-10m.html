Ensimbi za Omacom Foundation zituuse 10 million USD ne Founding Patrons babiri abapya abegasse ku mission: Drew Houston, cofounder ne co-CEO wa Dropbox, ne Peter Steinberger, omutonzi wa OpenClaw.

Nga Founding Patrons munaana abasooka, Drew ne Peter buli omu awaayo 1 million USD. Ekyo kiwa foundation patrons kkumi ne TEN MILLION DOLLARS ennongoofu okusindika mission yaffe ey'obuvumu okufuula Linux ku desktop okubaawo mu ngeri ennene nnyo.

Kinyuma nnyo okubeera ne Drew ne Peter kubanga nze kennyini mbadde fan munene wa bombi n'ebyo bye batuuseeko. Mbadde customer wa Dropbox ow'essanyu okumpi emyaka ebiri, era kikulu mu ngeri gye nsobola okubeera ne no backup, no cry naye nga nkyusa kompyuta bulijjo.

Ekyo kye kiva Omarchy esindika ne great Dropbox integration butereevu okuva mu box. Genda yokka ku Install > Service > Dropbox, era ojja kubeera ne file manager integration ne bespoke menu panel ya controls.

Era OpenClaw ewaddeyo roadmap ku engeri Omarchy gy'erina okukwataamu explosive growth yaayo. Okozesa otya PR backlogs ezifuuka parabolic amangu? Ate security around plugins? Meetups mu linnya lya pulojekiti zirina okukwatibwa zitya? Dda nafunye essanyu ly'okukozesa advice ne experience ya Peter ku topics ezimu ku zino.

Okusukka ekyo, bombi bakiikirira ddala ekika kya technical ambition kye njagala okwetoloola Omarchy. Drew yafuula personal itch ku kutambuza files wakati wa kompyuta Dropbox. Peter yafuula weekend project OpenClaw, era mu kitundu ekyo yajjukiza buli omu appetite gye eriyo ku kompyuta eziwulirwa nga personal, programmable, era fun.

Eyo ye spirit eri emabega wa Omacom Foundation. Tuli wano okuwaayo ensimbi ku infrastructure, open-source projects, ne developers abeetaagibwa okufuula Linux desktop home ennyumira eri next generation ya computer users.

Mwaniriziddwa, Drew ne Peter. Founding Patrons kkumi. Million dollars kkumi. Prophecy esembedde 2 million USD endala!
