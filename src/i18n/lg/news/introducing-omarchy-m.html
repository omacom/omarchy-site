Apple ekola hardware ennungi. Bulijjo bakoze (okuggyako omulundi ogumu ne butterfly keyboard). Era bangi ku bantu abatuuka ku Omarchy bava ku Mac. Bangi ku bo bandyagadde okukuuma machine n'okufiirwa operating system. Kale tugenda kumaliriza kye Asahi yatandika: Omarchy eddukana bulungi ku Apple Silicon, nga setup fuss ntono.

Omulimu ogwo dda gulina team emabega waayo. Leero tugufuula official: Omarchy M, team yaffe ya Macs ne Apple Silicon (wamu ne members abamu abakuuma Old Macs mu fold).

Mundeke mbategeeze members.

Installer. Marcelo Alcantara azimba native Mac installer. Osindika app ku macOS, efuna signed Omarchy release, etema partitions, era ekusimba mu Omarchy wamu ne macOS yo eriwo. Tewali USB stick, tewali incantations mu terminal. Era yakata build eyayakaza external monitors ng'ayita mu USB-C.

Omarchy Mac. Naeem Malik alabiridde Omarchy Mac okumala omwaka, ng'atuusa Omarchy ku M1 ne M2 machines nga kino kyonna tekinnaba official, era ng'azimba Discord community ya abantu abasukka 800 okwetooloola ekyo. Scott Jones ye half endala eya pulojekiti eyo ku Linux side ya install. Obusembayo yafuna display ya M3 Air nga edduka ku Apple's hardware display controller, ekyava machine eyo okuva ku unusable okutuuka ku usable mu kiro.

Try Omarchy. Eduardo Martinez yazimba Try Omarchy: real Omarchy desktop edduka nga native, hardware-accelerated app ku Mac yo, ne camera yo, audio, clipboard, ne shared folder nga biyungiddwa. Tewali kya kupartition. Sindika yokka era olabe fuss kye kivuddeko.

Packages. Shun Li alaba nti buli kintu Omarchy ky'yeetaaga kiriwo nga aarch64 package, wamu ne generic ARM64 disk images za virtual machines ne guest tools ezibifuula ennyumira okukozesa.

Drivers ne kernel. Ryan Murray azza Neo era azimba foundations team yonna gy'esinziirako. Akola SPTM emulator efuula kisoboka okutrace macOS ku M4 ne chips empya. Traces ezo nnyingi ku kufuna displays, GPUs, audio, wireless, ne Bluetooth nga zikola. Dj awandiika GPU drivers, era yafuna Touch ID nga ekola ng'ayogera butereevu ne Secure Enclave. Miguel Cruz ye specialist wa M5: yakola reverse-engineer ku Apple's proprietary N1 chip era n'afuna Wi-Fi ne Bluetooth zaayo nga zikola mu Linux. Era awaddeyo hardware video encoding ne decoding, Bluetooth, n'okulongoosa kwa desktop ku Neo. Liam azza trackpad ya Neo, power management, speakers, ne headphone output, ne macOS traces za Ryan eziwa essential groundwork. Eryk Wieliczko alabirira M4 support ne clean-room implementations, era akola ku integration ne packaging. Jaidip Subedi yafuna external displays ng'ayita mu USB-C ku M2 MacBook Air.

MLX. Joshua Warren azimba mlx-omarchy, ereeta Apple's MLX machine learning framework ku Linux ku Apple GPUs ng'ayita mu Vulkan. Code yo ekyayogera import mlx.core as mx, naye kati edduka ku Omarchy.

Older Macs. Shawn Yeager alina stack ya pull requests za 2016 ne 2017 Touch Bar MacBook Pros: speakers, Wi-Fi, ambient light sensor, Touch Bar yennyini. Randy akola reverse-engineer ku T2 security chip okuleeta Touch ID ku Intel Macs, era ng'awandiika buli fix ya 2019 MacBook Pro ng'agenda. Jon Kinney yali akola ku ARM support ya Omarchy omwaka oguwedde, era kati akuuma team yonna nga coordinated ku ani akola ki.

Awamu, ye formidable group. Abantu kkumi na bataano abatalinda lukusa, era ababadde bakozesa ba agents okutambula ku sipiidi eyandirabise absurd omwaka oguwedde.

Target ya first release ye perfect compatibility ne M1 ne M2, omuli Pro ne Max variants:

  • Seamless installer, ne install ne try options
  • Full GPU support
  • External monitors ng'ayita mu USB-C
  • Touch ID
  • MLX
  • Disk encryption

Machines empya zikolebwako mu parallel, naye M1 ne M2 we tusimba flag okusooka. Okwongera ku M machines, waliwo millions za vintage laptops ebweru, nnyingi ku zo dda zawummula okuva mu latest macOS ambitions za Apple, era buli emu ye wonderful Omarchy computer elindirira okubaawo.

Toweetaaga kulinda polished release okutandika. Try Omarchy ejja kukuleetera ng'otunula ku real desktop ku Mac yo mu ddakiika. Era bw'oba wetegefu okweewaayo, installer ejja kuteeka Omarchy okumpi ne macOS ku M1 oba M2 leero.
