Omukutu gw'beautiful, fun Linux distribution gulina okuba beautiful ne fun. Kale tuwadde Omarchy.org redesign ejjuvu, era tugutongola mu nnimi 29 (kubanga bw'oba omaze okukuŋŋaanya ~2m USD mu tokens, lwaki nedda?). Retro magic ntono, bingi eby'okulambulako, era okuyingiza ensi okunene.

Guno mukutu ogwagala ozannye ne kompyuta yo! Pixels, colors, ne animations bitwala spirit ya Omarchy butereevu mu browser. Osobola okufuna feel y'ekifo nga tonnaba kusindika ISO emu. Era ye personal connection yange ku formative years ezo mu pre-internet online world ya ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, ne vibrant aesthetic eyo eya 90s. Color nnyingi, polish ntono. Pixels nnyingi, soft gradients ntono.

Kati waliwo ekifo eky'okulaga editors, terminals, agents, games, Windows VM, ne hardware ezifuula Omarchy useful buli lunaku. Lambula community themes, nonya plugin, laba real workstations, oba zuula meetup ekumpi naawe. Sisinkana abantu abagizimba ne patrons abagiwaayo ensimbi. Ktuuse okubeera ne front door erina ekifo ekimala buli kintu ekibaawo munda.
