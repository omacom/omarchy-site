Emyaka 21 egiyise, mu 2005, nnali nkomalirizza college bwe nalaba video yo ng'ozimba blog ne Ruby on Rails ne TextMate ku Mac OS X mu ddakiika kumpi 15. Nagula white MacBook oluvannyuma lw'ebyo, naleka Windows emabega, nagula TextMate, era natandika okuyiga Ruby. Kyakyusa nnyo engeri gye nanyumirwamu coding.
