Omarchy Plugin Marketplace eka dda eya plugins ezisukka 500 era ekula mangu nnyo. Tulina million ideas ku engeri gye tusobola okulongoosaamu enteekateeka eno, omuli automated agent-powered security reviews, naye tuleme okukkiriza perfect okuba omulabe wa good ne fun kati!

Kale mu kujaguza outburst ennene eno eya creativity, tujja kubeera nga tuwakasa empaka za plugin ezisooka nga tukozesa 4,000 USD ze nafuna okuva mu kwogera ennyo ku Omarchy ku X mu week eyayita.

Amateeka gali bwe gati:

  1. Plugins zonna ezisindikibwa ku repository nga Monday August 24 ku 9am CEST tannatuuka zikkirizibwa (omuli ezo eziriwo dda).
  2. Abawangula bajja kulondebwa Omarchy Core empya, ejja kuvote okuzuula podium.
  3. Prize money ejja kuba 2,500 USD ku asooka, 1,000 USD ku okubiri, ne 500 USD ku okusatu.
  4. Olina okusobola okufuna payment ng'oyita mu Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, oba EU IBAN okufuna prize money.

Abawangula bajja kulangirirwa mu week eddako nga tumaze okukebera submissions zonna, naye nga Friday, August 28 tannatuuka.

Munyumirwe era ebirowoozo ebirungi n'okukola okulungi biwangule!
