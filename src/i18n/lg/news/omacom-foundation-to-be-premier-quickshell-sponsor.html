Update, September 9, 2026: Sponsorship eno ekyusiddwa outfoxxed okwegatta ku Omacom Foundation full time nga Head of Omarchy Shell ne member wa Omarchy Core.

More money in, more money out! Second major sponsorship yaffe okuva ku Omacom Foundation egenda eri outfoxxed olw'omulimu gwe omulungi nnyo ku Quickshell. Emaze week emu yokka nga kitundu kya public release, naye dda kiragibwa bulungi nti yali missing, pivotal piece tulyoke tutuukirize ddala The Malleable Computer.

Obujulizi buli mu plugins! Plugins ezisukka lukumi zitondedwa mu week Quattro mwe ebadde ebweru. Eyo testament ya kitalo nnyo ku technical flexibility ya Quickshell, ne engeri ba agents gye baagala nnyo okuwandiika QML code.

Sponsorship eno bwe kityo ewa outfoxxed empeera olw'contribution ye outstanding eri Omarchy, era eyamba okunyweza connection ku long-term plans zaffe eza Age ya Agents (toteka ekyo ku Bluesky, bajja kwesika!). Etermed okumala emyaka esatu out the gate.

Webale nyo mu ngeri ey'enjawulo eri Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, ne Peter Steinberger olw'okufuula kino okusoboka. Oligarchy singa yali tegiriwo, tetwandisobodde okuwagira open-source developers nga bano!
