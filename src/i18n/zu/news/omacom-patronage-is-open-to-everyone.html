Kodwa isisekelo asenzelwe i-Oligarchy nama-Corporate Patron kuphela. Senenzelwe wonke umuntu. Noma ubani ongasekela i-Linux enhle, ejabulisayo ne-agentic.

Ngakho kusukela namuhla, ubuxhasi buvulelwe wonke umuntu. Kunezigaba ezine: i-16 USD, i-256 USD, i-2,048 USD, ne-8,192 USD. Amandla amabhithi, kunjalo! Wonke umxhasi uyingxenye yomgomo ofanayo, futhi yonke idola lithenga into efanayo: ithuba langempela lokufeza isiprofetho se-Linux ku-Desktop!
