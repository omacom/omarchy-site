Iwebhusayithi yokusatshalaliswa kwe-Linux okunhle, okujabulisayo kufanele ibe yinhle futhi ijabulise futhi. Ngakho sinikeze i-Omarchy.org ukwethulwa kabusha okuphelele, futhi siyayethula ngezilimi ezingu-29 (ngoba uma usanda kuqoqa i-~2M USD ngamathokheni, kungani kungenjalo?). Umlingo omncane wakudala, okuningi okuzohlolwa, nokwamukela umhlaba okukhulu kakhulu.

Leyi yiwebhusayithi efuna udlale ngekhompyutha yakho! Amaphikseli, imibala, nezithombe ezinyakazayo zithwala umoya we-Omarchy ziwufake ngqo kusiphequluli. Ungayizwa indawo ngaphambi kokuba ulande i-ISO eyodwa. Futhi kuwuxhumano lwami mathupha kuleyo minyaka yokwakha emhlabeni we-inthanethi wangaphambi kwe-inthanethi wobuciko be-ASCII, i-demoscene ye-Amiga, nalowo buhle obuqhakazile bama-90s. Umbala owengeziwe, ukupholisha okuncane. Amaphikseli amaningi, ama-gradient athambile ambalwa.

Manje kunegumbi lokubonisa abahleli, amatheminali, ama-ejenti, imidlalo, i-Windows VM, nehadiwe eyenza i-Omarchy ibe wusizo nsuku zonke. Phequlula amatimu omphakathi, thola i-plugin, bona ama-workstation angempela, noma thola umhlangano eduze kwakho. Hlangana nabantu abayakhayo kanye nabaxhasi abayixhasayo. Ekugcineni sinomnyango wangaphambili onegumbi elanele lakho konke okwenzeka ngaphakathi.

Futhi lowo mnyango wangaphambili manje ukhuluma ulimi lwakho. Isayithi eliyinhloko nazo zonke izindaba zihunyushiwe. Imanuwali isesiNgisini; sizoyisingatha ngokulandelayo. Lokhu ukuhumusha kokuqala, futhi ukulungiswa okuvela kuzikhulumi zomdabu kwamukelekile kakhulu nge-site repository.

Lokhu kwethulwa kabusha kukhule ngomsebenzi omuhle kakhulu eqenjini le-Omarchy Design. U-Barış Girişmen wadala idizayini eyaba yisisekelo sesayithi entsha, wakha i-pixel-grid shader, futhi waqhuba inani elikhulu kakhulu lokusebenzisa nokupholisha. U-Christoffer Hallas waletha ama-prototype, umsebenzi we-animation, izinguquko zamatimu, kanye nomfudlana oqhubekayo wokucwengwa. U-Daniel Schmier wanikela ngemiklamo nokusebenzisa kwe-logo, izindaba, ukufaka, ithimba, isisekelo, nezigaba zobufakazi.
