I-Omacom Foundation isiqashe umsebenzi wayo wokuqala ogcwele! U-Krzysztof Wilczyński ujoyina ukuhola umsebenzi wethu ku-Omarchy Kernel. Yilokhu ncamashi isisekelo esamiselwa ukukwenza: ukukhokhela abantu abahle ukuze basebenze ezinkingeni ezinzima ukuze kuzuze wonke umuntu.

U-Krzysztof ungumthuthukisi we-Linux kernel wesikhathi eside. Uqhubeka nokunakekela i-PCI Endpoint subsystem kanye ne-native PCI host bridge kanye nama-endpoint driver. Lokho wukupayipi okuhlanganisa i-Linux ne-GPU yakho, idrayivu yakho ye-NVMe, nekhadi lakho le-WiFi. Uphinde abe ngumbukezi emsebenzini omusha we-Rust PCI we-kernel. Ngaphambi kokujula ku-kernel, uchithe iminyaka kubunjiniyela bezistimu, esebenza ku-Kubernetes kanye nama-container runtime.

Manje uzoletha konke lokho kwisipiliyoni ku-Omarchy Kernel. Okugxilwe kukho: ukusebenza, ukuhambisana, nezokuphepha. Sifuna i-Linux indize kumakhompyutha aphathekayo amasha sha kanye nama-ThinkPad akudala ngokufanayo. Sifuna isebenze ngaphandle kwebhokisi emishinini eminingi. Futhi sifuna ikhiywe iqinile.

U-Krzysztof uphinde ajoyine i-Omarchy Core, ngakho uzosiza ukubeka indlela ye-distro iyonke.

Wamukelekile eqenjini, Krzysztof! Ake senze i-kernel ikhale.
