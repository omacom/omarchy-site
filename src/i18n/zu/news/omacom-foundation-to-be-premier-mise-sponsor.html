Uxhaso lwesithathu oluphumile! I-Omacom Foundation iba ngumxhasi oyinhloko we-mise, futhi ngaleyo ndlela ka-jdx, ochithe iminyaka ekha ithuluzi elenza ngokuthula ukuphatha izinguqulo ezahlukene ze-Ruby, i-Node, i-Go kube yinjabulo ukuziphatha, futhi linika wonke amathuluzi akho e-ejenti isiteshi sokuthuthukisa esingahambisana nejubane lokuthumela.

Uma usebenzise i-Omarchy, usebenzise i-mise, noma ngabe uqaphele noma cha. Yilokho okupatha ama-runtime olimi, ngakho mise use -g ruby ikunika i-Ruby ngaphandle komugqa owodwa we-PATH archaeology. Kodwa ingxenye engiyikhathalela kakhulu njengamanje ngama-ejenti.

Yonke i-CLI ye-coding-agent enkulu ku-Omarchy ithunyelwa njenge-stub ye-mise elayisha kancane ku-~/.local/bin/. I-Claude Code, i-Codex, i-OpenCode, i-Antigravity, i-Copilot, i-Crush, i-Grok, i-Pi, i-Oh My Pi - konke kuhleli lapho, kungakubizi lutho kuze kube yisikhathi sokuqala ochaya ngaso eyodwa ngempela. Bese iyazifaka futhi uyasebenza. Alikho iphakheji okufanele ulizingele, ayikho inguqulo okufanele uyinamathisele, alikho ithuluzi okufanele ucabange ngalo. I-omarchy-mise-install <package> igoqa noma iyiphi enye i-CLI ngendlela efanayo, futhi i-omarchy update igcina yonke into yokugcina isesikhathini.

Leyo yindlela esheshayo. Ku-The Age of Agents, igebe phakathi kokuzwa ngethuluzi nokulisebenzisa lidinga ukuba cishe imizuzwana emine, futhi i-mise ikuletha lokho. Akekho owayedinga ukupakisha noma yini ye-distro kuqala.

Intshisekelo ihamba zombili izindlela, futhi: i-traffic eya ku-mise inyuke ngo-24% selokhu yethulwa i-Omarchy! Abantu abaningi bahlangana naleli thuluzi okokuqala ngathi, okuyilokho ncamashi uxhaso okufanele uluqaphele kunokuluthatha kalula.

Ngiphinde, ngiyabonga ngokukhethekile ku-Tobi Lütke, u-Patrick Collison, u-Michael Dell, u-Jack Dorsey, u-Matthew Prince, u-Brendan Iribe, u-Jason Fried, u-Drew Houston, no-Peter Steinberger ngokwenza lokhu kwenzeke.
