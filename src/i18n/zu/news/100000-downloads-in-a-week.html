Ukulanwa kwe-Omarchy ISO okuyikhulu lamawaka evikini eledlule! Abantu abayikhulu lamawaka abanquma ukuthi leli yiviki abazozama ngalo i-Linux (noma ukubuyela kuyo). Yiqembu lamaphathi ngempela!

Ngokwe-bandwidth, sithumele cishe i-petabyte eyodwa kulezo zinsuku eziyisikhombisa. I-terabyte eyinkulungwane ye-Linux ngeviki phakathi kwama-ISO nezibuyekezo zamaphakheji!

Lokhu ngokuqinisekile kwakuzoyiwisa i-Omarchy kwenye inkathi. Kwakuzosithumela sigijimela izibuko noma ama-torrent (futhi sicabanga ngokudinga enye i-mortgage yendlu ukukhokha isikweletu sokusingatha). Kodwa asizange sidinge.

Lokho kungenxa yokuthi i-Cloudflare ihlala phambi kwakho konke: ama-ISO, amaphakheji e-Omarchy, nesibuko sethu se-Arch. Konke ku-R2 nasemuva kwe-CDN yabo. Okungakaze kusebenze kanzima. Futhi yikho isizathu sokuthi abantu abayikhulu lamawaka bathola ukulanda okusheshayo, okunesithukuthezi, okungenazigameko kusuka noma kuphi emhlabeni evikini eledlule.
