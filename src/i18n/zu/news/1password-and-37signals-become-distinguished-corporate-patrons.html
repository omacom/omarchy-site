I-Omacom Foundation inama-Distinguished Corporate Patron ayo okuqala amabili! I-1Password ne-37signals ngayinye inikela nge-100,000 USD ngonyaka eminyakeni emithathu ezayo, okuthatha uxhaso lwesisekelo lwaya ku-12.6 million USD.

I-1Password ibiyingxenye ye-Omarchy kusukela osukwini lokuqala (nengxenye ye-Omakub ngaphambi kwalokho!). Ngoba igcine iziqinisekiso zami ziphephile iminyaka eminingi ngaphambi kokuba ukushintshela ku-Linux kube yinhlansi esweni lami.

Njengayo i-Dropbox, ingelinye lamathuluzi abalulekile enza indlela ye-no backup, no cry. Noma nini lapho ngimisa umshini omusha (okucishe kube kathathu ngeviki kulezi zinsuku!), into yokuqala engiyenzayo yi-Install > Service > 1Password. Bese ukumisa ama-ejenti ami, ukungena ku-Basecamp naku-HEY, nakho konke okunye kulula nje. Noma kunjalo kuphephe ngokuphelele, kuvikelekile ngokuphelele.

I-37signals ibiyisiteshi sokufukamela i-Omarchy selokhu ingakabi nagama. Sesivele sishintshe ithimba lonke lezobuchwepheshe laya ku-Omarchy, futhi besikhokha zonke izinhlobo zezikweletu endleleni eya ku-Quattro (nangaphambili). Manje ukwesekwa kusemthethweni, ngesisekelo, ngemigomo efanayo nawo wonke umuntu.

Kufanele kuqashelwe, futhi, ukuthi imali yenkampani nemali yomuntu ngamunye konke kuthenga into efanayo: ukuqashelwa ngokuba yingxenye yomgomo. Akukho ukushintshana lapha.

I-Omarchy iyohlala isekela izinqumo zayo kulokho okwenza i-OS eguquguqukayo engcono kakhulu emhlabeni. Futhi lokho kuchazwe ngokucacile ngomugqa othi “by DHH” uma ufaka isistimu. Ngaphambi kokuba kube isistimu emangalisayo kuwo wonke umuntu, kufanele kuqala ibe isistimu emangalisayo kimi, mathupha. Gqoka imaski yakho kuqala nakho konke lokho!
