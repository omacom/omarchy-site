I-Omarchy akuyona nje ukusatshalaliswa kwe-Linux okunhle, okujabulisayo ne-agentic kulabo abagcina beyisebenzisa. Yakhiwe ngaleyo ndlela futhi! Ngobuhle, ubumnandi, namathokheni amaningi. Eqinisweni, i-Omarchy Quattro icishe yakhiwe ngama-ejenti kuphela. Ngoba mahle ngendlela emangalisayo ezintweni ezifana ne-bash, i-QML, nokulungisa izinkinga ze-Linux.

Ukuqhubeka nokwenza i-Omarchy ibe ngcono manje njengoba sesihehe amakhulu ezinkulungwane zabasebenzisi nabadali, sidinga ukugcina amathokheni egeleza. Yindlela esheshisa ngayo, sithuthukise, futhi sifeze yonke iminikelo emangalisayo ebilokhu igeleza. Sesivele sine-backlog ye-PR engaphezu kwe-1,600 kusukela emasontweni ambalwa nje ngemuva kokwethulwa! Akekho ozodlula kulokho ngaphandle kosizo lwe-agentic.

Ngakho-ke, ngithokoze kakhulu ukumemezela ukuthi sithole umthwalo omkhulu wamathokheni avela kuma-lab amaningi amakhulu kulo mgomo! Owokuqala yi-Meta Superintelligence Labs eza njenge-Founding Token Patron nge-1.5 million USD ngamathokheni! Imodeli yabo entsha ye-Meta Muse Spark ibeka izinombolo ezinhle ngentengo emangalisayo. Ngakho i-1.5 million USD icishe ibe ngamathokheni angenamkhawulo! (Sizozama ukuwaqeda noma kunjalo!)

Kodwa sithokoze futhi ukwamukela amanye ama-lab amabili ahamba phambili, i-Anthropic ne-OpenAI, njengama-Distinguished Token Patron nge-150,000 USD ngeminikelo yamathokheni ngalinye. Lokhu kuzoba wusizo ikakhulukazi uma kuhlanganiswe nemvume yezokuphepha edingekayo ukukhumula imigoqo esiyidingayo ocwaningweni lwezokuphepha nokulungiswa.

Futhi kumamodeli avulekile, ngithokoze ngokulinganayo ukumemezela i-Fireworks njenge-Distinguished Token Patron futhi nge-150,000 USD ngamathokheni athembisiwe. Bengingumsebenzisi ovamile wamamodeli e-Kimi K ikakhulukazi, futhi ikakhulukazi uma esebenza ngemodi esheshayo. Sizosebenzisa la mathokheni asheshayo kahle futhi!

Kuhlanganiswe, lokhu kungenye i-1.95 million USD ethembiswe Omacom Foundation. Okubeka izithembiso zethu zizonke ku-14.95 million USD. Yiyiphi inombolo ehlanyayo ukuyibhala ye-distro abagxeki bokuqala abayichitha njengeqoqo lama-dotfile (okuthi ngokwethembeka kwakungephosisi ngokuphelele ngaleso sikhathi, kodwa sase siqhubeka silungisa yonke into!).
