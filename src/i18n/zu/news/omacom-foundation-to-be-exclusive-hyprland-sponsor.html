Yiyiphi indlela engcono yokuqala ukusebenzisa enye yengcebo esanda kuyiqoqela i-Omacom Foundation kunengane ye-Linux ehlakaniphe kakhulu e-Poland: u-Vaxry!

Njengomdali we-Hyprland, unesibopho sobunye bobuchwepheshe obuthathu obuyinhloko ku-Omarchy (i-Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), futhi ngithokoze kakhulu ukumemezela uxhaso olukhethekile olumvumela ukuthi agxile ngokugcwele ekuyithuthukiseni phambili ngaphandle kwesidingo sokucabanga ngokuthuthukiswa kwezentengiso noma ngokuthola imali.

Sibhalisela isikhathi eside no-Vaxry ukusekela ikusasa lesikhathi eside le-Omarchy. Isivumelwano sethu soxhaso olukhethekile simiselwe iminyaka emithathu, nenketho yokunye emibili.

Isivumelwano siphinde sivumele uhlelo lwe-Hyprland olwalukhona lwe-Hyprperks ukuba lukhishwe mahhala, kuwo wonke umuntu, njengoba ukubhalisa okukhokhelwayo kuzophela. Kodwa i-Hyprland isayamukela iminikelo yomuntu siqu ukuvumela abantu bathenge ekuthuthukisweni kwephrojekthi futhi basekele u-Vaxry nethimba.

Ngiyabonga kakhulu ku-37signals (sawubona mina! 😂), i-Butterfly, ne-Framework ngokusiza i-Hyprland ifike lapha. Ngithokoze ukuthi i-Omacom Foundation ingathatha kusukela ngo-Okthoba 10, futhi inike omunye wabathuthukisi abahle kakhulu kulesi sikhathi esisha se-Linux ithuba lokungena ngokugcwele.

(* Lesi sivumelwano asifaki noma yiluphi usizo oluqondile ekutholeleni u-Vaxry intombi, kodwa ngizotholakala mathupha njengereferensi yanoma yimiphi imibuzo ngalolu daba.)
