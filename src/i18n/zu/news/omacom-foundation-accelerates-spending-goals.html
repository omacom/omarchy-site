Leli yithuba elikhulu le-Linux. Ideskithophu isitholakala ngendlela ebingakaze ibe khona emashumini eminyaka. Ama-ejenti enze isistimu yokusebenza yaguquguquka. Ihadiwe ayikaze ibe ngcono. Futhi umncintiswano awukaze ufise kakhulu ukucasula abasebenzisi bawo. Ngeke sivumele lo mzuzu udlule!

Ngakho i-Omacom Foundation isheshisa imigomo yayo yokusebenzisa imali. Yonke into eqoqiwe kulo nyaka izosetshenziswa eminyakeni emithathu ezayo. Ayibekwanga kwi-endowment. Ayiconsi kancane eshumini leminyaka. Isetshenziswa!

Nge-13 million USD esezithembisile, lokho kusho izabelo zonyaka ezingaphezu kwe-4 million USD zango-2027, 2028, no-2029. Nangaphezulu njengoba imali ingenela ivela kubaxhasi bazo zonke izinhlobo. Ama-Founding, ama-Distinguished, ama-Corporate, nawo wonke umuntu owajoyina umgomo namuhla.

Le mali iya kumaphrojekthi omthombo ovulekile i-Omarchy eyakhelwe phezu kwawo. Kubaculi be-AIR abayenza ibe yinhle. Kubathuthukisi be-developers abayenza isheshe, ihambisane, futhi ivikeleke. Kwiminqhudelwano, izibonelelo, nengqalasizinda. Kukho konke okudingekayo ukufeza isiprofetho se-Linux ku-Desktop.

Imali akuyona iphuzu. Umgomo yiwona. Kodwa kusiza kakhulu ukuba nakho kokubili!
