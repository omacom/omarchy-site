Ukukhula okuqhumayo kwe-Omarchy kudinga amaqembu ahlelekile njengempendulo, futhi sizokuqala ngokufaka Ithimba le-Omarchy Core. Leli qembu lizokwabelana ngomthwalo wokuqhuba indlela ye-distro yethu, liqinisekise ukuthi siqhubeka nokuqhubekela phambili, futhi lisize abanye abanikeli.

Ake nginethule Ithimba le-Core lokuqala:

U-Ryan Hughes ubeyiphiko lami ku-Omarchy kusukela osukwini lokuqala. Salwa ndawonye nokuvalwa kwe-AUR okuphuthumayo ngo-3am ezinsukwini zokuqala, futhi sahlela ukwakha indawo yethu yokugcina amaphakheji. Iminikelo ka-Ryan inaba zonke izingqimba ze-distro: ukufaka, ukupakisha, ukusekelwa kwehadiwe, ukudlala, ukwenza amatimu, amathuluzi e-CLI, namanje i-Quattro. Usize ukuqhuba ukuthuthela ku-Quickshell, isistimu yama-plugin, kanye nengqalasizinda eningi eguqule i-Omarchy isuka ekusethweni kwe-Linux okuhlanganiswe ngokucophelela yaba isistimu yokusebenza efanele. Uma engehacki ku-Omarchy, u-Ryan ungumlingani ku-Oodle.

U-Tobi Lütke uwukuphela komuntu owenze womabili amaqembu ami ayinhloko! Njengelungu le-Rails Core Team yokuqala eyaqala ngo-2004, waqhuba hhayi kuphela irekhodi elinzulu leminikelo ku-framework, kodwa wabhala ne-Liquid, i-Delayed Job, ne-Active Merchant. Lowo msebenzi wagcina uguquke waba yinkampani encane ebizwa nge-Shopify, uke wezwa ngabo? Manje ungumnikeli okhiqizayo ku-Omarchy ngamaphrojekthi afana ne-try, i-Omasnap, amakhodi e-Wi-Fi QR, kanye nekhonsoli ye-ejenti yesitayela se-Quake. Uphinde abe ngumshayeli nami kwimoto ye-#11 Shopify Racing LMP2 egijima ku-IMSA.

U-Bjarne Øverli uyi-CTO ye-Unite AS kanye nomdali we-Aether, umakhi wamatimu obonakalayo esiwuthumela ku-Omarchy, kanye ne-cliamp, isidlali sethu somculo se-TUI. Usize ukuguqula isistimu yethu yamatimu isuka eqoqweni lamafayela okucushwa yaba yinto ehleleke kakhulu, ngemibala ye-semantic namatimu akhiqizwa ku-Neovim, i-VS Code, i-btop, nayo yonke isitaki. Uphinde abe nesibopho samatimu afana ne-Ethereal, i-Hackerman, ne-Lupine. U-Bjarne unenhlanganisela engavamile yokuqina komhleli kanye nokunambitheka okubonakalayo okuhambelana ne-Omarchy ngokuphelele.

U-HANCORE ungunjiniyela wemishini ngandlela thize owagcina eba ngelinye lamandla achaza ubuhle emphakathini we-Omarchy. Ukhiqize inani elingenangqondo lamatimu, okuhlanganisa i-Last Horizon ne-Solitude, ethunyelwa ngokuzenzakalelayo ku-Omarchy. Manje usiza ukuphatha i-plugin ecosystem ezungeze i-Quattro. I-Omarchy ihlale igcizelela ukuthi ikhompyutha kufanele ibe yinhle futhi ibe wusizo, futhi u-HANCORE uqhubhe lelo sisekelo kude kunacishe wonke umuntu.

U-Spencer Bull ungu-Distinguished Engineer e-Dell osebenza ekuvumelweni kweklayenti le-Linux, i-AI, nesofthiwe yeplatifomu. Ubeyibhuloho lethu eqinisweni elixubile lehadiwe entsha sha, esiza i-Omarchy isebenze kahle ku-Panther Lake nasemishinini ye-XPS yakamuva ngaphambi kokuba ukusalela kwe-Linux okuvamile kuqale. Izinkinga ze-Kernel, abashayeli, izibonisi, i-Wi-Fi, izipikha, ukuphathwa kwamandla: u-Spencer usebenza kungqimba lapho isofthiwe ihlangana khona nama-athomu, futhi ukuba nalolo buchwepheshe ngaphakathi kwe-Core Team kubaluleke kakhulu.
