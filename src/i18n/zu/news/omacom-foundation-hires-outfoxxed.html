Siqasha u-outfoxxed, umdali we-Quickshell, njenge-Nhloko ye-Omarchy Shell! Ujoyina i-Omacom Foundation ngokugcwele futhi uba yilungu le-Omarchy Core. Umuntu owakha ikhithi yamathuluzi eyenza i-Quattro yenzeke uza eqenjini ukusisiza sethule yonke into enyangeni.

Lokhu kudlula uxhaso lweminyaka emithathu lwe-Quickshell esalwamemezela ngo-Agasti. Esikhundleni salolo xhaso, u-outfoxxed uzoba ngumsebenzi ogcwele we-Omacom Foundation, ehola umsebenzi wethu ku-Omarchy Shell. Sibeka umdali ngqo emqoka walokho esikwakhayo.

I-Shell ibalulekile ku-OS ye-agentic esiyenzayo. Yibha, amamenyu, izaziso, amawijethi, nakho konke ukusebenzisana okuncane okwenza ikhompyutha izizwe eyakho. I-Quickshell isinika amabhlokhi okwakha ukubumba lokho kwisipiliyoni ku-QML. Hlanganisa lokho nama-ejenti amahle kakhulu ekubhaleni i-QML, futhi kungazelelwe ukushintsha ideskithophu yakho kuyinto ongayicela, uyizame, futhi wabelane ngayo.

Ubufakazi busekuma-plugin! Uma simemezela uxhaso, umphakathi wawusuvele udale ngaphezu kwenkulungwane evikini lokuqala le-Quattro. Manje ikhathalogi isiseduze nama-plugin ayizinkulungwane ezintathu. Imidlalo ongayidlala ngqo kusuka kubha, amaphaneli alandelela ama-ejenti akho okubhala, abadlali bomculo, ngisho nekati elizulazula kubha yakho lilinde ukuphululwa. Abantu bathatha isistimu eyayizizwa imile futhi bayenza eyabo. Leyo yikhompyutha eguquguqukayo ephila.

Ukuba nomdali we-Quickshell eqenjini kusho ukuthi singasebenza kuso sonke isitaki ndawonye. Senze igobolondo lisheshe futhi lithembeke. Senze kube lula ukwakha i-plugin enhle. Sithuthukise izisekelo uma umbhali we-plugin ehlangabezana nomkhawulo. Siguqule esikufunda ezinkulungwaneni zezivivinyo sibe yisipiliyoni esingcono kuwo wonke umuntu oqala i-Omarchy. Futhi siqinisekise ukuthi yonke into iphephile futhi ivikelekile.

Futhi ngokujoyina i-Omarchy Core, u-outfoxxed uzosiza ukubeka indlela ye-distro iyonke. I-Shell isemqoka kakhulu kwisipiliyoni ukuba ibe umcabango wangemuva. Yilapho okuningi kobuhle, ubumnandi, namathuba e-OS ye-agentic kuba yinto ongayibona ngempela futhi uyisebenzise.

U-outfoxxed ukuqashwa kwesibili okugcwele kwe-Omacom Foundation, elandela u-Krzysztof Wilczyński, iNhloko yethu ye-Omarchy Kernel. Ngabantu abahola zombili i-Kernel ne-Shell eqenjini, sitshala ezisekelweni zayo yonke isipiliyoni. Ungahlangana nabasebenzi besisekelo kusayithi.

Sizoqhubeka nokuqasha ngesimemo njengoba sithola abantu abafanele ngomsebenzi wabo emphakathini. (Ngicela ungagcwalisi ibhokisi lami lokungenayo ngama-CV!)

Yilokhu ukwesekwa kwesisekelo okusivumela ukukwenza: ukunika abathuthukisi abavelele bomthombo ovulekile isikhathi sokungena ngokugcwele. Ngiyabonga kuwo wonke umuntu oxhasa lowo msebenzi, siguqula okuningi kwezifiso zethu sibe yimisebenzi egcwele.

Wamukelekile eqenjini, outfoxxed! Ake senze le khompyutha iguquguquke nakakhulu.
