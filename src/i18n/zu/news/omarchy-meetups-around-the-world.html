I-Omarchy isikhule yaba umphakathi womhlaba wonke, onamalungu acishe abe ngu-35,000 ku-Discord, futhi manje sesifikile isikhathi sokuqala ukuhlangana ubuso nobuso. Ngakho sethula Omarchy Meetups nekhalenda eyabelwana ngayo yemibuthano emhlabeni wonke.

Lena yimicimbi eqhutshwa ngumphakathi. Awudingi imvume ukuhlela eyodwa, akekho onedolobha, futhi noma ubani angahlela eyodwa noma kuphi. Khuluma nge-Omarchy, i-Linux, umthombo ovulekile, ukuhlela, ukwenza ngokwezifiso, noma noma yisiphi isiko lama-hacker eliseduze elenza abantu bajabulele ukwenza amakhompyutha ajabulise futhi.

Yigcine ivulelwe wonke umuntu, dalula noma yibuphi ubuxhasi, futhi ungaguquli igama le-Omarchy libe yibhizinisi lemicimbi noma inkulumo yokuthengisa. Ngale kwalokho, ziphathe njengabantu abadala abaphucuzekile (ngomangaliso wengane), futhi ujabule kakhulu.

Letha ikhompyutha yakho ephathekayo. Bonisa ukusetha kwakho. Shintshana ngamatimu nama-plugin. Siza umuntu ekufakeni kwakhe kokuqala. Iphuzu ukuhlanganisa abantu abakhathalela amakhompyutha omuntu siqu, ahlelekayo, amahle egumbini elilodwa.

Ungakwazi ukuthola umhlangano ozayo noma uthumele owakho ngekhalenda ye-Omarchy yomhlaba wonke ku-Luma. Ake sikwenze lokhu kube yinhlangano yomhlaba wonke!
