U-Brian Armstrong ungumsunguli kanye ne-CEO ye-Coinbase, ezelwe isebenzisa i-Ruby on Rails, yakhula yaba yindawo ephezulu ye-crypto enabasebenzisi abangaphezu kwezigidi eziyikhulu. Eminyakeni engu-21 edlule, ngo-2005, ngangsanda kuphothula ekolishi ngesikhathi ngibuka ividiyo yakho yakha ibhulogi nge-Ruby on Rails ne-TextMate ku-Mac OS X cishe ngemizuzu engu-15.
