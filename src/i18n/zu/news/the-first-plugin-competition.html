I-Omarchy Plugin Marketplace isilikhaya lama-plugin angaphezu kuka-500 futhi ikhula ngokushesha okukhulu. Sinemibono eyisigidi yokuthi singayithuthukisa kanjani le ndlela, okuhlanganisa nokubuyekezwa kwezokuphepha okuzenzakalelayo okusebenza ngama-ejenti, kodwa masingavumeli okuphelele kube yisitha sokuhle nokujabulisa okwamanje!

Ngakho-ke ukugubha lokhu kuqhuma okukhulu kobuciko, sizosingatha umncintiswano wokuqala wama-plugin sisebenzisa ama-4,000 USD engiwathole ngokukhuluma ngokungapheli nge-Omarchy ku-X evikini eledlule.

Imithetho imi kanje:

  1. Wonke ama-plugin athunyelwe endaweni yokugcina ngaphambi kukaMsombuluko Agasti 24 ngo-9am CEST afanelekile (okuhlanganisa nalabo asevele abhaliwe).
  2. Abawinile bazokhethwa yi-Omarchy Core entsha, ezovota ukuthola i-podium.
  3. Imali yomklomelo izoba yi-2,500 USD eyokuqala, i-1,000 USD eyesibili, ne-500 USD eyesithathu.
  4. Kufanele ukwazi ukuthola inkokhelo nge-Zelle, i-Venmo, i-PayPal, noma i-EU IBAN ukuze uqoqe imali yomklomelo.

Abawinile bazomenyezelwa evikini elizayo uma sesiqedile ukubuyekeza konke okuthunyelwe, kodwa kungadluli uLwesihlanu, Agasti 28.

Zijabuliseni futhi imibono engcono nokusebenza okuhle kakhulu kuwine!
