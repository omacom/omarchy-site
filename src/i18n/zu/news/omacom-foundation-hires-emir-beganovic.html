Siqasha u-Emir Beganović njenge-Nhloko yeNgqalasizinda ye-Omarchy! Uqala ngo-Novemba 1 njengokuqashwa kwesithathu okugcwele kwe-Omacom Foundation full-time hire, ejoyina u-Krzysztof Wilczyński ku-Kernel no-outfoxxed ku-Shell.

Nakhu ukuthi u-Emir uchaza kanjani uhambo lwakhe oluya ku-Omarchy:

Uthando lwami lwe-Linux lubuyela emuva iminyaka engaphezu kwamashumi amabili. Ngaba ngumthuthukisi we-Ubuntu ngiseyintsha futhi ngangibandakanyeka kakhulu emphakathini wendawo. Umsebenzi wami ungingenise emazweni amathathu, ngisebenza ngokuthembeka nokusebenza kuma-startup nama-scaleup futhi ngihola amaqembu. Ngichithe iminyaka ecishe ibe yisikhombisa emnyangweni we-SRE we-Booking.com, ngihola umsebenzi ezinhlelweni zokwakha nengqalasizinda ye-CI. Muva nje, ngakha i-Isorun, lapho Bengisebenza khona kuma-microVM, izithombe, ukuhlukaniswa, ukuhlela, nekhompyutha elahlwayo. Manje ngibuyisela lokho kwisipiliyoni othandweni lwami lokuqala.

Okwangibamba ngempela ngo-Emir yi-TryOmarchy. Wathatha ideskithophu ye-Omarchy, wayibeka kusiphequluli (hhayi ukuphinda ukusebenzisa, kodwa into yangempela), futhi wayenza iqale ngemizuzwana engaphansi kwengqalasizinda ye-CPU evamile e-Cloudflare. Saqala ukukhuluma ngalolo vivinyo oluyisilima, futhi ingxoxo yanda ngokushesha kumakhompyutha aphathekayo, izakhiwo ezintsha, ama-OEM, nengqalasizinda esizoyidinga uma i-Omarchy izosebenza yonke indawo esifuna ibe khona. Saqaphela ukuthi sabelana ngombono ofanayo.

Yilokho u-Emir eza ukuzohola.

Ingqalasizinda ye-Omarchy isho okungaphezu kokusebenzisa amaseva. Yilokho esikusebenzisayo ukwakha, ukuhlola, nokuthumela i-Omarchy, nokugcina isistimu isebenza ngokuthembekile njengoba sinabela kwenye ihadiwe nezakhiwo eziningi. Yenza ukukhishwa kuthembeke, yenza ukwehluleka kuphindeke, futhi inike wonke umuntu ophrojekthi isisekelo esiqinile ukuze akwazi ukuhamba ngokushesha.

Futhi kuningi okuzohlolwa! I-Omarchy kufanele isebenze kahle emshinini ohleli edeskini lakho, kodwa asikho isizathu sokuthi ime lapho. I-TryOmarchy isinikeze umbono walokho okwenzeka uma ukuqala ikhompyutha ye-Linux kuba lula njengokuvula ikhasi lewebhu, kubeka ukunambitheka kwe-Linux ngokuchofoza okukodwa nje. Sizobona ukuthi kuholelaphi.

Ngiyabonga kuwo wonke umuntu oxhasa lo msebenzi, singanika abantu abafana no-Emir isikhathi sokungena ngokugcwele ku-Omarchy. Abaxhasi bethu benza kwenzeke ukuguqula izivivinyo ezinesifiso zibe umsebenzi ohlala njalo, nabantu besikhathi esigcwele abazinikele ekwenzeni yonke isistimu ibe ngcono. Sibabongela ukuxhasa kwabo.

Wamukelekile eqenjini, Emir! Ake senze i-Omarchy isebenze yonke indawo.
