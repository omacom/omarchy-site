I-Omacom Foundation iphinde yaqoqa enye ingxenye yesigidi samadola! Phakathi kokunikezwa okusha kwamathokheni, isithembiso senkampani seminyaka emithathu, nezinkulungwane zamadola ezivela kubuxhasi obuvulekile, lokho kucishe kube yi-510,000 USD eyengeziwe ethembiswe ku-Linux enhle, ejabulisayo ne-agentic. Lokho kuletha inani lethu lezithembiso neminikelo licishe libe yi-15.5 million USD!

Okokuqala, samukela i-OpenRouter njenge-Distinguished Corporate Patron nge-150,000 USD ngamathokheni. Uma leso sabelo sesiphelile, singavuselela ilungiselelo ngesivumelwano sobabili. La mathokheni azosekela ama-ejenti akha, ahlola, futhi anakekela i-Omarchy. Ngaphezulu esikwazi ukubeka ngako ama-ejenti emsebenzini elungisa amaphutha, ebuyekeza iminikelo, futhi ethuthukisa i-distro, ngokushesha esingenza ngako ikhompyutha yawo wonke umuntu ibe ngcono. Uphethiloli owengeziwe womgomo!

Sizodlulisela nezigidi zamadola ngamathokheni anikelwe ngeplatifomu enhle kakhulu ye-OpenRouter. Ngakho singabela amathokheni afanele emaqenjini afanele avela kumamodeli afanele.

Bese kukhona i-Four Technologies, ejoyina njenge-Distinguished Corporate Patron ngesithembiso se-100,000 USD ngonyaka iminyaka emithathu. Lokho yi-300,000 USD yokusekela amaphrojekthi omphakathi i-Omarchy ethembele kuwo, ingqalasizinda eyigcina isebenza, nomsebenzi wokuletha i-Linux enhle kubantu abaningi. Ukuba nezinkampani zizinikele iminyaka kusinika ukuzethemba kokwenza okufanayo.

Nakhu ukuthi ithimba le-Four Technologies likubeka kanjani:

Siyinkampani ye-fintech ebheke phambili ku-AI, futhi izinkampani ezifana ne-Four Technologies zithola ukwakha ngendlela esakha ngayo ngoba yengqalasizinda yomthombo ovulekile abanye abantu abayakha kuqala. Ukusekela i-Omacom Foundation yindlela esibeka ngayo imali ngemuva kwalokho. Sicabanga ukuthi isigaba esilandelayo se-AI akuyona enye i-app ebekwe phezu kwesistimu yokusebenza, yi-AI eyakhelwe ngqo kusistimu yokusebenza, futhi yilokho ukubheja i-Omarchy okwenzayo. Besifuna ukuba yingxenye yomphakathi owenza lokho kubheja, hhayi ukuzuza kukho kamuva nje.

Futhi selokhu sivulela wonke umuntu ubuxhasi, sithole cishe i-60,000 USD kubanikeli abacishe babe ngu-500 nge-open patronage. Lokho wukusabela okumangalisayo okuvela kubantu abafuna ukusiza ukwenza lokhu kwenzeke. Wonke umnikelo wengeza kulokho esingenza ndawonye.

Amathokheni okwakha ngawo. Imali yokusekela amaphrojekthi esithembele kuwo. Abantu nezinkampani ezizimisele ukubeka okuthile ngemuva kwenkolelo yazo ku-Linux kudeskithophu. Yindlela esiqhubeka ngayo!

Ngiyabonga ku-OpenRouter, i-Four Technologies, nawo wonke umuntu osebe ngumxhasi. Uma ungathanda ukujoyina bona, bhala ku-david@omarchy.org ngobuxhasi be-founding nobe-distinguished, futhi nikele ngqo kubuxhasi obuvulekile.
