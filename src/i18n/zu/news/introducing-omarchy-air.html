I-Omarchy igcizelela ukuthi ikhompyutha kufanele ibe yinhle njengoba ikhiqiza. Leso sisekelo sithwalwe ngabenzi bamatimu nabanye abaculi be-Omarchy kusukela osukwini lokuqala. Bakha amaphalethi aphelele, ngesikhathi sabo, mahhala, ngoba befuna umshini wabo ubukeke njengokuthile.

Ngakho siqala i-Omarchy AIR - Artists in Residence - ukusekela abahle kakhulu kubo. Indawo yokuhlala yezinyanga eziyisithupha yabaculi ukuze basebenze ngokukhululeka kumatimu, ama-plugin, nanoma yini enye abacabanga ukuthi i-Omarchy iyayidinga ngokulandelayo. Ayikho imephu yendlela, awekho amathikithi. Iphuzu ukuvumela ubuciko bugeleze ngosekelo lwe-Omacom Foundation.

Indawo yokuhlala ngayinye iza ne-2,500 USD ngenyanga ezinyangeni eziyisithupha zonke, i-akhawunti yamathokheni ukuze ama-ejenti angalokothi ame, nokuqashelwa unomphela njenge-alumni ye-AIR uma izinyanga eziyisithupha seziphelile.

Kunezihlalo ezifika kwezintathu ezitholakalayo ngasikhathi sinye, futhi ezimbili zazo sezithathiwe kule sizini yokuqala.

U-HANCORE unamatimu amabili athunyelwa ne-Omarchy - i-Solitude ne-Last Horizon - kanti amanye angamashumi amabili ekhasini lezongezo, ngaphezu kwalokho okunikelwe ngomunye umuntu: i-Batou, i-Oxo Carbon, i-Velvet Night, i-Rose of Dune, i-Shades of Jade, futhi kuqhubeka. Ungunjiniyela wemishini owaba ngelinye lamandla achaza ubuhle kulo mphakathi, uhlala ku-Omarchy Core, futhi usize ukuphatha i-plugin ecosystem.

U-OldJobobo unesibopho samatimu amathathu athunyelwa ne-Omarchy - i-Lumon, i-Miasma, ne-Retro 82 - kanye nesinye esilishumi ekhasini lezongezo, kusukela ku-Batman kuya ku-City-783 kuya ku-Sakura Mochi. Usebenza ngamaphalethi aphelele kunemibala eyodwa, yikho amatimu akhe ebambana kutheminali, i-Neovim, i-btop, negobolondo esikhundleni sokubukeka kahle esithombeni kuphela.
