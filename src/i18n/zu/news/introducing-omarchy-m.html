I-Apple yenza ihadiwe enhle. Ihlale ikwenza (ngaphandle kwaleso sikhathi sekhibhodi ye-butterfly). Futhi iningi labantu abafika ku-Omarchy bavela ku-Mac. Iningi labo lingathanda ukugcina umshini futhi lilahle isistimu yokusebenza. Ngakho sizokuqeda lokho u-Asahi akuqala: i-Omarchy isebenza kahle ku-Apple Silicon, ngomzamo omncane wokusetha.

Lo msebenzi usuvele unethimba ngemuva kwawo. Namuhla sikwenza kube semthethweni: Omarchy M, ithimba lethu lama-Mac ane-Apple Silicon (kanye namalungu ambalwa agcina ama-Mac amadala eseqenjini).

Ake nginethule amalungu.

Isifaki. U-Marcelo Alcantara wakha i-native Mac installer. Ulanda uhlelo ku-macOS, lulanda ukukhishwa kwe-Omarchy okusayiniwe, luqopha ama-partition, futhi lukuqala ku-Omarchy eceleni kwe-macOS yakho ekhona. Ayikho intonga ye-USB, awekho amagama omlingo kutheminali. Uphinde wasika ukwakha okukhanyise izibonisi zangaphandle nge-USB-C.

Omarchy Mac. U-Naeem Malik unakekele i-Omarchy Mac unyaka, efaka i-Omarchy emishinini ye-M1 ne-M2 kudala ngaphambi kokuba noma yikuphi kwalokhu kube semthethweni, futhi ekha umphakathi we-Discord wabantu abangaphezu kuka-800 wayo. U-Scott Jones uyingxenye enye yaleyo phrojekthi ohlangothini lwe-Linux lokufaka. Muva nje wenze isibonisi se-M3 Air sasebenza kusilawuli sesibonisi sehadiwe se-Apple, okuthathe lowo mshini kusukela kokungasebenziseki kuya kokusebenzisekayo ebusuku.

Try Omarchy. U-Eduardo Martinez wakha i-Try Omarchy: ideskithophu ye-Omarchy yangempela isebenza njengohlelo lomdabu, olusheshiswa yihadiwe ku-Mac yakho, nekhamera yakho, umsindo, ibhodi lokunamathisela, nefolda eyabelwana ngayo kuxhunyiwe. Akukho okumele kuhlukaniswe. Mane uyilande futhi ubone ukuthi umsindo umayelana nani.

Amaphakheji. U-Shun Li wenza isiqiniseko sokuthi yonke into i-Omarchy eyidingayo ikhona njenge-aarch64 package, kanye nezithombe zediski ze-ARM64 ezijwayelekile zemishini ebonakalayo namathuluzi ezivakashi awenza amnandi ukuwasebenzisa.

Abashayeli kanye ne-kernel. U-Ryan Murray uletha i-Neo futhi wakha izisekelo ithimba lonke elisebenza kuzo. Wenza i-emulator ye-SPTM eyenza kwenzeke ukulandelela i-macOS kuma-chip e-M4 nasha. Leyo mikhondo ibalulekile ekusebenzeni kwezibonisi, ama-GPU, umsindo, okungenantambo, ne-Bluetooth. U-Dj ubhala abashayeli be-GPU, futhi wenze i-Touch ID yasebenza ngokukhuluma ngqo ne-Secure Enclave. U-Miguel Cruz ugxile emsebenzini we-M5: wenze i-reverse-engineer chip ye-N1 ye-Apple futhi wenze i-Wi-Fi ne-Bluetooth yayo yasebenza ku-Linux. Uphinde wanikela ngokubhala ngekhodi kwevidiyo yehadiwe kanye nokususa ikhodi, i-Bluetooth, nokuthuthukiswa kwedeskithophu ku-Neo. U-Liam uletha i-trackpad ye-Neo, ukuphathwa kwamandla, izipikha, nokuphuma kwama-headphone, nemikhondo ye-macOS ka-Ryan ihlinzeka isisekelo esibalulekile. U-Eryk Wieliczko unakekela ukusekelwa kwe-M4 kanye nokusebenzisa kwe-clean-room, futhi usebenza ekuhlanganiseni nasekupakisheni. U-Jaidip Subedi wenze izibonisi zangaphandle nge-USB-C ku-M2 MacBook Air zasebenza.

MLX. U-Joshua Warren wakha i-mlx-omarchy, eletha uhlaka lwe-MLX lokufunda komshini lwe-Apple ku-Linux kuma-GPU e-Apple nge-Vulkan. Ikhodi yakho isathi import mlx.core as mx, kodwa manje isebenza ku-Omarchy.

Ama-Mac amadala. U-Shawn Yeager unenqwaba yezicelo zokudonsa zama-MacBook Pro e-Touch Bar ango-2016 nango-2017: izipikha, i-Wi-Fi, inzwa yokukhanya kwe-ambient, i-Touch Bar uqobo. U-Randy wenza i-reverse-engineer chip ye-T2 ukuletha i-Touch ID kuma-Mac e-Intel, futhi ubhala yonke indlela yokulungisa ye-MacBook Pro yango-2019. U-Jon Kinney wayesebenza ekusekelweni kwe-ARM kwe-Omarchy emuva konyaka, futhi manje ugcina lonke ithimba lixhumene ngokuthi ubani owenzani.

Ndawonye, bayiqembu elesabekayo. Abantu abayishumi nanhlanu abangalindanga imvume, futhi abebesebenzisa ama-ejenti ukuhamba ngejubane ebelizobonakala lingenangqondo emuva konyaka.

Ithagethi yokukhishwa kokuqala wukuhambisana okuphelele ne-M1 ne-M2, okuhlanganisa izinhlobo ze-Pro ne-Max:

  • Isifaki esibushelelezi, esinezinketho zombili zokufaka nokuzama
  • Ukusekelwa okuphelele kwe-GPU
  • Izibonisi zangaphandle nge-USB-C
  • Touch ID
  • MLX
  • Ukubethelwa kwediski

Imishini emisha isetshenzwa ngokufana, kodwa i-M1 ne-M2 yilapho sitshala khona ifulegi kuqala. Ngaphezu kwemishini ye-M, kunamakhompyutha aphathekayo akudala ayizigidi laphaya, iningi lawo eselathathe umhlalaphansi ezifisweni zakamuva ze-macOS ze-Apple, futhi wonke uwo ngamunye uyikhompyutha ye-Omarchy emnandi elinde ukwenzeka.

Awudingi ukulinda ukukhishwa okupholishiwe ukuze uqale. I-Try Omarchy izokwenza ubuke ideskithophu yangempela ku-Mac yakho ngemizuzu. Futhi uma ukulungele ukuzinikela, isifaki sizobeka i-Omarchy eceleni kwe-macOS ku-M1 noma ku-M2 namuhla.

Okokugcina, isicelo. Uma ubusebenza nge-Linux yehadiwe ye-Apple, noma ngabe abashayeli be-kernel, i-firmware, ukupakisha, izifaki, noma ukufunda komshini, singathanda ukuzwa ngawe. Kunethuba elihle lokuthi uxazulule okuthile esisalokhu sikujaha, noma okuphambene. Xhumana nathi ku-apple@omarchy.org.

Ake siqede lokho u-Asahi akuqala.
