Sesifikile isikhathi sokuphupha okukhulu. I-Omarchy Quattro inike abantu ithuba lokuzwa ukuthi ikhompyutha eguquguqukayo yekusasa ibukeka kanjani, futhi bayayithanda (kakhulu!). Manje kuzwakala njengomthwalo wokuziphatha ukwenza leli kusasa litholakale kabanzi futhi siguqule ngokuyisisekelo indlela abantu abahlobana ngayo namakhompyutha abo okokuqala ngqa kulokho okubonakala kungunaphakade.

Ukwenza lokho ncamashi, ngifaka i-Omacom Foundation ukuqinisekisa ukuthi lo mgomo uxhaswe ngokugcwele, uhlala njalo, futhi ulungele ukusheshisa.

Lesisisekelo esingenzi nzuzo sizobamba izimpawu zokuthengisa, sixhase ingqalasizinda, sikhuthaze umsebenzi, futhi sisekele amaphrojekthi omthombo ovulekile nabathuthukisi i-Omarchy ethembele kubo.
