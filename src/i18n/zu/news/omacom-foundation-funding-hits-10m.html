Uxhaso lwe-Omacom Foundation lusanda kufinyelela ku-10 million USD ngama-Founding Patron amabili amasha ajoyina umgomo: u-Drew Houston, umsunguli kanye ne-co-CEO ye-Dropbox, no-Peter Steinberger, umdali we-OpenClaw.

Njengama-Founding Patron ayisishiyagalombili oqobo, u-Drew no-Peter ngayinye inikela nge-1 million USD. Lokho kunika isisekelo abaxhasi abayishumi kanye ne-TEN MILLION USD ecocekile ukuqhubela phambili umgomo wethu onesibindi wokwenza i-Linux kudeskithophu yenzeke ngendlela enkulu kakhulu.

Kumnandi kakhulu ukuba no-Drew no-Peter eqenjini ngoba mathupha bengumlandeli omkhulu wabo bobabili nempumelelo yabo. Bengiyikhasimende elishisekayo le-Dropbox cishe iminyaka engamashumi amabili, futhi isemqoka endleleni engikwazi ngayo ukuphila nge-no backup, no cry noma ngishintsha amakhompyutha njalo.

Yikho i-Omarchy ithunyelwa ngokuhlanganiswa okuhle kwe-Dropbox ngaphandle kwebhokisi. Mane uye ku-Install > Service > Dropbox, futhi uzoba nokuhlanganiswa kwemenenja yamafayela kanye nephaneli yemenyu eyenziwe ngokwezifiso yezilawuli.

Futhi i-OpenClaw isinikeze impela imephu yendlela yokuthi i-Omarchy kufanele ibhekane kanjani nokukhula kwayo okuqhumayo. Ubhekana kanjani ne-PR backlog eya phezulu kungazelelwe? Kuthiwani ngezokuphepha eduze kwama-plugin? Imihlangano egameni lephrojekthi kufanele iphathwe kanjani? Sesivele ngibe nenjabulo yokusebenzisa iseluleko sika-Peter nesipiliyoni sakhe kwezinye zalezi zihloko.

Ngale kwalokho, bobabili bamele impela uhlobo lwesifiso sezobuchwepheshe engisifuna eduze kwe-Omarchy. U-Drew uguqule ukulunywa komuntu siqu mayelana nokuhambisa amafayela phakathi kwamakhompyutha waba yi-Dropbox. U-Peter uguqule iphrojekthi yangeweekend waba yi-OpenClaw, futhi enqubweni ukhumbuze wonke umuntu ukuthi sikhulu kangakanani isifiso samakhompyutha azizwa engawomuntu, ahlelekayo, najabulisayo.

Yilowo moya ongemuva kwe-Omacom Foundation futhi. Silapha ukuxhasa ingqalasizinda, amaphrojekthi omthombo ovulekile, nabathuthukisi abadingekayo ukwenza ideskithophu ye-Linux ibe yikhaya elimnandi lesizukulwane esilandelayo sabasebenzisi bamakhompyutha.

Wamukelekile, Drew no-Peter. Ama-Founding Patron ayishumi. Izigidi eziyishumi zamadola. Isiprofetho sisanda kusondela nge-2 million USD!
