Isibuyekezo, Septhemba 9, 2026: Lolu xhaso seludluliwe ngu-outfoxxed ojoyina i-Omacom Foundation ngokugcwele njengeNhloko ye-Omarchy Shell kanye nelungu le-Omarchy Core.

Imali eningi engenayo, imali eningi ephumayo! Uxhaso lwethu lwesibili olukhulu oluvela kwi-Omacom Foundation luya ku-outfoxxed ngomsebenzi wakhe omuhle kakhulu ku-Quickshell. Sekuyiviki nje iyingxenye yokukhishwa komphakathi, kodwa sekucacile ukuthi kwakuyisiqephu esilahlekile, esibalulekile ukuze sifinyelele ngempela The Malleable Computer.

Ubufakazi busekuma-plugin! Ama-plugin angaphezu kwenkulungwane adalwe evikini i-Quattro ephumile ngalo. Lokho wubufakazi obumangalisayo ngokuphelele buguquguquko lobuchwepheshe be-Quickshell, nokuthi ama-ejenti ayithanda kangakanani ukubhala ikhodi ye-QML.

Lolu xhaso ngaleyo ndlela luvuza u-outfoxxed ngegalelo lakhe elivelele ku-Omarchy, futhi lusiza ukuqinisa ukuxhumana nezinhlelo zethu zesikhathi eside ze-The Age of Agents (ungakuthumeli lokho ku-Bluesky, bazohlanya!). Lunesikhathi seminyaka emithathu ekuqaleni.

Ngiyabonga ngokukhethekile ku-Tobi Lütke, u-Patrick Collison, u-Michael Dell, u-Jack Dorsey, u-Matthew Prince, u-Brendan Iribe, u-Jason Fried, u-Drew Houston, no-Peter Steinberger ngokwenza lokhu kwenzeke. Ukube kwakungeyona i-Oligarchy, besingeke sikwazi ukuxhasa abathuthukisi bomthombo ovulekile kanje!
