I-Omacom Foundation isivele yeqa i-13 million USD! Ama-Distinguished Patron ethu okuqala amane athembise i-100,000 USD ngalinye emgomweni:

U-Ryan R. Hughes akadingi singethulo lapha. Uyilungu le-Omarchy Core, iphoyisa lami ku-distro kusukela osukwini lokuqala, kanye nomlingani ku-Oodle. Manje usekela isisekelo ngemali yakhe futhi. Lokho wukuzinikela!

U-Ed Huang ungumsunguli kanye ne-CTO ye-PingCAP, inkampani engemuva kwe-TiDB, i-database ye-SQL esatshalaliswa. Uchithe iminyaka eyishumi efakazela ukuthi ingqalasizinda enzulu yomthombo ovulekile ingaba yibhizinisi elinzulu.

U-Adrien Treccani wasungula i-Metaco, inkundla ye-Swiss yokugcinwa kwempahla yedijithali i-Ripple eyayithenga. Omunye umpionir we-crypto ojoyina umgomo wokwenza i-Omarchy ibe yindawo enhle ye-crypto.

U-Max Schoening uyi-Head of Product ku-Notion, futhi ngaphambi kwalokho wayephethe idizayini ku-Heroku futhi eyi-VP ye-Design ku-GitHub. Ubumbe iningi lamathuluzi abathuthukisi abawathandayo kunalokho abantu abaningi abayoke bakwazi.

Ndawonye nama-Founding Patron ayishumi nambili kanye nama-Distinguished Corporate Patron ethu amabili, lokho wabaxhasi abayishumi nesishiyagalombili kanye ne-13 million USD ezithembisiwe ngaphansi kwamasonto amabili.

Wamukelekile eqenjini, nobane. Umgomo uyakhula!
