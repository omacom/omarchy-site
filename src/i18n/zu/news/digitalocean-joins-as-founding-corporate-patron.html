I-DigitalOcean ijoyina i-Omacom Foundation njenge-Founding Corporate Patron, inikela nge-1 million USD ngonyaka iminyaka emithathu! Lokho wukuzinikela kwe-3 million USD ekuthuthukisweni, ukunakekelwa, nokusabalala kwe-Omarchy. Yivoti elingakanani lokuzethemba ku-OS enhle, ejabulisayo ne-agentic esiyakhayo.

Sengisebenzise DigitalOcean Droplets iminyaka engaphezu kweshumi. Ziyindlela enhle eqondile yokubeka umshini we-Linux ku-inthanethi bese uqhubeka nokwakha okuthile. Sizisebenzisa ukuqhuba i-contributors.rubyonrails.org, isayithi egubha izinkulungwane zabantu abasize ukwenza i-Rails yaba yilokhu eyikho. Lena yingqalasizinda engithembele kuyo iminyaka, ngakho ukuba inkampani engemuva kwayo ijoyine kithi kuzwakala kuhle kakhulu.

Futhi bangumxhasi ophelele we-OS ye-agentic. I-DigitalOcean isivele inikeza ama-Droplet achofozeka kanye e-Hermes nase-OpenClaw. Ungaba ne-ejenti ekhaya kudeskithophu yakho ye-Omarchy futhi enye isebenza ubusuku nemini ku-Droplet.

Kodwa ingxenye yalobuxhasi eyangenza ngamomotheka ngempela wukuthi konke kwenzeka ngokushesha okungakanani. Ngaxhumana no-Paddy Srinivasan, i-CEO ye-DigitalOcean, ku-X ngoLwesithathu. Saba nocingo ngalobo busuku. Ngathumela isiphakamiso ngoMgqibelo. NgeSonto, sase siqedile yonke into.

Lokho yijubane lokubambisana okwenzeka nge-X, ama-ejenti, nesifiso esingenamkhawulo! Uma abantu bebona ithuba elifanayo futhi befuna ukwenza okuthile kwenzeke, asikho isizathu sokuchitha izinyanga ukufinyelela ekuvumeni. Singahamba ngejubane lamathuba aphambi kwethu.

I-DigitalOcean ijoyina i-Meta Superintelligence Labs njenge-Founding Corporate Patron, kanye nabo bonke abantu nezinkampani esezivele zinyathelele phambili ukuxhasa lo mgomo. Isithembiso sabo siletha uxhaso lwethu luphelele lucishe lube yi-18.5 million USD. Isikhala esiningi sokutshala kubantu, amaphrojekthi, nengqalasizinda eyenza i-Omarchy ibe ngcono kuwo wonke umuntu.

Njengokuqasha abantu abaningi abamangalisayo! Sisanda kuqasha u-Krzysztof Wilczyński njengeNhloko ye-Omarchy Kernel kanye no-outfoxxed, umdali we-Quickshell, njengeNhloko ye-Omarchy Shell. Ukunika abantu abanjengalaba ithuba lokusebenza ngokugcwele ekwenzeni i-Omarchy isheshe, ithembeke, futhi ibe namandla kungenzeka kuphela ngabaxhasi abafana ne-DigitalOcean. Ukwesekwa kwabo kuguqula izifiso zethu zibe yimisebenzi yangempela yabantu abangakufeza.

Ngiyabonga, Paddy nawo wonke umuntu e-DigitalOcean. Ake sibeke lolu xhaso emsebenzini futhi senze ikusasa le-Linux elihle, elijabulisayo, ne-agentic lenzeke!
