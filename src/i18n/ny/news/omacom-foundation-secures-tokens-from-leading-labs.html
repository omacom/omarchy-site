Omarchy si Linux yokongola, yosangalatsa komanso ya ma agent yokha kwa iwo omwe adzayigwiritse ntchito. Yamangidwanso choncho! Ndi kukongola, zosangalatsa, ndi ma tokeni ambiri. Ndipotu, Omarchy Quattro yamangidwa pafupifupi ndi ma agent okha. Chifukwa ndi abwino kwambiri pa zinthu ngati bash, QML, ndi kukonza mavuto a Linux.

Kuti tipitirize kupanga Omarchy kukhala yabwino tsopano popeza takopa mazana a masauzande a ogwiritsa ntchito ndi opanga nawo, tifunika kusunga ma tokeni akuyenda. Umu ndi momwe tikufulumira, kukonza, ndi kukwaniritsa zopereka zodabwitsa zomwe zakhala zikutsanulira. Tili kale ndi mulu wa ma PR oposa 1,600 kuchokera m'milungu yochepa chitulutso chitatha! Palibe amene angadutse pamenepo popanda thandizo la ma agent.

Chotero, ndine wokondwa kulengeza kuti tapeza ma tokeni ambiri kuchokera ku ma lab ambiri akulu pa ntchitoyi! Choyamba ndi Meta Superintelligence Labs omwe akubwera monga Wothandizira Tokeni Woyambitsa ndi 1.5 million USD mu ma tokeni! Model yawo yatsopano ya Meta Muse Spark ikulemba manambala abwino pa mtengo wodabwitsa. Kotero 1.5 million USD ndi ma tokeni opanda malire! (Tidzayesabe kuwatha!)

Koma ndine wokondwa kulandira ma lab ena awiri otsogola, Anthropic ndi OpenAI, monga Othandizira Tokeni Olemekezeka ndi 150,000 USD mu zopereka za tokeni aliyense. Izi zidzakhala zothandiza makamaka zikaphatikizidwa ndi chilolezo cha chitetezo chofunika kuchotsa zotchingira zomwe tikufuna pa kafukufuku wa chitetezo ndi kukonza.

Ndipo pa ma model a open-weight, ndine wokondwa mofanana kulengeza Fireworks monga Wothandizira Tokeni Wolemekezeka nawonso ndi 150,000 USD mu ma tokeni alonjezedwa. Ndakhala wogwiritsa ntchito pafupipafupi wa ma model a Kimi K2 makamaka, ndipo makamaka akuthamanga pa fast mode. Tidzagwiritsa ntchito ma tokeni othamanga awa bwino!

Pamodzi, awa ndi 1.95 million USD ina yalonjezedwa ku Omacom Foundation. Zomwe zitengera malonjezo athu onse ku 14.95 million USD. Ndi nambala yamisala kulemba pa distro yomwe otsutsa oyamba anainyoza ngati mulu wa ma dotfile (zomwe kunena chilungamo sizinali zolakwika kwathunthu nthawi imeneyo, koma kenako tinapitiriza kukonza chilichonse!).
