Omacom Foundation ili ndi Othandizira Makampani Olemekezeka awiri oyamba! 1Password ndi 37signals aliyense akupereka 100,000 USD pa chaka kwa zaka zitatu zotsatira, zomwe zimatengera ndalama za maziko ku 12.6 million USD.

1Password yakhala gawo la Omarchy kuyambira tsiku loyamba (ndi gawo la Omakub zisanachitike!). Chifukwa yasunga mapasiwedi anga otetezeka kwa zaka zambiri ndisanaganize zosinthira ku Linux.

Monga Dropbox, ndi chimodzi mwa zida zofunika kuti njira ya no backup, no cry igwire ntchito. Nthawi iliyonse ndikakhazikitsa makina atsopano (zomwe ndi katatu pa sabata masiku ano!), chinthu choyamba chimene ndimachita ndi Install > Service > 1Password. Kenako kukhazikitsa ma agent anga, kulowa mu Basecamp ndi HEY, ndi china chilichonse ndi chophweka. Koma chotetezeka kwathunthu, chotetezeka kwathunthu.

37signals yakhala malo oberekera Omarchy kuyambira isanakhale ndi dzina. Talowetsa kale gulu lonse la zaukadaulo ku Omarchy, ndipo takhala tikulipira ngongole zosiyanasiyana panjira yopita ku Quattro (ndi zisanachitike). Tsopano thandizo ndi lovomerezeka, kudzera mu maziko, pa malamulo omwewo monga aliyense.

Ndizofunikanso kudziwa kuti ndalama za kampani ndi ndalama za munthu zimagula chinthu chimodzi: kuzindikira kuti ndi gawo la ntchito. Palibe kusinthana apa.

Omarchy nthawi zonse idzakhazikitsa zisankho zake pa zomwe zimapanga OS yosinthika yabwino kwambiri padziko lapansi. Ndipo izo zafotokozedwa makamaka ndi dzina la "by DHH" mukakhazikitsa dongosolo. Lisanakhale dongosolo lodabwitsa kwa aliyense, poyamba liyenera kukhala dongosolo lodabwitsa kwa ine, pandekha. Valani chigoba chanu poyamba ndi zina zotero!
