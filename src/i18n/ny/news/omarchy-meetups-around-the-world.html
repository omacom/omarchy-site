Omarchy yakula kukhala dera lapadziko lonse, ndi mamembala ~35,000 pa Discord, ndipo tsopano ndi nthawi yoti tiyambe kukumana maso ndi maso. Kotero tikuyambitsa Misonkhano ya Omarchy ndi kalendala yogawidwa ya misonkhano padziko lonse.

Imeneyi ndi misonkhano yoyendetsedwa ndi dera. Simukufuna chilolezo kukonza imodzi, palibe amene ali ndi mzinda, ndipo aliyense akhoza kukonza imodzi kulikonse. Kambiranani za Omarchy, Linux, open source, kupanga mapulogalamu, kusintha, kapena chikhalidwe chilichonse cha hacker chapafupi chomwe chimasangalatsa anthu kupanga makompyuta kukhala osangalatsa kachiwiri.

Isungeni yotseguka kwa aliyense, fotokozani zothandizira zilizonse, ndipo musatembenuze dzina la Omarchy kukhala bizinesi ya misonkhano kapena malonda ogulitsa. Kuposa apo, khalani ngati akulu olemekezeka (ndi kudabwa kwa ana), ndipo sangalalani.

Bweretsani laptop yanu. Wonetsani kukhazikitsa kwanu. Sinthanani mitu ndi mapulagi. Thandizani wina pa kukhazikitsa kwawo koyamba. Cholinga ndikutengera anthu omwe amasamala za makompyuta aumwini, opangidwa, okongola pamodzi m'chipinda chimodzi.

Mutha kupeza msonkhano ukubwera kapena kupereka wanu kudzera mu kalendala yapadziko lonse ya Omarchy pa Luma. Tiyeni tipange izi gulu lapadziko lonse!
