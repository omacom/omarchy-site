Tikulemba outfoxxed, mlengi wa Quickshell, monga Mtsogoleri wa Omarchy Shell! Akulowa mu Omacom Foundation nthawi yonse ndikukhala membala wa Omarchy Core. Munthu amene anamanga zida zomwe zinapangitsa Quattro kukhala kotheka akubwera kuthandiza kutumiza chinthu chonsechi ku mwezi.

Izi zimalowa m'malo mwa thandizo la Quickshell la zaka zitatu lomwe tidalengeza mu August. M'malo mwa thandizolo, outfoxxed adzakhala wogwira ntchito nthawi yonse wa Omacom Foundation, akutsogolera ntchito yathu pa Omarchy Shell. Tikuika mlengi pakatikati pa zimene tikumanga.

Shell ndi yofunika ku OS ya ma agent yomwe tikupanga. Ndi bar, menyu, zidziwitso, ma widget, ndi kuyanjana ting'onoting'ono konse komwe kumapangitsa kompyuta kumveka ngati yanu. Quickshell imatipatsa zomangira zokonza zochitikazo mu QML. Phatikizani izo ndi ma agent omwe ali abwino kwambiri polemba QML, ndipo mwadzidzidzi kusintha desktop yanu ndi china chomwe mungapemphe, kuyesa, ndi kugawana.

Umboni uli mu mapulagi! Pamene tidalengeza thandizo, dera linali litapanga kale oposa chikwi mu sabata yoyamba ya Quattro. Tsopano kabukhu kali ndi pafupifupi mapulagi zikwi zitatu. Masewera omwe mungasewere kuchokera pa bar, mapanelo omwe amatsatira ma coding agent anu, zosewerera nyimbo, ndipo ngakhale mphaka yomwe imayendayenda pa bar yanu ikudikirira kukumbatidwa. Anthu akutenga dongosolo lomwe kale linkamveka lokhazikika ndikulipanga lawo. Imeneyo ndi kompyuta yosinthika ikukhala yamoyo.

Kukhala ndi mlengi wa Quickshell m'bwato kumatanthauza kuti titha kugwira ntchito pa stack yonse limodzi. Kupanga chipolopolo kukhala chofulumira ndi chodalirika. Kupanga kukhala kosavuta kumanga pulagi yabwino. Kukonza maziko pamene wolemba pulagi akumana ndi malire. Kutembenuza zimene tikuphunzira kuchokera ku kuyesa masauzandi kukhala chokumana nacho chabwino kwa aliyense amene amayatsa Omarchy. Ndi kuonetsetsa kuti chinthu chonsecho ndi chotetezeka.

Ndipo polowa Omarchy Core, outfoxxed athandiza kuyika njira ya distro yonse. Shell ndi yapakati kwambiri pa zochitikazo kuti ikhale yaganiziridwa pambuyo. Ndi pamene zambiri za kukongola, zosangalatsa, ndi mwayi wa OS ya ma agent zimakhala china chomwe mungathe kuchiwona ndi kuchigwiritsa ntchito.

outfoxxed ndi wogwira ntchito nthawi yonse wachiwiri wa Omacom Foundation, kutsatira Krzysztof Wilczyński, Mtsogoleri wathu wa Omarchy Kernel. Ndi anthu omwe akutsogolera Kernel ndi Shell m'bwato, tikuyika ndalama mu maziko a chokumana nacho chonse. Mutha kukumana ndi ogwira ntchito a maziko pa tsambali.

Tipitiriza kulemba mwa kuyitanidwa pamene tipeze anthu oyenera kudzera mu ntchito yawo m'dera. (Chonde musasefukitse inbox yanga ndi ma CV!)

Ichi ndi chimene thandizo la maziko limatilola kuchita: kupatsa opanga apadera a open-source nthawi yodzipereka kwathunthu. Zikomo kwa aliyense amene akulipira ntchitoyo, tikutembenuza zambiri za zokhumba zathu kukhala ntchito zanthawi yonse.

Takulandirani, outfoxxed! Tiyeni tipange kompyuta ino kukhala yosinthika kwambiri.
