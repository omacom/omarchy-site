Omacom Foundation ikupitiriza kukula! Othandizira Oyambitsa ena awiri alowa nawo ntchito yathu ndi 1 million USD aliyense, kutengera chuma chathu ku 12 million USD: Brian Armstrong ndi Yunjie Dai.

Brian Armstrong ndi woyambitsa nawo ndi CEO wa Coinbase, yomwe inabadwa ikugwiritsa ntchito Ruby on Rails, ndipo inakula kukhala malo apamwamba a crypto ndi ogwiritsa ntchito oposa mamiliyoni zana. Brian analinso woyamba kukana zopanda pake za ndale kuntchito pamene adalengeza Coinbase kampani yoyang'ana ntchito mu 2020. Anatenga kutsutsidwa kwakukulu pa maganizo amenewa nthawi imeneyo, koma kuyambira pamenepo watsimikiziridwa kwathunthu (munthu woona wa Night's Watch!).

Ndipo pa crypto, ndinasintha maganizo zaka zapitazo. Ndi Brian m'bwato, tipanga Omarchy malo abwino a crypto. Chifukwa titha kukonza chilichonse!

Yunjie Dai ndi wochita bizinesi wa masewera wochokera ku China yemwe amayendetsa TapTap, imodzi mwa nsanja zazikulu kwambiri za dziko zopezera ndi kugawa masewera. Imene sitenga gawo la ndalama kuchokera kwa opanga. Koma ndimulola adzidziwitse yekha, chifukwa kalata yomwe adatumiza inali yabwino:

Zaka 21 zapitazo, mu 2005, ndinali nditamaliza kumene koleji pamene ndinaonera vidiyo yanu yomanga blog ndi Ruby on Rails ndi TextMate pa Mac OS X m'mphindi pafupifupi 15. Ndinagula MacBook yoyera posakhalitsa, ndinasiya Windows, ndinagula TextMate, ndipo ndinayamba kuphunzira Ruby. Zinasintha kwambiri kuchuluka kwa kukondwa kwanga polemba khodi.

Ndine wochita bizinesi wa masewera wochokera ku China, ndipo banja langa ndi ine tsopano tikukhala ku U.S. Timamanga masewera, ndipo timayendetsanso TapTap… Mosiyana ndi App Store kapena Google Play, sititenga gawo la ndalama kuchokera kwa opanga, ngakhale tikugwira ntchito pamlingo watanthauzo komanso mopupindula. Chibadwa chimenechi chikumveka pafupi ndi chimene nthawi zonse ndimachikonda za 37signals: khalani odziyimira pawokha, pezani zanu, musatenge renti chifukwa mungathe, ndipo mangani china chothandiza kuti anthu asankhe kukhala.

Zaka 20 zapitazo, Apple inapanga mapulogalamu oziziritsa kwambiri padziko lapansi. Lero, maganizo amenewo okhumba akuwoneka atapita. Ndikuganiza kuti ambiri mwa ife tikudikirira makina ogwiritsira ntchito omwe amamveka osangalatsa kachiwiri - amakono, okhala ndi maganizo, ndipo opangidwira opanga. Ndipo tsopano, ndi AI ikupanga mapulogalamu kukhala osinthika kwambiri, kutseguka kumafunika kuposa kale: dongosolo lokha liyenera kukhala china chomwe tingachimvetse, kuchisintha, ndi kuchipanga chathu. Omarchy ikumveka ngati ingakhale chimenecho.
