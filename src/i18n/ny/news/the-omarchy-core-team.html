Kukula kophulika kwa Omarchy kumafuna magulu okonzedwa poyankha, ndipo tiyamba mwa kukhazikitsa Gulu Loyambitsa la Omarchy. Gululi ligawana udindo woyendetsa njira ya distro yathu, kuonetsetsa kuti tikupitiriza kupita patsogolo, ndi kuthandiza othandizira ena.

Ndiloledzeni ndikudziwitseni Gulu Loyambitsa loyamba:

Ryan Hughes wakhala mnzanga pa Omarchy kuyambira tsiku loyamba. Tinamenyana limodzi ndi mavuto adzidzidzi a AUR a 3am m'masiku oyamba, ndipo tidakonza kumanga repository yathu ya mapaketi. Zopereka za Ryan zimafalikira pa zigawo zonse za distro: kukhazikitsa, kupakira, thandizo la hardware, masewera, mitu, zida za CLI, ndipo tsopano Quattro. Anathandiza kuyendetsa kusuntha kupita ku Quickshell, dongosolo la mapulagi, ndi zambiri za zomangamanga zomwe zinatembenuza Omarchy kuchoka pa kukhazikitsa kwa Linux kosonkhanitsidwa mosamala kukhala makina ogwiritsira ntchito oyenera. Pamene sakupanga Omarchy, Ryan ndi mnzake ku Oodle.

Tobi Lütke ndi munthu yekha amene wapanga magulu anga awiri oyambira! Monga membala wa Gulu Loyambitsa la Rails loyamba lomwe linayamba mu 2004, adayendetsa osati mbiri yayikulu ya zopereka ku framework, komanso analemba Liquid, Delayed Job, ndi Active Merchant. Ntchito imeneyo pamapeto inasanduka kampani kakang'ono kotchedwa Shopify, mwamvapo? Tsopano ndi wopereka wochuluka ku Omarchy ndi maprojekiti ngati try, Omasnap, Wi-Fi QR codes, ndi the Quake-style agent console. Amakhalanso mnzanga woyendetsa mu galimoto ya #11 Shopify Racing LMP2 ikuthamanga mu IMSA.

Bjarne Øverli ndi CTO wa Unite AS ndi mlengi wa Aether, chomanga mitu chowoneka chomwe timatumiza mu Omarchy, ndi cliamp, chosewerera nyimbo chathu cha TUI. Wathandiza kutembenuza dongosolo lathu la mitu kuchoka pa gulu la mafayilo okonza kukhala china chadongosolo kwambiri, ndi mitundu ya tanthauzo ndi mitu yopangidwa pa Neovim, VS Code, btop, ndi zina zonse za stack. Alinso ndi udindo pa mitu ngati Ethereal, Hackerman, ndi Lupine. Bjarne ali ndi kuphatikiza kosowa kwa kulimba kwa wopanga ndi kukoma kowoneka komwe kumagwirizana ndi Omarchy mwangwiro.

HANCORE ndi injiniya wa makina yemwe mwanjira ina anakhala mmodzi mwa mphamvu zofotokoza kukongola m'dera la Omarchy. Watulutsa mitu yambiri yopusa, kuphatikiza Last Horizon ndi Solitude, yomwe imatumizidwa yokha mu Omarchy. Tsopano akuthandiza kuyang'anira chilengedwe cha mapulagi chozungulira Quattro. Omarchy nthawi zonse yaumirira kuti kompyuta iyenera kukhala yokongola komanso yothandiza, ndipo HANCORE wakankhira lingaliro limenelo kutali kuposa pafupifupi aliyense.

Spencer Bull ndi Distinguished Engineer ku Dell akugwira ntchito yothandiza Linux client, AI, ndi mapulogalamu a platform. Wakhala mlatho wathu ku zenizeni zosokoneza za hardware yatsopano kwambiri, akuthandiza Omarchy kugwira ntchito bwino pa Panther Lake ndi makina aposachedwa a XPS zchedwa wamba za Linux zisanayambe. Mavuto a kernel, madalaivala, ziwonetsero, Wi-Fi, masipika, kasamalidwe ka mphamvu: Spencer amagwira ntchito pa zigawo kumene mapulogalamu amakumana ndi ma atomu, ndipo kukhala ndi ukadaulo umenewu mkati mwa Gulu Loyambitsa ndi kofunika kwambiri.

Asanu awa akhala kale akugwira ntchitoyi. Izi zimangopanga kukhala kovomerezeka. Pamodzi ndi ine, ndi Gulu Loyambitsa loyamba lodabwitsa. Koma silidzakhala gulu lokhalo. Khalani tcheru pa chilengezo cha Omarchy Rangers!

Pamodzi tipanga Omarchy kukhala distro yotsogola ya Linux ya anthu omwe ali okondwa za nthawi ya ma agent, kompyuta yosinthika, ndi makompyuta okongola, amakono, okhala ndi maganizo.

Ku muyaya ndi kupitirira!
