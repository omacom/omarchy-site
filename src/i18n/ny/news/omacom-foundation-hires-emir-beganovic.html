Tikulemba Emir Beganović monga Mtsogoleri wa Zomangamanga za Omarchy! Amayamba November 1 monga Omacom Foundation wachitatu wogwira ntchito nthawi yonse, polowa nawo Krzysztof Wilczyński pa Kernel ndi outfoxxed pa Shell.

Umu ndi momwe Emir amafotokozera ulendo wake ku Omarchy:

Chikondi changa cha Linux chimabwerera zaka zoposa makumi awiri. Ndinakhala wopanga Ubuntu ndili wachinyamata ndipo ndinali kutenga nawo mbali mozama m'dera lathu. Ntchito yanga yandidutsa m'maiko atatu, ndikugwira ntchito yodalirika ndi liwiro m'ma startup ndi ma scaleup ndikutsogolera magulu. Ndinakhala pafupifupi zaka zisanu ndi ziwiri mu dipatimenti ya SRE ya Booking.com, ndikutsogolera ntchito pa build systems ndi CI infrastructure. Posachedwapa, ndinamanga Isorun, kumene ndakhala ndikugwira ntchito pa ma microVM, ma snapshot, kudzipatula, kukonza, ndi compute yotayika. Tsopano ndikubweretsa zochitikazo ku chikondi changa choyamba.

Chimene chinandidabwitsa kwambiri za Emir chinali TryOmarchy. Anatenga desktop ya Omarchy, anaiika mu browser (osati kukhazikitsanso, koma chenicheni), ndipo anaiyambitsa mu masekondi pa zomangamanga wamba za CPU ku Cloudflare. Tinayamba kukambirana za kuyesa kopusa kumeneko, ndipo zokambirana zinakula msanga ku ma laptop, ma architecture atsopano, ma OEM, ndi zomangamanga zomwe tidzafunika ngati Omarchy iyenda kulikonse kumene tikufuna. Tinazindikira kuti timagawana masomphenya omwewo.

Imeneyo ndi imene Emir akubwera kudzatsogolera.

Zomangamanga za Omarchy zimatanthauza zambiri kuposa kuyendetsa ma seva. Ndi zimene timagwiritsa ntchito kumanga, kuyesa, ndi kutumiza Omarchy, ndi kusunga dongosolo likugwira ntchito modalirika pamene tikukula ku hardware ndi ma architecture ambiri. Ndi kupanga zotulutsa zodalirika, kupanga zolephera zobwerezabwereza, ndi kupatsa aliyense pa projekiti maziko olimba kuti athe kuyenda mwachangu.

Ndipo pali zambiri zofufuza! Omarchy iyenera kugwira ntchito mokongola pa makina omwe ali pa tebulo lanu, koma palibe chifukwa iyime pamenepo. TryOmarchy inatipatsa chithunzi cha chimene chimachitika kuyambitsa kompyuta ya Linux kukakhala kophweka monga kutsegula tsamba la web, kuyika kukoma kwa Linux dinani kamodzi kokha. Tidzawona kumene izo zikutitsogolera.

Zikomo kwa aliyense amene akulipira ntchitoyi, titha kupatsa anthu ngati Emir nthawi yodzipereka kwathunthu ku Omarchy. Othandizira athu amapangitsa kukhala kotheka kutembenuza kuyesa kolimba mtima kukhala ntchito yokhalitsa, ndi anthu anthawi yonse odzipereka kupanga dongosolo lonse kukhala labwino. Ndife oyamika pa uthandizira wawo.

Takulandirani, Emir! Tiyeni tipange Omarchy iyende kulikonse.
