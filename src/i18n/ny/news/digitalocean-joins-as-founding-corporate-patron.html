DigitalOcean yalowa mu Omacom Foundation monga Wothandizira Kampani Woyambitsa, ikupereka 1 million USD pa chaka kwa zaka zitatu! Imeneyo ndi lonjezo la 3 million USD ku chitukuko, kukonza, ndi kufalitsa kwa Omarchy. Ndivoti yodabwitsa ya chidaliro mu OS yokongola, yosangalatsa komanso ya ma agent yomwe tikumanga.

Ndakhala ndikugwiritsa ntchito DigitalOcean Droplets kwa zaka zoposa khumi. Ndi njira yosavuta modabwitsa yoyika makina a Linux pa intaneti ndikupitiriza kupanga china. Timawagwiritsa ntchito kuyendetsa contributors.rubyonrails.org, tsamba lomwe limakondwerera masauzandi a anthu omwe athandiza kupanga Rails kukhala chimene ili. Izi ndi zomangamanga zomwe ndakhala ndikudalira kwa zaka, kotero kukhala ndi kampani yomwe ili kuseri kwake kuli nafe kumamveka bwino makamaka.

Ndipo ndi wothandizira wangwiro wa OS ya ma agent. DigitalOcean ili kale ndi ma Droplet a dinani kamodzi a Hermes ndi OpenClaw. Mutha kukhala ndi agent kunyumba pa desktop yanu ya Omarchy ndi wina akugwira ntchito nthawi zonse pa Droplet.

Koma gawo la uthandizira umenewu lomwe linandisekeretsa kwambiri ndi momwe zonse zinasonkhana mwachangu. Ndidafikira kwa Paddy Srinivasan, CEO wa DigitalOcean, pa X pa Lachitatu. Tidayankhulana usiku womwewo. Ndidatumiza lingaliro pa Loweruka. Pofika Lamlungu, tinali tamaliza zonse.

Umenewu ndi liwiro la mgwirizano lomwe ndi lotheka ndi X, ma agent, ndi zokhumba zopanda malire! Anthu akaona mwayi womwewo ndikufuna kupanga china chichitike, palibe chifukwa chotaya miyezi kufika pa inde. Titha kuyenda pa liwiro la mwayi umene uli patsogolo pathu.

DigitalOcean yalowa nawo Meta Superintelligence Labs monga Wothandizira Kampani Woyambitsa, pamodzi ndi anthu ndi makampani onse omwe ayamba kale kulipira ntchitoyi. Lonjezo lawo latengera thandizo lathu lonse ku pafupifupi 18.5 million USD. Malo ambiri oikamo ndalama mwa anthu, maprojekiti, ndi zomangamanga zomwe zimapanga Omarchy kukhala yabwino kwa aliyense.

Monga kulemba anthu ambiri odabwitsa! Tabweretsa kumene Krzysztof Wilczyński monga Mtsogoleri wa Omarchy Kernel ndi outfoxxed, mlengi wa Quickshell, monga Mtsogoleri wa Omarchy Shell. Kupatsa anthu ngati awa mwayi wogwira ntchito nthawi yonse kupanga Omarchy kukhala yofulumira, yodalirika, ndi yokhoza zambiri ndi kotheka kokha ndi othandizira ngati DigitalOcean. Thandizo lawo limatembenuza zokhumba zathu kukhala ntchito zenizeni za anthu omwe angathe kuzikwaniritsa.

Zikomo, Paddy ndi aliyense ku DigitalOcean. Tiyeni tigwiritse ntchito thandizo ili ndipo tipange tsogolo lokongola, losangalatsa, la ma agent la Linux lichitike!
