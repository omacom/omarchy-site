Omacom Foundation yapezanso theka lina la million USD! Pakati pa ma tokeni atsopano, lonjezo la kampani la zaka zitatu, ndi masauzandi a USD kuchokera ku uthandizira wotseguka, amenewo ndi pafupifupi 510,000 USD ina yalonjezedwa ku Linux yokongola, yosangalatsa komanso ya ma agent. Izi zitengera ndalama zathu zonse za malonjezo ndi zopereka ku pafupifupi 15.5 million USD!

Choyamba, tikulandira OpenRouter monga Wothandizira Kampani Wolemekezeka ndi 150,000 USD mu ma tokeni. Gawo limenelo likatha, titha kukonzanso mgwirizano mwa mgwirizano. Ma tokeni awa adzayendetsa ma agent omwe amamanga, kuyesa, ndi kusunga Omarchy. Tikamatha kuyika ma agent kugwira ntchito kukonza zolakwika, kuwunikanso zopereka, ndi kukonza distro, mofulumira tingapangire kompyuta ya aliyense kukhala yabwino. Mafuta ambiri a ntchito!

Tidzayendetsanso mamiliyoni a USD mu ma tokeni omwe aperekedwa kudzera mu nsanja yabwino ya OpenRouter. Kuti tigawire ma tokeni oyenera ku magulu oyenera kuchokera ku ma model oyenera.

Kenako pali Four Technologies, ikulowa monga Wothandizira Kampani Wolemekezeka ndi lonjezo la 100,000 USD pa chaka kwa zaka zitatu. Amenewo ndi 300,000 USD kuthandiza maprojekiti a dera omwe Omarchy imadalira, zomangamanga zomwe zimaisunga ikuyenda, ndi ntchito yobweretsa Linux yokongola kwa anthu ambiri. Kukhala ndi makampani odzipereka kwa zaka kumatipatsa chidaliro chochita chimodzimodzi.

Umu ndi momwe gulu la Four Technologies limayikira:

Ndife kampani ya fintech yoyang'ana AI, ndipo makampani ngati Four Technologies amatha kumanga momwe timamangira chifukwa cha zomangamanga za open-source zomwe anthu ena anamanga poyamba. Kuthandiza Omacom Foundation ndi momwe timayikira ndalama kuseri kwake. Tikuganiza kuti siteji yotsatira ya AI si pulogalamu ina pamwamba pa makina ogwiritsira ntchito, ndi AI yomangidwa mwachindunji mu makina ogwiritsira ntchito, ndipo ndilo beti lomwe Omarchy ikupanga. Tinkafuna kukhala gawo la dera lomwe likupanga beti limenelo, osati kungopindula nalo pambuyo.

Ndipo kuyambira kutsegula uthandizira kwa aliyense, talandira pafupifupi 60,000 USD kuchokera kwa pafupifupi opereka 500 kudzera mu uthandizira wotseguka. Imeneyo ndi yankho lodabwitsa kuchokera kwa anthu omwe akufuna kuthandiza kuti izi zichitike. Chopereka chilichonse chimawonjezera pa zimene tingachite limodzi.

Ma tokeni omangira. Ndalama zothandizira maprojekiti omwe timadalira. Anthu ndi makampani okonzeka kuyika china kuseri kwa chikhulupiriro chawo mu Linux pa desktop. Umu ndi momwe tikupitirira!

Zikomo kwa OpenRouter, Four Technologies, ndi aliyense amene wakhala wothandizira. Ngati mukufuna kulowa nawo, lembani david@omarchy.org pa uthandizira woyambitsa ndi wolemekezeka, ndipo perekanso mwachindunji ku uthandizira wotseguka.
