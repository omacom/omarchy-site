Ndalama za Omacom Foundation zafika pa 10 million USD ndi Othandizira Oyambitsa awiri atsopano omwe alowa nawo ntchito: Drew Houston, woyambitsa nawo ndi co-CEO wa Dropbox, ndi Peter Steinberger, mlengi wa OpenClaw.

Monga Othandizira Oyambitsa asanu ndi atatu oyamba, Drew ndi Peter aliyense akupereka 1 million USD. Izi zimapatsa maziko othandizira khumi ndi 10 MILLION USD yoyera kukankhira ntchito yathu yolimba mtima yopanga Linux pa desktop kuchitika m'njira yayikulu kwambiri.

Ndi chokoma kwambiri kukhala ndi Drew ndi Peter m'bwato chifukwa ndakhala wokonda kwambiri onse awiri ndi zomwe akwaniritsa. Ndakhala kasitomala wokondwa wa Dropbox kwa pafupifupi zaka makumi awiri, ndipo ndi yapakati pa momwe ndingakhalire ndi no backup, no cry koma ndikusinthabe makompyuta nthawi zonse.

Ndi chifukwa chimene Omarchy imabwera ndi mgwirizano wabwino wa Dropbox kuchokera m'bokosi. Ingopitani ku Install > Service > Dropbox, ndipo mudzakhala ndi mgwirizano wa file manager ndi panel ya menyu yopangira zowongolera.

Ndipo OpenClaw yapereka mapu a momwe Omarchy iyenera kuthana ndi kukula kwake kophulika. Mumatani ndi milu ya PR yomwe mwadzidzidzi yakwera? Nanga za chitetezo chozungulira mapulagi? Kodi misonkhano m'dzina la projekiti iyenera kuyendetsedwa bwanji? Ndakhala kale ndi mwayi wogwiritsa ntchito malangizo ndi zochitika za Peter pa mitu ingapo mwa iyi.

Kuposa apo, onse awiri akuimira mtundu wa zokhumba zaukadaulo zomwe ndikufuna mozungulira Omarchy. Drew anatembenuza chikhumbo chaumwini chosuntha mafayilo pakati pa makompyuta kukhala Dropbox. Peter anatembenuza projekiti ya kumapeto kwa sabata kukhala OpenClaw, ndipo panjira anakumbutsa aliyense kuchuluka kwa njala ya makompyuta omwe amamveka aumwini, opangidwa, ndi osangalatsa.

Umenewu ndi mzimu wakuseri kwa Omacom Foundation. Tili pano kulipira zomangamanga, maprojekiti a open-source, ndi opanga omwe akufunika kuti desktop ya Linux ikhale nyumba yosangalatsa ya m'badwo wotsatira wa ogwiritsa ntchito makompyuta.

Takulandirani, Drew ndi Peter. Othandizira Oyambitsa khumi. Madola mamiliyoni khumi. Uneneri wangoyandikira ndi 2 million USD ina!
