Ndi nthawi yolota zazikulu. Omarchy Quattro yapatsa anthu mwayi wokumana ndi kompyuta yosinthika ya m'tsogolo, ndipo akuikonda (kwambiri!). Tsopano zikumveka ngati udindo wamakhalidwe kupanga tsogolo lino kupezeka m'njira yayikulu ndiksintha kwathunthu momwe anthu amagwirizana ndi makompyuta awo kwa nthawi yoyamba m'zinthu zomwe zimawoneka ngati muyaya.

Kuti tichite zimenezo, ndikukhazikitsa Omacom Foundation kuonetsetsa kuti ntchitoyi ili ndi ndalama zonse, yokhalitsa, ndi yokonzeka kufulumira.

Maziko awa opanda phindu adzasunga zizindikiro, adzalipira zomangamanga, adzalimbikitsa ntchito, ndipo adzathandiza maprojekiti a open-source ndi opanga omwe Omarchy imadalira.

Othandizira Oyambitsa khumi ndi awiri awa aliyense akupereka 1 million USD ku ntchitoyi:

  • Tobi Lütke, CEO wa Shopify
  • Patrick Collison, CEO wa Stripe
  • Michael Dell, Chairman ndi CEO wa Dell Technologies
  • Jack Dorsey, Block Head ndi Chairman wa Block
  • Matthew Prince, CEO wa Cloudflare
  • Brendan Iribe, Woyambitsa nawo wa Sesame ndi Oculus
  • Jason Fried, CEO wa 37signals
  • Drew Houston, woyambitsa nawo ndi co-CEO wa Dropbox
  • Peter Steinberger, mlengi wa OpenClaw
  • Brian Armstrong, CEO wa Coinbase
  • Yunjie Dai, woyambitsa nawo wa TapTap
  • Ine mwini

Othandizira Makampani Oyambitsa awa aliyense akupereka 1 million USD pa chaka kwa zaka zitatu kapena 1.5 million USD mu ma tokeni, ndi kukonzanso mwa mgwirizano:

  • DigitalOcean
  • Meta Superintelligence Labs

Othandizira Olemekezeka awa aliyense akupereka 100,000 USD ku ntchitoyi:

  • Ryan R. Hughes, mnzake ku Oodle
  • Ed Huang, woyambitsa nawo ndi CTO wa PingCAP
  • Adrien Treccani, woyambitsa Metaco
  • Max Schoening, Mtsogoleri wa Zopanga ku Notion

Ndipo Othandizira Makampani Olemekezeka awa aliyense akupereka 100,000 USD pa chaka kwa zaka zitatu kapena 150,000 USD mu ma tokeni, ndi kukonzanso mwa mgwirizano:

  • 1Password
  • 37signals
  • Four Technologies
  • Anthropic
  • Fireworks
  • OpenAI
  • OpenRouter

Pafupifupi opereka 500 aperekanso pafupifupi 60,000 USD kudzera mu uthandizira wotseguka, kutengera ndalama zonse za malonjezo ndi zopereka ku pafupifupi 18.5 million USD.

Iyi ndi ndalama yopusa kwambiri, kotero ndikufuna kuonetsetsa kuti ikhala nthawi yayitali, ndi kuti tigwiritse ntchito m'njira yabwino kwambiri. Koma chofunika monga khushoni yodabwitsa ndi voti ya chidaliro yoperekedwa ndi malonjezo awa.

Tidzapanga uneneri wa Chaka cha Linux pa Desktop kukhala woona. Zidutswa zonse zili m'malo tsopano. Nthawi yodzipereka kwathunthu!
