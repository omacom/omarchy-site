Apple imapanga hardware yabwino. Nthawi zonse yakhala ikupanga (kupatulapo nthawi imodzi ya kiyibodi ya butterfly). Ndipo ambiri mwa anthu omwe akubwera ku Omarchy akuchokera ku Mac. Ambiri mwa iwo angakonde kusunga makina ndikuchotsa makina ogwiritsira ntchito. Kotero timaliza chimene Asahi anayamba: Omarchy ikuyenda mokongola pa Apple Silicon, ndi zovuta zochepa pakukhazikitsa.

Ntchitoyi ili kale ndi gulu kuseri kwake. Lero tikupanga kukhala kovomerezeka: Omarchy M, gulu lathu la ma Mac a Apple Silicon (kuphatikiza mamembala ochepa omwe akusunga ma Mac akale).

Ndiloledzeni ndikudziwitseni mamembala.

Wokhazikitsa. Marcelo Alcantara akumanga wokhazikitsa weniweni wa Mac. Mumatsitsa pulogalamu pa macOS, imatenga kutulutsa kosainidwa kwa Omarchy, imagawa ma partition, ndipo imakulowetsani mu Omarchy pafupi ndi macOS yanu yomwe ilipo. Palibe ndodo ya USB, palibe mawu achinsinsi mu terminal. Iye anadulanso build yomwe inayatsa ma monitor akunja kudzera USB-C.

Omarchy Mac. Naeem Malik wasunga Omarchy Mac kwa chaka, akutengera Omarchy pa makina a M1 ndi M2 izi zisanakhale zovomerezeka, ndikumanga dera la Discord la anthu oposa 800 mozungulira. Scott Jones ndi theka lina la projekiti imeneyo ku mbali ya Linux ya kukhazikitsa. Posachedwapa adayendetsa chiwonetsero cha M3 Air pa hardware display controller ya Apple, zomwe zinatengera makinawo kuchoka posagwiritsika ntchito kupita pogwiritsika ntchito mu usiku umodzi.

Yesani Omarchy. Eduardo Martinez anamanga Try Omarchy: desktop yeniyeni ya Omarchy ikuyenda monga pulogalamu yachibadwa, yofulumizitsidwa ndi hardware pa Mac yanu, ndi kamera yanu, mawu, clipboard, ndi foda yogawidwa yolumikizidwa. Palibe chogawa. Ingotsitsani ndipo muone phokoso lake.

Mapaketi. Shun Li akuonetsetsa kuti chilichonse chimene Omarchy ikufuna chilipo monga paketi ya aarch64, pamodzi ndi zithunzi za disk za ARM64 za makina enieni ndi zida za alendo zomwe zimawapangitsa kukhala osangalatsa kugwiritsa ntchito.

Madalaivala ndi kernel. Ryan Murray akubweretsa Neo ndikamanga maziko omwe gulu lonse limagwirira ntchito. Akupanga emulator ya SPTM yomwe imapangitsa kukhala kotheka kutsatira macOS pa ma chip a M4 ndi atsopano. Kutsatira kumeneku ndi kofunika kuti ziwonetsero, ma GPU, mawu, wireless, ndi Bluetooth zigwire ntchito. Dj akulemba madalaivala a GPU, ndipo adayendetsa Touch ID polankhula mwachindunji ndi Secure Enclave. Miguel Cruz ndi katswiri wa ntchito za M5: adasanthula chip chachinsinsi cha N1 cha Apple ndipo adayendetsa Wi-Fi ndi Bluetooth yake mu Linux. Waperekanso kulemba ndi kuwerenga mavidiyo a hardware, Bluetooth, ndi kuwongolera kwa desktop pa Neo. Liam akubweretsa trackpad ya Neo, kasamalidwe ka mphamvu, masipika, ndi kotulutsa ma headphone, ndi kutsatira kwa macOS kwa Ryan komwe kumapereka maziko ofunika. Eryk Wieliczko amasunga thandizo la M4 ndi kukhazikitsa koyera, ndipo amagwira ntchito yolumikiza ndi kupakira. Jaidip Subedi adayendetsa ziwonetsero zakunja kudzera USB-C pa M2 MacBook Air.

MLX. Joshua Warren akumanga mlx-omarchy, yomwe imabweretsa MLX machine learning framework ya Apple ku Linux pa ma GPU a Apple kudzera Vulkan. Khodi yanu imatinso import mlx.core as mx, koma tsopano imayenda pa Omarchy.

Ma Mac akale. Shawn Yeager ali ndi mulu wa ma pull request a 2016 ndi 2017 Touch Bar MacBook Pros: masipika, Wi-Fi, sensor ya kuwala kozungulira, Touch Bar yokha. Randy akusanthula chip cha chitetezo cha T2 kuti abweretse Touch ID ku ma Intel Mac, ndikulemba kukonza kulikonse kwa 2019 MacBook Pro panjira. Jon Kinney anali akugwira ntchito thandizo la ARM la Omarchy chaka chatha, ndipo tsopano amasunga gulu lonse logwirizana pa amene akuchita chiyani.

Pamodzi, ndi gulu lochititsa mantha. Anthu khumi ndi asanu omwe sanadikirire chilolezo, ndipo akhala akugwiritsa ntchito ma agent kuyenda pa liwiro lomwe likanaoneka lopusa chaka chatha.

Cholinga cha kutulutsa koyamba ndi kugwirizana kwangwiro ndi M1 ndi M2, kuphatikiza mitundu ya Pro ndi Max:

  • Wokhazikitsa wosalala, ndi zosankha zonse zokhazikitsa ndi kuyesa
  • Thandizo lathunthu la GPU
  • Ma monitor akunja kudzera USB-C
  • Touch ID
  • MLX
  • Kubisa kwa disk

Makina atsopano akugwiridwa ntchito mofanana, koma M1 ndi M2 ndi pamene timayika mbendera poyamba. Kuphatikiza pa makina a M, pali mamiliyoni a ma laptop akale kunja uko, ambiri awo apuma kale ku zokhumba zaposachedwa za macOS za Apple, ndipo iliyonse mwa iyo ndi kompyuta yabwino ya Omarchy yomwe ikudikirira kuchitika.

Simuyenera kudikirira kutulutsa kokonzedwa kuti muyambe. Try Omarchy ikuwonetsani desktop yeniyeni pa Mac yanu mu mphindi. Ndipo ngati mwakonzeka kudzipereka, wokhazikitsa adzayika Omarchy pafupi ndi macOS pa M1 kapena M2 lero.
