Uwu ndi mwayi waukulu wa Linux. Desktop yatseguka m'njira yomwe sinakhalepo kwa zaka makumi. Ma agent apanga makina ogwiritsira ntchito kukhala osinthika. Hardware sinakhalepo yabwino kuposa pano. Ndipo mpikisano sunakhalepo wofunitsitsa kukwiyitsa ogwiritsa ntchito ake. Sitilola mphindi ino idutse!

Kotero Omacom Foundation ikufulumizitsa zolinga zake zogwiritsa ntchito ndalama. Chilichonse chomwe chapezedwa chaka chino chidzagwiritsidwa ntchito m'zaka zitatu zotsatira. Osati kusungidwa mu endowment. Osati kutulutsidwa pang'onopang'ono kwa zaka khumi. Kugwiritsidwa ntchito!

Ndi 13 million USD yalonjezedwa kale, izi zikutanthauza bajeti za pachaka zoposa 4 million USD za 2027, 2028, ndi 2029. Ndi zambiri pamene ndalama zikulowa kuchokera kwa othandizira a mitundu yonse. Oyambitsa, Olemekezeka, Makampani, ndi aliyense amene walowa nawo ntchito lero.

Ndalama zimenezi zimapita ku maprojekiti a open-source omwe Omarchy yamangidwapo. Kwa ojambula omwe amaiikongoletsa. Kwa opanga omwe amaiipanga kukhala yofulumira, yogwirizana, ndi yotetezeka. Ku mipikisano, zopereka, ndi zomangamanga. Ku chilichonse chomwe chikufunika kukwaniritsa uneneri wa Linux pa Desktop.

Ndalama si cholinga. Ntchito ndi. Koma zimathandiza kukhala ndi zonse ziwiri!

Mukufuna kukhala gawo lake? Khalani wothandizira kudzera mu uthandizira wathu watsopano wotseguka. Ndipo ngati mukufuna zosankha za Wothandizira Woyambitsa kapena Wothandizira Wolemekezeka, kwa anthu kapena makampani, lembani david@omarchy.org.
