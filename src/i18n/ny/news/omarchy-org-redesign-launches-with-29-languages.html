Tsamba la distro yokongola, yosangalatsa ya Linux liyenera kukhala lokongola komanso losangalatsa. Kotero tapatsa Omarchy.org mapangidwe atsopano athunthu, ndipo tikuyambitsa mu zilankhulo 29 (chifukwa mutapeza ~2 million USD mu ma tokeni, bwanji osatero?). Matsenga pang'ono akale, zambiri zofufuza, ndi kulandiridwa kwakukulu ku dziko.

Imeneyi ndi tsamba lomwe limafuna musewere ndi kompyuta yanu! Ma pixel, mitundu, ndi makanema amanyamula mzimu wa Omarchy molunjika mu browser. Mutha kumva malowa musanatsitse ISO imodzi. Ndikugwirizananso kwanga ku zaka zakukula m'dziko la pa intaneti la ASCII art lisanabwere intaneti, demoscene ya Amiga, ndi kukongola kowoneka kwa ma 90. Mtundu wambiri, kupukuta kochepa. Ma pixel ambiri, ma gradient ofewa ochepa.

Tsopano pali malo oWonetsela ma editor, ma terminal, ma agent, masewera, Windows VM, ndi hardware zomwe zimapanga Omarchy kukhala yothandiza tsiku lililonse. Onani mitu ya dera, pezani pulagi, onani ma workstation enieni, kapena pezani msonkhano pafupi nanu. Kumanani ndi anthu akuimanga ndi othandizira akuilipira. Pomaliza tili ndi khomo lakutsogolo lokhala ndi malo okwanira chilichonse chomwe chikuchitika mkati.

Ndipo khomo lakutsogololo tsopano limalankhula chilankhulo chanu. Chiarabu, Chibengali, Chikatalani, Chidanishi, Chingerezi, Chifilipino, Chifinishi, Chifalansa, Chigiriki, Chihindi, Chihangari, Chiaisilandi, Chiairishi, Chiitaliya, Chijapani, Chikoreya, Chilithuania, Chipolishi, Chipwitikizi, Chitchaina Chosavuta, Chisinhala, Chispanishi, Chiswidi, Chitamil, Chithai, Chitaki, Chiurdu, Chiuzbeki, ndi Chivietnamu. Tsamba lalikulu ndi nkhani iliyonse zatanthauziridwa. Buku likadali Chingerezi; tidzalithetsa lotsatira. Izi ndi matanthauzidwe oyamba, ndipo kukonza kuchokera kwa olankhula achibadwa ndi kolandiridwa kwambiri kudzera mu repository ya tsambali.

Tili ndi ma domain a m'deranso: omarchy.ae, omarchy.dk, omarchy.fi, omarchy.fr, omarchy.gr, omarchy.hu, omarchy.in, omarchy.is, omarchy.jp, omarchy.kr, omarchy.mx, omarchy.ph, omarchy.pt, omarchy.se, ndi omarchy.tr.

Pamene tikulumikiza mayina a m'dera otsalira, mutha kugwiritsa ntchito bn.omarchy.org, ca.omarchy.org, ga.omarchy.org, it.omarchy.org, lt.omarchy.org, pl.omarchy.org, si.omarchy.org, ta.omarchy.org, th.omarchy.org, ur.omarchy.org, uz.omarchy.org, vi.omarchy.org, ndi zh.omarchy.org. Gwiritsani ntchito globe pafupi ndi chosankha mutu kudumpha pakati pa zilankhulo.

Mapangidwe atsopanowa anakula kuchokera ku ntchito yabwino kwambiri mu gulu la Omarchy Design. Barış Girişmen adapanga mapangidwe omwe anakhala maziko a tsamba latsopano, anamanga pixel-grid shader, ndikuyendetsa zambiri za kukhazikitsa ndi kupukuta. Christoffer Hallas anabweretsa ma prototype, ntchito ya makanema, kusintha kwa mitu, ndi mtsinje wokhazikika wa kukonza. Daniel Schmier anapereka mapangidwe ndi kukhazikitsa kwa logo, nkhani, kukhazikitsa, gulu, maziko, ndi zigawo za umboni.

Zikomo nawonso kwa Andrés Villagrán, Ryan Hughes, Yam Catzenelson, Eric Carmichael, Wes Oudshoorn, Taha, Aloïs Deniel, Adrian Rangel, Niklas Jul, HANCORE, OldJobobo, and Rick Blalock pa malingaliro, kuyesa, maganizo, ndi ndemanga zogawidwa panjira. Ndi kwa aliyense akuthandiza kuteteza ma domain a m'dera ndi kulandira anthu m'chilankhulo chawo. Kwakhala kosangalatsa kuona zonsezi zikubwera pamodzi.
