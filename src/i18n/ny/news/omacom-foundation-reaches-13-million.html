Omacom Foundation yangodutsa 13 million USD! Othandizira Olemekezeka anayi oyamba alonjeza 100,000 USD aliyense ku ntchitoyi:

Ryan R. Hughes safuna mawu oyamba kuno. Ndi membala wa Omarchy Core, mnzanga pa distro kuyambira tsiku loyamba, ndi mnzake ku Oodle. Tsopano akuthandiza maziko ndi ndalama zake. Uko ndi kutsimikiza!

Ed Huang ndi woyambitsa nawo ndi CTO wa PingCAP, kampani yakuseri kwa TiDB, database ya SQL yogawidwa. Wakhala zaka khumi kutsimikizira kuti zomangamanga zazikulu za open-source zingathekenso kukhala bizinesi yayikulu.

Adrien Treccani anayambitsa Metaco, nsanja ya ku Swiss yosungira katundu wa digito yomwe Ripple idagula. Mpainiya wina wa crypto akulowa nawo ntchito yopanga Omarchy malo abwino a crypto.

Max Schoening ndi Mtsogoleri wa Zopanga ku Notion, ndipo zisanachitike adayendetsa mapangidwe ku Heroku ndipo anali VP wa Mapangidwe ku GitHub. Waumba zambiri mwa zida zomwe opanga amakonda kuposa anthu ambiri angadziwe.

Pamodzi ndi Othandizira Oyambitsa khumi ndi awiri ndi Othandizira Makampani Olemekezeka awiri athu, amenewo ndi othandizira khumi ndi asanu ndi atatu ndi 13 million USD yalonjezedwa m'pasanathe milungu iwiri.

Takulandirani, nonse anayi. Ntchitoyi ikukula!
