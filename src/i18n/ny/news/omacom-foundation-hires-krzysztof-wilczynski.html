Omacom Foundation yangolemba wogwira ntchito nthawi yonse woyamba! Krzysztof Wilczyński alowa kudzatsogolera ntchito yathu pa Omarchy Kernel. Ichi ndi chimene maziko anakhazikitsidwa kuchita: kulipira anthu abwino kuti agwire ntchito zovuta kuti apindule aliyense.

Krzysztof ndi wopanga kernel wa Linux wakale. Amayang'anira limodzi PCI Endpoint subsystem pamodzi ndi madalaivala a native PCI host bridge ndi endpoint. Imeneyo ndi mipope yomwe imalumikiza Linux ku GPU yanu, drive yanu ya NVMe, ndi khadi yanu ya WiFi. Alinso wowunikira pa ntchito yatsopano ya Rust PCI ya kernel. Asanalowe mozama mu kernel, anakhala zaka mu systems engineering, akugwira ntchito pa Kubernetes ndi ma container runtime.

Tsopano adzabweretsa zochitikazo zonse ku Omarchy Kernel. Cholinga: liwiro, kugwirizana, ndi chitetezo. Tikufuna Linux iwuluke pa ma laptop atsopano ndi ma ThinkPad akale mofanana. Tikufuna igwire ntchito kuchokera m'bokosi pa makina ambiri. Ndipo tikufuna ikhale yotsekedwa mwamphamvu.

Krzysztof alinso kulowa Omarchy Core, kotero athandiza kuyika njira ya distro yonse.

Takulandirani, Krzysztof! Tiyeni tipange kernel ifuule.
