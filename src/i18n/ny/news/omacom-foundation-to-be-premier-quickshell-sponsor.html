Zosintha, September 9, 2026: Thandizo limeneli lalowedwa m'malo ndi outfoxxed kulowa Omacom Foundation nthawi yonse monga Mtsogoleri wa Omarchy Shell ndi membala wa Omarchy Core.

Ndalama zambiri zolowa, ndalama zambiri zotuluka! Thandizo lathu lachiwiri lalikulu kuchokera ku Omacom Foundation likupita kwa outfoxxed pa ntchito yake yabwino pa Quickshell. Yakhala gawo la kutulutsa kwa anthu kwa sabata yokha, koma zikuonekeratu kuti inali gawo losowa, lofunika kuti tikwaniritse The Malleable Computer.

Umboni uli mu mapulagi! Mapulagi oposa chikwi chimodzi apangidwa mu sabata yomwe Quattro yatulutsidwa. Umenewu ndi umboni wodabwitsa wa kusinthika kwaukadaulo kwa Quickshell, ndi kuchuluka kwa ma agent omwe amakonda kulemba khodi ya QML.

Thandizo limeneli limapereka mphotho kwa outfoxxed pa chopereka chake chapadera ku Omarchy, ndipo limathandiza kulimbitsa mgwirizano ku zolinga zathu zanthawi yayitali za The Age of Agents (musayalemba zimenezo pa Bluesky, adzapenga!). Lapangidwa kwa zaka zitatu kuyambira pachiyambi.

Zikomo zapadera kwa Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, ndi Peter Steinberger popanga izi kukhala kotheka. Sizikanakhala Oligarchy, sitikanatha kulipira opanga a open-source chonchi!
