Thandizo lachitatu latuluka! Omacom Foundation ikukhala wothandizira wamkulu wa mise, ndipo motero wa jdx, amene wakhala zaka akumanga chida chomwe mwakachetechete chimapangitsa kuyang'anira mitundu yosiyana ya Ruby, Node, Go kukhala kosangalatsa, ndipo chimapatsa zida zanu zonse za agent njira yosinthira yomwe ingayendere liwiro la kutulutsa.

Ngati mwagwiritsapo Omarchy, mwagwiritsa ntchito mise, kaya munazindikira kapena ayi. Ndi chimene chimayang'anira ma runtime a zilankhulo, kotero mise use -g ruby imakupatsani Ruby popanda mzere umodzi wa PATH archaeology. Koma gawo lomwe ndimasamala kwambiri tsopano ndi ma agent.

CLI iliyonse yayikulu ya coding-agent mu Omarchy imatumizidwa monga stub ya mise yodzaza pang'onopang'ono mu ~/.local/bin/. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - zonse zili pamenepo, sizikuwonongerani chilichonse mpaka nthawi yoyamba yomwe mulemba imodzi. Kenako imadziika yokha ndipo mukugwira ntchito. Palibe phukusi lofuna, palibe mtundu wokhazikitsa, palibe toolchain yoganizira. omarchy-mise-install <package> imakulunga CLI ina iliyonse m'njira yomweyo, ndipo omarchy update imasunga iliyonse yaposachedwa.

Imeneyo ndi njira yofulumira. Mu The Age of Agents, kusiyana pakati pa kumva za chida ndi kuchiyendetsa kuyenera kukhala pafupifupi masekondi anayi, ndipo mise imapereka zimenezo. Palibe amene anayenera kupakira chilichonse pa distro poyamba.

Chidwi chimayenda mbali zonse: anthu obwera ku mise akwera 24% kuyambira Omarchy itayambika! Anthu ambiri akukumana ndi chida ichi kwa nthawi yoyamba kudzera mwa ife, chomwe ndi chimene thandizo liyenera kuzindikira osati kutenga mopepuka.

Kachiwiri, zikomo zapadera kwa Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, ndi Peter Steinberger popanga izi kukhala kotheka. Sizikanakhala Oligarchy, sitikanatha kulipira opanga a open-source chonchi!
