Omarchy imaumirira kuti kompyuta iyenera kukhala yokongola monga momwe imagwirira ntchito. Lingaliro limeneli lanyamulidwa ndi opanga mitu ndi ojambula ena a Omarchy kuyambira tsiku loyamba. Kumanga mitundu yonse, pa nthawi yawo, kwaulere, chifukwa ankafuna makina awo awoneke ngati china.

Kotero tikuyamba Omarchy AIR - Ojambula Oitanidwa - kuthandiza abwino kwambiri mwa iwo. Kukhala kwa miyezi isanu ndi umodzi kuti ojambula agwire ntchito momasuka pa mitu, mapulagi, ndi china chilichonse chimene akuona kuti Omarchy ikufuna. Palibe roadmap, palibe matikiti. Cholinga ndikulola luso liyende ndi thandizo la Omacom Foundation.

Kukhala kulikonse kumabwera ndi 2,500 USD pa mwezi kwa miyezi isanu ndi umodzi yonse, akaunti ya tokeni kuti ma agent asayime, ndi kuzindikira kosatha monga alumni a AIR miyezi isanu ndi umodzi ikatha.

Pali mipando itatu yokha pa nthawi imodzi, ndipo iwiri mwa iyo yatengedwa kale pa nyengo yoyamba ino.

HANCORE ili ndi mitu iwiri yomwe imabwera ndi Omarchy - Solitude and Last Horizon - ndi ina makumi awiri pa tsamba lowonjezera, yoposa imene wina aliyense wapereka: Batou, Oxo Carbon, Velvet Night, Rose of Dune, Shades of Jade, ndipo ikupitirira. Iye ndi injiniya wa makina yemwe anakhala mmodzi mwa anthu ofotokoza kukongola m'dera lino, ali pa Omarchy Core, ndipo amathandiza kuyang'anira chilengedwe cha mapulagi.

OldJobobo ndi oyang'anira mitu itatu ya mitu yomwe imabwera ndi Omarchy - Lumon, Miasma, and Retro 82 - kuphatikiza ina khumi pa tsamba lowonjezera, kuchokera ku Batman kupita ku City-783 kupita ku Sakura Mochi. Amagwira ntchito m'mitundu yonse osati dongosolo limodzi la mtundu, chimene ndi chifukwa mitu yake imagwirizana pa terminal, Neovim, btop, ndi chipolopolo m'malo mongowoneka bwino pa chithunzi.

Mpando wotsalira ndi woitanidwa. Pulogalamu imayamba mu October.
