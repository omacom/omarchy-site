We dey hire outfoxxed, di creator of Quickshell, as Head of Omarchy Shell! E dey join di Omacom Foundation full time and e dey become member of Omarchy Core. Di pesin wey build di toolkit wey make Quattro possible dey come aboard to help us launch di whole thing go moon.

Dis one don supersede di three-year Quickshell sponsorship wey we announce for August. Instead of dat sponsorship, outfoxxed go be full-time worker of Omacom Foundation, e dey lead our work for Omarchy Shell. We dey put di creator right for di center of wetin we dey build.

Shell na pivotal to di agentic OS wey we dey make. Na di bar, di menus, di notifications, di widgets, and all di small-small interactions wey dey make computer feel like yours. Quickshell dey give us di building blocks to shape dat experience for QML. Combine dat with agents wey sabi QML well-well, and suddenly to change your desktop na sometin you fit ask for, try, and share.

Di proof dey for di plugins! When we announce di sponsorship, di community don already create pass one thousand for Quattro first week. Now di catalogue don near three thousand plugins. Games wey you fit play right from di bar, panels wey dey track your coding agents, music players, and even cat wey dey waka along your bar dey wait make dem pet am. Pipo dey take system wey bin dey feel fixed and dem dey make am dia own. Na di malleable computer dey come to life be dat.

To get di creator of Quickshell on board mean say we fit work on di whole stack togeda. Make di shell faster and more reliable. Make am easier to build great plugin. Improve di foundations when plugin author jam limitation. Turn wetin we dey learn from thousands of experiments to beta experience for everybody wey boot Omarchy. And make sure say di whole thing safe and secure.

And by to join Omarchy Core, outfoxxed go help set direction for di distro as a whole. Shell too central to di experience to be afterthought. Na where plenty of di beauty, di fun, and di possibilities of agentic OS dey become sometin wey you fit actually see and use.

outfoxxed na Omacom Foundation second full-time hire, e dey follow Krzysztof Wilczyński, our Head of Omarchy Kernel. With di pipo wey dey lead both Kernel and Shell on board, we dey invest for di foundations of di whole experience. You fit meet the foundation staff for di site.

We go continue to dey hire by invitation as we dey see di correct pipo through dia work for di community. (Abeg no flood my inbox with CVs!)

Na wetin di foundation backing dey let us do be dis: give exceptional open-source developers time to go all in. Tanks to everybody wey dey fund dat work, we dey turn more of our ambitions to full-time jobs.

Welcome aboard, outfoxxed! Make we make dis computer even more malleable.
