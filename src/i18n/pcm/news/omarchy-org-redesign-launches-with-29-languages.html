Di website for fine, sweet Linux distribution suppose fine and sweet too. So we don give Omarchy.org complete redesign, and we dey launch am for 29 languages (because when you just don raise ~2m USD inside tokens, why di hell not?). Small retro magic, plenty more to explore, and much bigger welcome to di world.

Dis na website wey wan make you play with your computer! Di pixels, colors, and animations dey carry di spirit of Omarchy straight enter browser. You fit get feel of di place before you don even daunlod single ISO. E na still my own personal connection to those formative years for pre-internet online world of ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, and dat vibrant aesthetic of di 90s. More color, less polish. More pixels, fewer soft gradients.

Room don dey now to show off di editors, terminals, agents, games, Windows VM, and hardware wey dey make Omarchy useful everyday. Browse community themes, find plugin, see real workstations, or discover mitop near you. Meet di pipo wey dey build am and di patrons wey dey fund am. We finally get front door with enough room for everything wey dey happen inside.

And dat front door now dey speak your language. Arabic, Bengali, Catalan, Danish, English, Filipino, Finnish, French, Greek, Hindi, Hungarian, Icelandic, Irish, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Lithuanian, Polish, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Sinhala, Spanish, Swedish, Tamil, Thai, Turkish, Urdu, Uzbek, and Vietnamese. Dem don translate di main site and every news tori. Di manual still dey for English; we go tackle dat one next. Dese na initial translations, and corrections from native speakers dey very welcome through the site repository.

We get local domains too: omarchy.ae, omarchy.dk, omarchy.fi, omarchy.fr, omarchy.gr, omarchy.hu, omarchy.in, omarchy.is, omarchy.jp, omarchy.kr, omarchy.mx, omarchy.ph, omarchy.pt, omarchy.se, and omarchy.tr.
