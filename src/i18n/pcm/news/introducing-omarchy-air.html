Omarchy dey insist say computer suppose fine as e take productive. Artists wey dey make themes and oda Omarchy artists don carry dat premise since day one. Dem dey build whole palettes, for dia own time, for free, because dem wan make dia machine look like sometin.

So we dey start Omarchy AIR - Artists in Residence - to support di best of dem. Six-month residency for artists to work freely on themes, plugins, and whatever else dem tink say Omarchy need next. No roadmap, no tickets. Di point na to let creativity flow with Omacom Foundation support.

Each residency come with 2,500 USD per month for di full six months, token account so di agents no go ever need to stop, and permanent recognition as AIR alumni once di six months don finish.

Three seats dey available at any one time, and dem don take two of dem for dis first season.

HANCORE get two themes wey dey ship with Omarchy - Solitude and Last Horizon - and anoda twenty for the extras page, pass wetin anybody else don contribute: Batou, Oxo Carbon, Velvet Night, Rose of Dune, Shades of Jade, and e still dey go. E na mechanical engineer wey don become one of di defining aesthetic forces for dis community, e dey sit on Omarchy Core, and e dey help steward the plugin ecosystem.

OldJobobo na im dey responsible for three of di themes wey dey ship with Omarchy - Lumon, Miasma, and Retro 82 - plus anoda ten for di extras page, from Batman to City-783 to Sakura Mochi. E dey work with whole palettes instead of one-off colour schemes, na why im themes dey hold togeda across terminal, Neovim, btop, and di shell instead of only to fine for screenshot.

Di last seat na by invitation. Di program dey begin for October.

I like Vitruvius three requirements for good architecture: firmitas, utilitas, venustas. E must stand, e must useful, and e must fine. No be two out of three. We go invest for all of dem. We fit fix everything.
