We dey hire Emir Beganović as Head of Infrastructure for Omarchy! E dey start November 1 as di Omacom Foundation third full-time hire, e dey join Krzysztof Wilczyński for Kernel and outfoxxed for Shell.

Na so Emir take describe im own journey to Omarchy:

My love for Linux don pass twenty years. I become Ubuntu developer while I still be teenager and I bin dey deeply involved for di local community. My career don carry me pass three countries, I dey work on reliability and performance for startups and scaleups and I dey lead teams. I spend nearly seven years for Booking.com SRE department, I dey lead work on build systems and CI infrastructure. Most recently, I build Isorun, where I don dey work on microVMs, snapshots, isolation, scheduling, and disposable compute. Now I dey bring dat experience back to my first love.

Wetin really catch my attention about Emir na TryOmarchy. E take Omarchy desktop, put am for browser (no be reimplementation, but di real thing), and e bin dey boot inside seconds for ordinary CPU infrastructure for Cloudflare. We start to dey tok about dat silly experiment, and di conversation quick expand go laptops, new architectures, OEMs, and di infrastructure wey we go need if Omarchy go run everywhere wey we wan am to. We realise say we share di same vision.

Na wetin Emir dey come aboard to lead be dat.

Infrastructure for Omarchy mean much more dan to dey run servers. Na wetin we dey use take build, test, and ship Omarchy, and to keep di system dey work reliably as we dey expand go more hardware and more architectures. E dey make releases dependable, e dey make failures reproducible, and e dey give everybody else for di project solid foundation so dem fit move quickly.

And plenty dey to explore! Omarchy suppose work fine-fine for di machine wey sit down for your desk, but no reason why e suppose stop there. TryOmarchy give us glimpse of wetin dey happen when to start Linux computer become as easy as to open web page, e dey put taste of Linux just one click away. We go see where dat one dey lead go.

Tanks to everybody wey dey fund dis work, we fit give pipo like Emir time to go all in for Omarchy. Our patrons dey make am possible to turn ambitious experiments to lasting work, with full-time pipo wey dedicate to make di whole system beta. We dey grateful for dia patronage.

Welcome aboard, Emir! Make we make Omarchy run everywhere.
