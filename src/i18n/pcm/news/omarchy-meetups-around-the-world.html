Omarchy don grow enter global community, with ~35,000 members for the Discord, and now e don reach time make we start to dey meet face to face. So we dey launch Omarchy Mitops with shared calendar for gatherings around di world.

Dese na community-run events. You no need permission to organise one, nobody own city, and anybody fit put one togeda anywhere. Tok about Omarchy, Linux, open source, programming, customization, or whatever adjacent hacker culture wey dey make pipo excited about to make computers fun again.

Keep am open to everybody, disclose any sponsorships, and no turn Omarchy name to events business or sales pitch. Pass dat, behave like civilized adults (with child-like wonder), and catch great time.

Bring your laptop. Show off your setup. Swap themes and plugins. Help somebody pass dia first installation. Di point na to get di pipo wey care about personal, programmable, fine computers togeda for di same room.

You fit find mitop wey dey come or submit your own through di global Omarchy calendar for Luma. Make we make dis worldwide movement!
