Third sponsorship don comot door! Omacom Foundation dey become premier sponsor of mise, and thereby of jdx, wey don spend years dey build di tool wey quietly dey make managing different versions of Ruby, Node, Go joy to manage, and dey give all your agent tooling upgrade channel wey fit keep up with pace of shipping.

If you don use Omarchy, you don use mise, whether or not you notice. Na wetin dey manage di language runtimes be dat, so mise use -g ruby go give you Ruby without single line of PATH archaeology. But di part wey I care most about right now na di agents.

Every major coding-agent CLI for Omarchy dey ship as lazy-loading mise stub for ~/.local/bin/. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - all of dem dey sit down there, e no dey cost you nothing until di first time you actually type one. Den e go instal imself and you don dey work. No package to hunt down, no version to pin, no toolchain to reason about. omarchy-mise-install <package> dey wrap any oda CLI di same way, and omarchy update dey keep every last one current.

Na di fast track be dat. For The Age of Agents, di gap between to hear about tool and to run am need to be like four seconds, and mise dey deliver dat. Nobody need to package anything for distro first.

Di interest dey run both ways, too: traffic to mise don go up 24% since Omarchy launch! Plenty pipo dey meet dis tool for di first time through us, wey na exactly di kind thing wey sponsorship suppose recognise instead of to take am for granted.
