Omarchy don grow so fast say thousands of pipo dey arrive at once, and most of dem dey arrive from macOS or Windows. Dat na plenty first installations, plenty “where I even go start”, and plenty questions wey real answer from somebody wey sabi help odas go benefit.

Enter Omarchy Rangers: new official team wey dem set up to help pipo find dia road.

Di first three members (and dia Discord handles):

  • Mihai @Michael91, Romania
  • Mateo Vaz @m4teo, Uruguay
  • Nira @.+ N1R4 .+, Nepal

Three pipo, three continents, wey be roughly di point. Omarchy community no dey keep office hours for single time zone, and di pipo wey dey help am sef no suppose. The Discord dey always buzz.

Because almost nobody dey switch go Linux alone. And dem dey often stay because somebody make di first week feel welcoming. Some of dem den take return di favor by to do di same for di next pesin. Na so dis whole thing take dey grow.

If you like to help new users find dia road, consider to join Omarchy Rangers. Apply go rangers@omarchy.org.
