Omarchy Plugin Marketplace isanzwe irimo plugin zirenga 500 kandi ikura vuba cyane. Dufite ibitekerezo miliyoni ku buryo dushobora kunoza iyi setup, harimo na automated agent-powered security reviews, ariko reka indashyikirwa itaba umwanzi w’ibyiza n’umunezero ubu!

Ngwino twizihize uku kwaguka gukomeye k’ubuhanga, tuzakira irushanwa rya mbere rya plugin dukoresheje 4,000 USD nakiriye kubera kuvuga Omarchy kuri X icyumweru gishize nta gahunda.

Amategeko ni aya akurikira:

  1. Plugin zose zoherejwe muri repository mbere ya Mbere August 24 saa 9am CEST zirajyamo (harimo n’izisanzwe ziri).
  2. Abatsinze bazatoranywa na Omarchy Core nshya, izatora kugira ngo ibone podium.
  3. Amafaranga y’igihembo azaba 2,500 USD ku wa mbere, 1,000 USD ku wa kabiri, na 500 USD ku wa gatatu.
  4. Ugomba kuba ushobora kwakira ubwishyu na Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, cyangwa EU IBAN kugira ngo wakire amafaranga y’igihembo.

Abatsinze bazatangazwa icyumweru gitaha tumaze gusuzuma submissions zose, ariko bitarenze Gatanu, August 28.
