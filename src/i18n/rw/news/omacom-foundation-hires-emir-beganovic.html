Tugiye guha akazi Emir Beganović nka Head of Infrastructure ya Omarchy! Atangira November 1 muri Omacom Foundation nka full-time hire wa gatatu, yinjiye na Krzysztof Wilczyński kuri Kernel na outfoxxed kuri Shell.

Dore uko Emir asobanura urugendo rwe bwite rugana kuri Omarchy:

Urukundo rwanjye rwa Linux rumaze imyaka irenga makumyabiri. Nabaye Ubuntu developer nkiri muto kandi nari active cyane mu muryango w’aho. Umwuga wanjye wanyujije mu bihugu bitatu, nkora ku kwizerwa no ku muvuduko muri startups na scaleups kandi nyoboye amakipe. Namaze imyaka hafi irindwi muri departemani ya SRE ya Booking.com, nyoboye umurimo kuri build systems na CI infrastructure. Vuba aha, nubatswe Isorun, aho nakoraga kuri microVMs, snapshots, isolation, scheduling, na disposable compute. Ubu nzanye ubwo bunararibonye ku rukundo rwanjye rwa mbere.

Icyamfashe cyane kuri Emir ni TryOmarchy. Yafashe desktop ya Omarchy, ayishyira muri browser (si iyisubiramo, ahubwo ni iy’ukuri), maze ayituma ifunguka mu masegonda kuri CPU infrastructure isanzwe muri Cloudflare. Twatangiye kuganira kuri icyo experiment cy’ubupfu, maze ikiganiro cyagutse vuba kuri laptops, architectures nshya, OEMs, na infrastructure tuzakenera niba Omarchy igiye gukorera hose tuyishaka. Twabonye dusangiye vision imwe.

Ibyo ni byo Emir aje kuyobora.

Infrastructure ya Omarchy isobanura byinshi kurusha gukoresha seriveri. Ni icyo dukoresha kubaka, kugerageza, no kohereza Omarchy, no gukomeza sisitemu ikora neza uko twaguka kuri hardware nyinshi na architectures nyinshi. Ni gutuma releases zizerwa, gutuma failures zisubirwamo, no guha bose ku mushinga foundation ikomeye kugira ngo bashobore kugenda vuba.

Kandi hari byinshi byo gushakashaka! Omarchy igomba gukora neza kuri machine iri ku biro byawe, ariko nta mpamvu igomba guhagarara aho. TryOmarchy yaduhaye akaboneka k’ibiba iyo gutangiza Linux computer biba byoroshye nko gufungura paji ya web, ishyira uburyohe bwa Linux click imwe away gusa. Tugiye kubona aho bigana.

Murakoze kuri bose batera inkunga uyu murimo, dushobora guha abantu nka Emir igihe cyo kwinjiramo byose kuri Omarchy. Abaterankunga bacu bituma bishoboka guhindura experiments z’icyifuzo imirimo irambye, hamwe n’abantu ba full-time bahariye gutuma sisitemu yose iba nziza. Tubashimira patronage yabo.

Murakaze neza, Emir! Reka dutume Omarchy ikorera hose.
