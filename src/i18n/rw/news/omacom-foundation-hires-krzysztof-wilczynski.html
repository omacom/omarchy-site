Omacom Foundation imaze guha akazi full-time wa mbere! Krzysztof Wilczyński arinjiye kuyobora umurimo wacu kuri Omarchy Kernel. Iki ni neza icyo fondasiyo yashyiriweho: guhemba abantu beza gukora ku bibazo bigoye ku nyungu za bose.

Krzysztof ni umwubatsi wa Linux kernel wa kera. Afatanyije kuyobora PCI Endpoint subsystem hamwe na native PCI host bridge na endpoint drivers. Iyo ni imiyoboro ihuza Linux na GPU yawe, NVMe drive yawe, na WiFi card yawe. Ni reviewer na we ku murimo mushya wa Rust PCI wa kernel. Mbere yo kwinjira cyane muri kernel, yamaze imyaka muri systems engineering, akora kuri Kubernetes na container runtimes.

Ubu azazana ubwo bunararibonye bwose kuri Omarchy Kernel. Icyibandwaho: umuvuduko, kujyana, n’umutekano. Dushaka Linux iguruka kuri laptops nshya na ThinkPads za kera kimwe. Dushaka ikora kuva mu gasanduku ku mashini nyinshi. Kandi dushaka ifunze neza.

Krzysztof arinjiye na Omarchy Core, ngwino azafasha kugena icyerekezo cya distro muri rusange.

Murakaze neza, Krzysztof! Reka dutume kernel isakuza.
