Urubuga rwa distribution nziza, ishimishije rwa Linux rugomba kuba rwiza kandi rushimishije na rwo. Ngwino twahaye Omarchy.org ishusho nshya yuzuye, kandi turayitangiza mu ndimi 29 (kuko iyo umaze gukusanya hafi 2m USD mu ma token, kuki oya?!). Amarozi make ya retro, byinshi byo gushakashaka, no kwakira isi kwagutse cyane.

Uru ni urubuga rushaka ko ukina na mudasobwa yawe! Pixels, amabara, na animations bitwara spirit ya Omarchy ako kanya muri browser. Ushobora kumenya aho hantu mbere yuko upakurura ISO imwe. Ni na isano yanjye bwite ku myaka yambere mu isi ya online mbere ya internet ya ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, n’uburanga bukomeye bwa 90s. Amabara menshi, polish nke. Pixels nyinshi, soft gradients nke.

Ubu hari umwanya wo kwerekana editors, terminals, agents, games, Windows VM, na hardware ituma Omarchy ingirakamaro buri munsi. Reba insanganyamatsiko z’abaturage, shakisha plugin, reba workstations nyazo, cyangwa menya meetup hafi yawe. Hura na abantu bayubaka na patrons bayitera inkunga. Amaherezo dufite umuryango w’imbere ufite umwanya uhagije wa byose bibera imbere.

Kandi uwo muryango w’imbere ubu uvuga ururimi rwawe. Icyarabu, Ikibengali, Igikatalani, Ikidaninwa, Icyongereza, Igifilipino, Igifini, Igifaransa, Ikigereki, Ikihindi, Igihongiriya, Icyisilande, Icirilande, Igitaliyani, Ikiyapani, Igikoreya, Ikilituwaniya, Igipolonye, Igiporutigali, Igishinwa cyoroshye, Igisinhala, Icyesipanyoli, Igisuwedi, Igitamili, Igitayi, Igiturukiya, Icyurudu, Icyuzubeki, na Ikiviyetinamu. Site nkuru na buri nkuru zirahinduwe. Manual iracyari mu Cyongereza; tuzayikora ubutaha. Izi ni ibihinduwe bya mbere, kandi ibikosorwa bivuye ku bavuga kavukire byakirwa cyane binyuze muri site repository.

Iyi redesign yakuwe mu bwiyongere bwiza bw’umurimo muri Omarchy Design group. Barış Girişmen yaremye igishushanyo cyabaye foundation ya site nshya, yubatse pixel-grid shader, kandi yayoboye implementation na polish nyinshi cyane. Christoffer Hallas yazanye prototypes, umurimo wa animation, theme transitions, na refinements zikomeza. Daniel Schmier yatanze designs na implementation za logo, news, install, team, foundation, na testimonial sections.

Murakoze na Andrés Villagrán, Ryan Hughes, Yam Catzenelson, Eric Carmichael, Wes Oudshoorn, Taha, Aloïs Deniel, Adrian Rangel, Niklas Jul, HANCORE, OldJobobo, na Rick Blalock ku concepts, experiments, ibitekerezo, na feedback byasangiwe mu nzira.
