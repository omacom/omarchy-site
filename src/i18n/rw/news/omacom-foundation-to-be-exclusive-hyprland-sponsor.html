Ni iyihe nzira nziza yo gutangira gukoresha bimwe mu butunzi twakusanyije kuri Omacom Foundation kurusha ku mwana ukomeye wa Linux muri Poland: Vaxry!

Nk’uwar emye Hyprland, ashinzwe imwe mu technologies core eshatu muri Omarchy (Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), kandi nishimiye gutangaza exclusive sponsorship imwemerera kwibanda byose kuyiteza imbere adakeneye gutekereza commercial development cyangwa fundraising.

Twiyandikishije igihe kirekire na Vaxry gushyigikira ejo hazaza ha Omarchy igihe kirekire. Exclusive sponsorship deal yacu iteganyijwe imyaka itatu, hamwe n’amahitamo y’indi ibiri.

Deal kandi yemera ko Hyprperks program isanzwe ya Hyprland isohokera ubuntu, kuri bose, kuko paid subscriptions zizarangira. Ariko Hyprland izakomeza kwemera personal donations kugira ngo individuals bagure iterambere ry’umushinga no gushyigikira Vaxry n’ikipe.

Murakoze cyane kuri 37signals (muraho njye! 😂), Butterfly, na Framework gufasha Hyprland kugera aha. Nishimiye ko Omacom Foundation ishobora gusimbura kuva October 10, no guha umwe mu ba developers beza muri iki gihe gishya cya Linux amahirwe yo kwinjiramo byose.
