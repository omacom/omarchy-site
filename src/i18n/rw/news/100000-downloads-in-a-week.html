Ibihumbi ijana ipakurura rya Omarchy ISO mu cyumweru gishize! Abantu ibihumbi ijana bafashe icyemezo ko iki ari cyo cyumweru bazageragerezamo Linux (cyangwa bayigarukaho). Ni agatsiko k’abahanga!

Mu bijyanye na bandwidth, twohereje hafi petabyte imwe muri iyo minsi irindwi. Terabyte igihumbi ya Linux mu cyumweru hagati ya ISOs na package updates!

Ibi byari gutuma Omarchy igwa mu kindi gihe. Byari kutwohereza gushakisha mirrors cyangwa torrents (no gutekereza ko dukeneye indi mortgage ku nzu kugira ngo twishyure hosting bill). Ariko ntibyari bikenewe.

Ibyo ni uko Cloudflare iri imbere yabyo byose: ISOs, packages za Omarchy, na Arch mirror yacu. Byose biri kuri R2 kandi inyuma ya CDN yabo. Nta na kimwe muri byo cyigeze gicika intege. Kandi ibyo ni byo bituma abantu ibihumbi ijana bagize ipakurura ryihuta, risanzwe, ridafite ibibazo bava ahantu hose ku isi mu cyumweru gishize.

Murakoze kuri bose muri Cloudflare, na Matthew Prince by’umwihariko. Matthew ni umwe muri Founding Patrons, ibyo bisobanura ko ashyigikiye uyu mushinga na infrastructure n’amafaranga ye bwite. Muchas gracias, señor!

ISOs ibihumbi ijana mu cyumweru. Wow.
