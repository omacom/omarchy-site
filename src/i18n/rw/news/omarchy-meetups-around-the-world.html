Omarchy yakuze iba umuryango mpuzamahanga, ufite ~35,000 members kuri Discord, kandi ubu ni igihe twatangira guhura face to face. Ngwino dutangize Omarchy Meetups hamwe na kalenseri isangiwe y’amateraniro hirya no hino ku isi.

Izi ni events ziyobowe n’umuryango. Ntukeneye uburenganzira gutegura imwe, ntawe ufite umujyi, kandi uwo ari we wese ashobora guteranya imwe ahantu hose. Vuga kuri Omarchy, Linux, open source, programming, customization, cyangwa ikindi hacker culture cyegereye gitera abantu ishyaka ryo gutuma mudasobwa zongera gushimisha.

Bigire bifunguye kuri bose, menyesha sponsorships zose, kandi ntuhindure izina rya Omarchy ubucuruzi bw’events cyangwa sales pitch. Kurenga ibyo, zachukire nk’abantu bakuru b’umuco (bafite gutangara nk’abana), maze mwishimishe cyane.

Zana laptop yawe. Werekanes setup yawe. Mungurane themes na plugins. Fasha somebody mu installation ye ya mbere. Ikigamijwe ni uguhuza abantu bitaye kuri mudasobwa personal, programmable, nziza mu cyumba kimwe.

Ushobora gushaka meetup iri imbere cyangwa kohereza iyawe ukoresheje kalenseri mpuzamahanga ya Omarchy kuri Luma. Reka tubigire worldwide movement!
