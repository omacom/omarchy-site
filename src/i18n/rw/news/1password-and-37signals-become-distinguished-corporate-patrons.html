Omacom Foundation ifite Distinguished Corporate Patrons bayo babiri ba mbere! 1Password na 37signals buri imwe itanga 100,000 USD mu mwaka mu myaka itatu iri imbere, ibyo bizana inkunga ya fondasiyo kuri 12.6 million USD.

1Password yari igice cya Omarchy kuva ku munsi wa mbere (n’igice cya Omakub mbere yaho!). Kuko yakomeje credentials zanjye zitekanye imyaka myinshi mbere yuko guhindukira kuri Linux biba twinkle mu jisho ryanjye.

Nka Dropbox, ni imwe mu tools z’ingenzi zo gushoboza uburyo bwa no backup, no cry. Iyo nteguye mashini nshya (ni nka inshuro eshatu mu cyumweru muri iyi minsi!), ikintu cya mbere nkora ni Install > Service > 1Password. Hanyuma gutegura aba agenti banjye, kwinjira muri Basecamp na HEY, n’ibindi byose ni trivial gusa. Nyamara bitekanye neza, birinzwe neza.

37signals yari incubation station ya Omarchy kuva mbere yuko igira izina. Tumaze guhindura ikipe yose ya technical kuri Omarchy, kandi tumaze kwishyura bills zose mu nzira igana kuri Quattro (na mbere). Ubu inkunga ni formal, binyuze muri fondasiyo, ku mategeko amwe n’abandi bose.

Bikwiye kwibutsa, na byo, ko amafaranga ya corporate n’aya individual byose bigura ikintu kimwe: kwemerwa kuba igice cya mission. Nta quid pro quo hano.

Omarchy izahora ishingira ibyemezo byayo ku kintu gikora OS yoroha nziza cyane ku isi. Kandi ibyo birasobanutse cyane na “by DHH” byline iyo ushyizeho sisitemu. Mbere yuko iba sisitemu itangaje kuri bose, igomba kuba sisitemu itangaje kuri njye, njye bwite. Banza wambare mask yawe ubanza n’ibyo!
