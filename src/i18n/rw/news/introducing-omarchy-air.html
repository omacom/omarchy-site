Omarchy ishimangira ko mudasobwa igomba kuba nziza nk’uko igira akamaro. Iyo premise yakomejwe n’abakora themes n’abandi bahanzi ba Omarchy kuva ku munsi wa mbere. Kubaka palettes zose, ku gihe cyabo, ubuntu, kuko bashakaga ko machine yabo isa n’ikintu.

Ngwino dutangije Omarchy AIR - Artists in Residence - gushyigikira beza muri bo. Icumbi ry’amezi atandatu ku bahanzi gukora mu bwisanzure kuri themes, plugins, n’ibindi byose batekereza Omarchy ikeneye ubutaha. Nta roadmap, nta tickets. Ikigamijwe ni ukureka ubuhanga bugenda bufashijwe na Omacom Foundation.

Buri residency izana 2,500 USD buri kwezi mu mezi atandatu yose, token account kugira ngo aba agenti ntibigere bahagarara, na permanent recognition nka AIR alumni amezi atandatu arangiye.

Hari imyanya itatu cyane iyo yuzuye, kandi ibiri muri yo imaze gufatwa kuri iyi season ya mbere.

HANCORE afite themes ebyiri zijyana na Omarchy - Solitude na Last Horizon - n’izindi makumyabiri kuri paji y’inyongera, nyinshi kurusha iz’undi wese yatanze: Batou, Oxo Carbon, Velvet Night, Rose of Dune, Shades of Jade, kandi birakomeza. Ni mechanical engineer wavuye kuba imwe mu mbaraga zisobanura uburanga muri uyu muryango, aba muri Omarchy Core, kandi afasha kuyobora ecosystem ya plugin.

OldJobobo ashinzwe eshatu mu themes zijyana na Omarchy - Lumon, Miasma, na Retro 82 - n’izindi icumi kuri paji y’inyongera, kuva kuri Batman kugeza kuri City-783 kugeza kuri Sakura Mochi. Akora muri palettes zose aho gukora colour schemes imwe, ibyo ni byo bituma themes ze zihura muri terminal, Neovim, btop, na shell aho kugaragara neza muri screenshot gusa.

Intebe ya nyuma ni ku butumire. Program itangira muri October.

Nkunda ibisabwa bitatu bya Vitruvius kuri architecture nziza: firmitas, utilitas, venustas. Igomba guhagarara, igomba kugira akamaro, kandi igomba kuba nziza. Si ebyiri muri eshatu. Tuzashora muri byose. Dushobora gukosora byose.
