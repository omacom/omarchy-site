Ubwiyongere bukabije bwa Omarchy busaba amakipe ateguye, kandi tugiye gutangirana no gushinga Ikipe Core ya Omarchy. Iri tsinda rizasangira inshingano zo kuyobora icyerekezo cya distro yacu, kwemeza ko dukomeza gutera imbere, no gufasha abandi bagira uruhare.

Mureke mbamenyekanishe Ikipe Core ya mbere:

Ryan Hughes yari mugenzi wanjye wa hafi kuri Omarchy kuva ku munsi wa mbere. Twarwanye hamwe emergency z’AUR za saa 9 z’ijoro mu minsi ya mbere, kandi twagambaniye kubaka package repository yacu bwite. Imisanzu ya Ryan ikwira buri gice cya distro: installation, packaging, hardware support, gaming, theming, CLI tooling, n’ubu Quattro. Yafashije kuyobora kwimukira kuri Quickshell, sisitemu ya plugin, na infrastructure nyinshi yahinduye Omarchy iva muri Linux setup yateguwe neza iba operating system nyayo. Iyo atari gukora kuri Omarchy, Ryan ni partner muri Oodle.

Tobi Lütke ni we muntu wenyine wakoze mu makipe yanjye core yombi! Nkumunyamuryango wa Ikipe Core ya Rails ya mbere yatangiye muri 2004, yayoboye si record ikomeye y’imisanzu kuri framework gusa, ahubwo yanditse na Liquid, Delayed Job, na Active Merchant. Uwo murimo wahindutse company nto yitwa Shopify, waba warayumvise? Ubu ni umutanga umusanzu mwinshi kuri Omarchy ufite projects nka try, Omasnap, Wi-Fi QR codes, na Quake-style agent console. Kandi ni co-driver wanjye mu modoka #11 Shopify Racing LMP2 racing muri IMSA.

Bjarne Øverli ni CTO wa Unite AS n’uwashinze Aether, visual theme builder twohereza muri Omarchy, na cliamp, music player yacu ishingiye kuri TUI. Yafashije guhindura sisitemu yacu ya theming iva mu kusanya kwa configuration files iba ikintu giteguye kurushaho, gifite semantic colors na generated themes muri Neovim, VS Code, btop, na stack isigaye. Ashinzwe na themes nka Ethereal, Hackerman, na Lupine. Bjarne afite iyo combination idasanzwe ya programmer rigor na visual taste ijyanye na Omarchy neza.

HANCORE ni mechanical engineer waje kuba imwe mu mbaraga zisobanura uburanga mu muryango wa Omarchy. Yakoze themes nyinshi cyane, harimo Last Horizon na Solitude, ijyana na default muri Omarchy. Ubu afasha kuyobora ecosystem ya plugin impande za Quattro. Omarchy yahoraga ishimangira ko mudasobwa igomba kuba nziza ndetse n’ingirakamaro, kandi HANCORE yasunitse iyo premise kure kurusha hafi uwo ari we wese.

Spencer Bull ni Distinguished Engineer muri Dell akora kuri Linux client enablement, AI, na platform software. Yari ikiraro cyacu mu kuri kuzuye akajagari ka hardware nshya, afasha Omarchy gukora neza kuri Panther Lake na machines nshya za XPS mbere yuko gutinda gusanzwe kwa Linux gushobora kwinjira. Kernel issues, drivers, displays, Wi-Fi, speakers, power management: Spencer akorera mu gice aho software ihura na atoms, kandi kugira ubwo buhanga muri Core Team ni ingirakamaro cyane.

Aba batanu bamaze gukora umurimo. Iki gikora ngombwa gusa. Hamwe nanjye, ni Core Team ya mbere itangaje. Ariko si yo team yonyine izabaho. Tegereza itangazo kuri Omarchy Rangers!

Hamwe tugiye kubaka Omarchy iba premier Linux distribution ku bantu bishimiye age of agents, mudasobwa yoroha, na computing nziza, igezweho, ifite ibitekerezo.

Kugeza ku iherezo n’aharenze!
