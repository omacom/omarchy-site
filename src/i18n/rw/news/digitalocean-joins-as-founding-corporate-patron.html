DigitalOcean irinjiye muri Omacom Foundation nka Founding Corporate Patron, itanga 1 million USD mu mwaka mu myaka itatu! Iyo ni commitment ya 3 million USD ku iterambere, ikomeza, n’ikwirakwiza rya Omarchy. Ni vote of confidence itangaje muri OS nziza, ishimishije & ikorana na agenti twubaka.

Nakoresheje DigitalOcean Droplets arenga decade. Ni uburyo bworoshye bwo gushyira Linux machine kuri internet no gukomeza kubaka ikintu. Tuzikoresha gukoresha contributors.rubyonrails.org, site yizihiza ibihumbi by’abantu bafashije Rails kuba icyo ari cyo. Iyi ni infrastructure nishingikirijeho imyaka, ngwino company iri inyuma yayo kutwinjiraho yumvikana neza by’umwihariko.

Kandi ni patron nziza ku agentic OS. DigitalOcean isanzwe itanga one-click Droplets za Hermes na OpenClaw. Ushobora kugira agenti iri murugo kuri Omarchy desktop yawe n’indi ikora amasaha yose kuri Droplet.

Ariko igice cy’ubu buterankunga cyantangaje cyane ni ukuntu byose byaje vuba. Negereye Paddy Srinivasan, CEO wa DigitalOcean, kuri X kuri Wednesday. Twagiranye call iryo joro. Noherereje proposal kuri Saturday. Ku Sunday, twari twarangije byose.

Uyo ni muvuduko w’ubufatanye bushoboka na X, aba agenti, n’icyifuzo kitagira ingano! Iyo abantu babona amahirwe amwe kandi bashaka gutuma ikintu kiba, nta mpamvu yo kumara amezi ugera kuri yes. Dushobora kugenda ku muvuduko wa possibilities ziri imbere yacu.

DigitalOcean yinjiye na Meta Superintelligence Labs nka Founding Corporate Patron, hamwe na individuals na companies zose zamaze gutera intambwe gutera inkunga iyi mission. Pledge yabo izana total backing yacu kuri hafi 18.5 million USD. Umwanya munini wo gushora mu bantu, imishinga, na infrastructure ituma Omarchy iba nziza kuri bose.

Nka guha akazi abantu bandi batangaje! Tumaze kuzana Krzysztof Wilczyński nka Head of Omarchy Kernel na outfoxxed, creator wa Quickshell, nka Head of Omarchy Shell. Guha abantu nka aba amahirwe yo gukora full time gutuma Omarchy yihuta, yizerwa, kandi ishoboye birushaho bishoboka gusa na patrons nka DigitalOcean. Inkunga yabo ihindura ambitions zacu actual jobs ku bantu bashobora kubigeraho.

Murakoze, Paddy na bose muri DigitalOcean. Reka dukoreshe iyi nkunga maze dutume ejo hazaza ha Linux nziza, ishimishije, ikorana na agenti riba!
