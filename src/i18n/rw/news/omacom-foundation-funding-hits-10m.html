Inkunga ya Omacom Foundation imaze kugera kuri 10 million USD hamwe na Founding Patrons babiri bashya binjiye muri mission: Drew Houston, cofounder na co-CEO wa Dropbox, na Peter Steinberger, creator wa OpenClaw.

Nk’uko Founding Patrons umunani ba mbere, Drew na Peter buri wese atanga 1 million USD. Ibyo biha fondasiyo patrons icumi na million USD icumi byuzuye yo gutera mission yacu ikomeye yo gutuma Linux kuri desktop iba mu buryo bunini cyane.

Biraryoshye cyane kugira Drew na Peter kuko njye bwite maze igihe ndi fan ukomeye wa bombi n’ibyo bagezeho. Maze igihe ndi enthusiastic customer wa Dropbox hafi imyaka makumyabiri, kandi ni central ku buryo nshobora kubaho na no backup, no cry nyamara ngahora mpindura mudasobwa.

Ibyo ni byo bituma Omarchy yoherezwa na Dropbox integration nziza kuva mu gasanduku. Genda gusa kuri Install > Service > Dropbox, maze ugire file manager integration na bespoke menu panel ya controls.

Kandi OpenClaw yatanze roadmap y’ingenzi y’uko Omarchy igomba gukemura growth yayo explosive. Ukemura ute PR backlogs zihinduka parabolic mu kanya? Bite kuri security impande za plugins? Meetups mu izina ry’umushinga zigomba gucungwa zite? Maze nishimiye gushingira ku nama na experience bya Peter kuri zimwe muri izi topics.

Kurenga ibyo, bombi bahagarariye neza ubwoko bw’technical ambition nshaka impande za Omarchy. Drew yahinduye personal itch yo kwimura dosiye hagati ya mudasobwa iba Dropbox. Peter yahinduye weekend project iba OpenClaw, kandi mu nzira yibukije bose appetite ihari ya mudasobwa zumvikana personal, programmable, n’umunezero.

Iyo ni spirit iri inyuma ya Omacom Foundation na yo. Turi hano gutera inkunga infrastructure, imishinga ya open-source, n’aba developers bakenewe kugira ngo Linux desktop iba murugo mwiza ku generation itaha y’abakoresha mudasobwa.

Murakaze neza, Drew na Peter. Founding Patrons icumi. Million USD icumi. Prophecy imaze kwegera indi 2 million USD!
