Ibihumbi magana abiri ipakurura rya ISO rya Omarchy Quattro mu minsi 18 gusa! Ku mpuzandengo, somebody yakuye Omarchy nshya buri masegonda 8 kuva Quattro isohotse. Ku peak, twohereje ISOs 1,165 mu isaha imwe. Imwe buri masegonda 3!

Ipakurura riva mu bihugu na territories 215. Ibyo ni ahantu henshi kurusha members za UN! Arenga kimwe cya kabiri cy’ipakurura kiva hanze y’ibihugu bitanu bya mbere. Kandi ku bipimo bya per-capita, Nordics ziyoboye: Iceland, Norway, na Denmark byose bikurura Omarchy arenga inshuro ebyiri igipimo cya Amerika. Vikings zegukane intsinzi! (Ariko Amerika iracyari 28 ku ijana by’ipakurura mu mibare rusange.)

Ikindi gishushanyo cyiza cyo kubona momentum: 1Password izi Linux distros abakoresha babo bakoresha, kandi mu cyumweru, Omarchy ubu ni distro yabo #2, imbere ya Fedora. Mu weekends? Turi #1! Abantu bakoresha Omarchy ku munezero. Iyo ntawe ubabwira icyo gukoresha, iki ni cyo bahitamo.

Ngwino tw extrapolated wildly: Ku muvuduko w’icyumweru gishize, dukurura ISO ya millionth ya Quattro kuri November 4. Kandi iyo ni conservative count kuri installs: ni completed-download equivalents ziva kuri servers zacu bwite, mbere yuko wongeraho abantu bakoresha ISO imwe kabiri.
