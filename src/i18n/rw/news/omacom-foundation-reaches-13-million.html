Omacom Foundation imaze kurenga 13 million USD! Distinguished Patrons bacu bane ba mbere basezeranyije 100,000 USD buri wese kuri mission:

Ryan R. Hughes ntiykeneye introduction hano. Ni member wa Omarchy Core, wingman wanjye kuri distro kuva ku munsi wa mbere, na partner muri Oodle. Ubu ashyigikiye fondasiyo n’amafaranga ye bwite na yo. Iyo ni conviction!

Ed Huang ni cofounder na CTO wa PingCAP, company iri inyuma ya TiDB, distributed SQL database. Yamaze decade agaragaza ko serious open-source infrastructure ishobora kuba serious business na yo.

Adrien Treccani yashinze Metaco, Swiss digital-asset custody platform Ripple yaguze. Undi crypto pioneer yinjiye muri mission yo gutuma Omarchy iba ahantu heza ha crypto.

Max Schoening ni Head of Product muri Notion, kandi mbere yaho yayoboraga design muri Heroku akaba VP of Design muri GitHub. Yatanze ishusho ku bikoresho aba developers bakunda kurusha abantu benshi bazamenya.

Hamwe na Founding Patrons icumi na babiri n’aba Distinguished Corporate Patrons babiri, abo ni backers cumi n’umunani na 13 million USD committed munsi y’ibyumweru bibiri.

Murakaze neza, mwose uko muri bane. Mission irakura!
