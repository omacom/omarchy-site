Iyi ni amahirwe manini ya Linux. Desktop iboneka mu buryo itabonekamo mu myaka icumi. Aba agenti batumye operating system iba malleable. Hardware ntiyigeze iba nziza. Kandi competition ntiyigeze ishimishwa no kubabaza abayikoresha bayo bwite. Ntitureka uyu mwanya ngo ucike!

Ngwino Omacom Foundation irihutisha intego zayo zo gukoresha. Byose byakusanyijwe uyu mwaka bizakoreshwa mu myaka itatu iri imbere. Si kubitswa muri endowment. Si gutonyanga mu myaka icumi. Bizakoreshwa!

Na 13 million USD zamaze committed, ibyo bisobanura yearly budgets zirenga 4 million USD za 2027, 2028, na 2029. N’izindi uko amafaranga yinjira ava kuri patrons z’ubwoko bwose. Founding, Distinguished, Corporate, na bose binjiye muri mission uyu munsi.

Ayo mafaranga ajya kuri imishinga ya open-source Omarchy yubatsweho. Kuri abahanzi bayituma iba nziza. Kuri aba developers bayituma yihuta, compatible, kandi secure. Ku marushanwa, grants, na infrastructure. Kuri byose bisaba fulfilling prophecy ya Linux kuri Desktop.

Amafaranga si ngombwa. Mission ni yo. Ariko bifasha cyane kugira byombi!
