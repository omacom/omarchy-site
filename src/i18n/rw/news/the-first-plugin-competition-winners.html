🥇 Umwanya wa mbere: Radio Atlas na Akshar Patel. Zungurutsa globe, gera ku mujyi, maze wumve icyo bumva. Itwara 2,500 USD.

🥈 Umwanya wa kabiri: Omagotchi na SLcode777. Ikiremwa gito kiba muri bar yawe kandi kikukeneye. Ntawe wari ukikeneye rwose kandi bose baragishaka. Iyo ni 1,000 USD.

🥉 Umwanya wa gatatu: AirPods na GM. Urwego rwa bateri no guhindura kwa click imwe kuri AirPods zawe, muri bar. Ubwoko bw’ikintu wibura gusa uje uva kuri Mac. 500 USD kuri icyo.

Ishimwe ryihariye kuri GitHub na Rob Zolkos, ishyira notifications zawe na pull requests muri bar. Nta mafaranga y’igihembo, ariko amajwi menshi!

Turabashimiye mwese uko muri bane, kandi murakoze kuri bose bohereje. Kubanyuramo byari bigoye koko, ari ryo problema nziza ishoboka.

Kandi turabikora ukundi! Amarushanwa menshi, amafaranga menshi y’ibihembo, impamvu nyinshi zo kubaka ikintu gishishikaje kuri Linux desktop. Mukomeze kureba hano.
