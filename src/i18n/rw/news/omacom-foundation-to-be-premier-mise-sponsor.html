Sponsorship ya gatatu irasohotse! Omacom Foundation iri kuba premier sponsor ya mise, bityo na jdx, wamaze imyaka yubaka tool ituma gucunga versions zitandukanye za Ruby, Node, Go kuba joy, kandi iha agent tooling zawe zose upgrade channel ishobora kujyana n’umuvuduko wo kohereza.

Niba warakoresheje Omarchy, warakoresheje mise, waba warabibonye cyangwa utarabibonye. Ni yo icunga language runtimes, kugira ngo mise use -g ruby iguhe Ruby nta murongo n’umwe wa PATH archaeology. Ariko igice nitayeho cyane ubu ni aba agenti.

Buri major coding-agent CLI muri Omarchy yoherezwa nka lazy-loading mise stub muri ~/.local/bin/. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - zose ziri aho, ntizikuryo icyo kugeza ubwo wanditse imwe bwa mbere koko. Hanyuma yiyishyiriraho maze urakora. Nta package yo gushakisha, nta version yo gukomaho, nta toolchain yo gutekerezaho. omarchy-mise-install <package> izingira indi CLI iyo ari yo yose kimwe, kandi omarchy update ikomeza buri imwe current.

Iyo ni fast track. Muri The Age of Agents, icyuho hagati yo kumva tool no kuyikoresha kigomba kuba amasegonda ane, kandi mise ibigeraho. Ntawe yagombaga gupakira ikintu kuri distro mbere.

Inyungu zigenda impande zombi, na zo: traffic kuri mise yazamutse 24 ku ijana kuva Omarchy yatangira! Abantu benshi bari kumenya iyi tool bwa mbere banyuze kuri twe, ibyo ni neza ubwoko bw’ikintu sponsorship igomba kwemera aho kubyifatira nk’ibisanzwe.

Na none, murakoze by’umwihariko kuri Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, na Peter Steinberger kubigira possible. Iyo Oligarchy itabaho, ntitwashobora gutera inkunga aba developers ba open-source gutya!
