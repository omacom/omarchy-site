Ivugurura, September 9, 2026: Iyi sponsorship yasimbuwe na outfoxxed yinjiye muri Omacom Foundation full time nka Head of Omarchy Shell akaba uri muri Omarchy Core.

Amafaranga menshi yinjiye, amafaranga menshi arasohoka! Sponsorship yacu ya kabiri nini iva muri Omacom Foundation igiye kuri outfoxxed ku murimo we mwiza kuri Quickshell. Imaze icyumweru gusa iri muri public release, ariko birasobanutse neza ko yari igice cyabuzemo, pivotal kugira ngo tugere koko kuri The Malleable Computer.

Proof iri muri plugins! Plugin zirenga igihumbi zaremwe mu cyumweru Quattro imaze hanze. Iyo ni testament itangaje rwose ku technical flexibility ya Quickshell, n’uko aba agenti bakunda kwandika QML code.

Iyi sponsorship ihemba outfoxxed ku musanzu we mwiza kuri Omarchy, kandi ifasha gushimangira isano n’imigambi yacu y’igihe kirekire kuri The Age of Agents (ntubyandike kuri Bluesky, bazabira ubwoba!). Iteganyijwe imyaka itatu kuva mu irembo.

Murakoze by’umwihariko kuri Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, na Peter Steinberger kubigira possible. Iyo Oligarchy itabaho, ntitwashobora gutera inkunga aba developers ba open-source gutya!
