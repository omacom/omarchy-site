Ni igihe cyo kurota binini. Omarchy Quattro yahaye abantu amahirwe yo kugerageza uko malleable computer y’ejo isa, kandi barayikunda (cyane!). Ubu yumvikana nka moral obligation gutuma iki ejo kiboneka kuri benshi no guhindura fundamentally uko abantu babana na mudasobwa zabo bwa mbere mu kigaragara nka forever.

Kugira ngo dukore ibyo, nshyizeho Omacom Foundation kugira ngo iyi mission ifite inkunga yuzuye, irambe, kandi yitegure kwihuta.

Iyi nonprofit foundation izafata trademarks, itere inkunga infrastructure, yamamaze umurimo, kandi ishyigikire imishinga n’aba developers ba open-source Omarchy yishingikirizaho.
