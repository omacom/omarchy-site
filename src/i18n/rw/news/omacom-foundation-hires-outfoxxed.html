Tugiye guha akazi outfoxxed, uwashinze Quickshell, nka Head of Omarchy Shell! Arinjiye muri Omacom Foundation full time kandi aba umunyamuryango wa Omarchy Core. Umuntu wubatse toolkit yatumye Quattro ibaho araje kudufasha kuyizamura hejuru cyane.

Ibi bisimbura three-year Quickshell sponsorship twatangaje muri August. Aho iyo sponsorship, outfoxxed azaba umukozi wa full-time wa Omacom Foundation, ayoboye umurimo wacu kuri Omarchy Shell. Tushyize uwashinze hagati y’icyo twubaka.

Shell ni ingenzi cyane kuri agentic OS twubaka. Ni bar, menus, notifications, widgets, n’utuntu duto dukorana dutuma computer yumvikana iyawe. Quickshell iduha ibikoresho byo gutunganya iyo experience muri QML. Ongeraho aba agenti barushije cyane kwandika QML, maze mu kanya guhindura desktop yawe biba ikintu ushobora gusaba, kugerageza, no gusangiza.

Gihamya kiri muri plugins! Ubwo twatangazaga sponsorship, umuryango wari wamaze kurema zirenga igihumbi mu cyumweru cya mbere cya Quattro. Ubu catalogue ifite hafi ibihumbi bitatu bya plugins. Imikino ushobora gukina uvuye muri bar, panels zikurikirana coding agents zawe, music players, ndetse na cat igenda muri bar yawe itegereje gukorwaho. Abantu bafata sisitemu yigeze yumvikana idahinduka maze bakayigira iyabo. Iyo ni mudasobwa yoroha iba nzima.

Kugira uwashinze Quickshell ari kumwe bisobanura ko dushobora gukora kuri stack yose hamwe. Gutuma shell yihuta kandi yizerwa. Koroshya kubaka plugin nziza. Kunoza foundations iyo uwanditse plugin ahuye na limitation. Guhindura ibyo twiga mu ma experiments ibihumbi experience nziza kuri bose bafungura Omarchy. No kwemeza ko ikintu cyose gitekanye kandi kirinzwe.

Kandi yinjiye muri Omarchy Core, outfoxxed azafasha kugena icyerekezo cya distro muri rusange. Shell ni ingenzi cyane kuri experience ku buryo itaba igitekerezo cya nyuma. Ni ho byinshi mu bwiza, umunezero, na possibilities za agentic OS bihinduka ikintu ushobora koko kubona no gukoresha.

outfoxxed ni full-time hire wa kabiri wa Omacom Foundation, akurikiye Krzysztof Wilczyński, Head wacu wa Omarchy Kernel. Na abantu bayoboye Kernel na Shell bari kumwe, dushora muri foundations za experience yose. Ushobora guhura na staff ya fondasiyo kuri site.

Tuzakomeza guha akazi ku butumire uko tubona abantu beza binyuze mu murimo wabo mu muryango. (Nyamuneka ntimwuzuze inbox yanjye na CVs!)

Iki ni cyo backing ya fondasiyo itwemerera gukora: guha aba developers ba open-source b’indashyikirwa igihe cyo kwinjiramo byose. Murakoze kuri bose batera inkunga uwo murimo, duhinduye icyifuzo cyacu kinini full-time jobs nyinshi.

Murakaze neza, outfoxxed! Reka dutume iyi computer iba yoroha kurushaho.
