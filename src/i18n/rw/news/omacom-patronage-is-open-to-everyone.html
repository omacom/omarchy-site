Kwakirwa kwa Omacom Foundation kwari nothing short ya amazing. Founding Patrons icumi na babiri, Distinguished Patrons bane, Distinguished Corporate Patrons babiri, na 13 million USD committed kuri mission munsi y’ibyumweru bibiri.

Ariko fondasiyo si iya Oligarchy gusa na Corporate Patrons. Ni iya bose. Uwo ari we wese ushobora gushyigikira Linux nziza, ishimishije & ikorana na agenti.

Ngwino kuva uyu munsi, patronage ifunguye kuri bose. Hari tiers enye: 16 USD, 256 USD, 2,048 USD, na 8,192 USD. Imbaraga za bits, bisanzwe! Buri patron ni igice cya mission imwe, kandi buri dollar igura ikintu kimwe: real shot yo fulfilling prophecy ya Linux kuri Desktop!

Kandi buri patron ahabwa digital rally credential yo kwerekana. Buri tier ifite badge, social card, na wallpapers zayo, zakozwe na Artist in Residence wacu OldJobobo. Class 016 ikunyura muri Forest Stage. Class 8K ikoresha Night Stage. Hitamo class yawe! N’izindi variations ziva ku bandi bahanzi ziraza vuba.

Amafaranga amaze gukora cyane. Fondasiyo ni exclusive sponsor ya Hyprland na premier sponsor ya Quickshell na mise, eshatu mu pillars Omarchy yubatsweho. Itera inkunga Artists in Residence batuma distro iba nziza. Ikomeza amatara kuri infrastructure yose Linux distribution ikeneye. Ikoresha plugin competitions, hamwe n’ishya ya 10,000 USD iri kuza vuba ifite winners nyinshi kurusha iya mbere. Kandi kuva uyu munsi, ifite umukozi wayo wa mbere wa full-time: Linux kernel developer uyoboye umurimo kuri Omarchy Kernel. Hari byinshi kuri byose biri mu nzira!

Kuko aya mafaranga ntiyakusanyijwe gutangiza Iron Bank. Tugamije gukoresha byose byakusanyijwe uyu mwaka mu myaka itatu iri imbere. Patrons nyinshi, spending nyinshi. Bose bashobora kuba igice cy’ibi.

Ba umuterankunga uyu munsi.
