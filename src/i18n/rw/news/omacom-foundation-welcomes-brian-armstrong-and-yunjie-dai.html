Brian Armstrong ni cofounder na CEO wa Coinbase, yavutse ikoresha Ruby on Rails, maze ikura iba umwanya wa mbere wa crypto na users barenga million ijana. Brian yari early kuvuga oya kuri political nonsense ku kazi ubwo y declared Coinbase company yibanda kuri mission muri 2020. Yakiriye heat nyinshi kuri icyo gihe, ariko kuva icyo gihe yaratsindishirijwe rwose (umugabo nyakuri wa Night’s Watch!).

Kandi kuri crypto, well, nahinduye ibitekerezo myaka yashize. Na Brian ari kumwe, tugiye gutuma Omarchy iba ahantu heza ha crypto. Kuko dushobora gukosora byose!

Yunjie Dai ni games entrepreneur wo mu Bushinwa ukora TapTap, imwe muri platforms nini z’igihugu za game discovery na distribution. Imwe ifata nta revenue share ku ba developers. Ariko nzamureka yivuge, kuko ibaruwa yoherereje yari nziza:

Myaka 21 yashize, muri 2005, nari maze kurangiza college ubwo narebaga video yawe yubaka blog na Ruby on Rails na TextMate kuri Mac OS X mu minota hafi 15. Naguze white MacBook vuba nyuma, ndeka Windows inyuma, nguza TextMate, maze ntangira kwiga Ruby. Byahinduye cyane ukuntu nishimiraga coding.

Ndi games entrepreneur wo mu Bushinwa, kandi umuryango wanjye nanjye ubu tuba muri Amerika. Twubaka imikino, kandi dukora TapTap… Bitandukanye na App Store cyangwa Google Play, ntitufata revenue share ku ba developers, nyamara dukora ku scale ifatika kandi mu nyungu. Iyo myumvire yumvikana hafi y’icyo nahoraga nishimira kuri 37signals: kuguma wigenga, kwitunga, ntugakure rent kuko ubishoboye, kandi wubake ikintu gifite akamaro abantu bahitamo kuguma.
