Omacom Foundation yongeye gukusanya ikindi gice cya million USD! Hagati y’isoko nshya rya tokens, pledge ya corporate y’imyaka itatu, n’ibihumbi bya dollars bivuye muri open patronage, iyo ni hafi 510,000 USD yiyongereye yiyemeje kuri Linux nziza, ishimishije & ikorana na agenti. Ibyo bizana total yacu ya pledges na donations kuri hafi 15.5 million USD!

Bwa mbere, twakiriye OpenRouter nka Distinguished Corporate Patron na 150,000 USD mu ma token. Iyo allotment imaze gukoreshwa, dushobora kongera arrangement byemejwe impande zombi. Izi tokens zizakoresha aba agenti bubaka, bagerageza, kandi bakomeza Omarchy. Uko dushobora gushyira aba agenti gukora gukosora bugs, gusuzuma contributions, no kunoza distro, niko dushobora gutuma mudasobwa ya bose iba nziza vuba. More fuel kuri mission!

Tugiye kandi kunyuza millions ya dollars mu ma token yatanzwe binyuze muri platform nziza ya OpenRouter. Ngwino dushobore kugabanya tokens zikwiye ku teams zikwiye ziva kuri models zikwiye.

Hanyuma hari Four Technologies, yinjiye nka Distinguished Corporate Patron na pledge ya 100,000 USD mu mwaka mu myaka itatu. Iyo ni 300,000 USD gushyigikira community projects Omarchy yishingikirizaho, infrastructure ikomeza kuyikoresha, n’umurimo wo kuzana Linux nziza ku bantu benshi. Kugira companies zi commit imyaka biduha confidence yo gukora kimwe.

Dore uko ikipe ya Four Technologies ibivuga:

Turi AI-forward fintech company, kandi companies nka Four Technologies zibona kubaka uko zubaka kuko open-source infrastructure abandi bubatse mbere. Gushyigikira Omacom Foundation ni uko dushyira amafaranga inyuma yabyo. Dutekereza ko stage itaha ya AI si indi app iri hejuru ya operating system, ni AI yubatswe imbere muri operating system, kandi iyo ni bet Omarchy ikora. Twashakaga kuba igice cy’umuryango ukora iyo bet, si kunguka benefits nyuma gusa.

Kandi kuva gufungurira patronage bose, twakiriye hafi 60,000 USD ziva kuri hafi 500 donors binyuze muri open patronage. Iyo ni incredible response ziva ku bantu bashaka gufasha ibi biba. Buri contribution yongeraho icyo dushobora gukora hamwe.

Tokens zo kubakisha. Amafaranga yo gushyigikira imishinga tushingiyeho. Abantu na companies biteguye gushyira ikintu inyuma yo kwizera kwabo muri Linux kuri desktop. Uko ni ko dukomeza!

Murakoze kuri OpenRouter, Four Technologies, na bose babaye patron. Niba ushaka kubajyaho, andika david@omarchy.org kuri founding na distinguished patronage, maze tanga ku open patronage.
