Ishiramwe Omacom rifise Abaterankunga B'icubahiro b'Amashirahamwe babiri ba mbere! 1Password na 37signals buri wese atanga 100,000 USD ku mwaka mu myaka itatu iri imbere, ivyo bizana inkunga y'ishiramwe kuri 12.6 million USD.

1Password yabaye muri Omarchy kuva ku musi wa mbere (kandi muri Omakub imbere yaho!). Kuko yabungabunze impapuro zanje z'ibanga imyaka myinshi imbere yuko guhindukira kuri Linux biba n'icizere mw'ijisho.

Nka Dropbox, ni kimwe mu bikoresho ngombwa vyo gushoboza uburyo bwa no backup, no cry. Iyo nteguwe imashini nshasha (ikaba hafi incuro zitatu mu cyumweru muri iyi misi!), ikintu ca mbere nkora ni Install > Service > 1Password. Hanyuma gutegura abafasha banje, kwinjira muri Basecamp na HEY, n'ibindi vyose ni vyoroshe gusa. Ndetse vyizewe, vyizewe rwose.

37signals yabaye aho Omarchy yarererwa kuva imbere yuko ifise izina. Tumaze guhindura ishingiro ryose rya tekiniki kuri Omarchy, kandi twamaze kwishura fagitire z'ubwoko bwose mu nzira ija kuri Quattro (n'imbere). Ubu inkunga iri ku mugaragaro, biciye mw'ishiramwe, ku mategeko amwe na bose.

Birakwiye kwibukwa, kandi, ko amafaranga y'amashirahamwe n'amafaranga y'abantu bose bagura ikintu kimwe: kwibukwa kuba muri umugambi. Nta guhanahana hano.

Omarchy izoguma ishingira ingingo zayo ku kintu gituma OS igonjoka nziza gusumba izindi kw'isi. Kandi ivyo bisobanuye cane cane n'"na DHH" iyo ushyizeho sisitemu. Imbere yuko iba sisitemu itangaje kuri bose, ubanza iba sisitemu itangaje kuri jewe, jenyene. Banza wambare mask yawe n'ibindi!
