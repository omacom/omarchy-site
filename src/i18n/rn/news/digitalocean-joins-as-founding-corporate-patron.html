DigitalOcean yinjiye muri Ishiramwe Omacom nk'Umuterankunga Mukuru w'Ishirahamwe, atanga 1 million USD ku mwaka mu myaka itatu! Iyo ni inyemera ya 3 million USD ku iterambere, iyungurungwa, n'ikwirakwizwa rya Omarchy. Mbega ijwi ridasanzwe ry'izera muri OS nziza, iryoshye kandi ikoresha abafasha twubaka.

Nakoresheje DigitalOcean Droplets imyaka irenga icumi. Ni uburyo bwiza bworoshye bwo gushira imashini ya Linux kuri internet no kubandanya kubaka ikintu. Tuzikoresha gukoresha contributors.rubyonrails.org, urubuga rwizihiza ibihumbi vy'abantu bafashije gutuma Rails iba ico iri. Izi ni ibikorwa remezo nishingikirijeho imyaka, ngaho kugira ishiramwe riri inyuma yazo ryinjire muri twe biraryoshe bidasanzwe.

Kandi ni bo baterankunga beza kuri OS ikoresha abafasha. DigitalOcean isanzwe itanga Droplets zikanda rimwe kuri Hermes na OpenClaw. Ushobora kugira umufasha muhira ku biro vyawe vya Omarchy n'uwundi akora amasaha yose kuri Droplet.

Ariko igice c'ubu buterankunga cyane cyane cyantwengeje ni ukwihuta kwavyo vyose. Negereye Paddy Srinivasan, CEO wa DigitalOcean, kuri X ku wa Gatatu. Twagiranye ikiganiro iryo joro nyene. Nohereje icifuzo ku wa Gatandatu. Ku cyumweru, twari twarangije vyose.

Uwo ni umuvuduko w'ubufatanyabikorwa bushoboka na X, abafasha, n'ishaka ritagira imbibe! Iyo abantu babona amahirwe amwe kandi bashaka gutuma ikintu kiba, nta mpamvu yo kumara amezi ugera kuri ego. Dushobora kugenda ku muvuduko w'ibishoboka biri imbere yacu.

DigitalOcean yinjiye hamwe na Meta Superintelligence Labs nk'Umuterankunga Mukuru w'Ishirahamwe, hamwe n'abantu bose n'amashirahamwe yamaze kwitanga gutera inkunga uyu mugambi. Inyemera yabo izana inkunga yacu yose kuri hafi 18.5 million USD. Icibanza kinini co gushiramwo abantu, imigambi, n'ibikorwa remezo bituma Omarchy iba nziza kuri bose.

Nko gukoresha abantu batangaje benshi! Tumaze kuzana Krzysztof Wilczyński nk'Mukuru wa Kernel ya Omarchy na outfoxxed, uwaremye Quickshell, nk'Mukuru wa Shell ya Omarchy. Guha abantu nka bo akanya ko gukora igihe cose gutuma Omarchy yihuta, yizigirwa, kandi ishoboye birashoboka gusa n'abaterankunga nka DigitalOcean. Inkunga yabo ihindura ishaka ryacu imirimo nyayo ku bantu bashobora kuyishitsa.

Murakoze, Paddy na bose muri DigitalOcean. Reka dushire iyi nkunga gukora maze dutume hazo nziza, riryoshye, rikoresha abafasha rya Linux riba!
