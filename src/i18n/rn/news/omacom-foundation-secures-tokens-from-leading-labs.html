Omarchy si Linux nziza, iryoshye kandi ikoresha abafasha gusa ku barenga bayikoresha. Yubatswe uko nyene! N'ubwiza, ikinezero, na tokens nyinshi cane. Nkako, Omarchy Quattro yubatswe hafi yose n'abafasha. Kuko barushije cane ibintu nka bash, QML, no gukosora ibibazo vya Linux.

Kugira ngo tubandanye kunoza Omarchy ubu tumaze gukurura abakoresha n'abafatanya kurema ibihumbi amajana, dukeneye kugumisha tokens ziza. Uko ni ko twihuta, tunooza, kandi dushitsa imisanzu yose itangaje yamaze gusohoka. Dusanzwe dufise umurongo wa PRs 1,600+ kuva mu misi mike gusa inyuma yo gutangura! Nta n'umwe ashobora kubirenga adafashijwe n'abafasha.

Ngaho, nshimishijwe no kumenyesha ko twaronse tokens nyinshi zivuye mu ma labs manini kuri uyu mugambi! Uwa mbere ni Meta Superintelligence Labs uza nk'Umuterankunga Mukuru wa Token afatiye 1.5m USD mu ma tokens! Moderi yabo nshasha ya Meta Muse Spark irerekana imibare myiza ku giciro ciza. Ngaho 1.5m USD ni tokens zitarangira! (Tuzogerageza kuzimara nubwo!)

Ariko twishimiye kandi kwakira abindi bibiri mu ma labs ari imbere, Anthropic na OpenAI, nk'Abaterankunga B'icubahiro ba Token bafatiye 150,000 USD mu mpano za token buri wese. Izi zizobera ingirakamaro cane zifatanyijwe n'uruhushya rw'umutekano rukenewe gukuraho inzitizi dukeneye ku bushakashatsi n'ikosora ry'umutekano.

Kandi ku moderi ziguruye, nshimishijwe kimwe no kumenyesha Fireworks nk'Umuterankunga w'icubahiro wa Token afatiye 150,000 USD mu ma tokens yemerewe. Namaze kuba umukoresha wa moderi za Kimi K mu buryo budasanzwe, cane cane iyo zikora mu buryo bwihuta. Tuzokoresha neza izo tokens zihuta!

Vyose hamwe, ayo ni ayandi 1.95M USD yemerewe Ishiramwe Omacom. Ivyo bizana ivyemerewe vyacu vyose kuri 14.95 million USD. Mbega igitigiri giteye ubwoba kwandika kuri distro abakosozi ba mbere batesheje agaciro nk'umurwi wa dotfiles (ivyo mu kuri ntivyari bifutitse rwose ico gihe, ariko hanyuma twabandanyije gukosora vyose!).

Hamwe na zose izo tokens, dushobora gukosora vyose. Kandi ico ni co tugiye gukora!
