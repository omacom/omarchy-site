Ipakurura ibihumbi ijana rya ISO ya Omarchy mu cyumweru giheze! Abantu ibihumbi ijana biyumviye ko iki ari co cyumweru bagerageza Linux (canke basubira kuri yo). Mbega umurwi w'abakuru!

Ku bijanye na bandwidth, twatanze hafi petabyte imwe muri iyo misi indwi nyene. Terabyte igihumbi za Linux mu cyumweru hagati ya ISOs n'ivugurura rya packages!

Ibi bishobora kuba vyarahenutse Omarchy mu kindi gihe. Vyatuma twiruka dushaka mirrors canke torrents (kandi twiyumvira gukenera indi mortgage ku nzu kwishura fagitire yo kwakira). Ariko ntivyabaye ngombwa.

Ico ni kuko Cloudflare iri imbere ya vyose: ISOs, packages za Omarchy, na mirror yacu ya Arch. Vyose biri kuri R2 no inyuma ya CDN yabo. Nta na kimwe cose cyacumuye. Kandi ico ni co gituma abantu ibihumbi ijana baronse ipakurura ryihuta, risanzwe, ritagira ingorane uhereye hose kw'isi mu cyumweru giheze.

Murakoze kuri bose muri Cloudflare, no kuri Matthew Prince mu buryo budasanzwe. Matthew ni umwe mu Baterankunga Bashinga, ivyo bisobanura ko ashingiye uyu mugambi n'ibikorwa remezo n'amafaranga yiwe bwite. Murakoze cane, mufasha!

ISOs ibihumbi ijana mu cyumweru. Wow.
