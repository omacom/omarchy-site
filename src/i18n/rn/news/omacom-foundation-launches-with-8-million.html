Ni igihe co kurora kure. Omarchy Quattro yahaye abantu akanya ko kubona uko mudasobwa igonjoka y'ahazo isa, kandi barayikunda (cane!). Ubu bumvikana nk'inshingano yo gutuma uno hazo uboneka kuri benshi no guhindura burundu uko abantu bafatanya na mudasobwa zabo ubwa mbere mu gihe gisa n'igeno.

Kugira ngo bibe uko nyene, ndashiraho Ishiramwe Omacom kugira ngo uyu mugambi uterwe inkunga rwose, urambe, kandi witeguye kwihuta.

Iri shiramwe ridaharanira inyungu rizobika ibimenyetso, rishigikire ibikorwa remezo, rimenyekanishe akazi, kandi rifashe imigambi ya open-source n'abakora porogaramu Omarchy yishingikirizako.
