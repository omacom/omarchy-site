Inkunga y'Ishirahamwe Omacom igeze ubu kuri 10 million USD hamwe n'Baterankunga Bashinga babiri bashasha binjiye mu mugambi: Drew Houston, uwashinze hamwe na co-CEO wa Dropbox, na Peter Steinberger, uwaremye OpenClaw.

Nk'Abaterankunga Bashinga umunani ba mbere, Drew na Peter buri wese atanga 1 million USD. Ibyo biha ishiramwe abaterankunga icumi n'amadolari miliyoni icumi meza guteza imbere umugambi wacu w'ubutwari wo gutuma Linux kuri desktop iba mu buryo bunini cane.

Biraryoshe kurushaho kugira Drew na Peter muri twe kuko nanjye nabanze nkaba umufana wabo ukomeye n'ibyo bagezeho. Namaze imyaka irenga icumi ndi umukiriya wa Dropbox ushimye, kandi ni ngombwa mu buryo nshobora kubaho na no backup, no cry ndetse mpindura mudasobwa buri gihe.

Ico ni co gituma Omarchy izana ihuza rya Dropbox ryiza uhereye mu gasanduku. Genda gusa kuri Install > Service > Dropbox, maze uronke ihuza rya file manager n'ibiro ryihariye rya menu ku bugenzuzi.

Kandi OpenClaw yatanze ahanini inzira y'uko Omarchy ikwiye guhangana n'ukwiyongera kwayo gutangaje. Ukemura ute umurongo wa PR uhita ujya hejuru? Umutekano ku migereka uwugira ute? Amahuriro mw'izina ry'umugambi yokorwa ate? Ndamaze kugira amahirwe yo gukoresha inama na uburambe bwa Peter kuri zimwe muri izo ngingo.

Urengeje ivyo, bombi basobanura nyene ubwoko bw'ishaka rya tekiniki nshaka hafi ya Omarchy. Drew yahinduye ikibazo ciwe bwite co kwimura idosiye hagati ya mudasobwa iba Dropbox. Peter yahinduye umugambi w'impera z'icyumweru iba OpenClaw, kandi muri iyo nzira yibukije bose ingene hari isoko rinini rya mudasobwa zumvikana zabo bwite, zikoreshwa, kandi zinezeza.

Uwo ni wo mwuka uri inyuma y'Ishiramwe Omacom. Turi hano gutera inkunga ibikorwa remezo, imigambi ya open-source, n'abakora porogaramu bakenewe gutuma biro ya Linux iba muhira heza ku gisekuru giza c'abakoresha mudasobwa.

Murakaza neza, Drew na Peter. Abaterankunga Bashinga icumi. Amadolari miliyoni icumi. Ubuhanuzi bwegereye 2 million USD ukundi!
