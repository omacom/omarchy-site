UPDATE, September 9, 2026: Iyi ntererano yasimbuwe na outfoxxed yinjiye mw'Ishiramwe Omacom igihe cose nk'Mukuru wa Shell ya Omarchy kandi ari muri Ishingiro rya Omarchy.

Amafaranga menshi yinjiye, amafaranga menshi asohotse! Intererano yacu ya kabiri ikomeye ivuye mw'Ishiramwe Omacom ija kuri outfoxxed ku kazi kiwe ciza kuri Quickshell. Imaze gusa icyumweru mu bisohoka rusangi, ariko birasobanutse neza ko yari igice cakabuze, ngombwa kugira ngo tugere koko kuri The Malleable Computer.

Ikimenyetso kiri mu migereka! Imigereka irenga igihumbi yaremewe mu cyumweru Quattro imaze isohotse. Uko ni ukwemeza gutangaje rwose k'ubworoherane bwa tekiniki bwa Quickshell, n'ingene abafasha bakunda kwandika code ya QML.

Iyi ntererano ihemba rero outfoxxed ku musanzu wiwe udasanzwe kuri Omarchy, kandi ifasha gushimangira ihuza n'imigambi yacu y'igihe kirekire kuri The Age of Agents (ntubishire kuri Bluesky, bazokwirabura!). Itegekanijwe imyaka itatu uhereye ubu.

Murakoze bidasanzwe kuri Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, na Peter Steinberger gutuma bishoboka. Iyo bitaba Oligarchy, ntituba dushobora gutera inkunga abakora open-source uku!
