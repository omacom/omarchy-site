Ishiramwe Omacom rirabandanya kwiyongera! Abaterankunga Bashinga babiri bashasha binjiye mu mugambi wacu bafatiye 1 million USD buri wese, bazana isandugu yacu kuri 12 million USD: Brian Armstrong na Yunjie Dai.

Brian Armstrong ni uwashinze hamwe na CEO wa Coinbase, yavukiye gukoresha Ruby on Rails, maze ikakura iba iya mbere muri crypto ifise abakoresha barenga umiliyoni ijana. Brian yari uwa mbere kuvuga oya ku bintu vya politiki ku kazi igihe yatangaza Coinbase nk'ishirahamwe rishira imbere umugambi mu 2020. Yaronse ubushyuhe bwinshi kubera iyo nzira ico gihe, ariko kuva ubwo yaratsindanishijwe rwose (umugabo w'ukuri wa Night's Watch!).

Kandi kuri crypto, nahinduye ivyiyumviro imyaka iheze. Hamwe na Brian muri twe, tugiye gutuma Omarchy ahantu heza ha crypto. Kuko dushobora gukosora vyose!

Yunjie Dai ni rwiyemezamirimo w'imikino wo mu Bushinwa akoresha TapTap, rumwe mu mbuga ngari z'igihugu zo kurondera no gukwirakwiza imikino. Imwe idafata umugabane w'amafaranga ku bakora. Ariko ndamureka yimenyekanishe, kuko ikete yohereje cari ciza:

Imyaka 21 iheze, mu 2005, nari nhejeje kurangiza kaminuza igihe nareba videwo yawe wubaka blog na Ruby on Rails na TextMate kuri Mac OS X mu minota hafi 15. Naguze MacBook yera vuba inyuma, ndeka Windows, nguze TextMate, maze ntangura kwiga Ruby. Byahinduye cane ingene nishimira kwandika code.

Ndi rwiyemezamirimo w'imikino wo mu Bushinwa, kandi umuryango wanje nanje ubu tuba muri U.S. Twubaka imikino, kandi dukoresha TapTap… Bitandukanye na App Store canke Google Play, ntitufata umugabane w'amafaranga ku bakora, ndetse dukora ku rugero rukomeye kandi twunguka. Iyo nzira yumvikana hafi y'ico nama nshimira kuri 37signals: gama wigenga, wironkere, ntusabe ubukode kuko ushobora, kandi wubake ikintu c'ingirakamaro abantu bahitamwo kugumako.

Imyaka 20 iheze, Apple yakora porogaramu zidasanzwe kw'isi. Uyu musi, iyo nzira yumvikana yazimiye ahanini. Nibaza ko benshi muri twe bategereje sisitemu ikora yumvikana inezeza ukundi - igezweho, ifise ivyiyumviro, kandi yakorewe koko abakora porogaramu. Kandi ubu, hamwe na AI ituma porogaramu zigonjoka cane, ukwigurura birakomeye kurusha kera: sisitemu ubwayo ikwiye kuba ikintu dushobora gutahura, guhindura, no kugira icacu. Omarchy yumvikana nka yo.
