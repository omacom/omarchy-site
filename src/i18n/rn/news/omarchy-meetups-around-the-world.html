Omarchy yakuze iba umuryango w'isi yose, ufise ~35,000 abanyamuryango kuri Discord, kandi ubu ni igihe twatangura guhura mu maso. Ngaho turatangura Amahuriro ya Omarchy hamwe na kalandari isangiwe y'amakoraniro kw'isi yose.

Izi ni ibirori bikoreshwa n'umuryango. Ntukeneye uruhushya gutegura rimwe, nta muntu afise igisagara, kandi umuntu uwo ari we wese ashobora kuritegura hose. Vuga kuri Omarchy, Linux, open source, gukora porogaramu, gutunganya, canke ikindi muco wa hacker wegereye gitera abantu ishasa yo gutuma mudasobwa zinezeza ukundi.

Biri ugururire bose, menyesha intererano zose, kandi ntuhindure izina rya Omarchy ubucuruzi bw'ibirori canke ivyamamaza. Urengeje ivyo, zachana nk'abantu bakuze (n'igitangaza c'abana), maze mwinezerwe cane.

Zana mudasobwa yawe ngendanwa. Erekana uko wayiteguye. Hanahanana imyambarire n'imigereka. Fasha umuntu mu ishirwaho ryiwe rya mbere. Intumbero ni uguhuza abantu bitaho mudasobwa zabo bwite, zikoreshwa, nziza mu cyumba kimwe.

Ushobora kurondera ihuriro riri imbere canke gutanga iryawe biciye kuri kalandari ya Omarchy y'isi yose kuri Luma. Reka tubigire ihuriro ry'isi yose!
