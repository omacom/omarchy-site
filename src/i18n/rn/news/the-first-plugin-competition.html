Isoko ry'Imigereka ya Omarchy risanzwe ribarizwamwo imigereka irenga 500 kandi rirakura vuba cane. Dufise ivyiyumviro umiliyoni vy'uko dushobora kunoza ubu buryo, harimwo isuzuma ry'umutekano rikoreshwa n'abafasha, ariko reka ubutungane ntibube umwansi w'ivyiza n'ikinezero ubu!

Ngaho mu kwizihiza uku gusohoka kunini k'ubuhangange, tuzokira ihiganwa rya mbere ry'imigereka dukoresheje ibihumbi bine bya USD naronse ndonza bidasiba ivyerekeye Omarchy kuri X ico cyumweru giheze.

Amategeko ni aya akurikira:

  1. Imigereka yose yatanzwe mu bubiko imbere yo ku wa Mbere August 24 saa 9am CEST irakwiriye (harimwo n'isanzwe iri ku rutonde).
  2. Abatsinze bazotoranywa na Ishingiro rya Omarchy nshasha, izotora kuronka podium.
  3. Amafaranga y'igihembo azoba 2,500 USD ku wa mbere, 1,000 USD ku wa kabiri, na 500 USD ku wa gatatu.
  4. Ukwiye kuba ushobora kwakira ubwishyu biciye kuri Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, canke EU IBAN kugira ngo wakire amafaranga y'igihembo.

Abatsinze bazomenyekanishwa ico cyumweru giza tumaze gusuzuma ivyatanzwe vyose, ariko bitarenze ku wa Gatanu, August 28.

Mwinezerwe kandi ivyiyumviro vyiza n'ibikorwa bibe ari vyo bitsinda!
