Ishiramwe Omacom ryarunze ikindi gice c'umamiliyoni muri USD! Hagati y'itangwa nshasha rya tokens, inyemera y'imyaka itatu y'ishirahamwe, n'ibihumbi vya USD bivuye mu buterankunga bwiguruye, ayo ni hafi 510,000 USD yongeweko yemerewe Linux nziza, iryoshye kandi ikoresha abafasha. Ibyo bizana igiteranyo cacu c'ivyemerewe n'impano kuri hafi 15.5 million USD!

Ubwa mbere, twakira OpenRouter nk'Umuterankunga w'icubahiro w'Ishirahamwe afatiye 150,000 USD mu ma tokens. Ico gice kimaze gukoreshwa, dushobora kongera amasezerano ku bwumvikane. Izo tokens zizakoresha abafasha bubaka, basuzuma, kandi bungurunga Omarchy. Uko dushobora gushira abafasha gukora gukosora amakosa, gusuzuma imisanzu, no kunoza distro, ni ko dushobora kwihutisha gutuma mudasobwa ya bose iba nziza. Lisansi nyinshi ku mugambi!

Tugiye kandi gucisha amamiliyoni ya USD mu ma tokens yatanzwe biciye ku rubuga rwiza rwa OpenRouter. Ngaho dushobora kugabura tokens zikwiriye ku makipe akwiriye avuye mu moderi zikwiriye.

Hanyuma hari Four Technologies, yinjiye nk'Umuterankunga w'icubahiro w'Ishirahamwe afatiye inyemera ya 100,000 USD ku mwaka mu myaka itatu. Ayo ni 300,000 USD yo gushigikira imigambi y'umuryango Omarchy yishingikirizako, ibikorwa remezo biyituma ikora, n'akazi ko kuzana Linux nziza ku bantu benshi. Kugira amashirahamwe yemera imyaka biduha izera yo gukora ivyo nyene.

Ngaha uko ishingiro rya Four Technologies ribivuga:

Turi ishiramwe rya fintech rishira AI imbere, kandi amashirahamwe nka Four Technologies ashorwa kubaka uko abubaka kubera ibikorwa remezo vya open-source abandi bubatse ubanza. Gushigikira Ishiramwe Omacom ni ko dushira amafaranga inyuma y'ivyo. Twibaza ko igice giza ca AI si porogaramu ikindi isongwe hejuru ya sisitemu, ni AI yubatswe nyene muri sisitemu, kandi iyo ni yo nzira Omarchy iriko irafata. Twashaka kuba muri umuryango ufata iyo nzira, si kungukira kuri yo hanyuma gusa.

Kandi kuva twugurura ubuterankunga kuri bose, twaronse hafi 60,000 USD avuye kuri hafi abatanga 500 biciye mu buterankunga bwiguruye. Iryo ni isubizo ridasanzwe rivuye ku bantu bashaka gufasha gutuma biba. Buri musanzu wongera ku vyo dushobora gukora hamwe.

Tokens zo kwubakisha. Amafaranga yo gushigikira imigambi twishingikirizako. Abantu n'amashirahamwe biteguye gushira ikintu inyuma yo kwizera kwabo muri Linux kuri desktop. Uko ni ko tubandanya!
