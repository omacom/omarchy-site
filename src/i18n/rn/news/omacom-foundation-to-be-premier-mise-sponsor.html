Intererano ya gatatu isohotse! Ishiramwe Omacom rihindutse umuterankunga wa mbere wa mise, kandi bityo wa jdx, yamaze imyaka yubaka igikoresho gituma gucunga verisiyo zitandukanye za Ruby, Node, Go kinezeza gucunga, kandi giha ibikoresho vyawe vyose vy'abafasha umurongo wo kuzamurwa ushobora kugendana n'umuvuduko wo gutanga.

Niwaba warakoresheje Omarchy, warakoresheje mise, waba wabibonye canke oya. Ni co cunganira runtimes z'indimi, ngaho mise use -g ruby iraguha Ruby ata murongo n'umwe wo gucukura PATH. Ariko igice nitaho cane ubu ni abafasha.

Buri CLI ikomeye y'umufasha wandika code muri Omarchy itangwa nka stub ya mise itunganya buhoro muri ~/.local/bin/. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - vyose biri aho, bitakuriha na kimwe gushika ubwa mbere uvyanditse koko. Hanyuma yishiraho ubwayo maze urakora. Nta package yo kurondera, nta verisiyo yo gushimangira, nta toolchain yo gutahura. omarchy-mise-install <package> izingira indi CLI iyo ari yo yose uko nyene, maze omarchy update ibika iya nyuma iriho.

Iyo ni inzira yihuta. Muri The Age of Agents, ikinyuranyo hagati yo kwumva igikoresho no kugikoresha gikwiye kuba hafi amasegonda ane, kandi mise ibishitsa. Nta muntu yagize ngombwa gupakira ikintu kuri distro ubanza.

Ishaka riraja impande zombi, kandi: traffic ija kuri mise yazamutse 24% kuva Omarchy yatangura! Abantu benshi barahura n'iki gikoresho ubwa mbere biciye kuri twe, ico ni nyene ubwoko bw'ikintu intererano ikwiye kwemera aho kugifata nk'ibisanzwe.

Ukundi, murakoze bidasanzwe kuri Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, na Peter Steinberger gutuma bishoboka. Iyo bitaba Oligarchy, ntituba dushobora gutera inkunga abakora open-source uku!
