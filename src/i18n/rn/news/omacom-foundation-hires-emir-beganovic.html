Turakoresha Emir Beganović nk'Mukuru w'Ibikorwa Remezo vya Omarchy! Atangura November 1 nk'umukozi wa gatatu w'Ishiramwe Omacom w'igihe cose, yinjiye hamwe na Krzysztof Wilczyński kuri Kernel na outfoxxed kuri Shell.

Ngaha uko Emir asobanura urugendo rwiwe ija kuri Omarchy:

Urukundo rwanje rwa Linux rujana inyuma imyaka irenga makumyabiri. Nahindutse umukora wa Ubuntu nkiri umwangavu kandi nari ngize uruhara runini mu muryango. Umwuga wanje wancishije mu bihugu bitatu, nkora ku kwizigirwa n'umuvuduko muri startups na scaleups kandi ndongora amakipe. Namaze imyaka irenga itandatu muri departement ya SRE ya Booking.com, ndongora akazi kuri build systems na CI infrastructure. Vuba aha, nubatswe Isorun, aho nari nkora kuri microVMs, snapshots, isolation, scheduling, na compute ibora. Ubu nzana ubwo burambe ku rukundo rwanje rwa mbere.

Icyafashe cane ivyiyumviro vyanje kuri Emir ni TryOmarchy. Yafashe biro ya Omarchy, ayishira muri browser (si ukongera kuyikora, ahubwo ni nyayo), maze itangura gukora mu masegonda ku bikorwa remezo bisanzwe vya CPU kuri Cloudflare. Twatanguye kuvugana kuri ico kigeragezo giteye ubwoba, maze ikiganiro cagutse vuba kuri laptops, architectures nshasha, OEMs, n'ibikorwa remezo tuzokenera niba Omarchy igiye gukora hose tuyishaka. Twatahuye dusangiye icerekezo kimwe.

Ico ni co Emir aza kurongora.

Ibikorwa remezo vya Omarchy bisobanura vyinshi kurusha gukoresha seriveri. Ni vyo dukoresha kwubaka, gusuzuma, no gutanga Omarchy, no kugumisha sisitemu ikora neza uko twaguka ku bikoresho vyinshi na architectures nyinshi. Ni ugutuma ibisohoka vyizigirwa, ugutuma amakosa asubirwamo, no guha abandi bose ku mugambi ishingiro rikomeye kugira ngo bashobore kwihuta.

Kandi hari vyinshi vyo gutunganya! Omarchy ikwiye gukora neza ku mashini iri ku meza yawe, ariko nta mpamvu yokwira aho. TryOmarchy yaduhaye akayira k'ibiba iyo gutangura mudasobwa ya Linux biba vyoroshe nko gufungura urupapuro rwa web, bishira uburyohe bwa Linux ukoze rimwe gusa. Tugiye kubona aho bija.

Murakoze kuri bose batera inkunga iki gikorwa, dushobora guha abantu nka Emir igihe co kwinjira vyose muri Omarchy. Abaterankunga bacu bituma bishoboka guhindura ibigeragezo vy'ishaka imirimo irambye, hamwe n'abantu b'igihe cose bitanze gutuma sisitemu yose iba nziza. Tubashimira ubuterankunga bwabo.

Murakaza neza, Emir! Reka dutume Omarchy ikora hose.
