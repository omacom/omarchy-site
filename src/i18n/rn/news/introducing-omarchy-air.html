Omarchy ishimangira ko mudasobwa ikwiye kuba nziza nk'uko itanga umusaruro. Iryo hame ryashizwe n'abakora imyambarire n'abandi bahanzi ba Omarchy kuva ku musi wa mbere. Bubaka palettes zuzuye, ku gihe cabo, ubuntu, kuko bashaka ko imashini yabo isa n'ikintu.

Ngaho turatangura Omarchy AIR - Abahanzi b'Icumbi - gushigikira beza muribo. Icumbi ry'amezi atandatu ku bahanzi gukora bidegemvya ku myambarire, imigereka, n'ibindi vyose biyumvira ko Omarchy ikeneye gikurikira. Nta roadmap, nta tickets. Intumbero ni ukureka ubuhangange bugende n'inkunga y'Ishirahamwe Omacom.

Buri cumbi kizana 2,500 USD ku kwezi mu mezi atandatu yose, konti ya token kugira ngo abafasha ntibigere bakeneye guhagarara, no kwibukwa ubudaheruka nk'uwarangije AIR amezi atandatu aheze.

Hari ibibanza bitatu birengeje ico gihe cose, kandi bibiri murivyo ubu bifashwe ku gihe cambere.

HANCORE afise imyambarire ibiri ijana na Omarchy - Solitude na Last Horizon - n'izindi makumyabiri ku rupapuro rw'inyongera, vyinshi kurusha uwundi yose yatanze: Batou, Oxo Carbon, Velvet Night, Rose of Dune, Shades of Jade, kandi birabandanya. Ni injeniyeri mu mashini yahindutse kimwe mu bintu bikomeye vy'ubwiza muri uyu muryango, ari muri Ishingiro rya Omarchy, kandi afasha kurongoza ibikorwa vy'imigereka.

OldJobobo afise inshingano ku myambarire itatu ijana na Omarchy - Lumon, Miasma, na Retro 82 - wongeyeko izindi icumi ku rupapuro rw'inyongera, uhereye kuri Batman gushika kuri City-783 gushika kuri Sakura Mochi. Akora mu palettes zuzuye aho kuba imyambarire y'ibara rimwe, ni co gituma imyambarire yiwe ihura kuri terminal, Neovim, btop, na shell aho kugaragara neza gusa mw'ifoto.

Icibanza ca nyuma ni ku butumire. Porogaramu itangura muri October.
