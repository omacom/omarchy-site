Ipakurura ibihumbi amajana abiri rya ISO rya Omarchy Quattro mu misi 18 gusa! Ku mpuzandengo, umuntu yarapakuruye Omarchy nshasha buri masegonda 8 kuva Quattro yasohoka. Hejuru cane, twatanze ISOs 1,165 mu isaha imwe. Imwe buri masegonda 3!

Ipakurura riva mu bihugu 215 n'uturere. Iryo ni ahantu henshi kurusha abanyamuryango ba UN! Igice kirenga c'ipakurura kiva hanze y'ibihugu bitanu vya mbere. Kandi ku muntu, Nordics ni bo bari imbere: Iceland, Noroveje, na Danimarike bose bapakurura Omarchy incuro zirenga zibiri igipimo ca Amerika. Ba Vikings batsinze! (Ariko Amerika igifise 28% y'ipakurura mu mibare yuzuye.)

Ikindi buryo bwiza bwo kubona intambwe: 1Password izi isaranganya rya Linux abakoresha babo bakoresha, kandi muri ico cyumweru, Omarchy ubu ni isaranganya #2 cabo, imbere ya Fedora. Ku mpera z'icyumweru? Turi #1! Abantu bakoresha Omarchy ku kinezero. Iyo nta muntu ubabwira ico gukoresha, iki ni co bahitamwo.

Ngaho reka twongere cane: Ku muvuduko w'ubu w'icyumweru giheze, dupakurura ISO ya miliyoni ya Quattro kuri November 4. Kandi iyo ni igitigiri c'isanzwe ku ishirwaho: ni ibipakurura byarangiye bingana n'ibiva kuri seriveri zacu, imbere yo kongerako abantu bakoresha ISO imwe kabiri.
