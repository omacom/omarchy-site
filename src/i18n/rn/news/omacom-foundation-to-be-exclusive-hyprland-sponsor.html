Ni ubuhe buryo bwiza bwo gutangura gukoresha bumwe mu butunzi tumaze kurundanyiriza Ishiramwe Omacom kurusha ku mwana wa Linux uhambaye cane muri Polonye: Vaxry!

Nk'uwaremye Hyprland, afise inshingano kuri kimwe mu buhangange butatu bukuru muri Omarchy (Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), kandi nshimishijwe no kumenyesha intererano yihariye imureka kwibanda rwose kuyiteza imbere adakeneye kwiyumvira iterambere ryo kudandaza canke kurondera inkunga.

Twiyandikishije igihe kirekire na Vaxry gushigikira hazo rirerire rya Omarchy. Amasezerano yacu yihariye y'inkunga ategekanijwe imyaka itatu, hamwe n'uburyo bw'indi ibiri.

Amasezerano areka kandi porogaramu isanzwe ya Hyprland ya Hyprperks isohorwa ubuntu, kuri bose, nk'uko abiyandikisha bishyura bazohagarara. Ariko Hyprland izobandanya kwakira impano z'abantu kugira ngo abantu bagure iterambere ry'umugambi no gushigikira Vaxry n'ishingiro.

Murakoze cane kuri 37signals (muraho jewe! 😂), Butterfly, na Framework gufasha Hyprland kugera hano. Nishimiye ko Ishiramwe Omacom rishobora gufata kuva October 10, no guha umwe mu bakora beza muri iki gihe nshasha ca Linux akanya ko kwinjira vyose.

(* Aya masezerano ntarimwo ubufasha butomoye bwo kurondera Vaxry umukunzi, ariko nzoboneka jenyene nk'icemeza ku bibazo vyose kuri iyo ngingo.)
