Turakoresha outfoxxed, uwaremye Quickshell, nk'Mukuru wa Shell ya Omarchy! Yinjiye mw'Ishiramwe Omacom igihe cose kandi ahindutse muri Ishingiro rya Omarchy. Umuntu yubatse ibikoresho vyatumye Quattro ishoboka aza gufasha kuyizana hose ku kwezi.

Ibi bisimbura intererano ya Quickshell y'imyaka itatu twatangaje muri August. Aho iyo ntererano, outfoxxed azoba umukozi w'Ishiramwe Omacom w'igihe cose, arongora akazi kacu kuri Shell ya Omarchy. Turashira uwayiremye nyene hagati y'ivyo twubaka.

Shell ni ngombwa kuri OS ikoresha abafasha twubaka. Ni umurongo, menu, amamenyesha, widgets, n'ukwumvikana guto guto gutuma mudasobwa yumvikana iyawe. Quickshell iduha ibice vyo gutunganya ubwo burambe muri QML. Fatanisha ivyo n'abafasha barushije cane kwandika QML, maze guhindura biro vyawe bihinduka ikintu ushobora gusaba, kugerageza, no gusangiza.

Ikimenyetso kiri mu migereka! Igihe twatangaza intererano, umuryango wari umaze kurema irenga igihumbi mu cyumweru ca mbere ca Quattro. Ubu urutonde rwegereye imigereka ibihumbi bitatu. Imikino ushobora gukina nyene uhereye ku murongo, ibiro bikurikirana abafasha bawe bandika code, abacuranga umuziki, ndetse n'injangwe igenda ku murongo wawe itegereje gukorwako. Abantu bafata sisitemu yahora yumvikana ihamye maze bakayigira iyabo. Iyo ni mudasobwa igonjoka iza mu buzima.

Kugira uwaremye Quickshell muri twe bisobanura ko dushobora gukorera kuri stack yose hamwe. Gutuma shell yihuta kandi yizigirwa. Gutuma vyoroshe kubaka umugereka mwiza. Kunoza ishingiro iyo umwanditsi w'umugereka ahuye n'imbibe. Guhindura ivyo twiga mu bigeragezo ibihumbi mo burambe bwiza ku wese winjiza Omarchy. Kandi kwemeza ko vyose vyizewe kandi vyumvikana umutekano.

Kandi yinjiye mu Ishingiro rya Omarchy, outfoxxed azofasha gutunganya inzira ya distro yose. Shell iri hagati cane y'uburambe kugira ngo ibe ivyiyumviro vyanyuma. Ni ho vyinshi vy'ubwiza, ikinezero, n'ibishoboka vya OS ikoresha abafasha bihinduka ikintu ushobora kubona koko no gukoresha.

outfoxxed ni umukozi wa kabiri w'Ishiramwe Omacom w'igihe cose, akurikiye Krzysztof Wilczyński, Mukuru wacu wa Kernel ya Omarchy. Hamwe n'abantu barongora Kernel na Shell bose muri twe, turashira mu nkingi z'uburambe bwose. Ushobora kuhura n'abakozi b'ishiramwe ku rubuga.

Tuzobandanya gukoresha ku butumire uko turonka abantu bakwiriye biciye mu kazi kabo mu muryango. (Nyabuna ntimwuzuze inbox yanje na CVs!)

Iki ni co inkunga y'ishiramwe ituma dukora: guha abakora porogaramu badasanzwe ba open-source igihe co kwinjira vyose. Murakoze kuri bose batera inkunga ako kazi, turahindura vyinshi mu ishaka ryacu imirimo y'igihe cose.

Murakaza neza, outfoxxed! Reka dutume iyi mudasobwa igonjoka kurushaho.
