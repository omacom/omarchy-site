Iyakirwa ry'Ishiramwe Omacom ntiryari rito ritangaje. Abaterankunga Bashinga icumi na babiri, Abaterankunga B'icubahiro bane, Abaterankunga B'icubahiro b'Amashirahamwe babiri, na 13 million USD yemerewe umugambi mu misi irenga ibiri.

Ariko ishiramwe si rya Oligarchy n'Abaterankunga b'Amashirahamwe gusa. Ni rya bose. Umuntu uwo ari we wese ashobora gushigikira Linux nziza, iryoshye kandi ikoresha abafasha.

Ngaho kuva uyu musi, ubuterankunga bwigururiwe bose. Hari imice ine: 16 USD, 256 USD, 2,048 USD, na 8,192 USD. Ubushobozi bw'ibice, bisanzwe! Buri muterankunga ari muri umugambi umwe, kandi buri dolari rigura ikintu kimwe: amahirwe nyayo yo gushitsa ubuhanuzi bwa Linux kuri Desktop!

Kandi buri muterankunga ahabwa icemezo c'ukwitabira kugira ngo acerekane. Buri rugero rufise ikimenyetso carwo, ikarata y'imbuga, n'amafoto y'inyuma, vyashushanyijwe n'Umuhanzi wacu w'Icumbi OldJobobo. Ubwoko 016 buraguca muri Forest Stage. Ubwoko 8K bukora Night Stage. Hitamo ubwoko bwawe! Kandi ibindi bihinduka bivuye ku bandi bahanzi biraza vuba.
