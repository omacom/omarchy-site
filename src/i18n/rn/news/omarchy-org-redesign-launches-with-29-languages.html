Urubuga rw'isaranganya rya Linux nziza, riryoshye rukwiye kuba rwiza kandi rwinezeza. Ngaho twahaye Omarchy.org ihinduka ryuzuye ry'igishushanyo, kandi turayitangura mu ndimi 29 (kuko iyo umaze kurundanya ~2m USD mu ma tokens, kuki oya?). Ubukorikori buto bwa retro, vyinshi cane vyo gutunganya, n'ikaze kinini cane ku isi.

Uru ni urubuga rushaka ko ukina na mudasobwa yawe! Pixels, amabara, n'ibinyeganyega bitwara umwuka wa Omarchy nyene muri browser. Ushobora kwumva aho hantu imbere yuko upakurura ISO n'imwe. Ni na ko guhuza kwanje bwite n'iyo myaka yambere mu isi ya online imbere ya internet ya ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, n'ubwiza bwiza bwa 90s. Ibara ryinshi, polish nke. Pixels nyinshi, gradients nke zoroshye.

Ubu hari icibanza co kwerekana editeri, terminals, abafasha, imikino, Windows VM, n'ibikoresho bituma Omarchy ingirakamaro buri musi. Raba imyambarire y'umuryango, rondera umugereka, raba ahakorerwa nyayo, canke uronke ihuriro rikwegere. Hura n'abayubaka n'abaterankunga bayishigikira. Amaherezo dufise urugi rw'imbere rufise icibanza gihagije kuri vyose bibera imbere.

Ubu buhinduzi bwakuwe mu gikorwa ciza cane mu murwi wa Omarchy Design. Barış Girişmen yaremye igishushanyo cyahindutse ishingiro ry'urubuga nshasha, yubatse shader ya pixel-grid, kandi arongora igice kinini c'ishirwa mu ngiro no gutunganya. Christoffer Hallas yazanye prototypes, akazi k'ibinyeganyega, impinduka z'imyambarire, n'umurongo uhoraho w'iterambere. Daniel Schmier yatanze ibishushanyo n'ishirwa mu ngiro ku kirango, amakuru, ishirwaho, ishingiro, ishiramwe, n'ibice vy'ubuhamya.
