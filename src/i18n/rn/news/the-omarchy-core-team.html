Ukwiyongera gutangaje kwa Omarchy gusaba amakipe atunganye mu gusubiza, kandi tugiye gutangura dushiraho Ishingiro Rya Omarchy. Uyu murwi uzosangira inshingano yo kurungika inzira ya distro yacu, kwemeza ko tubandanya twerekeza imbere, no gufasha abandi batanga imisanzu.

Nimunkundire mbamenyekanishe Ishingiro rya mbere:

Ryan Hughes yabaye umufasha wanje kuri Omarchy kuva ku musi wa mbere. Twarwanye ibibazo vya AUR vya 3am hamwe mu misi ya mbere, kandi twagambanye kwubaka ububiko bwacu bwa packages. Imisanzu ya Ryan ikwira ibice vyose vya distro: ishirwaho, gupakira, inkunga y'ibikoresho, imikino, imyambarire, ibikoresho vya CLI, n'ubu Quattro. Yafashije kurungika igenda kuri Quickshell, sisitemu y'imigereka, na vyinshi mu bikorwa remezo vyahinduye Omarchy iva mu isaranganya rya Linux ryateranyijwe neza iba sisitemu nyayo. Iyo adakora kuri Omarchy, Ryan ni partner kuri Oodle.

Tobi Lütke ni we wenyene yakoze amashingiro yanje yombi akuru! Nk'umunyamuryango wa Rails Core Team ya mbere yatanguwe mu 2004, yarungitse si urutonde rukomeye rw'imisanzu kuri framework, ariko yanditse kandi Liquid, Delayed Job, na Active Merchant. Ako kazi kahindutse ishiramwe rito bita Shopify, warab yumvise? Ubu ni umutanga w'umusaruro kuri Omarchy afise imigambi nka try, Omasnap, QR codes za Wi-Fi, na console y'umufasha yo mu buryo bwa Quake. Biba kandi ko ari umufasha wanje wo gutwara muri imodoka #11 ya Shopify Racing LMP2 irushanwa muri IMSA.

Bjarne Øverli ni CTO wa Unite AS kandi uwaremye Aether, uwubaka imyambarire abona twugurana muri Omarchy, na cliamp, umucuranga wacu w'umuziki wa TUI. Yafashije guhindura sisitemu yacu y'imyambarire iva mu murwi wa dosiye z'itunganywa iba ikintu gitunganye cane, gifise amabara afise insiguro n'imyambarire yakozwe kuri Neovim, VS Code, btop, n'ibisigaye vya stack. Afise kandi inshingano ku myambarire nka Ethereal, Hackerman, na Lupine. Bjarne afise iyo mvange idasanzwe y'ubukomerezi bw'umukora porogaramu n'uburyohe bw'amaso bihura neza na Omarchy.

HANCORE ni injeniyeri mu mashini yahindutse kimwe mu bintu bikomeye vy'ubwiza mu muryango wa Omarchy. Yakoze imyambarire myinshi itumvikana, harimwo Last Horizon na Solitude, ijana nyene muri Omarchy. Ubu afasha kurongoza ibikorwa vy'imigereka hafi ya Quattro. Omarchy yahora ishimangira ko mudasobwa ikwiye kuba nziza ndetse ingirakamaro, kandi HANCORE yashizeho iryo hame kurusha hafi uwundi wese.

Spencer Bull ni Injeniyeri W'icubahiro kuri Dell akora kuri Linux client enablement, AI, na porogaramu za platform. Yabaye ikiraro cacu mu kuri gukomeye kw'ibikoresho bishasha, afasha Omarchy gukora neza kuri Panther Lake na mashini nshasha za XPS imbere yuko gutinda gusanzwe kwa Linux gushobora gushinga. Ibibazo vya kernel, drivers, ekara, Wi-Fi, speakers, itunganywa ry'inguvu: Spencer akorera mu rwego aho porogaramu ihura na atoms, kandi kugira ubwo buhangange muri Ishingiro ni ingirakamaro idasanzwe.

Aba batanu bamaze bakora akazi. Ibi birabigira vyemewe gusa. Hamwe nanje, ni Ishingiro rya mbere ritangaje. Ariko kandi ntirizoba ishingiro ryonyene. Tegereza itangazo kuri Ba Rangers ba Omarchy!

Hamwe tugiye kwubaka Omarchy iba isaranganya rya Linux rya mbere ku bantu bashimishijwe n'igihe c'abafasha, mudasobwa igonjoka, na mudasobwa nziza, igezweho, ifise ivyiyumviro.

Gushika ku iherezo no kurenga!
