Apple ikora ibikoresho vyiza. Buri gihe (uretse rimwe rya clavier butterfly). Kandi benshi mu bantu baza kuri Omarchy bava kuri Mac. Benshi muribo bipfuza kugumana imashini no kubura sisitemu. Ngaho tugiye kurangiza ivyo Asahi yatanguye: Omarchy ikora neza kuri Apple Silicon, hamwe n'ibibazo bike vyo gushiraho.

Ako kazi kasanzwe gafise ishingiro inyuma yaryo. Uyu musi turabigira vyemewe: Omarchy M, ishingiro ryacu rya Macs zifise Apple Silicon (hamwe n'abanyamuryango bake bagumana Macs za kera).

Nimunkundire mbamenyekanishe abanyamuryango.

Uwushiraho. Marcelo Alcantara yubaka uwushiraho kavukire wa Mac. Upakurura porogaramu kuri macOS, ikura ibisohoka vya Omarchy vyashizweko umukono, icamo partitions, maze ikakwinjiza muri Omarchy iruhande rwa macOS yawe isanzwe. Nta USB, nta majambo yo kuvuga muri terminal. Yakoze kandi build yatwenyuje monitors zo hanze biciye kuri USB-C.

Omarchy Mac. Naeem Malik yabungabunze Omarchy Mac umwaka, azana Omarchy kuri mashini za M1 na M2 kera imbere yuko ibi biba vyemewe, kandi yubaka umuryango wa Discord w'abantu barenga 800 hafi yayo. Scott Jones ni ikindi gice c'uwo mugambi ku ruhande rwa Linux rw'ishirwaho. Vuba aha yakoresheje ekara ya M3 Air kuri display controller ya Apple, ivyo vyahinduye iyo mashini iva idakoreshwa ija ikoreshwa mu joro rimwe.

Gerageza Omarchy. Eduardo Martinez yubatse Gerageza Omarchy: biro nyayo ya Omarchy ikora nka porogaramu kavukire, yihutishwa n'ibikoresho kuri Mac yawe, hamwe na kamera yawe, amajwi, clipboard, n'idosiye isangiwe bihujwe. Nta gice co gutema. Pakurura gusa maze ubone ico bavuga.

Packages. Shun Li yemeza ko vyose Omarchy ikeneye biboneka nka package ya aarch64, hamwe n'amafoto ya ARM64 rusangi ya disiki ku mashini ibonetse n'ibikoresho vy'abashitsi bibatuma banezeza gukoresha.

Drivers na kernel. Ryan Murray azana Neo kandi yubaka ishingiro ishingiro risigaye rikorerako. Arateza imbere emulator ya SPTM ituma bishoboka gukurikirana macOS kuri chips za M4 nshasha. Izo nkurikirana ni ngombwa gutuma ekara, GPUs, amajwi, wireless, na Bluetooth bikora. Dj yandika drivers za GPU, maze akoresha Touch ID avugana nyene na Secure Enclave. Miguel Cruz yiharije akazi ka M5: yasubiranyije chip ya Apple ya N1 maze akoresha Wi-Fi na Bluetooth yayo muri Linux. Yatanze kandi kwandika na gusoma videwo y'ibikoresho, Bluetooth, n'iterambere rya biro kuri Neo. Liam azana trackpad ya Neo, itunganywa ry'inguvu, speakers, n'isohoka rya headphone, hamwe n'inkurikirana za Ryan za macOS zitanga ishingiro ngombwa. Eryk Wieliczko abungabunga inkunga ya M4 n'ibikorwa vyanditswe bushasha, kandi akora ku huza no gupakira. Jaidip Subedi yashoboje ekara zo hanze biciye kuri USB-C kuri M2 MacBook Air.

MLX. Joshua Warren yubaka mlx-omarchy, izana framework ya Apple ya MLX yo kwiga kw'imashini kuri Linux kuri GPUs za Apple biciye kuri Vulkan. Code yawe igisoma import mlx.core as mx, ariko ubu ikora kuri Omarchy.

Macs za kera. Shawn Yeager afise umugongo wa pull requests za 2016 na 2017 Touch Bar MacBook Pros: speakers, Wi-Fi, sensor y'umuco, Touch Bar ubwayo. Randy asubiranya chip ya T2 yo kwizera kuzana Touch ID kuri Intel Macs, kandi yandika buri kosora rya 2019 MacBook Pro mu nzira. Jon Kinney yari akora kuri inkunga ya ARM kuri Omarchy umwaka uheze, kandi ubu agumisha ishingiro ryose rihujwe ku wukora iki.

Hamwe, ni umurwi ukomeye. Abantu icumi n'batano batategereje uruhushya, kandi bamaze bakoresha abafasha kugenda ku muvuduko uba wabonetse nk'ubusazi umwaka uheze.

Intezo y'igisohoka ca mbere ni ukwuzuzanya kwuzuye na M1 na M2, harimwo Pro na Max variants:

  • Uwushiraho woroshye, ufise uburyo bwo gushiraho no kugerageza
  • Inkunga yuzuye ya GPU
  • Monitors zo hanze biciye kuri USB-C
  • Touch ID
  • MLX
  • Encryption ya disiki

Mashini nshasha zirakorwako icyarimwe, ariko M1 na M2 ni ho dutera ibendera ubwa mbere. Urengeje mashini za M, hari mudasobwa ngendanwa za kera z'umiliyoni, nyinshi zamaze kujana mu kiruhuko zivuye mu nzozi nshasha za Apple za macOS, kandi buri imwe ni mudasobwa nziza ya Omarchy itegereje kuba.

Ntukeneye kurindira igisohoka gitunganye gutangura. Gerageza Omarchy izogutuma ubona biro nyayo kuri Mac yawe mu minota. Kandi niba witeguye kwiyemeza, uwushiraho azoshira Omarchy iruhande rwa macOS kuri M1 canke M2 uyu musi.

Amaherezo, gusaba. Niwaba warakora kuri Linux ku bikoresho vya Apple, baba drivers za kernel, firmware, gupakira, abashiraho, canke kwiga kw'imashini, twipfuza kukwumva. Hari amahirwe meza wakemuye ikintu tukirondera, canke kabiri. Tuvugishe kuri apple@omarchy.org.
