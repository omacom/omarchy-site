Ishiramwe Omacom rihejeje kurenga 13 million USD! Baterankunga Bacu B'icubahiro bane ba mbere bemereye 100,000 USD buri wese ku mugambi:

Ryan R. Hughes ntakeneye kwerekana hano. Ari muri Ishingiro rya Omarchy, umufasha wanje kuri distro kuva ku musi wa mbere, kandi ari partner kuri Oodle. Ubu ashingiye ishiramwe n'amafaranga yiwe bwite. Iryo ni izera!

Ed Huang ni uwashinze hamwe na CTO wa PingCAP, ishiramwe riri inyuma ya TiDB, ububiko bwa SQL bukwijwe. Yamaze imyaka icumi yerekana ko ibikorwa remezo bikomeye vya open-source bishobora kuba ubucuruzi bukomeye.

Adrien Treccani yashinze Metaco, urubuga rwa Swiss rwo kubika umutungo wa digital Ripple yaguze. Ikindi gihangange ca crypto kinjiye mu mugambi wo gutuma Omarchy ahantu heza ha crypto.

Max Schoening ni Mukuru w'Ibicuruzwa kuri Notion, kandi imbere yaho yarongoye design kuri Heroku kandi yari VP wa Design kuri GitHub. Yabungabunze ibikoresho abakora porogaramu bakunda kurusha abantu benshi bazomenya.

Hamwe n'Abaterankunga Bashinga icumi na babiri n'Abaterankunga Bacu B'icubahiro b'Amashirahamwe babiri, abo ni abashigikiye icumi n'umunani na 13 million USD bemerewe mu misi irenga ibiri.

Murakaza neza, mose mune. Umugambi urakura!
