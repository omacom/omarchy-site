1Password Omarchy guyyaa tokkoffaa irraa kutaa ture (Omakub dura kanaa!). Sababii credentials koo waggaa hedduuf nageenya qabaachee, dura Linux tti switch gochuun illee twinkle ija koo keessatti ta'uu.

Dropbox akka, tools barbaachisoo method no backup, no cry enable gochuuf keessaa tokko. Yoomiyyuu maashina haaraa qopheessu (kun torbanitti yeroo sadii guyyoota kana!), waan jalqabaa nan godhu Install > Service > 1Password. Erra agentoota koo qopheessuu, Basecamp fi HEY seenuu, fi waan biraa hunduma trivial qofa. Garuu perfectly safe, perfectly secure.

37signals station incubation Omarchy dura maqaa qabaachuu ture. Dursee garee teeknikaa guutuu gara Omarchy tti switch godhee, bills adda addaa hunduma karaa Quattro (fi dura) kaffalaa turre. Amma deeggarsi formal dha, foundation keessaan, terms waloo namoota hunduma waliin.

Akkaasumas worth noting, maallaqni corporate fi maallaqni individual waanuma tokko bita: recognition mission kutaa ta'uuf. Quid pro quo as hin jiru.

Omarchy murtoo isaa waan OS flexiblaa addunyaa keessatti gaarii ta'u irratti hundaa'e yoomiyyuu murteessa. Kunis addatti "by DHH" byline tiin define godhame yoo system galchitu. Dura system hundumaaf ajaa'ibaa ta'uuf, jalqaba anaaf personally system ajaa'ibaa ta'uu qaba. Mask kee ofii jalqaba kaa'i hunduma sana!
