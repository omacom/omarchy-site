Apple hardware gaarii hojjeta. Yoomiyyuu hojjete (minus yeroo tokko keyboard butterfly waliin). Namoonni Omarchy tti dhufan baay'een Mac irraa dhufaa jiru. Baay'een isaanii maashina qabaachuu operating system dhiisuu jaallatu. Kanaaf waan Asahi jalqabe xumuruuf deemna: Omarchy Apple Silicon irratti bareechaa hojjetu, setup fuss minimum waliin.

Hojii kun dursee garee duuba qaba. Har'a official godhna: Omarchy M, garee keenya Macs Apple Silicon'f (plus miseensota muraasa Macs moofaa fold keessa tursan).
