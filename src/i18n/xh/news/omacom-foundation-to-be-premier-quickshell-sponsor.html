Uhlaziyo, Septemba 9, 2026: Le nkxaso ithathelwe indawo kukujoyina kwe-outfoxxed kwi-Omacom Foundation ixesha elizeleyo njengeNtloko ye-Omarchy Shell nelungu le-Omarchy Core.

Imali eninzi ngaphakathi, imali eninzi ngaphandle! Inkxaso yethu yesibini enkulu evela kwi-Omacom Foundation iya ku-outfoxxed ngomsebenzi wakhe obalaseleyo kwi-Quickshell. Sele kuyiveki iyinxalenye yokukhutshwa esidlangalaleni, kodwa sele kucacile ukuba yayiyinxalenye elahlekileyo, ebalulekileyo ukuze sifezekise ngokwenene The Malleable Computer.

Ubungqina buseeplagini! Iiplagi ezingaphezu kwewaka zenziwe ngeveki le Quattro iphumileyo. Obo bubungqina obumangalisayo buguquguquko lobugcisa be-Quickshell, kunye nokuba iiarhente ziyithanda kangakanani ukubhala ikhowudi ye-QML.

Le nkxaso ivuza i-outfoxxed ngegalelo layo elibalaseleyo kwi-Omarchy, kwaye inceda ukuqinisa uxhulumaniso kwizicwangciso zethu zexesha elide ze-The Age of Agents (musani ukuthumela oko ku-Bluesky, baya kuphambana!). Imiselwe iminyaka emithathu ukuqala.

Umbulelo okhethekileyo ku-Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, kunye Peter Steinberger ngokwenza oku kwenzeke. Ukuba bekunge-Oligarchy, besingenakukwazi ukuxhasa abaphuhlisi bomthombo ovulekileyo ngolu hlobo!
