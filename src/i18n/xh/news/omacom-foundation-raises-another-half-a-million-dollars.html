I-Omacom Foundation iphakamise esinye isiqingatha sesigidi seedola! Phakathi konikezelo olutsha lweetokeni, isithembiso seenkampani seminyaka emithathu, kunye namawaka eedola ukusuka kubuxhasi obuvulekileyo, oko kumalunga ne-510,000 USD ezithembisiweyo kwi-Linux entle, eyonwabisayo nene-arhente. Oko kuzisa iyonke yethu kwizithembiso kunye neminikelo malunga ne-15.5 million USD!

Okokuqala, samkela i-OpenRouter njenge-Distinguished Corporate Patron nge-150,000 USD ngeetokeni. Sakuba eso sabelo sichithiwe, singavuselela ilungiselelo ngesivumelwano. Ezi tokens ziya kunika amandla iiarhente ezakha, zivavanya, zigcina i-Omarchy. Okukhona sinokubeka iiarhente emsebenzini zilungisa iibhagi, ziphonononga iigalelo, ziphucula i-distro, kokukhona sinokwenza ikhompyutha yomntu wonke ibe ngcono ngokukhawuleza. Amanye amafutha enjongo!

Siza kuhambisa izigidi zeedola ngeetokeni ezinikelweyo ngeqonga le-OpenRouter elibalaseleyo. Ngoko sinokwabela iitokeni ezifanelekileyo kumaqela afanelekileyo ukusuka kwiimodeli ezifanelekileyo.

Emva koko kukho i-Four Technologies, ijoyina njenge-Distinguished Corporate Patron ngesithembiso se-100,000 USD ngonyaka iminyaka emithathu. Eyo yi-300,000 USD ukuxhasa iiprojekthi zoluntu i-Omarchy exhomekeke kuzo, iziseko eziyigcina isebenza, kunye nomsebenzi wokuza ne-Linux entle kubantu abaninzi. Ukuba iinkampani zizibophelela iminyaka kusinika ukuzithemba kokwenza okufanayo.

Nantsi indlela iqela le-Four Technologies elibeka ngayo:

Singinkampani ye-fintech ejonge phambili kwi-AI, kwaye iinkampani ezifana ne-Four Technologies zifumana kuphela ukwakha ngendlela esakha ngayo kungenxa yeziseko zomthombo ovulekileyo abanye abantu abazakhe kuqala. Ukuxhasa i-Omacom Foundation yindlela esibeka ngayo imali ngasemva koko. Sicinga ukuba inqanaba elilandelayo le-AI ayisiyenye i-app ebekwe phezu kwesistim sokusebenza, yi-AI eyakhelwe ngqo kwisistim sokusebenza, kwaye loo kubheja i-Omarchy eyenzayo. Besifuna ukuba yinxalenye yoluntu olwenza loo kubheja, hayi ukuzuza kuyo kamva.

Kwaye ukusukela ukuvula ubuxhasi kumntu wonke, sifumene malunga ne-60,000 USD ukusuka kubanikeli abaphantse babe ngama-500 nge-open patronage. Eyo yimpendulo emangalisayo evela kubantu abafuna ukunceda oku kwenzeke. Igalelo ngalinye longeza kwinto esinokuyenza kunye.

Iitokeni zokwakha ngazo. Imali yokuxhasa iiprojekthi esixhomekeke kuzo. Abantu kunye neenkampani ezizimisele ukubeka into ngasemva kwenkolelo yazo kwi-Linux kwidesktophu. Yindlela esiqhubeka ngayo!

Enkosi kwi-OpenRouter, i-Four Technologies, kunye nomntu wonke obe ngumxhasi. Ukuba ungathanda ukujoyina, bhalela david@omarchy.org ngobuxhasi baseka nobubalaseleyo, kwaye nikela ngqo kubuxhasi obuvulekileyo.
