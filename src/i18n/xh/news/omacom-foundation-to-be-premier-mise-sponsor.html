Inkxaso yesithathu ngaphandle komnyango! I-Omacom Foundation iba ngumxhasi ophambili we-mise, kwaye ngaloo ndlela ka-jdx, ochithe iminyaka eyakha isixhobo esenza ngokuzolileyo ukulawula iinguqulelo ezahlukeneyo ze-Ruby, i-Node, i-Go kube mnandi ukulawula, kwaye inika zonke izixhobo zakho zearhente kwitshaneli yokuphucula enokuhambisana nesantya sokuthumela.

Ukuba uke wasebenzisa i-Omarchy, uke wasebenzisa i-mise, nokuba awuqaphelanga. Yinto elawula ii-runtimes zeelwimi, ngoko mise use -g ruby ikunika i-Ruby ngaphandle komgca omnye we-PATH archaeology. Kodwa inxalenye endiyikhathalele kakhulu ngoku zi iarhente.

Yonke i-CLI ye-coding-agent ephambili kwi-Omarchy ithunyelwa njenge-mise stub elayisha ngokuvilapha ku-~/.local/bin/. I-Claude Code, i-Codex, i-OpenCode, i-Antigravity, i-Copilot, i-Crush, i-Grok, i-Pi, i-Oh My Pi - zonke zihleli apho, zingakubizi nto de ube uchwetheza enye okokuqala. Emva koko izifaka kwaye uyasebenza. Akukho package yokuzingela, akukho version yokuncamathisela, akukho toolchain yokucinga ngayo. omarchy-mise-install <package> isonga nayiphi na enye i-CLI ngendlela efanayo, kwaye omarchy update igcina yonke into yangoku.

Yindlela ekhawulezayo. Ku-The Age of Agents, umsantsa phakathi kokuva ngesixhobo nokusisebenzisa kufuneka ube malunga nemizuzwana emine, kwaye i-mise iyakuzisa oko. Akukho mntu bekufuneka apakishe nantoni na ye-distro kuqala.

Umdla uqhuba macala omabini, nako: itrafikhi eya kwi-mise inyuke nge-24% ukusukela oko i-Omarchy yasungulwa! Abantu abaninzi badibana nesi sixhobo okokuqala ngathi, eyona nto inkxaso kufuneka iyazi kunokuba ithathe njengento eqhelekileyo.

Kwakhona, enkosi ekhethekileyo ku-Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, kunye Peter Steinberger ngokwenza oku kwenzeke. Ukuba bekunge-Oligarchy, besingenakukwazi ukuxhasa abaphuhlisi bomthombo ovulekileyo ngolu hlobo!
