I-Apple yenza ihardware enkulu. Ihlala isenza (ngaphandle kwela xesha linye nekhibhodi ye-butterfly). Kwaye abantu abaninzi abafika kwi-Omarchy bavela kwi-Mac. Uninzi lwabo lungathanda ukugcina umatshini luphose isistim sokusebenza. Ngoko siza kugqiba into i-Asahi eyayiqalile: i-Omarchy isebenza kakuhle kwi-Apple Silicon, ngengxabano encinci yokuseta.

Loo msebenzi sele uneqela ngasemva. Namhlanje siwenza ube semthethweni: i-Omarchy M, iqela lethu lee-Macs ezine-Apple Silicon (kunye namalungu ambalwa agcina ii-Macs ezindala zibandakanyiwe).

Ndivumele ndingenise amalungu.

Isifaki. U-Marcelo Alcantara wakha isifaki se-Mac semveli. Ukhuphela i-app kwi-macOS, ilanda ukukhutshwa kwe-Omarchy okusayiniweyo, yahlula iipartitions, ikuqalise kwi-Omarchy ecaleni kwe-macOS yakho ekhoyo. Akukho ntonga ye-USB, akukho mantsoe kwitheminali. Ukwanciphisa ukwakha okukhanyise iimonitors zangaphandle nge-USB-C.

I-Omarchy Mac. U-Naeem Malik ugcine i-Omarchy Mac unyaka, efaka i-Omarchy koomatshini be-M1 kunye ne-M2 kudala ngaphambi kokuba nayiphi na le nto ibe semthethweni, kwaye wakha uluntu lwe-Discord lwabantu abangaphezu kwama-800 olujikelezayo. U-Scott Jones sesinye isiqingatha saloo projekthi kwicala le-Linux lofakelo. Usanda kwenza umboniso we-M3 Air usebenze kumlawuli womboniso wehardware ye-Apple, nto leyo ethatha loo matshini ukusuka ekungenakusebenziseka ukuya ekusebenzisekeni ngobusuku obunye.

Zama i-Omarchy. U-Eduardo Martinez wakhe i-Try Omarchy: idesktophu yokwenyani ye-Omarchy esebenza njenge-app yemveli, ekhawuleziswe yihardware kwi-Mac yakho, kunye nekhamera yakho, iaudio, ibhodi ekwabelwana ngayo, kunye nefolda ekwabelwana ngayo ezifakwe iingcingo. Akukho nto yokwahlula. Yikhuphele nje ubone ukuba yintoni ingxabano.

Iipackages. U-Shun Li uqinisekisa ukuba yonke into i-Omarchy eyidingayo ikhona njenge-aarch64 package, kunye nemifanekiso yediski ye-ARM64 eqhelekileyo koomatshini obonakalayo kunye nezixhobo zeendwendwe ezenza zibe mnandi ukuzisebenzisa.

Iidrivers kunye ne-kernel. U-Ryan Murray uzisa i-Neo kwaye wakha iziseko iqela lonke elisebenza kuzo. Uphuhlisa i-emulator ye-SPTM eyenza kwenzeke ukulandelela i-macOS kwii-chips ze-M4 nangaphezulu. Ezo traces zibalulekile ekwenzeni imiboniso, ii-GPUs, iaudio, i-wireless, kunye ne-Bluetooth zisebenze. U-Dj ubhala iidrivers ze-GPU, kwaye wenze i-Touch ID yasebenza ngokuthetha ngqo kwi-Secure Enclave. U-Miguel Cruz ugxile kumsebenzi we-M5: wayenza i-reverse-engineer i-chip ye-N1 ye-Apple kwaye wenze i-Wi-Fi kunye ne-Bluetooth yayo yasebenza kwi-Linux. Ukwagalelo kufakelo lwevidiyo lwehardware kunye nokukhupha ikhowudi, i-Bluetooth, kunye nophuculo lwedesktophu kwi-Neo. U-Liam uzisa itrackpad ye-Neo, ulawulo lwamandla, izithethi, kunye nemveliso yee-headphone, kunye neetraces ze-macOS zika-Ryan zinika isiseko esibalulekileyo. U-Eryk Wieliczko ugcina inkxaso ye-M4 kunye nokuphunyezwa kwegumbi elicocekileyo, kwaye usebenza ekuhlanganiseni nasekupakisheni. U-Jaidip Subedi wenze imiboniso yangaphandle nge-USB-C kwi-M2 MacBook Air yasebenza.

MLX. U-Joshua Warren wakha i-mlx-omarchy, ezisa isakhelo sokufunda koomatshini se-MLX se-Apple kwi-Linux kwii-GPUs ze-Apple nge-Vulkan. Ikhowudi yakho isathi import mlx.core as mx, kodwa ngoku isebenza kwi-Omarchy.

Ii-Macs ezindala. U-Shawn Yeager unestaki yezicelo zokutsalwa zee-MacBook Pros ze-2016 kunye ne-2017 ze-Touch Bar: izithethi, i-Wi-Fi, isensor sokukhanya, i-Touch Bar ngokwayo. U-Randy wenza i-reverse-engineer i-chip yokhuseleko ye-T2 ukuzisa i-Touch ID kwii-Macs ze-Intel, kwaye ebhala yonke into yokulungisa i-MacBook Pro ka-2019 endleleni. U-Jon Kinney wayesusa inkxaso ye-ARM ye-Omarchy kunyaka odlulileyo, kwaye ngoku ugcina iqela liphela lilungelelaniswe kwinto eyenziwa ngubani.

Kunye, liqela eloyikekayo. Abantu abalishumi elinesihlanu abangalindanga mvume, kwaye abasebenzise iiarhente ukuhamba ngesantya ebesiya kubonakala singenangqondo kunyaka odlulileyo.

Ithagi yokuqala yokukhutshwa kukuhambelana okugqibeleleyo ne-M1 kunye ne-M2, kuquka iintlobo ze-Pro kunye ne-Max:

  • Isifaki esingenamthungo, kunye neenketho zombini zofakelo kunye nokuzama
  • Inkxaso epheleleyo ye-GPU
  • Iimonitors zangaphandle nge-USB-C
  • I-Touch ID
  • MLX
  • Ufihlo lwediski

Oomatshini abatsha basebenza ngokufanayo, kodwa i-M1 kunye ne-M2 kulapho sihlaba iflegi kuqala. Ukongeza koomatshini be-M, kukho izigidi zeelaptops zakudala phandle, uninzi lwazo sele zidla umhlala-phantsi kwiinjongo zamva nje ze-macOS ze-Apple, kwaye nganye kuzo yikhompyutha emangalisayo ye-Omarchy elindileyo.

Awudingi kulinda ukukhutshwa okucociweyo ukuze uqale. I-Try Omarchy iya kukubonisa idesktophu yokwenyani kwi-Mac yakho ngemizuzu. Kwaye ukuba ukulungele ukuzibophelela, isifaki siya kubeka i-Omarchy ecaleni kwe-macOS kwi-M1 okanye kwi-M2 namhlanje.
