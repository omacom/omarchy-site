Ukukhuphela kwe-ISO okungamakhulu amabini amawaka kwe-Omarchy Quattro kwiintsuku nje ezili-18! Ngokomyinge, umntu utsala i-Omarchy entsha yonke imizuzwana esi-8 ukusukela oko i-Quattro yathunyelwa. Encotsheni, sathumela ii-ISOs ezili-1,165 ngeyure enye. Enye yonke imizuzwana emi-3!

Ukukhuphela kuvela kumazwe nakwimimandla engama-215. Ezo ziindawo ezininzi kune-UN inamalungu! Ngaphezu kwesiqingatha sokukhuphela kuvela ngaphandle kwamazwe aphezulu amahlanu. Kwaye ngokomntu ngamnye, ii-Nordics zikhokela: i-Iceland, i-Norway, kunye ne-Denmark zonke zitsala i-Omarchy ngaphezu kabini izinga laseMelika. Ii-Vikings zophumelele! (Kodwa i-US iseyi-28% yokukhuphela ngamanani apheleleyo.)

Enye indlela enhle yokubona umfutho: i-1Password iyazi ukuba zeziphi iidistros ze-Linux abasebenzisi bayo abasebenzisayo, kwaye ngexesha leveki, i-Omarchy ngoku yi-distro yabo ye-#2, ngaphambi kwe-Fedora. Ngeeveki? Siyi-#1! Abantu basebenzisa i-Omarchy ukonwaba. Xa kungekho mntu ubaxelela into yokusebenzisa, le yinto abayikhethayo.

Ngoko masandise kakhulu: ngesantya sangoku seveki edlulileyo, sitsala i-Quattro ISO yesigidi ngoNovemba 4. Kwaye olo lubalo olugcinayo lofakelo: zizinto ezilingana nokukhuphela okugqityiweyo ezivela kwiiseva zethu, ngaphambi kokuba wongeze abantu abasebenzisa i-ISO enye kabini.
