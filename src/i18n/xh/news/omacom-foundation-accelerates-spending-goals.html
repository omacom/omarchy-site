Eli lithuba elikhulu le-Linux. Idesktophu ikulungele ukuthathwa ngendlela ebingekho kumashumi eminyaka. Iiarhente zenze isistim sokusebenza siguquguquke. Ihardware ayizange ibe ngcono. Kwaye ukhuphiswano aluzange lulangazelele ngakumbi ukucaphukisa abasebenzisi balo. Asizukuvumela lo mzuzu udlule!

Ngoko i-Omacom Foundation ikhawulezisa iinjongo zayo zenkcitho. Yonke into ephakanyiswe kulo nyaka iya kuchithwa kwiminyaka emithathu ezayo. Ayigcinwanga kwi-endowment. Ayichitshiswanga kancinci kwishumi leminyaka. Ichithiwe!

Nge-13 million USD esele zithembisiwe, oko kuthetha uhlahlo-lwabiwo mali lonyaka olungaphezu kwe-4 million USD lowama-2027, 2028, kunye nowama-2029. Kwaye ngakumbi njengoko imali ingena ivela kubaxhasi bazo zonke iintlobo. Abaseka, abaBalaseleyo, iiNkampani, kunye nomntu wonke ojoyine injongo namhlanje.

Loo mali iya kwii-projekthi zomthombo ovulekileyo i-Omarchy eyakhelwe kuzo. Kuma-gcisa ayenza ibe ntle. Kuma-baphuhlisi ayenza ikhawuleze, ihambelane, ikhuseleke. Kukokhuphiswano, izibonelelo, kunye neziseko. Kuyo yonke into efunekayo ukuzalisekisa isiprofeto se-Linux kwi-Desktop.

Imali ayiyonto. Injongo yiyo. Kodwa kuyanceda ukuba nazo zombini!
