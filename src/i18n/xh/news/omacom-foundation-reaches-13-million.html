I-Omacom Foundation isanda kuwela i-13 million USD! AbaXhasi bethu bokuqala abane abaBalaseleyo bathembise nge-100,000 USD ngamnye kwinjongo:

Ryan R. Hughes akadingi ntshayelelo apha. Lilungu le-Omarchy Core, iphiko lam kwi-distro ukusukela kusuku lokuqala, kunye neqabane e-Oodle. Ngoku uxhasa isiseko ngemali yakhe nako. Loo kukuzithemba!

Ed Huang ngumseki kunye ne-CTO ye-PingCAP, inkampani engasemva kwe-TiDB, idatabase ye-SQL esasazwayo. Uchithe ishumi leminyaka ebonakalisa ukuba iziseko zomthombo ovulekileyo ezinzulu zinokuba lishishini elinzulu.

Adrien Treccani waseka Metaco, iplatform ye-Swiss ye-custody ye-digital-asset i-Ripple eyayifumana. Omnye umvulindlela we-crypto ojoyina injongo yokwenza i-Omarchy ibe yindawo enkulu ye-crypto.

Max Schoening yiNtloko yeMveliso e-Notion, kwaye ngaphambi koko waqhuba uyilo e-Heroku kwaye wayeyi-VP yoYilo e-GitHub. Ubumbe ezininzi zezixhobo abaphuhlisi abazithandayo kuninzi lwabantu abaya kwazi.

Kunye nabaXhasi balishumi elinababini baseka kunye nabaXhasi bethu ababini beNkampani abaBalaseleyo, ngabaxhasi abalishumi elinesibhozo kunye ne-13 million USD ezithembisiweyo ngaphantsi kweeveki ezimbini.
