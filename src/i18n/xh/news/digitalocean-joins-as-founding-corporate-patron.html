I-DigitalOcean ijoyina i-Omacom Foundation njenge-Founding Corporate Patron, inikela nge-1 million USD ngonyaka iminyaka emithathu! Oko kukuzibophelela kwe-3 million USD kuphuhliso, ulondolozo, nokusasazwa kwe-Omarchy. Yivoti emangalisayo yokuzithemba kwi-OS entle, eyonwabisayo nene-arhente esiyakhayo.

Ndisebenzise ii-DigitalOcean Droplets ngaphezu kweshumi leminyaka. Ziyindlela elula emangalisayo yokubeka umatshini we-Linux kwi-intanethi uqale ukwakha into. Sizisebenzisa ukuqhuba contributors.rubyonrails.org, isayithi ebhiyozela amawaka abantu abancedileyo benza i-Rails yaba yile nto iyiyo. Esi siziseko endithembele kuso iminyaka, ngoko ukuba inkampani engasemva kwayo ijoyine ivakala kamnandi ngakumbi.

Kwaye bangumxhasi ogqibeleleyo we-OS ene-arhente. I-DigitalOcean sele inika ii-Droplets zokucofa okukanye ze-Hermes kunye ne-OpenClaw. Ungaba nearhente ekhaya kwidesktophu yakho ye-Omarchy kunye nenye esebenza ubusuku nemini kwi-Droplet.

Kodwa inxalenye yolu xhaso endenze ndancuma kakhulu yindlela yonke into edibene ngayo ngokukhawuleza. Ndafikelela ku-Paddy Srinivasan, i-CEO ye-DigitalOcean, ku-X ngoLwesithathu. Saba nomnxeba ngobo busuku. Ndathumela isindululo ngoMgqibelo. NgeCawa, besigqibile yonke into.

Eso santya sentsebenziswano senziwa yi-X, iiarhente, kunye namabhongo angenamda! Xa abantu bebona ithuba elifanayo befuna ukwenza into yenzeke, akukho sizathu sokuchitha iinyanga ukufika ku-ewe. Singahamba ngesantya samathuba aphambi kwethu.

I-DigitalOcean ijoyina i-Meta Superintelligence Labs njenge-Founding Corporate Patron, ecaleni kwabo bonke abantu kunye neenkampani esele zinyukele ukuxhasa le njongo. Isithembiso sabo siza nenkxaso yethu iyonke malunga ne-18.5 million USD. Indawo eninzi yokutyala kubantu, iiprojekthi, kunye neziseko ezenza i-Omarchy ibe ngcono kumntu wonke.

Njengokuqesha abantu abamangalisayo abaninzi! Sanda kuzisa u-Krzysztof Wilczyński njengeNtloko ye-Omarchy Kernel kunye ne-outfoxxed, umdali we-Quickshell, njengeNtloko ye-Omarchy Shell. Ukunika abantu abanjengaba ithuba lokusebenza ixesha elizeleyo ekwenzeni i-Omarchy ikhawuleze, ithembeke ngakumbi, ikwazi ngakumbi kunziwa kuphela ngabaxhasi abanjengo-DigitalOcean. Inkxaso yabo iguqula amabhongo ethu abe yimisebenzi yokwenyani yabantu abanokuwenza.

Enkosi, Paddy nomntu wonke kwa-DigitalOcean. Masibeke le nkxaso emsebenzini senze ikamva le-Linux elihle, elonwabisayo, elinearhente lenzeke!
