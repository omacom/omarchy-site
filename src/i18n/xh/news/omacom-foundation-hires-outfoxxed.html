Siqesha i-outfoxxed, umdali we-Quickshell, njenge-Ntloko ye-Omarchy Shell! Ujoyina i-Omacom Foundation ixesha elizeleyo kwaye uba lilungu le-Omarchy Core. Umntu owakhe ikhithi yezixhobo eyenze i-Quattro yenzeke uza ebhodini ukusinceda siqhubeke yonke le nto siye enyangeni.

Oku kuthatha indawo yenkxaso ye-Quickshell yeminyaka emithathu esayibhengeza ngo-Agasti. Endaweni yaloo nkxaso, i-outfoxxed iza kuba ngumsebenzi wexesha elizeleyo we-Omacom Foundation, ikhokela umsebenzi wethu kwi-Omarchy Shell. Sibeka umdali ngqo kumbindi wento esiyakhayo.

I-Shell ibalulekile kwi-OS ene-arhente esiyenzayo. Yibhari, iimenyu, izaziso, iiwijethi, kunye nentsebenziswano encinci eyenza ikhompyutha ivakale njengeyakho. I-Quickshell isinika iibhloko zokwakha ukubumba loo mava kwi-QML. Dibanisa oko neearhente ezilungileyo kakhulu ekubhaleni i-QML, kwaye ngequbuliso ukutshintsha idesktophu yakho yinto onokuyicela, uyizame, wabelane ngayo.

Ubungqina buseeplagini! Xa sibhengeza inkxaso, uluntu lwalu sele lwenza ngaphezu kwewaka kwiveki yokuqala ye-Quattro. Ngoku ikhathalogu ineplagi ephantse ibe ngamawaka amathathu. Imidlalo onokuyidlala ngqo kwibar, iiphaneli ezilandelela iiarhente zakho zekhowudi, abadlali bomculo, kunye nekati ehamba ecaleni kwebar yakho ilinde ukuphathaphathwa. Abantu bathatha isistim eyayivakala imiselwe bayenza eyabo. Yikhompyutha eguquguqukayo ephilayo.

Ukuba nomdali we-Quickshell ebhodini kuthetha ukuba singasebenza kuyo yonke istaki kunye. Senze i-shell ikhawuleze ithembeke ngakumbi. Senze kube lula ukwakha iplagi enkulu. Siphucule iziseko xa umbhali weplagi edibana nomda. Siguqule into esiyifundayo kumawaka olingo ibe ngamava angcono kumntu wonke oqalisa i-Omarchy. Kwaye siqinisekise ukuba yonke into ikhuselekile.

Kwaye ngokujoyina i-Omarchy Core, i-outfoxxed iya kunceda ukubeka icala le-distro iyonke. I-Shell isembindini kakhulu wamava ukuba ibe yingcinga yasemva. Kulapho ubuhle obuninzi, ulonwabo, kunye namathuba e-OS ene-arhente aba yinto onokuyibona ngokwenene uyisebenzise.

I-outfoxxed kukuqeshwa kwesibini kwexesha elizeleyo kwe-Omacom Foundation, ilandela u-Krzysztof Wilczyński, iNtloko yethu ye-Omarchy Kernel. Ngabantu abakhokela zombini i-Kernel kunye ne-Shell bebhodini, sityala kwiziseko zamava aphela. Ungadibana nabasebenzi besiseko kwisayithi.

Siza kuqhubeka nokuqesha ngesimemo njengoko sifumana abantu abafanelekileyo ngomsebenzi wabo kuluntu. (Nceda musani ukuzalisa ibhokisi yam ngama-CV!)

Yile nto inkxaso yesiseko esivumela ukuba siyenze: nika abaphuhlisi bomthombo ovulekileyo abakhethekileyo ixesha lokungena ngokupheleleyo. Enkosi ku-umntu wonke oxhasa loo msebenzi, siguqula ngakumbi amabhongo ethu abe yimisebenzi yexesha elizeleyo.

Wamkelekile ebhodini, outfoxxed! Masenze le khompyutha iguquguquke ngakumbi.
