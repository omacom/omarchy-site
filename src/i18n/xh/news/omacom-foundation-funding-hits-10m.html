Inkxaso-mali ye-Omacom Foundation isanda kufikelela kwi-10 million USD nabaXhasi ababini abatsha baseka abajoyina injongo: Drew Houston, umseki kunye ne-co-CEO ye-Dropbox, kunye Peter Steinberger, umdali we-OpenClaw.

NjengabaXhasi bokuqala abasibhozo baseka, u-Drew no-Peter nganye inikela nge-1 million USD. Oko kunika isiseko abaxhasi abalishumi kunye ne-TEN MILLION DOLLARS ecocekileyo ukuqhubela phambili injongo yethu enesibindi yokwenza i-Linux kwidesktophu yenzeke ngendlela enkulu kakhulu.

Kumnandi ngakumbi ukuba no-Drew no-Peter bebhodini kungenxa yokuba ndingumlandeli omkhulu wabo bobabini kunye nempumelelo zabo. Bendingumthengi onomdla we-Dropbox phantse iminyaka engamashumi amabini, kwaye isembindini wendlela endikwazi ukuphila nge-no backup, no cry kodwa nditshintsha iikhompyutha rhoqo.

Yiyo loo nto i-Omarchy ithunyelwa nokudityaniswa okukhulu kwe-Dropbox ngaphandle kwebhokisi. Yiya nje ku-Install > Service > Dropbox, kwaye uya kuba nokudityaniswa komphathi wefayile kunye nephaneli yemenyu eyenzelwe ulawulo.

Kwaye i-OpenClaw inike i-roadmap yendlela i-Omarchy kufuneka ijongane ngayo nokukhula kwayo okuqhumayo. Ujongana njani nomthwalo we-PR oye phezulu ngequbuliso? Kuthekani ngokhuseleko lweeplagi? Iindibano zegama leprojekthi kufuneka ziphathwe njani? Sele ndonwabele ukusebenzisa iingcebiso kunye namava ka-Peter kwezinye zezi zihloko.

Ngaphaya koko, bobabini bamele kanye uhlobo lwamabhongo obugcisa endilufuna malunga ne-Omarchy. U-Drew uguqule ukurhawuzelelwa komntu malunga nokuhambisa iifayile phakathi kweekhompyutha waba yi-Dropbox. U-Peter uguqule iprojekthi yempelaveki waba yi-OpenClaw, kwaye kwinkqubo ukhumbuza wonke umntu ukuba mdala kangakanani umnqweno weekhompyutha ezivakala zobuqu, ezinokucwangciswa, ezonwabisayo.

Loo ngumoya osemva kwe-Omacom Foundation nako. Silapha ukuxhasa iziseko, iiprojekthi zomthombo ovulekileyo, kunye nabaphuhlisi abafunekayo ukwenza idesktophu ye-Linux ibe likhaya elimnandi kwisizukulwana esilandelayo sabasebenzisi beekhompyutha.

Wamkelekile, Drew no-Peter. Abaxhasi abalishumi baseka. Iidola ezilishumi zesigidi. Isiprofeto sisanda kusondela nge-2 million USD!
