Ukwamkelwa kwe-Omacom Foundation bekungaphantsi kokumangalisayo. AbaXhasi balishumi elinababini baseka, abaXhasi abane abaBalaseleyo, abaXhasi beNkampani ababini abaBalaseleyo, kunye ne-13 million USD ezithembisiweyo kwinjongo ngaphantsi kweeveki ezimbini.

Kodwa isiseko asisilo nje le-Oligarchy kunye nabaXhasi beNkampani. Sesomntu wonke. Nabani na onokuxhasa i-Linux entle, eyonwabisayo nene-arhente.

Ngoko ukusukela namhlanje, ubuxhasi buvulelekile kumntu wonke. Kukho amanqanaba amane: i-16 USD, i-256 USD, i-2,048 USD, kunye ne-8,192 USD. Amandla eebits, kunjalo! Umxhasi ngamnye uyinxalenye yenjongo enye, kwaye idola nganye ithenga into enye: ithuba lokwenyani lokuzalisekisa isiprofeto se-Linux kwi-Desktop!

Kwaye umxhasi ngamnye ufumana isiqinisekiso serali sedijithali ukuze abonise. Inqanaba ngalinye line-ibheji, ikhadi lezentlalo, kunye neephepha zodonga zalo, eziyilwe liGcisa lethu lokuHlala OldJobobo. Iklasi ye-016 ikuthatha kwi-Forest Stage. Iklasi ye-8K iqhuba i-Night Stage. Khetha iklasi yakho! Kwaye iintlobo ezininzi ezivela kwamanye amagcisa ziza kungekudala.

Imali sele isebenza nzima. Isiseko ngumxhasi okhethekileyo we-Hyprland kunye nomxhasi ophambili we-Quickshell kunye ne-mise, ezintathu kwiintsika i-Omarchy eyakhelwe kuzo. Ixhasa Amagcisa okuHlala enza i-distro ibe ntle. Igcina izibane zikhanya kuzo zonke iziseko uhajiso lwe-Linux oludingayo. Iqhuba ukhuphiswano lweeplagi, kunye nenye entsha ye-10,000 USD iza kungekudala enabaphumeleleyo abaninzi kuneyokuqala. Kwaye ukusukela namhlanje, ino-mqeshwa wayo wokuqala wexesha elizeleyo: umphuhlisi we-Linux kernel okhokela umsebenzi kwi-Omarchy Kernel. Kukho okungakumbi kuyo yonke endleleni!
