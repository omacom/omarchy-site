Brian Armstrong ngumseki kunye ne-CEO ye-Coinbase, eyazalwa isebenzisa Ruby on Rails, yakhula yaba yindawo ephezulu ye-crypto kunye nabasebenzisi abangaphezu kwezigidi ezilikhulu. U-Brian ukwaqala ukwala ububhanxa bezopolitiko emsebenzini xa ebhengeza Coinbase yinkampani egxile kwinjongo emva ngo-2020. Wathatha ubushushu obuninzi ngeso simo ngelo xesha, kodwa ukususela ngoko uqinisekisiwe ngokupheleleyo (indoda yokwenyani ye-Night Watch!).

Kwaye nge-crypto, nditshintshile ingcinga yam kwiminyaka edlulileyo. No-Brian ebhodini, siza kwenza i-Omarchy ibe yindawo enkulu ye-crypto. Kungenxa yokuba singalungisa yonke into!

Yunjie Dai ngusomashishini wemidlalo wase-China oqhuba TapTap, enye yeeplatforms ezinkulu zelizwe zokufumanisa nokuhanjiswa kwemidlalo. Enye engathathi sabelo sengeniso kubaphuhlisi. Kodwa ndiza kumvumela azingenise, kungenxa yokuba ileta ayithumelayo yayimangalisayo:

Kwiminyaka engama-21 edlulileyo, ngo-2005, ndandisanda kuphuma ekholejini xa ndibukela ividiyo yakho yakha ibhlog nge-Ruby on Rails kunye ne-TextMate kwi-Mac OS X malunga nemizuzu eli-15. Ndathenga i-MacBook emhlophe kungekudala emva koko, ndashiya i-Windows ngasemva, ndathenga i-TextMate, ndaqala ukufunda i-Ruby. Yaguqula kakhulu ukuba ndiwonwabela kangakanani ukubhala ikhowudi.

Ndingusomashishini wemidlalo wase-China, kwaye usapho lwam nam ngoku sihlala e-U.S. Sakha imidlalo, kwaye sikwaqhuba i-TapTap… Ngokungafaniyo ne-App Store okanye i-Google Play, asithathi sabelo sengeniso kubaphuhlisi, ngelixa sisebenza kwisikali esinentsingiselo nangenzuzo. Loo mvo uziva kufutshane nento ebesoloko ndiyithanda nge-37signals: hlala uzimele, zizuzele imali yakho, ungahluthi irente kungenxa yokuba unako, kwaye wakhe into eluncedo ngokwaneleyo ukuba abantu bakhethe ukuhlala.
