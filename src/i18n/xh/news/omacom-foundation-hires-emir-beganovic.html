Siqesha u-Emir Beganović njenge-Ntloko yeZiseko ze-Omarchy! Uqala nge-Novemba 1 njengokuqeshwa kwesithathu kwe-Omacom Foundation kwexesha elizeleyo, ejoyina u-Krzysztof Wilczyński kwi-Kernel kunye ne-outfoxxed kwi-Shell.

Nantsi indlela u-Emir achaza ngayo uhambo lwakhe ukuya kwi-Omarchy:

Uthando lwam lwe-Linux lubuyela emva ngaphezu kweminyaka engamashumi amabini. Ndaba ngumphuhlisi we-Ubuntu ndisengumfana kwaye ndabandakanyeka nzulu kuluntu lwasekuhlaleni. Umsebenzi wam undithathe kumazwe amathathu, ndisebenza kuthembeka kunye nokusebenza kwii-startups kunye nee-scaleups ndikhokela amaqela. Ndichithe phantse iminyaka esixhenxe kwisebe le-SRE le-Booking.com, ndikhokela umsebenzi kwiinkqubo zokwakha kunye neziseko ze-CI. Kutshanje, ndakhe i-Isorun, apho bendisebenza kwii-microVMs, ii-snapshots, ukwahlula, ukucwangcisa, kunye nekhompyutha elahlwayo. Ngoku ndibuyisela la mava kuthando lwam lokuqala.

Into endibambileyo ngqo ngo-Emir yayiyi-TryOmarchy. Uthathe idesktophu ye-Omarchy, wayibeka kwibhrawuza (ayilophindomiliselo, yinto yokwenyani), kwaye wayenza iqale ngemizuzwana kwiziseko ze-CPU eziqhelekileyo kwa-Cloudflare. Saqala ukuthetha ngolo lingelo, kwaye incoko yanda ngokukhawuleza kwiilaptops, ii-architectures ezintsha, ii-OEMs, kunye neziseko esiya kuzidinga ukuba i-Omarchy iza kusebenza naphi na sifuna. Saqonda ukuba sabelana ngumbono ofanayo.

Yinto u-Emir eza ebhodini ukuyikhokela.

Iziseko ze-Omarchy zithetha okungaphezulu kokuqhuba iiseva. Yinto esiyisebenzisayo ukwakha, ukuvavanya, nokuthumela i-Omarchy, kunye nokugcina isistim isebenza ngokuthembekileyo njengoko sisanda kwihardware eninzi kunye nee-architectures ezingakumbi. Yenza ukukhutshwa kuthembeke, yenza ukusilela kuphindaphindeke, kwaye inika wonke umntu kwi-projekthi isiseko esiqinileyo ukuze ahambe ngokukhawuleza.

Kwaye kukho okuninzi kokuhlola! I-Omarchy kufuneka isebenze kakuhle kumatshini ohleli edesikeni yakho, kodwa akukho sizathu sokuba ime apho. I-TryOmarchy isinike ukuqonda kwento eyenzekayo xa ukuqala ikhompyutha ye-Linux kuba lula njengokuvula iphepha lewebhu, kubeka incasa ye-Linux kukucofa okukanye. Siza kubona apho kukhokelela khona.

Enkosi ku-umntu wonke oxhasa lo msebenzi, singanika abantu abanjengo-Emir ixesha lokungena ngokupheleleyo kwi-Omarchy. Abaxhasi bethu benza kwenzeke ukuguqula ulingo olunamabhongo lube ngumsebenzi ohlala, nabantu bexesha elizeleyo abazinikele ekwenzeni yonke isistim ibe ngcono. Siyabulela ngobuxhasi babo.

Wamkelekile ebhodini, Emir! Masenze i-Omarchy isebenze naphi na.
