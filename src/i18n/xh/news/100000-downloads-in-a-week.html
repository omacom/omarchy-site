Ukukhuphela kwe-Omarchy ISO okwalikhulu lamawaka ngeveki edlulileyo! Abantu abalikhulu lamawaka abagqibe kwelokuba le yiveki abaya kuzama ngayo i-Linux (okanye babuyele kuyo). Yintoni iqela leebhosi!

Ngokwemigaqo ye-bandwidth, saphosa phantse i-petabyte enye kwezo ntsuku zisixhenxe. Iwaka leeterabytes ze-Linux ngeveki phakathi kwee-ISOs kunye nohlaziyo lweepackages!

Oku ngokuqinisekileyo bekuya kuyiwisa i-Omarchy kwelinye ixesha. Kusithumele sifuna iimirrors okanye iitorrents (sicinga ngokufuna enye imortgage endlwini ukuhlawula ibhili yokubamba). Kodwa asizange sifune.

Kungenxa yokuba i-Cloudflare ihleli phambi kwayo yonke: ii-ISOs, iipackages ze-Omarchy, kunye ne-Arch mirror yethu. Konke ku-R2 nasemva kwe-CDN yabo. Akukho nanye ebilana. Kwaye yiyo loo nto abantu abalikhulu lamawaka benokukhuphela okukhawulezayo, okungenamdla, okungenanto ngeveki edlulileyo ukusuka naphi na emhlabeni.

Enkosi kumntu wonke kwa-Cloudflare, naku-Matthew Prince ngakumbi. U-Matthew ukwayenye yee-Abaxhasi abaseki, nto leyo ithetha ukuba uxhasa le projekthi ngeziseko kunye nemali yakhe. Enkosi kakhulu, mhlekazi!

I-ISO ezalikhulu lamawaka ngeveki. Wow.
