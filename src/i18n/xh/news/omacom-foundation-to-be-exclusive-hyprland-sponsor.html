Yeyiphi indlela engcono yokuqala ukuchitha enye ye-treasure esanda ukuyiphakamisa ye-Omacom Foundation kunakowona mntwana we-Linux ugqwesileyo e-Poland: u-Vaxry!

Njengomdali we-Hyprland, unoxanduva lwenye yeeteknoloji ezintathu ezingundoqo kwi-Omarchy (i-Arch Linux/i-Hyprland/i-Quickshell), kwaye ndivuya kakhulu ukubhengeza inkxaso ekhethekileyo emvumela agxile ngokupheleleyo ekuyiphuhlisa ngakumbi ngaphandle kwesidingo sokucinga ngophuhliso lwezorhwebo okanye ukufuna imali.

Sisayina ixesha elide no-Vaxry ukuxhasa ikamva lexesha elide le-Omarchy. Isivumelwano sethu senkxaso ekhethekileyo simiselwe iminyaka emithathu, kunye nokukhetha eminye emibini.

Isivumelwano sikwavumela inkqubo ye-Hyprland ekhoyo ye-Hyprperks ukuba ikhutshwe simahla, kumntu wonke, njengoko imirhumo ehlawulwayo iya kuphela. Kodwa i-Hyprland isaya kwamkela iminikelo yobuqu ukuvumela abantu bathenge kuphuhliso lweprojekthi nokuxhasa u-Vaxry neqela.

Enkosi kakhulu ku-37signals (molo mna! 😂), i-Butterfly, kunye i-Framework ngokunceda i-Hyprland ifikelele apha. Ndivuya kakhulu ukuba i-Omacom Foundation inokuthatha ukusukela ngo-Oktobha 10, inike omnye wabaphuhlisi ababalaseleyo kweli xesha elitsha le-Linux ithuba lokungena ngokupheleleyo.

(* Esi sivumelwano asibandakanyi naluphi na uncedo oluthe ngqo ekumfumaniseni u-Vaxry intombi, kodwa ndiza kufumaneka buqu njengereferensi kuyo nayiphi na imibuzo ngalo mba.)
