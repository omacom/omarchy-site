Lixesha lokuphupha okukhulu. I-Omarchy Quattro inike abantu ithuba lokuva ukuba ikhompyutha eguquguqukayo yexesha elizayo ibonakala njani, kwaye bayayithanda (kakhulu!). Ngoku ivakala njengesibophelelo sokuziphatha ukwenza eli kamva lifumaneke ngokubanzi kwaye utshintshe ngokusisiseko indlela abantu abanxulumana ngayo neekhompyutha zabo okokuqala kwinto ebonakala ngonaphakade.

Ukwenza kanye oko, ndibandakanya i-Omacom Foundation ukuqinisekisa ukuba le njongo ixhaswe ngokupheleleyo ngemali, yomelele, kwaye ilungele ukukhawulezisa.

Esi siseko esingenzi nzuzo siya kubamba iimpawu zorhwebo, sixhase iziseko, sikhuthaze umsebenzi, sixhase iiprojekthi zomthombo ovulekileyo kunye nabaphuhlisi i-Omarchy exhomekeke kubo.
