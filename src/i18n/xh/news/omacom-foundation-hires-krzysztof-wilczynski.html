I-Omacom Foundation isanda kwenza ukuqeshwa kwayo kokuqala kwexesha elizeleyo! U-Krzysztof Wilczyński ujoyina ukukhokela umsebenzi wethu kwi-Omarchy Kernel. Yile nto kanye isiseko esasetiwe ukuyenza: hlawula abantu ababalaseleyo ukusebenza kwiingxaki ezinzima ukuze kuxhamle wonke umntu.

U-Krzysztof ngumphuhlisi we-Linux kernel wexesha elide. Ugcinana kunye i-PCI Endpoint subsystem kunye ne-native PCI host bridge kunye needrivers ze-endpoint. Loo yimibhobho edibanisa i-Linux kwi-GPU yakho, kwi-NVMe drive yakho, nakwikhadi lakho le-WiFi. Ukwangumhloli kumsebenzi omtsha we-Rust PCI we-kernel. Ngaphambi kokungena nzulu kwi-kernel, uchithe iminyaka kubunjineli beesistim, esebenza kwi-Kubernetes kunye nee-container runtimes.

Ngoku uza kuzisa lonke olo mava kwi-Omarchy Kernel. Ugxininiso: ukusebenza, ukuhambelana, kunye nokhuseleko. Sifuna i-Linux ibhabhe kwiilaptops ezintsha kunye nee-ThinkPads zakudala ngokufanayo. Sifuna isebenze ngaphandle kwebhokisi koomatshini abaninzi. Kwaye sifuna itshixwe ngokuqinileyo.

U-Krzysztof ukwajoyina i-Omarchy Core, ngoko uya kunceda ukubeka icala le-distro iyonke.

Wamkelekile ebhodini, Krzysztof! Masenze i-kernel ikhale.
