I-Omarchy ayisiyohle, eyonwabisayo kunye nokuhanjiswa kwe-Linux ene-arhente kwabo bagcina beyisebenzisa. Yakhiwe ngaloo ndlela nako! Ngobuhle, ulonwabo, kunye neetokeni ezininzi. Enyanisweni, i-Omarchy Quattro phantse yakhiwe ziiarhente kuphela. Kungenxa yokuba zilungile kakhulu kwizinto ezifana ne-bash, i-QML, kunye nokulungisa iingxaki ze-Linux.

Ukuqhubeka nokwenza i-Omarchy ibe ngcono ngoku sitsale amakhulu amawaka abasebenzisi kunye nabadali, kufuneka sigcine iitokeni zihamba. Yindlela esikhawulezisa, siphucula, sihanjisa ngayo zonke iigalelo ezimangalisayo ebezithululwa. Sele sinomthwalo wee-PRs ezingaphezu kwe-1,600 ukusuka kwiiveki ezimbalwa emva kokusungulwa! Akukho mntu uya kudlula apho ngaphandle koncedo lwearhente.

Ngoko, ndivuya kakhulu ukubhengeza ukuba sifumene umthwalo omkhulu weetokeni kwiilabs ezinkulu zale njongo! Eyokuqala yi-Meta Superintelligence Labs eza njenge-Founding Token Patron nge-1.5m USD ngeetokeni! Imodeli yabo entsha ye-Meta Muse Spark ibeka amanani amahle ngexabiso elimangalisayo. Ngoko i-1.5m USD zii-tokens ezingenamda! (Siza kuzama ukuziphelisa kunjalo!)

Kodwa sivuya nako ukwamkela ezinye iilabs ezimbini ezikhokelayo, i-Anthropic kunye i-OpenAI, njenge-Distinguished Token Patrons nge-150,000 USD ngeminikelo yeetokeni nganye. Ezi ziya kuba luncedo ngakumbi xa zidityaniswe nemvume yokhuseleko efunekayo ukuhluba i-guardrails esizidingayo kuphando lokhuseleko kunye nokulungiswa.

Kwaye kwiimodeli ze-open-weight, ndivuya ngokulinganayo ukubhengeza i-Fireworks njenge-Distinguished Token Patron nako nge-150,000 USD ngeetokeni ezithembisiweyo. Bendingumsebenzisi rhoqo weemodeli ze-Kimi K ngakumbi, kwaye ngakumbi xa isebenza kwimo ekhawulezayo. Siza kubeka ezi tokens zikhawulezayo kusetyenziso oluhle nako!

Dibanisa, yenye i-1.95 million USD ethembisiweyo kwi-Omacom Foundation. Emva koko kubeka izithembiso zethu zizonke kwi-14.95 million USD.
