Iwebhusayithi yohanjiso lwe-Linux oluhle, olonwabisayo kufuneka ibe ntle kwaye yonwabise nako. Ngoko sinike i-Omarchy.org ukulungiswa okupheleleyo, kwaye siyisungula kwiilwimi ezingama-29 (kungenxa yokuba xa usanda kuphakamisa ~2m USD ngeetokeni, kutheni ungavumi?). Umlingo omncinci we-retro, okungakumbi kokuhlola, kunye nokwamkela okukhulu kakhulu ehlabathini.

Le yiewebhusayithi efuna udlale ngekhompyutha yakho! Iipixels, imibala, kunye noopopayi bathwala umoya we-Omarchy ngqo kwibhrawuza. Ungayiva indawo ngaphambi kokuba ukhuphele i-ISO enye. Ikwanxibelelwano lwam lobuqu kule minyaka yoqalo kwihlabathi le-intanethi langaphambi kwe-intanethi lobugcisa be-ASCII, i-demoscene ye-Amiga, kunye nobuhle obuqaqambileyo bee-90s. Umbala ongaphezulu, ukucoca okuncinci. Iipixels ezingakumbi, iigradients ezithambileyo ezimbalwa.

Ngoku kukho indawo yokubonisa abahleli, iitheminali, iiarhente, imidlalo, i-Windows VM, kunye nehardware eyenza i-Omarchy ibe luncedo yonke imihla. Bhrawuza imixholo yoluntu, fumana iplagi, bona iiworkstations zokwenyani, okanye ufumane indibano ekufutshane nawe. Dibana nabantu abayakhayo kunye nabaxhasi abayixhasayo. Ekugqibeleni sinomnyango ongaphambili unendawo eyaneleyo yayo yonke into eyenzekayo ngaphakathi.

Kwaye loo mnyango ongaphambili ngoku uthetha ulwimi lwakho. isiArabhu, isiBengali, isiCatalan, isiDanish, isiNgesi, isiFilipino, isiFinnish, isiFrentshi, isiGrike, isiHindi, isiHungarian, isiIcelandic, isiIrish, isiTaliyane, isiJapan, isiKorea, isiLithuanian, isiPolishi, isiPhuthukezi, isiTshayina esiLula, isiSinhala, isiSpeyin, isiSwedish, isiTamil, isiThai, isiTurkish, isiUrdu, isiUzbek, kunye nesiVietnamese. Isayithi ephambili kunye nebali ngalinye leendaba ziguqulelwe. Incwadi isesiNgesini; siza kujongana nayo ngokulandelayo. Ezi zinguqulelo zokuqala, kwaye ukulungiswa okuvela kwizithethi zemveli kwamkelekile kakhulu nge-repository yesayithi.

Olu lungiso lukhule kumsebenzi omangalisayo kwiqela le-Omarchy Design. Barış Girişmen wenze uyilo olwaba sisiseko sesayithi entsha, wakha i-shader ye-pixel-grid, kwaye waqhuba inani elikhulu lokuphunyezwa kunye nokucoca. Christoffer Hallas uze neeprototypes, umsebenzi woopopayi, utshintsho lwemixholo, kunye nomjelo ozinzileyo wohlaziyo. Daniel Schmier unikele uyilo kunye nokuphunyezwa kwelogo, iindaba, ufakelo, iqela, isiseko, kunye namacandelo obungqina.
