I-Omacom Foundation inabaXhasi bayo bokuqala ababini beNkampani abaBalaseleyo! I-1Password kunye ne-37signals nganye inikela nge-100,000 USD ngonyaka kwiminyaka emithathu ezayo, nto leyo ethatha inkxaso-mali yesiseko ukuya kwi-12.6 million USD.

I-1Password ibiyinxalenye ye-Omarchy ukusukela kusuku lokuqala (kunye nenxalenye ye-Omakub phambi koko!). Kungenxa yokuba igcine iziqinisekiso zam zikhuselekile iminyaka emininzi ngaphambi kokuba ukutshintshela kwi-Linux kube yintshayi emehlweni am.

Njenge-Dropbox, sesinye sezixhobo ezibalulekileyo zokuvumela indlela ye-no backup, no cry. Nanini na ndiseta umatshini omtsha (omalunga namaxesha amathathu ngeveki kwezi ntsuku!), into yokuqala endiyenzayo yi-Install > Service > 1Password. Emva koko ukuseta iiarhente zam, ukungena kwi-Basecamp kunye ne-HEY, kunye nayo yonke enye into ilula. Kodwa ikhuselekile ngokugqibeleleyo, ikhuselekile ngokugqibeleleyo.

I-37signals ibiyindawo yokufukamela i-Omarchy ukusukela ngaphambi kokuba ibe negama. Sele sitshintshe iqela lonke lobugcisa saya kwi-Omarchy, kwaye besihlawula zonke iintlobo zeebhili endleleni eya kwi-Quattro (nangaphambili). Ngoku inkxaso isemthethweni, ngesiseko, ngemigaqo efanayo nomntu wonke.

Kufanelekile ukuqaphela, nako, ukuba imali yeenkampani kunye nemali yomntu zonke zithenga into enye: ukuqondwa ngokuba yinxalenye yenjongo. Akukho quid pro quo apha.

I-Omarchy iya kuhlala isekela izigqibo zayo kwinto eyenza i-OS eguquguqukayo ebalaseleyo emhlabeni. Kwaye oko kuchazwe ngakumbi ngomgca othi “by DHH” xa ufaka isistim. Ngaphambi kokuba oku kube yisistim emangalisayo kumntu wonke, kufuneka kuqala kube yisistim emangalisayo kum, buqu. Beka imaski yakho kuqala nako konke!
