I-Omarchy ikhule yaba luluntu lwehlabathi, kunye namalungu amalunga nama-35,000 ku-i-Discord, kwaye ngoku lixesha lokuba siqale ukudibana ubuso ngobuso. Ngoko sisungula ii-Omarchy Meetups kunye nekhalenda ekwabelwana ngayo yeendibano kwihlabathi liphela.

Ezi ziganeko eziqhutywa luluntu. Awudingi mvume ukulungiselela enye, akukho mntu unendawo yesixeko, kwaye nabani na unokuhlanganisa enye naphi na. Thetha nge-Omarchy, i-Linux, umthombo ovulekileyo, inkqubo, ukwenza ngokwezifiso, okanye nayiphi na inkcubeko yee-hacker ekufutshane eyenza abantu bavuye ngokwenza iikhompyutha zibe mnandi kwakhona.

Yigcine ivulelekile kumntu wonke, yazisa nayiphi na inkxaso, ungaguquli igama le-Omarchy libe lishishini leziganeko okanye intengiso. Ngaphaya koko, ziphathe njengabantu abadala abaphucukileyo (ngokumangaliswa kobuntwana), ube nexesha elimnandi.

Ze nelaptop yakho. Bonisa useto lwakho. Tshintshiselana ngemixholo kunye neeplagi. Nceda umntu kufakelo lwakhe lokuqala. Injongo kukudibanisa abantu abakhathalela iikhompyutha zobuqu, ezinokucwangciswa, ezintle kwigumbi elinye.

Ungakwazi ukufumana indibano ezayo okanye ungenise eyakho ngekhalenda ye-Omarchy yehlabathi ku-Luma. Masenze oku kube yintshukumo yehlabathi liphela!
