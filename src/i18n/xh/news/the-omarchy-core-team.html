Ukukhula okuqhumayo kwe-Omarchy kufuna amaqela acwangcisiweyo njengempendulo, kwaye siza kuqala ngokubandakanya iQela le-Omarchy Core. Eli qela liza kwabelana ngoxanduva lokuqhuba icala le-distro yethu, liqinisekisa ukuba siyaqhubeka nokuqhubela phambili, kwaye linceda abanye abanegalelo.

Ndivumele ndingenise iQela leCore lokuqala:

Ryan Hughes ebeyiphiko lam kwi-Omarchy ukusukela kusuku lokuqala. Salwa kunye ukuphazamiseka kwe-AUR okungxamisekileyo kuka-3am kwiintsuku zokuqala, kwaye saceba ukwakha i-repository yethu ye-package. Igalelo lika-Ryan lifikelela kumaleko onke e-distro: ufakelo, ukupakisha, inkxaso yehardware, imidlalo, imixholo, izixhobo ze-CLI, kwaye ngoku i-Quattro. Uncede ukuqhuba ukufudukela kwi-Quickshell, isistim yeeplagi, kunye noninzi lweziseko eziguqule i-Omarchy ukusuka kuseto lwe-Linux oluhlanganiswe ngononophelo ukuya kwisistim sokusebenza esifanelekileyo. Xa engahacki kwi-Omarchy, u-Ryan liqabane e-Oodle.

Tobi Lütke nguye kuphela umntu owenze zombini iindidi zam ze-core! Njengelungu le-Rails Core Team yokuqala eyaqala ngo-2004, waqhuba hayi nje irekhodi enzulu yegalelo kwisakhelo, kodwa wabhala i-Liquid, i-Delayed Job, kunye ne-Active Merchant. Loo msebenzi wagcina uguquke waba yinkampani encinci ebizwa ngokuba Shopify, ukhe weva ngabo? Ngoku ungumnikeli onemveliso kwi-Omarchy ngeeprojekthi ezifana try, Omasnap, Wi-Fi QR codes, kunye the Quake-style agent console. Ukwenzeka ukuba ngumqhubi wam kwi-the #11 Shopify Racing LMP2 car egijima kwi-IMSA.

Bjarne Øverli yi-CTO ye-Unite AS kunye nomdali we-Aether, umakhi wemixholo obonakalayo esiwuthumela kwi-Omarchy, kunye ne-cliamp, umdlali wethu womculo we-TUI. Uncede ukuguqula isistim yethu yemixholo ukusuka kwingqokelela yeefayile zoqwalaselo ukuya kwinto ecwangcisiweyo kakhulu, enemibala ye-semantic kunye nemixholo eyenziweyo kwi-Neovim, i-VS Code, i-btop, kunye nayo yonke istaki. Ukwanoxanduva lwemixholo efana ne-Ethereal, i-Hackerman, kunye ne-Lupine. U-Bjarne unayo loo ndibaniselwano inqabileyo yokuqina komprogrammer kunye nencasa ebonakalayo ehambelana ne-Omarchy ngokugqibeleleyo.

HANCORE njineli yematshini ngandlela ithile ephelele iba ngomnye wamandla obuhle achazayo kuluntu lwe-Omarchy. Uvelise inani elimangalisayo lemixholo, kuquka i-Last Horizon kunye ne-Solitude, ethunyelwa ngokungagqibekanga kwi-Omarchy. Ngoku unceda ukulawula i-ecosystem yeeplagi ejikeleze i-Quattro. I-Omarchy ihlala inyanzelisa ukuba ikhompyutha kufuneka ibe ntle kwaye ibe luncedo, kwaye i-HANCORE ityhale elo siseko kude kunaye nawuphi na omnye.

Spencer Bull yi-Distinguished Engineer e-Dell esebenza ekuvumelweni komthengi we-Linux, i-AI, kunye nesoftware yeqonga. Ebhuloho yethu kwinyani emdaka yehardware entsha, enceda i-Omarchy isebenze kakuhle kwi-Panther Lake nakoomatshini be-XPS bamva nje ngaphambi kokuba ukulibaziseka kwe-Linux okuqhelekileyo kuqalise. Iingxaki ze-Kernel, iidrivers, imiboniso, i-Wi-Fi, izithethi, ulawulo lwamandla: u-Spencer usebenza kumaleko apho isoftware idibana nee-athomu, kwaye ukuba nobo buchule ngaphakathi kwiQela leCore kubaluleke kakhulu.
