I-Omarchy inyanzelisa ukuba ikhompyutha kufuneka ibe ntle njengoko inemveliso. Esi siseko sithwalwe ngabenzi bemixholo kunye namanye amagcisa e-Omarchy ukusukela kusuku lokuqala. Ukwakha iipalethi ezipheleleyo, ngexesha labo, simahla, kungenxa yokuba befuna umatshini wabo ubonakale njengento.

Ngoko siqala i-Omarchy AIR - Amagcisa okuHlala - ukuxhasa ababalaseleyo babo. Indawo yokuhlala yeenyanga ezintandathu yamagcisa ukusebenza ngokukhululekileyo kwimixholo, iiplagi, nayo nantoni na abacinga ukuba i-Omarchy iyidinga ngokulandelayo. Akukho roadmap, akukho tickets. Injongo kukuvumela ubuchule buhambe ngenkxaso ye-Omacom Foundation.

Indawo yokuhlala nganye iza ne-2,500 USD ngenyanga kangangeenyanga ezintandathu ezipheleleyo, i-akhawunti yeetokeni ukuze iiarhente zingaze zime, kunye nokuqondwa ngonaphakade njenge-alumni ye-AIR xa iinyanga ezintandathu ziphelile.

Kukho izihlalo ezifikelela kwezintathu ezifumanekayo ngexesha elinye, kwaye ezibini kuzo sele zithathiwe kweli season yokuqala.

HANCORE inemixholo emibini ethunyelwa ne-Omarchy - i-Solitude kunye ne-Last Horizon - kunye neminye engamashumi amabini ku-iphepha lezongezelelweyo, ngaphezu kwayo nantoni na enikelwe ngomnye umntu: i-Batou, i-Oxo Carbon, i-Velvet Night, i-Rose of Dune, i-Shades of Jade, kwaye iyaqhubeka. Njineli yematshini owaba ngomnye wamandla obuhle achazayo kolu luntu, uhlala ku-Omarchy Core, kwaye unceda ukulawula i-ecosystem yeeplagi.

OldJobobo unoxanduva lwemixholo emithathu ethunyelwa ne-Omarchy - i-Lumon, i-Miasma, kunye ne-Retro 82 - kunye neminye elishumi kwiphepha lezongezelelweyo, ukusuka ku-Batman ukuya ku-City-783 ukuya ku-Sakura Mochi. Usebenza ngeepalethi ezipheleleyo kunesikim secolour esinye, yiyo loo nto imixholo yakhe ibambene kwitheminali, i-Neovim, i-btop, kunye ne-shell endaweni yokubukeka kakuhle kuphela kumfanekiso wesikrini.
