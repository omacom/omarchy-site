An bɛ Emir Beganović ta baara la i n'a fɔ Omarchy Infrastructure ɲɛmɔgɔ! A bɛ daminɛ Novembre 1 i n'a fɔ Omacom Foundation ka baarakɛla waati bɛɛ sabanan, ka fara Krzysztof Wilczyński Kernel kan ni outfoxxed Shell kan.

Emir bɛ a yɛrɛ ka taama Omarchy ma ɲɛfɔ cogo min, o filɛ:

Ne ka Linux kanu bɛ san mugan ni kɔ bɔ. Ne kɛra Ubuntu développeur ye ne kamalenninya waati la ka don jama sigidaw la kosɛbɛ. Ne ka baara yɛ ne ta jamana saba la, ka baara fiabilité ni teliya kan startupw ni scaleupw la ka jɛkuluw ɲɛminɛ. Ne yɛ san wolonwula ɲɔgɔn kɛ Booking.com ka SRE jɛkulu la, ka baara ɲɛminɛ build sistɛmw ni CI infrastructure kan. Labanko la, ne yɛ Isorun jɔ, ne bɛ baara min na microVMw, snapshotw, kɛrɛtigɛli, planbɔli, ni compute bɔli kan. Sisan ne bɛ na ni dɔnniya in ye ne ka kanu fɔlɔ ma.

Min yɛ ne hakili minɛ Emir la tiɲɛ na, o ye TryOmarchy ye. A yɛ Omarchy bureau ta k'a bila navigateur la (a kura jɔli tɛ, a yɛrɛ yɛrɛ), k'a bila a ka boot sekondi dama kɔnɔ CPU infrastructure basigilen kan Cloudflare la. An yɛ kuma daminɛ nin experiment ɲɛnamini in kan, baro jiginna joona laptopw, architecture kuraw, OEMw, ani infrastructure kan an mako bɛ min na ni Omarchy bɛna baara yɔrɔ bɛɛ an b'a fɛ min. An y'a faamu ko an bɛ vision kelen fa.

O de ye Emir bɛ na min ɲɛminɛ.

Infrastructure Omarchy kama kɔrɔ ka bon ni serveurw boli ye. An bɛ min baara ka Omarchy jɔ, a lajɛ, k'a ci, ka sistɛmu to a ka baara ka tiɲɛ an bɛ bonya ka taa hardware ni architecture wɛrɛw kan. A bɛ bɔliw kɛ jigi ye, fili kokuraw kɛ se ye, ka jɛkulu tɔ bɛɛ jusigilan barikama di walasa u ka se ka taa joona.

Sɛgɛsɛgɛli caman bɛ yen! Omarchy ka kan ka baara ka cɛ masin kan min bɛ i ka tabli kan, nka sababu tɛ a ka jɔ yen. TryOmarchy yɛ an bila ka ye min bɛ kɛ ni Linux ɔridinatɛr daminɛ kɛra nɔgɔn ye i n'a fɔ web page dayɛlɛli, ka Linux nɛnɛ bila klik kelen na. An bɛna ye a bɛ taa min.

Aw ni baraka mɔgɔ bɛɛ ma minnu bɛ baara in sara, an bɛ se ka waati di mɔgɔ ɲɔgɔnna Emir ma ka don Omarchy la kosɛbɛ. An ka dɛmɛbagaw b'a to ka experiment faratibaw yɛlɛmɛ baara Sharpe ye, ni mɔgɔ waati bɛɛ ye minnu bilala ka sistɛmu bɛɛ ɲɛnabɔ. An bɛ baraka da u ka dɛmɛ kan.

I bisimila, Emir! An ka Omarchy bila a ka baara yɔrɔ bɛɛ.
