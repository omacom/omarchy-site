Waati sera ka siko baw kɛ. Omarchy Quattro yɛ mɔgɔw bila ka ɔridinatɛr malleable siniɲɛsigi cogoya dɔn, u b'a fɛ (kosɛbɛ!). Sisan a bɛ i n'a fɔ wajibi ye ka siniɲɛsigi in lase mɔgɔ caman ma ka mɔgɔw ni u ka ɔridinatɛrw cɛ jɛɲɔgɔnya yɛlɛmɛ siɲɛ fɔlɔ waati jan kɔnɔ min bɛ i n'a fɔ ban tɛ.

O de kama, ne bɛ Omacom Foundation sigi walasa mission in ka sɔrɔ dafalen, ka mɛn, ka labɛn ka teliya.

Nonprofit fondation in bɛna trademarkw minɛ, infrastructure sara, baara yira, ka open-source projekɛw ni développeurw dɛmɛ Omarchy dalen don minnu kan.

Dɛmɛbaga Fɔlɔ tan ni fila in bɛɛ bɛ 1 million USD don mission in na:

  • Tobi Lütke, Shopify ka CEO
  • Patrick Collison, Stripe ka CEO
  • Michael Dell, Dell Technologies Chairman ni CEO
  • Jack Dorsey, Block Head ni Block Chairman
  • Matthew Prince, Cloudflare ka CEO
  • Brendan Iribe, Sesame ni Oculus Cofondateur
  • Jason Fried, 37signals ka CEO
  • Drew Houston, Dropbox cofondateur ni co-CEO
  • Peter Steinberger, OpenClaw dabaga
  • Brian Armstrong, Coinbase ka CEO
  • Yunjie Dai, TapTap cofondateur
  • Ne yɛrɛ

Founding Corporate Patron ninw bɛɛ bɛ 1 million USD don san o san san saba kɔnɔ walima 1.5 million USD tokenw na, ni kurayali ye ɲɔgɔn sɔnni fɛ:

  • DigitalOcean
  • Meta Superintelligence Labs

Dɛmɛbaga Dawulen ninw bɛɛ bɛ 100,000 USD don mission na:

  • Ryan R. Hughes, Oodle partner
  • Ed Huang, PingCAP cofondateur ni CTO
  • Adrien Treccani, Metaco fondateur
  • Max Schoening, Notion Head of Product

Distinguished Corporate Patron ninw bɛɛ bɛ 100,000 USD don san o san san saba kɔnɔ walima 150,000 USD tokenw na, ni kurayali ye ɲɔgɔn sɔnni fɛ:

  • 1Password
  • 37signals
  • Four Technologies
  • Anthropic
  • Fireworks
  • OpenAI
  • OpenRouter

Dɔnnikɛla 500 ɲɔgɔn fana yɛ 60,000 USD don dɛmɛ dayɛlɛlen fɛ, o bɛ layidu ni dɔnnikɛ bɛɛ lase 18.5 million USD ma.

Nin ye wari hakɛ kabakoma ye, o de la ne b'a laɲini k'a to a ka mɛn waati jan, an ka nafa bɛɛ sɔrɔ a la. Nka i n'a fɔ matofa kabakoma, jigiya voti nafa ka bon layidu ninw bɛ min di.

An bɛna The Year of Linux on the Desktop kiraya kɛ tiɲɛ ye. Fɛn bɛɛ bɛ a nɔ na sisan. Waati sera ka don a la kosɛbɛ!
