Apple bɛ hardware ɲumanw dilan. U tun bɛ o kɛ tuma bɛɛ (ka o kelen bɔ butterfly clavier waati la). Mɔgɔ caman minnu bɛ na Omarchy ma, u bɛ bɔ Mac la. U fanba bɛ a fɛ ka masin mara ka sistɛmu bila. O de la an bɛna Asahi y'a daminɛ min, o laban: Omarchy min bɛ baara ka cɛ Apple Silicon kan, ni sigili ɲagami dɔɔnin.

O baara in bɛ jɛkulu dɔ bolo ka ban. Bi an b'a kɛ sariya ye: Omarchy M, an ka jɛkulu Macw kama Apple Silicon fɛ (ni mɔgɔ dɔw faralen a kan minnu bɛ Mac kɔrɔlenw mara).

Aw ka mɔgɔw dɔn.

Installer. Marcelo Alcantara bɛ Mac installer natif jɔ. I bɛ aplikɛsɛn dɔ jigi macOS kan, a bɛ Omarchy bɔli signelen ta, a bɛ partitionw tigɛ, k'i bila Omarchy la i ka macOS kɔrɔ kɛrɛfɛ. USB clé tɛ, kuma ɲagaminen tɛ terminal la. Ale fana yɛ build in tigɛ min yɛ écran extérrw manamana USB-C fɛ.

Omarchy Mac. Naeem Malik yɛ Omarchy Mac ladon san kelen, ka Omarchy lase M1 ni M2 masinw ma sani nin bɛɛ ka kɛ sariya ye, ka Discord jama dɔ jɔ mɔgɔ 800 ni kɔ ni o ye. Scott Jones ye projeki in tila filanen ye sigili Linux fan fɛ. A yɛ M3 Air ka écran baara Apple ka hardware display controller kan, o yɛ masin in ta ka bɔ baarakɛbaliya la ka na baarakɛli la su kelen kɔnɔ.

Try Omarchy. Eduardo Martinez yɛ Try Omarchy jɔ: Omarchy bureau yɛrɛ min bɛ baara i n'a fɔ aplikɛsɛn natif, hardware-acceleré i ka Mac kan, ni i ka kamera, mankan, presse-papier, ni dossier tilalen ye minnu sirilen don. Foyi tɛ ka partition tigɛ. A jigi dɔrɔn k'a ye mankan in ye mun ye.

Packagew. Shun Li b'a lajɛ ko fɛn bɛɛ Omarchy mako bɛ min na, o bɛ yen i n'a fɔ package aarch64, ni ARM64 disque jaw ye masin kɔnɔnaw kama ani guest baarakɛminɛnw minnu b'u nɔgɔya ka baara.

Driverw ni kernel. Ryan Murray bɛ Neo wuli ka jusigilanw jɔ jɛkulu tɔ bɛ baara minnu kan. A bɛ SPTM emulator dɔ yiriwa min b'a to ka macOS tracer M4 ni chip kuraw kan. Trace ninw mako ka bon ka écranw, GPUw, mankan, wireless, ni Bluetooth baara. Dj bɛ GPU driverw sɛbɛn, ka Touch ID baara ni kuma ye Secure Enclave fɛ kɔnɔ. Miguel Cruz ye M5 baara kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen ye: a yɛ Apple ka chip N1 reverse-engineer ka a ka Wi-Fi ni Bluetooth baara Linux kan. A yɛ hardware video encoding ni decoding, Bluetooth, ani bureau ɲɛtaa dɔw fana di Neo kan. Liam bɛ Neo ka trackpad, fanga ɲɛnabɔ, mankanbɔlanw, ni casque bɔli wuli, ni Ryan ka macOS tracew ye minnu ye jusigilan kolomaw. Eryk Wieliczko bɛ M4 dɛmɛ ni clean-room baaraw ladon, ka baara intégration ni packaging kan. Jaidip Subedi yɛ écran extérrw bila USB-C fɛ M2 MacBook Air kan.

MLX. Joshua Warren bɛ mlx-omarchy jɔ, min bɛ Apple ka MLX machine learning framework lase Linux ma Apple GPUw kan Vulkan fɛ. I ka kodi bɛ import mlx.core as mx fɔ hali, nka sisan a bɛ baara Omarchy kan.

Mac kɔrɔlenw. Shawn Yeager ka pull request caman bɛ 2016 ni 2017 Touch Bar MacBook Prow kama: mankanbɔlanw, Wi-Fi, lumière capteur, Touch Bar yɛrɛ. Randy bɛ T2 lakana chip reverse-engineer ka Touch ID lase Intel Macw ma, ka ɲɛnabɔli kelen-kelen bɛɛ sɛbɛn 2019 MacBook Pro kama sira la. Jon Kinney tun bɛ ARM dɛmɛ ɲini ka ban Omarchy kama san kelen bɔlen, sisan a bɛ jɛkulu bɛɛ to ɲɔgɔn kan min bɛ min kɛ.

ɲɔgɔn fɛ, u ye jɛkulu barikama ye. Mɔgɔ tan ni duuru minnu ma dɛmɛ makoɲini, minnu bɛ baara ni agentw ye ka taa teliya na min tun bɛna kɛ hakili la ko ye san kelen bɔlen.

Bɔli fɔlɔ laɲini ye bɛn dafalen ye M1 ni M2 fɛ, Pro ni Max suguyaw fara a kan:

  • Installer nɔgɔn, ni sigili ni lajɛli sugandiliw
  • GPU dɛmɛ dafalen
  • Écran extérrw USB-C fɛ
  • Touch ID
  • MLX
  • Disque cryptage

Masin kuraw baarana ɲɔgɔn fɛ, nka M1 ni M2 ye an bɛ drapo turu min na fɔlɔ. Ka fara M masinw kan, laptop kɔrɔlen million caman bɛ yen, u fanba lafiɲɛlen Apple ka macOS laɲini kuraw ma, u kelen-kelen bɛɛ ye Omarchy ɔridinatɛr ɲuman ye min bɛ makoɲini.

I mako tɛ ka bɔli laban kɔnɔ ka daminɛ. Try Omarchy bɛna i to i ka bureau yɛrɛ lajɛli la i ka Mac kan miniti dama kɔnɔ. Ni i labɛnnen don ka don a la, installer bɛna Omarchy bila macOS kɛrɛfɛ M1 walima M2 kan bi.

Laban, deli dɔ. Ni i bɛ baara Linux kan Apple hardware kama, o ye kernel driverw ye, firmware, packaging, installerw, walima machine learning, an b'a fɛ k'i ka kuma mɛn. Se bɛ a ye ko i yɛ min ɲɛnabɔ an bɛ min ɲini hali, walima a wɛrɛ fɛ. Tugu apple@omarchy.org la.

An ka Asahi y'a daminɛ min, o laban.
