Omacom Foundation bɛrɛ tɛmɛna 13 million USD kan! An ka Dɛmɛbaga Dawulen naani fɔlɔw yɛ 100,000 USD layidu ta kelen-kelen mission kama:

Ryan R. Hughes mako tɛ a yɛrɛ yira yan. A ye Omarchy Core mɔgɔ ye, ne wingman distro kan kabini don fɔlɔ, ani Oodle partner. Sisan a bɛ fondation dɛmɛ n'a ka wari yɛrɛ ye fana. O ye dannaya ye!

Ed Huang ye PingCAP cofondateur ni CTO ye, entreprise min bɛ TiDB kɔfɛ, distributed SQL database. A yɛ san tan kɛ k'a jira ko open-source infrastructure seri bɛ se ka kɛ business seri fana ye.

Adrien Treccani yɛ Metaco founded, Suisse digital-asset custody plateforme Ripple yɛ min san. Crypto ɲɛmɔgɔ wɛrɛ min farala mission na ka Omarchy kɛ yɔrɔ ɲuman ye crypto kama.

Max Schoening ye Notion Head of Product ye, sani o ɲɛ a tun bɛ design ɲɛminɛ Heroku la ka kɛ VP of Design ye GitHub la. A yɛ baarakɛminɛn caman dilan développeurw bɛ minnu kanu ka tɛmɛ mɔgɔ fanba bɛna min dɔn kan.

Ni Dɛmɛbaga Fɔlɔ tan ni filaw ni an ka Distinguished Corporate Patron filaw ye, olu ye dɛmɛbaga tan ni seegin ye ni 13 million USD layidu tara dɔgɔkun fila jukɔrɔ.

Aw bisimila, naani bɛɛ. Mission bɛ bonya!
