Omarchy ISO jigili ba kɛmɛ dɔgɔkun tɛmɛnen kɔnɔ! Mɔgɔ ba kɛmɛ minnu yɛ latigɛ ko dɔgɔkun in ye u bɛna Linux lajɛ (walima u bɛna segin a ma). Boss caman!

Bandwidth fan fɛ, an yɛ petabyte kelen ɲɔgɔn ci don wolonwula kelenw kɔnɔ. Linux terabyte ba kelen dɔgɔkun kelen kɔnɔ ISOw ni package kuraw cɛ!

Nin tun bɛna Omarchy bin waati wɛrɛ la tiɲɛ na. A tun bɛna an bila ka mirrorw walima torrentw ɲini (k'an hakili to ka mortgage wɛrɛ mako ɲɛ so kan ka hosting facturi sara). Nka an mako tɛ o la.

O sababu ye ko Cloudflare bɛ o bɛɛ ɲɛfɛ: ISOw, Omarchy packagew, an ka Arch mirror. O bɛɛ bɛ R2 kan u ka CDN kɔfɛ. U si ma wɔsi bɔ. O de la mɔgɔ ba kɛmɛ yɛ jigili teliyalen, ɲɛnamini tɛ, ko tɛ sɔrɔ diɲɛ yɔrɔ bɛɛ dɔgɔkun tɛmɛnen kɔnɔ.

Aw ni baraka Cloudflare mɔgɔ bɛɛ ma, ani Matthew Prince ma kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenya na. Matthew ye Dɛmɛbaga Fɔlɔw dɔ fana ye, o kɔrɔ ye ko a bɛ projeki in dɛmɛ ni infrastructure ye n'a ka wari yɛrɛ ye. Muchas gracias, señor!
