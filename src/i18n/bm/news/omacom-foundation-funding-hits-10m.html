Omacom Foundation ka sɔrɔ bɛrɛ sera 10 million USD ma ni Dɛmɛbaga Fɔlɔ kura fila farali ye mission na: Drew Houston, Dropbox cofondateur ni co-CEO, ani Peter Steinberger, OpenClaw dabaga.

I n'a fɔ Dɛmɛbaga Fɔlɔ segin kɔnɔntɔnnaw, Drew ni Peter bɛɛ bɛ 1 million USD don. O bɛ dɛmɛbaga tan di fondation ma ani DOLAR MILLION TAN saniyalen ka an ka mission faratiba ɲɛtaa ka Linux na desktop la cogo belebele la.

A ka di kosɛbɛ ka Drew ni Peter sɔrɔ jɛkulu la sabu ne yɛrɛ tun ye u fila bɛɛ ni u ka kɛwale fanba ye. Ne ye Dropbox kiliyan nisɔndiya ye san mugan ɲɔgɔn, a ye fɛn koloma ye ne bɛ se ka min fɛ ka to ni no backup, no cry ye ka ɔridinatɛrw falen-falen tuma bɛɛ.

O de la Omarchy bɛ na ni Dropbox jɛɲɔgɔnya ɲuman ye kabini bɔli la. Taa Install > Service > Dropbox la, i bɛna fichier ɲɛnabɔlan jɛɲɔgɔnya sɔrɔ ni menu panel kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen ye komandiw kama.

OpenClaw yɛ sira yira Omarchy ka kan a bonya teliyalen ɲɛnabɔ cogo min. I bɛ PR kɔfɛlatɔnw ɲɛnabɔ cogo di minnu jiginna joona? Lakana mun pluginw fan fɛ? ɲɔgɔnbɛnw ka kan ka ɲɛnabɔ cogo di projeki tɔgɔ la? Ne yɛ nisɔndiya sɔrɔ ka ban ka Peter ka ladilikan ni dɔnniya ta liki in kama dɔw kan.

Ka tɛmɛ o kan, u fila bɛɛ ye tekniki laɲini suguya yɛrɛ ye ne b'a fɛ Omarchy dafɛ min. Drew yɛ a yɛrɛ ka ɲinini kɛra Dropbox ye ka fichyew jigin ɔridinatɛrw cɛ. Peter yɛ weekend projeki yɛlɛmɛ OpenClaw ye, ka bɛɛ hakili to a la ko ɔridinatɛr minnu bɛ mɔgɔ yɛrɛ ta ye, minnu bɛ se ka program, ka nisɔndiya, olu mako ka bon cogo min.

O hakili de bɛ Omacom Foundation kɔfɛ fana. An bɛ yan ka infrastructure, open-source projekɛw, ani développeurw sara minnu mako ɲɛ ka Linux bureau kɛ so ɲuman ye ordinatɛri baarakɛla nataw kama.

Aw bisimila, Drew ni Peter. Dɛmɛbaga Fɔlɔ tan. Dolar million tan. Kiraya bɛrɛ surunyana ni 2 million USD wɛrɛ ye!
