Omacom Foundation bisimili ma kɛ foyi ye ka tɛmɛ kabako kan. Dɛmɛbaga Fɔlɔ tan ni fila, Dɛmɛbaga Dawulen naani, Distinguished Corporate Patron fila, ni 13 million USD layidu tara mission kama dɔgɔkun fila jukɔrɔ.

Nka fondation tɛ Oligarchy ni Corporate Patronw dɔrɔn ta ye. A ye bɛɛ ta ye. Mɔgɔ o mɔgɔ min bɛ se ka bila Linux ɲuman, tulonkɛ, ni agentw ta kɔfɛ.

O de la kabini bi, dɛmɛ dayɛlɛlen don bɛɛ ma. Hakɛ naani bɛ yen: 16 USD, 256 USD, 2,048 USD, ani 8,192 USD. Bitw ka fanga, tiɲɛ na! Dɛmɛbaga kelen-kelen bɛɛ ye mission kelen dɔ ye, dolar kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ fɛn kelen san: seko yɛrɛ ka Linux on the Desktop kiraya dafa!

Dɛmɛbaga kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ sɛbɛn sɔrɔ ɛntɛrɛnɛti kan k'a jira. Hakɛ kelen-kelen bɛɛ ka badge, karti, ni fond d'écranw bɛ yen, an ka Artist in Residence OldJobobo yɛ minnu dilan. Classe 016 b'i ta Kungo Stage fɛ. Classe 8K bɛ Su Stage boli. I ka classe sugandi! Cogoya wɛrɛw bɛ na pankɛbaga wɛrɛw fɛ joona.

Wari in bɛ baara gɛlɛn kɔnɔ ka ban. Fondation ye Hyprland dɛmɛbaga kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen ye ni Quickshell ni mise dɛmɛbaga premier ye, saba ye Omarchy jɔlen don minnu kan. A bɛ Pankɛbagaw minnu bɛ sigiyɔrɔ la sara minnu bɛ distro cɛɲa. A bɛ fitiniw manamana infrastructure bɛɛ kama Linux distribution mako bɛ min na. A bɛ plugin competitionw boli, ni 10,000 USD kura nata ye min ɲɛmɔgɔw ka ca ni fɔlɔ ye. Kabini bi, a ka baarakɛla waati bɛɛ fɔlɔ bɛ yen: Linux kernel développeur min bɛ baara ɲɛminɛ Omarchy Kernel kan. O bɛɛ caman bɛ na!

Sabu wari in tɛ bɔna ka Iron Bank daminɛ. An laɲini ye ka wari min bɔra san in, o bɛɛ kɛ musaka ye san saba nataw kɔnɔ. Dɛmɛbaga caman, musaka caman. Bɛɛ bɛ se ka fara nin kan.

Kɛ dɛmɛbaga ye bi.
