Omacom Foundation bɛrɛ yɛ a ka baarakɛla waati bɛɛ fɔlɔ ta! Krzysztof Wilczyński bɛ fara an kan ka an ka baara ɲɛminɛ Omarchy Kernel kan. Nin de ye fondation sigira min kama: ka mɔgɔ ɲumanw sara ka baara gɛlɛya kanw kan bɛɛ kama.

Krzysztof ye Linux kernel développeur kɔrɔ ye. A bɛ PCI Endpoint sistɛmu ladon ni tɔw ye ni PCI host bridge natif ni endpoint driverw ye. O ye tuyau ye min bɛ Linux tugu i ka GPU ma, i ka NVMe disque ma, i ka WiFi karti ma. A ye reviewer ye kernel ka Rust PCI baara kura kan fana. Sani a ka don kernel la kosɛbɛ, a yɛ san caman kɛ systems engineering la, ka baara Kubernetes ni container runtimew kan.

Sisan a bɛna na ni dɔnniya o bɛɛ ye Omarchy Kernel ma. Laɲini: teliya, bɛnni, ani lakana. An b'a fɛ Linux ka panni laptop kuraw ni ThinkPad kɔrɔlenw bɛɛ kan. An b'a fɛ a ka baara kabini bɔli la masin caman kan. An b'a fɛ a datugulen ka gɛlɛn.

Krzysztof bɛ fara Omarchy Core fana kan, o de la a bɛna dɛmɛ don ka distro bɛɛ sira bila.

I bisimila, Krzysztof! An ka kernel kɛ a ka kule!
