Omacom Foundation yɛ dolar 500,000 wɛrɛ bɔ! Ni token sɔrɔ kura ye, entreprise layidu san saba ye, ani dolar ba caman dɛmɛ dayɛlɛlen na, o ye 510,000 USD ɲɔgɔn ye min layidu tara Linux ɲuman, tulonkɛ, ni agentw ta kama. O bɛ an ka layidu ni dɔnnikɛ bɛɛ lase 15.5 million USD ma!

Fɔlɔ, an bɛ OpenRouter bisimila i n'a fɔ Distinguished Corporate Patron ni 150,000 USD tokenw na. Ni o bɔra ka ban, an bɛ se ka bɛn kura kɛ ɲɔgɔn sɔnni fɛ. Token ninw bɛna agentw bila baara la minnu bɛ Omarchy jɔ, a lajɛ, k'a ladon. An bɛ se ka agentw bila baara la ka bugw ɲɛnabɔ, ka dɔnnikɛw lajɛ, ka distro ɲɛnabɔ, o bɛna an to ka mɔgɔ bɛɛ ka ɔridinatɛr ɲɛnabɔ joona. Tulu wɛrɛ mission kama!

An bɛna dolar million caman tokenw na minnu dɔnna, olu bila OpenRouter plateforme ɲuman fɛ fana. Walasa an ka se ka token ɲumanw tila jɛkulu ɲumanw ma model ɲumanw na.

O kɔfɛ Four Technologies bɛ yen, a bɛ fara Distinguished Corporate Patron kan ni layidu ye 100,000 USD san o san san saba kɔnɔ. O ye 300,000 USD ye ka jama projekɛw dɛmɛ Omarchy dalen don minnu kan, infrastructure min b'a to a ka baara, ani baara ka Linux ɲuman lase mɔgɔ caman ma. Ni entreprisew bɛ layidu ta sanw kama, o b'an to an ka se ka o kelen kɛ.

Four Technologies jɛkulu b'a fɔ cogo min, o filɛ:

An ye AI-forward fintech entreprise ye, entreprisew i n'a fɔ Four Technologies bɛ jɔ cogo min, o bɛ se sɔnni open-source infrastructure mɔgɔ wɛrɛw yɛ min jɔ fɔlɔ. Ka Omacom Foundation dɛmɛ, o de ye an ka wari bila o kɔfɛ cogo ye. An b'a miiri ko AI etapa nata tɛ aplikɛsɛn wɛrɛ ye min bɛ bila sistɛmu kan, AI ye min jɔlen don sistɛmu kɔnɔ kɔnɔ, o de ye pari Omarchy bɛ min ta. An tun b'a fɛ ka fara jama min bɛ o pari ta, olu kan, sɔnni ka nafa sɔrɔ a la kɔfɛ dɔrɔn.

Kabini an yɛ dɛmɛ dayɛlɛ bɛɛ ma, an yɛ 60,000 USD ɲɔgɔn sɔrɔ dɔnnikɛla 500 ɲɔgɔn fɛ dɛmɛ dayɛlɛlen fɛ. O ye jaabi kabakoma ye mɔgɔ minnu b'a fɛ ka dɛmɛ don nin ka kɛ. Dɔnnikɛ kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ fara min an bɛ se k'o kɛ ɲɔgɔn fɛ, o kan.

Tokenw ka jɔ ni ye. Wari ka projekɛw dɛmɛ an dalen don minnu kan. Mɔgɔw ni entreprisew minnu sɔnna ka fɛn dɔ bila u ka dannaya kɔfɛ Linux on the desktop la. O cogo la an bɛ to ka taa!

Aw ni baraka OpenRouter, Four Technologies, ani mɔgɔ o mɔgɔ min kɛra dɛmɛbaga ye. Ni i b'a fɛ ka fara u kan, sɛbɛn ci david@omarchy.org ma dɛmɛ fɔlɔ ni dawulen kama, ka dɔnni kɛ kɔnɔ dɛmɛ dayɛlɛlen na.
