Omacom Foundation yɛ a ka Entreprisew Dɛmɛbaga Dawulen fɔlɔ fila sɔrɔ! 1Password ni 37signals bɛɛ bɛ 100,000 USD don san o san san saba nataw kɔnɔ, o bɛ fondation ka sɔrɔ lase 12.6 million USD ma.

1Password tun bɛ Omarchy la kabini don fɔlɔ (ani Omakub la sani o ɲɛ!). Sabu a yɛ ne ka sɛbɛn dogolenw lakana san caman kɔnɔ sani Linux ma kɛ ɲɛjibɔn ye ne ɲɛ na.

I n'a fɔ Dropbox, a ye baarakɛminɛn kolomaw dɔ ye ka no backup, no cry fɛɛrɛ bila sen kan. Ni ne bɛ masin kura sigi waati o waati (min ye siɲɛ saba ye dɔgɔkun kɔnɔ bi!), fɛn fɔlɔ ne bɛ min kɛ, o ye Install > Service > 1Password ye. O kɔfɛ ne ka agentw sigili, donni Basecamp ni HEY la, ani tɔw bɛɛ ye nɔgɔn ye. Nka lakana na, lakana kosɛbɛ.

37signals kɛra Omarchy falen yɔrɔ ye sani a ka tɔgɔ sɔrɔ. An yɛ tekniki jɛkulu bɛɛ yɛlɛmɛ ka na Omarchy ma ka ban, an bɛ facturi suguya bɛɛ sara sira kan ka taa Quattro ma (ani sani o ɲɛ). Sisan dɛmɛ in bɛ sariya kɔnɔ, fondation fɛ, sariya kelenw na i n'a fɔ bɛɛ.

A nafa ka bon ka a fɔ fana ko entreprisew ka wari ni mɔgɔ kelen-kelen ka wari bɛɛ bɛ fɛn kelen san: tɔgɔbɔli mission dɔ la. Sarakabɔ tɛ yan.

Omarchy bɛna a ka latigɛw to tuma bɛɛ min bɛ OS malleable ɲumanba dilan diɲɛ na. O ɲɛfɔlen don kosɛbɛ ni “by DHH” tɔgɔ ye ni i bɛ sistɛmu sigi. Sani nin ka kɛ sistɛmu kabakoma ye bɛɛ kama, a ka kan ka kɛ sistɛmu kabakoma ye ne kama fɔlɔ, ne yɛrɛ. I ka masque don fɔlɔ ani o ɲɔgɔnnaw!

I b'a fɛ i ka entreprise ka fara sistɛmu yɛlɛmani tarikiba in na? Sɛbɛn ci david@omarchy.org ma.
