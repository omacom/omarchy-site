Nin ye seko ba ye Linux kama. Bureau bɛ se ka minɛ cogo min a tun tɛ se san tan caman kɔnɔ. Agentw yɛ sistɛmu kɛ malleable ye. Hardware ma ɲɛ abada. Ɲɔgɔndanw ma u ka baarakɛlaw tɔɔrɔ mako ɲɛ abada. An tɛna waati in tɛmɛ!

O de la Omacom Foundation bɛ a ka musaka laɲiniw teliya. Wari min bɔra san in, o bɛɛ bɛna kɛ musaka ye san saba nataw kɔnɔ. A tɛna bila endowment la. A tɛna bɔ dɔɔnin-dɔɔnin san tan kɔnɔ. A bɛna kɛ musaka ye!

Ni 13 million USD layidu tara ka ban, o kɔrɔ ye ko san o san budgetw bɛna tɛmɛ 4 million USD kan 2027, 2028, ani 2029. Dɔ ka fara a kan ni wari donna dɛmɛbagaw fɛ suguya bɛɛ. Fɔlɔw, Dawulenw, Entreprisew, ani mɔgɔ o mɔgɔ min farala mission na bi.

Wari in taara open-source projekɛw ma Omarchy jɔlen don minnu kan. Pankɛbagaw ma minnu b'a cɛɲa. Développeurw ma minnu b'a teliya, ka bɛn, ka lakana. Competitions, dɔnnikɛw, infrastructure ma. Fɛn bɛɛ ma a mako ɲɛ ka Linux on the Desktop kiraya dafa.

Wari tɛ laɲini ye. Mission de ye. Nka a bɛ dɛmɛ kosɛbɛ ka fila bɛɛ sɔrɔ!
