Update, Septembre 9, 2026: Dɛmɛ in tɛmɛna outfoxxed farali fɛ Omacom Foundation kan waati bɛɛ i n'a fɔ Omarchy Shell ɲɛmɔgɔ ni Omarchy Core mɔgɔ.

Wari wɛrɛ donna, wari wɛrɛ bɔra! An ka dɛmɛba filanen bɔra Omacom Foundation la ka taa outfoxxed ma a ka baara ɲuman kosɔn Quickshell kan. A bɔra kɛnɛ kan dɔgɔkun kelen dɔrɔn, nka a jɛlen don ko o de tun ye yɔrɔ dɔgɔlen koloma ye an ka The Malleable Computer sɔrɔ tiɲɛ na.

Seere bɛ pluginw na! Plugin ba kelen ni kɔ dara dɔgɔkun kɔnɔ Quattro bɔra. O ye seere kabakoma ye Quickshell seko yɛlɛmani kan, ani agentw bɛ QML kodi sɛbɛnni kanu cogo min.

Dɛmɛ in bɛ outfoxxed sara a ka dɔnnikɛ dawulen kosɔn Omarchy la, ka jɛɲɔgɔnya siri an ka plan janw na Agentw ka Waati kama (kana o to Bluesky kan, u bɛna fa!). A bɛnna san saba kama kabini daminɛ.

Aw ni baraka kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, ni Peter Steinberger ma ka nin se ka kɛ. Ni Oligarchy tun tɛ, an tun tɛna se ka open-source développeurw sara nin cogo la!
