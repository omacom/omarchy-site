Omarchy b'a fɔ ko ɔridinatɛr ka kan ka cɛ i n'a fɔ a bɛ baara ɲuman. Landa in taara cogoya dabagaw ni Omarchy pankɛbaga tɔw fɛ kabini don fɔlɔ. Ka palette dafalenw jɔ, u yɛrɛ ka waati la, gansan, sabu u tun b'a fɛ u ka masin ka kɛ fɛn dɔ ye.

O de la an bɛ Omarchy AIR daminɛ - Artists in Residence - ka u la ɲumanw dɛmɛ. Kalo wɔɔrɔ sigiyɔrɔ pankɛbagaw ye ka baara u yɛrɛma cogoyaw, pluginw, ani tɔw bɛɛ kan u b'a miiri ko Omarchy mako bɛ min na nata la. Roadmap tɛ, ticket tɛ. Laɲini ye ka dabali bila a ka bɔ ni Omacom Foundation ka dɛmɛ ye.

Sigiyɔrɔ kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ na ni 2,500 USD ye kalo o kalo kalo wɔɔrɔ bɛɛ kɔnɔ, token konti walasa agentw mako kana kɛ u ka jɔ, ani tɔgɔbɔli banbali i n'a fɔ AIR kɔfɛmɔgɔ ni kalo wɔɔrɔ banna.

Sigiyɔrɔ saba bɛ se ka sɔrɔ waati kelen na, fila bɛɛ minɛna saison fɔlɔ in na.

HANCORE ka cogoya fila bɛ na ni Omarchy ye - Solitude ni Last Horizon - mugan wɛrɛw extras page kan, ka tɛmɛ mɔgɔ tɔ bɛɛ ka dɔnnikɛ kan: Batou, Oxo Carbon, Velvet Night, Rose of Dune, Shades of Jade, a bɛ to ka taa. A ye mecanique ingénieur ye min kɛra cɛɲa fanga dawulen dɔ ye jama in na, a sigilen don Omarchy Core kan, ka dɛmɛ don ka plugin ecosystem ɲɛnabɔ.

OldJobobo ye cogoya saba dabaga ye minnu bɛ na ni Omarchy ye - Lumon, Miasma, ni Retro 82 - tan wɛrɛw fara a kan extras page kan, kabini Batman ka se City-783 ni Sakura Mochi ma. A bɛ baara palette dafalenw na sɔnni kulɛr sugandili kelen-kelenw, o de la a ka cogoyaw bɛ jɔ ɲɔgɔn fɛ terminal, Neovim, btop, ani shell fan bɛɛ sɔnni u ka cɛ screenshot dɔrɔn na.

Sigiyɔrɔ laban ye weleli fɛ. Porogaramu bɛ daminɛ Octobre.

Ne bɛ Vitruvius ka sariya saba fɛ architecture ɲuman kama: firmitas, utilitas, venustas. A ka kan ka jɔ, a ka kan ka nafa kɛ, a ka kan ka cɛ. Fila tɛ saba la. An bɛna wariba don o bɛɛ la. An bɛ se ka fɛn bɛɛ ɲɛnabɔ.
