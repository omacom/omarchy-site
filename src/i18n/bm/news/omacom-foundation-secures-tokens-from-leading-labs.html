Omarchy tɛ Linux distribution ɲuman, tulonkɛ, ni agentw ta ye dɔrɔn mɔgɔ minnu bɛna a baara. A jɔlen don o cogo la fana! Ni cɛɲa ye, nisɔndiya, ani token caman. Tiɲɛ na, Omarchy Quattro jɔra ni agentw ye dɔrɔn. Sabu u ka ɲi kosɛbɛ fɛnw na i n'a fɔ bash, QML, ani Linux ko ɲɛnabɔli.

Ka to ka Omarchy ɲɛnabɔ sisan an yɛ baarakɛla ni cocreateur ba caman sama, an mako bɛ tokenw na ka to ka bɔ. O cogo la an bɛ teliya, ka ɲɛtaa, ka dɔnnikɛ kabakoma bɛɛ dafa minnu bɛ bɔna. An ka PR 1,600 ni kɔ kɔfɛlatɔn bɛ an bolo ka ban bɔli kɔfɛ dɔgɔkun dama! Mɔgɔ si tɛna tɛmɛ o la ni agent dɛmɛ tɛ.

O de la, ne nisɔndiyalen don kosɛbɛ k'a lasɔmi ko an yɛ token ba caman sɔrɔ labo baw fanba fɛ mission in kama! Fɔlɔ ye Meta Superintelligence Labs ye min bɛ na i n'a fɔ Founding Token Patron ni 1.5m USD tokenw na! U ka Meta Muse Spark model kura bɛ jateden ɲumanw bila sɔngɔ kabakoma na. O de la 1.5m USD ye token banbaliw ye! (An bɛna u ban hali!)

Nka an nisɔndiyalen don fana ka frontier labo ɲɛmɔgɔ fila wɛrɛw bisimila, Anthropic ni OpenAI, i n'a fɔ Distinguished Token Patronw ni 150,000 USD token dɔnnikɛ ye kelen-kelen. Ninw bɛna nafa kɛ kosɛbɛ ni lakana sɛbɛn farala a kan min mako ɲɛ ka guardrailw bɔ an mako bɛ minnu na lakana ɲinini ni ɲɛnabɔliw kama.

Open-weight modelw kama, ne nisɔndiyalen don ka Fireworks lasɔmi Distinguished Token Patron ye fana ni 150,000 USD tokenw layidu tara. Ne ye Kimi K modelw baarakɛla ye tuma caman, kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenya na fast mode la. An bɛna token teliyalen ninw bila baara ɲuman na fana!

ɲɔgɔn fɛ, nin ye 1.95 million USD wɛrɛ ye layidu tara Omacom Foundation ye. O bɛ an ka layidu bɛɛ lase 14.95 million USD ma. Jateden hakili la ko ye ka sɛbɛn distro kama mɔgɔ minnu tun b'a mafiyɛ fɔlɔ u yɛ min fɔ dotfiles ye (min tiɲɛ na tun tɛ fili ye waati o waati, nka an yɛ to ka fɛn bɛɛ ɲɛnabɔ!).

Ni token ninw bɛɛ ye, an bɛ se ka fɛn bɛɛ ɲɛnabɔ. O de ye an bɛna min kɛ!
