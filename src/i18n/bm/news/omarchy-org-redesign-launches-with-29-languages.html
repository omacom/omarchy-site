Siti min ye Linux distribution ɲuman, tulonkɛ ta ye, o ka kan ka cɛ ka nisɔndiya fana. O de la an yɛ Omarchy.org cogoya yɛlɛmɛ dafalen, an b'a daminɛ kan 29 na (sabu ni i bɛrɛ yɛ 2m USD ɲɔgɔn bɔ tokenw na, mun na tɛ?). Retro ɲɛnamini dɔɔnin, fɛn caman ka sɛgɛsɛgɛ, bisimili belebele diɲɛ ma.

Siti in b'a fɛ i ka tulon kɛ n'i ka ɔridinatɛr ye! Pixelw, kulɛrw, animationw bɛ Omarchy hakili ta kɔnɔ ka don navigateur la. I bɛ se ka yɔrɔ in dɔn sani i ka ISO kelen jigi. A ye ne yɛrɛ ka jɛɲɔgɔnya ye san formatifw na ɛntɛrɛnɛti ɲɛ online diɲɛ la ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, ani 90s cɛɲa ɲɛnamini. Kulɛr caman, manamana dɔɔnin. Pixel caman, gradient mɔnɛ dɔɔnin.

Sisan yɔrɔ bɛ yen ka éditeurw, terminalw, agentw, tulonw, Windows VM, ni hardware yira minnu bɛ Omarchy kɛ nafamaw ye don o don. Jama ka cogoyaw lajɛ, plugin dɔ sɔrɔ, workstation yɛrɛw ye, walima ɲɔgɔnbɛn dɔ sɔrɔ i dafɛ. Mɔgɔ minnu b'a jɔ dɔn ni dɛmɛbagaw minnu b'a sara. An bɛ da dayɛlɛlen sɔrɔ ka ban ni yɔrɔ ye fɛn bɛɛ kama min bɛ kɛ kɔnɔ.

O da dayɛlɛlen bɛ i ka kan fɔ sisan. Arabu, Bengali, Katalan, Danwa, Angilɛ, Filipino, Finilandi, Faransɛ, Greki, Hindi, Hongirwa, Islandi, Irlandi, Itali, Zapɔnɛ, Kore, Litwani, Polɔnɛ, Poritigal, Shinwa nɔgɔnnen, Sinhala, Espaɲɔli, Suɛdi, Tamil, Tayi, Turki, Urdu, Uzbeki, ni Vietnamɛ. Siti kunbaba ni kibaru kelen-kelen bɛɛ bamanankan na. Gafe kunnafoni bɛ Angilɛ la hali; an bɛna o ɲɛnabɔ nata la. Ninw ye bamanankan daminɛw ye, ɲɛnabɔliw bɔra kan fɔlaw fɛ, olu bisimilalen don kosɛbɛ siti repo fɛ.

An ka domain sigidaw bɛ yen fana: omarchy.ae, omarchy.dk, omarchy.fi, omarchy.fr, omarchy.gr, omarchy.hu, omarchy.in, omarchy.is, omarchy.jp, omarchy.kr, omarchy.mx, omarchy.ph, omarchy.pt, omarchy.se, ani omarchy.tr.

Jama mɔgɔw yɛ domain wɛrɛw sɛbɛn ni dusukun ɲuman ye ka dɛmɛ don k'u bila an fan fɛ. An bɛ to ka tɔgɔ sigida tɔw siri, i bɛ se ka bn.omarchy.org, ca.omarchy.org, ga.omarchy.org, it.omarchy.org, lt.omarchy.org, pl.omarchy.org, si.omarchy.org, ta.omarchy.org, th.omarchy.org, ur.omarchy.org, uz.omarchy.org, vi.omarchy.org, ani zh.omarchy.org baara. Globe baara min bɛ cogoya sugandilan kɛrɛfɛ ka tɛmɛ kanw cɛ.

Cogoya yɛlɛmɛli in bɔra baara nisɔndiya ye Omarchy Design jɛkulu la. Barış Girişmen yɛ dilabɛn da min kɛra siti kura jusigilan ye, ka pixel-grid shader jɔ, ka baarali ni manamana fanba ɲɛminɛ. Christoffer Hallas yɛ prototypew, animation baaraw, cogoya yɛlɛmaniw, ni ɲɛtaa sira bila. Daniel Schmier yɛ dilabɛnw ni baarali di logo, kibaruw, sigili, jɛkulu, fondation, ni seere yɔrɔw kama.

Aw ni baraka Andrés Villagrán, Ryan Hughes, Yam Catzenelson, Eric Carmichael, Wes Oudshoorn, Taha, Aloïs Deniel, Adrian Rangel, Niklas Jul, HANCORE, OldJobobo, ani Rick Blalock ma fana miiriyaw, experimentw, hakilinaw, ni jaabiw kosɔn tilalen sira la. Mɔgɔ bɛɛ ma minnu bɛ dɛmɛ don ka domain sigidaw sɔrɔ ka mɔgɔw bisimila u yɛrɛ ka kan na. Nisɔndiya tun ye ka o bɛɛ ye ka fara ɲɔgɔn kan.

O de la cogoya dɔ sugandi. Pixelw sɔgɔsɔgɔ. Ɔridinatɛrw ka kan ka nisɔndiya!
