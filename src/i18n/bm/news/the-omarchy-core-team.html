Omarchy bonya teliyalen mako bɛ jɛkulu sigilenw na jaabi ye, an bɛna daminɛ ka The Omarchy Core Team sigi. Jɛkulu in bɛna an ka distro sira ɲɛminɛli baara tila, k'a lajɛ an ka to ka taa ɲɛ, ka dɛmɛdonbaga tɔw dɛmɛ.

Aw ka Core Team fɔlɔw dɔn:

Ryan Hughes ye ne wingman ye Omarchy kan kabini don fɔlɔ. An yɛ 3am emergency AUR outagew kɛlɛ ɲɔgɔn fɛ don fɔlɔw la, an yɛ jɛn ka an yɛrɛ ka package repo jɔ. Ryan ka dɔnnikɛw bɛ distro nɛgɛ bɛɛ kan: sigili, packaging, hardware dɛmɛ, tulon, cogoyaw, CLI baarakɛminɛnw, sisan Quattro. A yɛ dɛmɛ don ka taa Quickshell ma, plugin sistɛmu, ani infrastructure fanba min yɛ Omarchy yɛlɛmɛ ka bɔ Linux sigili labɛnnen na ka kɛ sistɛmu yɛrɛ ye. Ni a tɛ hack kɛ Omarchy kan, Ryan ye partner ye Oodle la.

Tobi Lütke ye mɔgɔ kelen ye min yɛ ne ka jɛkulu kolomaw fila bɛɛ kɛ! I n'a fɔ Rails Core Team fɔlɔ mɔgɔ min daminɛna 2004 la, a yɛ dɔnnikɛ sɛbɛn seri ɲɛminɛ framework la dɔrɔn tɛ, a yɛ Liquid, Delayed Job, ni Active Merchant fana sɛbɛn. O baara in yɛlɛmana ka kɛ entreprise misɛn ye Shopify, i y'u mɛn wa? Sisan a ye dɔnnikɛla caman ye Omarchy la ni projekɛw ye i n'a fɔ try, Omasnap, Wi-Fi QR codew, ani Quake-style agent console. A ye ne co-driver ye fana #11 Shopify Racing LMP2 mobili la min bɛ kɔrɔtɔ IMSA la.

Bjarne Øverli ye Unite AS ka CTO ye ni Aether dabaga ye, cogoya jɔlan yelen min an bɛ ci Omarchy la, ni cliamp ye, an ka TUI dɔnkili folan. A yɛ dɛmɛ don ka an ka cogoya sistɛmu yɛlɛmɛ ka bɔ config fichye ɲɔgɔn na ka kɛ fɛn systématique ye, ni kulɛr semanticw ni cogoya dilanenw ye Neovim, VS Code, btop, ani stack tɔ fan bɛɛ. A ye cogoya ɲɔgɔnna Ethereal, Hackerman, ni Lupine kuntigi ye fana. Bjarne ka programmer seri ni ɲɛ cɛɲa ɲagaminen sɔrɔlen don min bɛ bɛn Omarchy ma kosɛbɛ.

HANCORE ye mecanique ingénieur ye min yɛ a sɔrɔ ka kɛ cɛɲa fanga dawulen dɔ ye Omarchy jama la. A yɛ cogoya hakɛ kabakoma da, Last Horizon ni Solitude fara a kan, min bɛ ci Omarchy la kabini daminɛ. Sisan a bɛ dɛmɛ don ka plugin ecosystem ladon Quattro dafɛ. Omarchy tun b'a fɔ tuma bɛɛ ko ɔridinatɛr ka kan ka cɛ i n'a fɔ a bɛ nafa kɛ, HANCORE yɛ landa in ta ka tɛmɛ mɔgɔ fanba kan.

Spencer Bull ye Distinguished Engineer ye Dell la min bɛ baara Linux client enablement, AI, ni plateforme software kan. A ye an ka pont ye hardware kura tiɲɛ tiɲɛnaw la, ka Omarchy dɛmɛ a ka baara ka ɲɛ Panther Lake ni XPS masin kuraw kan sani Linux lag basigilen ka se ka don. Kernel kow, driverw, écranw, Wi-Fi, mankanbɔlanw, fanga ɲɛnabɔ: Spencer bɛ baara nɛgɛ la software bɛ atome kunbɛn min, ka dɔnniya in sɔrɔ Core Team kɔnɔ, o nafa ka bon kosɛbɛ.

Duuru ninw tun bɛ baara kɛ ka ban. Nin b'a kɛ sariya ye dɔrɔn. Ni ne ye, o ye Core Team fɔlɔ kabakoma ye. Nka a tɛna kɛ jɛkulu kelen ye. To kunnafoni na Omarchy Rangers lasɔmi kama!

ɲɔgɔn fɛ an bɛna Omarchy jɔ ka kɛ Linux distribution premier ye mɔgɔ minnu nisɔndiyalen don agentw ka waati, ɔridinatɛr malleable, ani ordinatɛri ɲuman, kura, miiriya barikama kama.

Ka taa banbali ni kɔfɛ!
