Sira jumɛn ka fisa ka nafolotigiya dɔ bɔ an bɔra Omacom Foundation kama ka tɛmɛ Linux kamalen ɲumanba kan Pologne: Vaxry!

I n'a fɔ Hyprland dabaga, a ye technologie koloma saba dɔ kuntigi ye Omarchy la (Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), ne nisɔndiyalen don ka dɛmɛ kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen lasɔmi min b'a to a ka sinsin a yiriwali kan kosɛbɛ ni a hakili tɛ commerce yiriwali walima waribɔli la.

An bɛ don Vaxry fɛ waati jan kama ka Omarchy siniɲɛsigi jan dɛmɛ. An ka dɛmɛ kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen bɛn in sigira san saba kama, ni sugandili ye ka fila wɛrɛ fara a kan.

Bɛn in b'a to fana Hyprland ka Hyprperks porogaramu kɔrɔ ka bɔ gansan, bɛɛ kama, sabu abonnement saralenw bɛna ban. Nka Hyprland bɛna mɔgɔ kelen-kelen ka dɔnnikɛw minɛ hali ka mɔgɔ kelen-kelen to ka don projeki yiriwali la ka Vaxry ni jɛkulu dɛmɛ.

Aw ni baraka kosɛbɛ 37signals (aw ni ce ne yɛrɛ! 😂), Butterfly, ni Framework ma ka Hyprland dɛmɛ ka se nin yɔrɔ la. Ne nisɔndiyalen don ko Omacom Foundation bɛ se ka ta October 10, ka Linux waati kura développeur ɲuman dɔ bila ka don a la kosɛbɛ.

(* Bɛn in tɛ dɛmɛ kɔnɔ ka Vaxry ka muso sɔrɔli la, nka ne yɛrɛ bɛna sɔrɔ i n'a fɔ seere ɲininkali o ɲininkali kan.)
