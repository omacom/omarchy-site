Dɛmɛ sabanan bɔra! Omacom Foundation bɛ kɛ mise dɛmɛbaga premier ye, o cogo la jdx ta, min yɛ san caman kɛ baarakɛminɛn jɔli la min bɛ Ruby, Node, Go version wɛrɛw ɲɛnabɔli kɛ nisɔndiya ye dogo la, ka agent baarakɛminɛn bɛɛ upgrade sira di min bɛ se ka tugu bɔli teliya kɔ.

Ni i yɛ Omarchy baara, i yɛ mise baara, i y'a ye walima i m'a ye. O de bɛ kan runtimew ɲɛnabɔ, o de la mise use -g ruby bɛ Ruby di i ma ni PATH sɛgɛsɛgɛli liyani kelen tɛ. Nka yɔrɔ min ka di ne ye kosɛbɛ sisan, o ye agentw ye.

Coding-agent CLI ba kelen-kelen bɛɛ Omarchy la bɛ ci i n'a fɔ mise stub lazy-loading ~/.local/bin/ kɔnɔ. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - u bɛɛ sigilen yen, u tɛ foyi bɔ i la fɔɔ siɲɛ fɔlɔ i bɛ kelen sɛbɛn tiɲɛ na. O tuma a bɛ a yɛrɛ sigi i bɛ baara. Package tɛ ka ɲini, version tɛ ka siri, toolchain tɛ ka miiri. omarchy-mise-install <package> bɛ CLI wɛrɛ o wɛrɛ datugu o cogo kelen na, omarchy update bɛ laban bɛɛ to kura ye.

O ye sira teliyalen ye. Agentw ka Waati la, waati min bɛ baarakɛminɛn mɛnni ni a boli cɛ, o ka kan ka kɛ sekondi naani ɲɔgɔn, mise bɛ o di. Mɔgɔ si mako tɛ ka fɛn dɔ package distro kama fɔlɔ.

Mako in bɛ fan fila bɛɛ fana: mɔgɔw ka taama mise ma yɛlɛnna 24% na kabini Omarchy daminɛna! Mɔgɔ caman bɛ baarakɛminɛn in dɔn siɲɛ fɔlɔ an fɛ, o suguya de ye fɛn ye dɛmɛ ka kan ka min dɔn sɔnni a ka minɛ gansan.

Kokura, aw ni baraka kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, ni Peter Steinberger ma ka nin se ka kɛ. Ni Oligarchy tun tɛ, an tun tɛna se ka open-source développeurw sara nin cogo la!
