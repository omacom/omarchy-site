Omacom Foundation ewelitela ọkara million USD ọzọ! N'etiti fresh supply nke tokens, corporate pledge afọ atọ, na puku USD site na open patronage, nke ahụ bụ ihe dị ka 510,000 USD ọzọ ekwelara maka Linux mara mma, na-atọ ọchị & agentic. Nke ahụ na-eweta total anyị na pledges na donations na ihe dị ka 15.5 million USD!

Nke mbụ, anyị na-anabata OpenRouter dị ka Distinguished Corporate Patron na 150,000 USD na tokens. Ozugbo allotment ahụ gwụchara, anyị nwere ike imeghari nhazi site na nkwekọrịta ọnụ. Tokens ndị a ga-akwado agents na-ewu, na-anwale, na-edobe Omarchy. Ka anyị nwere ike itinye agents n'ọrụ idozi bugs, nyochaa contributions, na imeziwanye distro, ngwa ngwa anyị nwere ike ime ka kọmputa mmadụ niile ka mma. Mmanụ ndị ọzọ maka mission!

Anyị ga-asọpụta millions USD na tokens enyere onyinye site na excellent OpenRouter platform. Ka anyị nwee ike kesaa right tokens na right teams site na right models.

Mgbe ahụ enwere Four Technologies, sonyere dị ka Distinguished Corporate Patron na nkwa 100,000 USD kwa afọ maka afọ atọ. Nke ahụ bụ 300,000 USD ịkwado community projects Omarchy dabere na ha, infrastructure na-eme ka ọ na-agba ọsọ, na ọrụ iweta Linux mara mma na ọtụtụ mmadụ. Inwe ụlọ ọrụ na-ekwe ruo afọ na-enye anyị ntụkwasị obi ime otu ihe.

Nke a bụ otú team Four Technologies si kwuo ya:

Anyị bụ AI-forward fintech company, ụlọ ọrụ dị ka Four Technologies na-enwe ike iwuo otú anyị si ewuo n'ihi open-source infrastructure ndị ọzọ wuru mbụ. Ịkwado Omacom Foundation bụ otú anyị si etinye ego n'azụ nke ahụ. Anyị chere na next stage maka AI abụghị app ọzọ layered n'elu operating system, ọ bụ AI wuru ozugbo n'ime operating system, nke ahụ bụ bet Omarchy na-eme. Anyị chọrọ ịbụ akụkụ nke obodo na-eme bet ahụ, ọ bụghị naanị irite uru na ya mgbe e mesịrị.

Kemgbe imeghe patronage nye mmadụ niile, anyị anatala ihe dị ka 60,000 USD site na ihe fọrọ nke nta ka ọ bụrụ 500 donors site na open patronage. Nke ahụ bụ nzaghachi dị ịtụnanya site na ndị mmadụ chọrọ inyere aka mee ka nke a mee. Contribution ọ bụla na-agbakwunye n'ihe anyị nwere ike ime ọnụ.

Tokens iji wuo. Ego iji kwado ọrụ anyị dabere na ha. Ndị mmadụ na ụlọ ọrụ dị njikere itinye ihe n'azụ nkwenye ha na Linux on the desktop. Otú a ka anyị si aga n'ihu!

Daalụ nye OpenRouter, Four Technologies, na mmadụ niile ghọrọ patron. Ọ bụrụ na ị ga-achọ isonye ha, deere david@omarchy.org maka founding na distinguished patronage, ma nye onyinye ozugbo na open patronage.
