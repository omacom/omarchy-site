Apple na-eme great hardware. Ha na-eme mgbe niile (wepụ otu oge ahụ na butterfly keyboard). Ọtụtụ ndị mmadụ na-abata na Omarchy na-esi na Mac abata. Ọtụtụ n'ime ha ga-achọ idebe igwe ma tufuo operating system. Ya mere anyị ga-emecha ihe Asahi malitere: Omarchy na-agba ọsọ nke ọma na Apple Silicon, na minimum nke setup fuss.

Ọrụ ahụ enweelarị team n'azụ ya. Taa anyị na-eme ya official: Omarchy M, team anyị maka Macs na Apple Silicon (gbakwunye members ole na ole na-edebe older Macs na fold).

Kwe ka m webata members.

Installer. Marcelo Alcantara na-ewu native Mac installer. Ị na-ebudata app na macOS, ọ na-eweta signed Omarchy release, na-akpụ partitions, ma na-boot gị na Omarchy n'akụkụ macOS dị adị gị. Enweghị USB stick, enweghị incantations na terminal. O bechapụrụ build nke mụnyere external monitors ọkụ site na USB-C.

Omarchy Mac. Naeem Malik edebewo Omarchy Mac otu afọ, na-eweta Omarchy na igwe M1 na M2 ogologo oge tupu ihe ọ bụla n'ime nke a bụrụ official, ma na-ewu Discord community nke ihe karịrị 800 mmadụ gburugburu ya. Scott Jones bụ ọkara ọzọ nke ọrụ ahụ na Linux side nke install. O nwetara display nke M3 Air na-agba ọsọ na Apple's hardware display controller n'oge na-adịbeghị anya, nke were igwe ahụ site na unusable ruo usable n'otu abalị.

Try Omarchy. Eduardo Martinez wuru Try Omarchy: ezigbo desktọpụ Omarchy na-agba ọsọ dị ka native, hardware-accelerated app na Mac gị, na camera gị, audio, clipboard, na shared folder wired through. Ọ dịghị ihe i-partition. Naanị budata ya hụ ihe fuss dị.

Packages. Shun Li na-ahụ na ihe niile Omarchy chọrọ dị dị ka aarch64 package, tinyere generic ARM64 disk images maka virtual machines na guest tools na-eme ha pleasant iji.

Drivers na kernel. Ryan Murray na-eweta Neo ma na-ewu ntọala ndị ọzọ nke team na-arụ ọrụ site na ha. Ọ na-azụlite SPTM emulator nke na-eme ka ọ kwe omume trace macOS na M4 na newer chips. Traces ndị ahụ dị mkpa iwere displays, GPUs, audio, wireless, na Bluetooth na-arụ ọrụ. Dj na-ede GPU drivers, wee mee ka Touch ID na-arụ ọrụ site n'ịgwa Secure Enclave okwu ozugbo. Miguel Cruz specialises na M5 work: ọ reverse-engineered Apple's proprietary N1 chip wee mee ka Wi-Fi na Bluetooth ya na-arụ ọrụ na Linux. O nyekwara hardware video encoding na decoding, Bluetooth, na desktop improvements na Neo. Liam na-eweta trackpad nke Neo, power management, speakers, na headphone output, na macOS traces nke Ryan na-enye essential groundwork. Eryk Wieliczko na-edebe M4 support na clean-room implementations, ma na-arụ ọrụ na integration na packaging. Jaidip Subedi mere ka external displays site na USB-C na M2 MacBook Air kwe omume.

MLX. Joshua Warren na-ewu mlx-omarchy, nke na-eweta Apple's MLX machine learning framework na Linux na Apple GPUs site na Vulkan. Koodu gị ka na-asị import mlx.core as mx, mana ugbu a ọ na-agba ọsọ na Omarchy.

Older Macs. Shawn Yeager nwere stack nke pull requests maka 2016 na 2017 Touch Bar MacBook Pros: speakers, Wi-Fi, ambient light sensor, Touch Bar n'onwe ya. Randy na-reverse-engineering T2 security chip iweta Touch ID na Intel Macs, ma na-edekọ fix ọ bụla maka 2019 MacBook Pro n'ụzọ. Jon Kinney na-arụsi ọrụ ike na ARM support maka Omarchy otu afọ gara aga, ugbu a na-eme ka team niile coordinated na onye na-eme ihe.

ọnụ, ha bụ formidable group. Mmadụ iri na ise na-echeghị ikike, ndị na-eji agents aga na pace nke gaara dị absurd otu afọ gara aga.

Target maka release mbụ bụ perfect compatibility na M1 na M2, gụnyere Pro na Max variants:

  • Seamless installer, na install na try options abụọ
  • Full GPU support
  • External monitors site na USB-C
  • Touch ID
  • MLX
  • Disk encryption

Igwe ọhụrụ ka a na-arụ ọrụ na parallel, mana M1 na M2 bụ ebe anyị na-akụ flag mbụ. Na mgbakwunye na igwe M, enwere millions nke vintage laptops n'èzí, ọtụtụ n'ime ha alala ezumike nká site na latest macOS ambitions nke Apple, nke ọ bụla n'ime ha bụ wonderful Omarchy computer na-eche ime.

Ị chọghị ichere polished release ịmalite. Try Omarchy ga-eme ka ị na-ele ezigbo desktọpụ na Mac gị na nkeji. Ma ọ bụrụ na ị dị njikere commit, installer ga-etinye Omarchy n'akụkụ macOS na M1 ma ọ bụ M2 taa.

N'ikpeazụ, arịrịọ. Ọ bụrụ na ị na-arụ ọrụ na Linux maka Apple hardware, ma nke ahụ bụ kernel drivers, firmware, packaging, installers, ma ọ bụ machine learning, anyị ga-achọ ịnụ site na gị. Enwere ezigbo ohere ị dozila ihe anyị ka na-achụ, ma ọ bụ vice versa. Kpọtụrụ na apple@omarchy.org.
