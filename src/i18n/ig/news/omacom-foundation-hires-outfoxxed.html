Anyị na-ewere outfoxxed, onye kere Quickshell, dị ka Head of Omarchy Shell! Ọ na-esonye na Omacom Foundation oge niile ma na-aghọ onye otu Omarchy Core. Onye wuru toolkit nke mere Quattro kwe omume na-abata inyere anyị aka ibupụta ihe niile na ọnwa.

Nke a na-anọchi three-year Quickshell sponsorship anyị kpọsara na August. Kama sponsorship ahụ, outfoxxed ga-abụ onye ọrụ Omacom Foundation oge niile, na-eduzi ọrụ anyị na Omarchy Shell. Anyị na-etinye onye kere ya n'etiti ihe anyị na-ewu.

Shell dị mkpa na agentic OS anyị na-eme. Ọ bụ bar, menus, notifications, widgets, na obere interactions niile na-eme ka kọmputa dị ka nke gị. Quickshell na-enye anyị building blocks ịkpụzi experience ahụ na QML. Jikọta nke ahụ na agents dị mma nke ukwuu na ide QML, na mberede ịgbanwe desktọpụ gị bụ ihe ị nwere ike ịrịọ, nwalee, ma kesaa.

Ihe akaebe dị na plugins! Mgbe anyị kpọsara sponsorship, obodo ekepụtala ihe karịrị otu puku na izu mbụ Quattro. Ugbu a catalogue nwere ihe fọrọ nke nta ka ọ bụrụ puku plugins atọ. Games ị nwere ike igwu ozugbo site na bar, panels na-enyocha coding agents gị, music players, na ọbụna cat na-agagharị na bar gị na-eche ka e metụ ya. Ndị mmadụ na-ewere sistemụ na-adị fixed ma na-eme ya nke ha. Nke ahụ bụ kọmputa malleable na-abata na ndụ.

Inwe onye kere Quickshell n'ụgbọ pụtara na anyị nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ na stack niile ọnụ. Mee shell ọsọ ọsọ na ntụkwasị obi karị. Mee ka ọ dị mfe iwuo great plugin. Meziwanye ntọala mgbe plugin author zutere limitation. Gbanwee ihe anyị na-amụta site na puku experiments ka better experience maka mmadụ niile na-boot Omarchy. Ma hụ na ihe niile dị safe na secure.

Site na isonye na Omarchy Core, outfoxxed ga-enyere aka ịhazi ntụziaka maka distro niile. Shell dị oke etiti na experience ịbụ afterthought. Ọ bụ ebe ọtụtụ ịma mma, anụrị, na ohere nke agentic OS na-aghọ ihe ị nwere ike ịhụ n'ezie ma jiri.

outfoxxed bụ full-time hire nke abụọ nke Omacom Foundation, na-eso Krzysztof Wilczyński, Head of Omarchy Kernel anyị. Na ndị mmadụ na-eduzi Kernel na Shell n'ụgbọ, anyị na-etinye ego na ntọala nke experience niile. Ị nwere ike izute staff nke foundation na saịtị.

Anyị ga-aga n'ihu were site na ọkpụkpọ ka anyị na-achọta ndị ziri ezi site na ọrụ ha na obodo. (Biko ejula inbox m na CVs!)

Nke a bụ ihe backing nke foundation na-ahapụ anyị ime: nye exceptional open-source developers oge ịbanye niile. Daalụ nye mmadụ niile na-akwado ọrụ ahụ, anyị na-agbanwe ọtụtụ ambitions anyị ka full-time jobs.

Nnọọ n'ụgbọ, outfoxxed! Ka anyị mee kọmputa a ka malleable karị.
