Weebụsaịtị maka beautiful, fun Linux distribution kwesịrị ịma mma ma tọọ ụtọ. Ya mere anyị enyela Omarchy.org complete redesign, anyị na-ebupụta ya na asụsụ 29 (n'ihi na mgbe ị welitelere ~2m USD na tokens, gịnị kpatara na ị gaghị eme ya?). Obere retro magic, ọtụtụ ihe inyocha, na nnabata ka ukwuu nye ụwa.

Nke a bụ weebụsaịtị chọrọ ka ị gwuo egwu na kọmputa gị! Pixels, colors, na animations na-ebu spirit nke Omarchy ozugbo na browser. Ị nwere ike ịnweta feel nke ebe tupu ị budata otu ISO. Ọ bụkwa personal connection nke m na formative years ndị ahụ na pre-internet online world nke ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, na vibrant aesthetic nke 90s. Agba ndị ọzọ, polish obere. Pixels ndị ọzọ, soft gradients ole na ole.

Enwere ọnụ ụlọ ugbu a igosipụta editors, terminals, agents, games, Windows VM, na hardware na-eme Omarchy bara uru kwa ụbọchị. Chọgharịa community themes, chọta plugin, hụ real workstations, ma ọ bụ chọpụta meetup dị gị nso. Zute ndị mmadụ na-ewu ya na patrons na-akwado ya. N'ikpeazụ anyị nwere front door nwere ọnụ ụlọ zuru ezu maka ihe niile na-eme n'ime.

Front door ahụ na-asụ asụsụ gị ugbu a.
