Update, September 9, 2026: Sponsorship a ka outfoxxed sonyere Omacom Foundation oge niile dị ka Head of Omarchy Shell na onye otu Omarchy Core nọchiri.

Ego ndị ọzọ batara, ego ndị ọzọ apụọ! Sponsorship major nke abụọ anyị site na Omacom Foundation na-aga na outfoxxed maka superb work ya na Quickshell. Ọ bụ naanị otu izu n'ime public release, mana o doro anya dị ka kristal na ọ bụ missing, pivotal piece maka anyị iji nweta n'ezie The Malleable Computer.

Ihe akaebe dị na plugins! Ihe karịrị otu puku plugins ekepụtara n'izu Quattro pụtara. Nke ahụ bụ astounding testament zuru oke na technical flexibility nke Quickshell, na otú agents si hụ ide QML code n'anya.

Sponsorship a na-akwụ outfoxxed ụgwọ maka outstanding contribution ya na Omarchy, ma na-enyere aka cement connection na long-term plans anyị maka The Age of Agents (ebinyela nke ahụ na Bluesky, ha ga-agba ara!). O nwere term maka afọ atọ site na mmalite.

Ekele pụrụ iche nye Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, na Peter Steinberger maka ime ka nke a kwe omume. Ọ bụrụ na ọ bụghị maka Oligarchy, anyị agaraghị enwe ike ịkwado open-source developers dị ka nke a!
