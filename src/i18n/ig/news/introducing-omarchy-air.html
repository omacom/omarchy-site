Omarchy na-ekwusi ike na kọmputa kwesịrị ịma mma dị ka ọ na-arụpụta ihe. Ndị na-eme theme na ndị na-ese ihe Omarchy ndị ọzọ ebuola premise ahụ kemgbe ụbọchị mbụ. Na-ewu palettes niile, n'oge nke ha, n'efu, n'ihi na ha chọrọ ka igwe ha dị ka ihe.

Ya mere anyị na-amalite Omarchy AIR - Artists in Residence - iji kwado ndị kachasị mma n'ime ha. Ebe obibi ọnwa isii maka ndị na-ese ihe ịrụ ọrụ n'efu na themes, plugins, na ihe ọ bụla ọzọ ha chere na Omarchy chọrọ ọzọ. Enweghị roadmap, enweghị tickets. Ebumnuche bụ ikwe ka creativity na-asọ na nkwado Omacom Foundation.

Ebe obibi ọ bụla na-abịa na 2,500 USD kwa ọnwa maka ọnwa isii niile, token account ka ndị agents ghara ịkwụsị, na nkweta na-adịgide adịgide dị ka AIR alumni ozugbo ọnwa isii gwụchara.

Enwere ruru oche atọ dị n'otu oge, abụọ n'ime ha ewerela ugbu a maka season mbụ a.

HANCORE nwere themes abụọ na-ebu na Omarchy - Solitude na Last Horizon - na iri abụọ ọzọ na extras page, karịa ihe onye ọ bụla ọzọ nyere: Batou, Oxo Carbon, Velvet Night, Rose of Dune, Shades of Jade, ọ na-aga n'ihu. Ọ bụ mechanical engineer ghọrọ otu n'ime ike ịchọ mma na-akọwa obodo a, nọ na Omarchy Core, ma na-enyere aka ilekọta plugin ecosystem.

OldJobobo bụ onye kpatara atọ n'ime themes na-ebu na Omarchy - Lumon, Miasma, na Retro 82 - gbakwunye iri ọzọ na extras page, site na Batman ruo City-783 ruo Sakura Mochi. Ọ na-arụ ọrụ na palettes niile kama one-off colour schemes, nke bụ ihe mere themes ya ji ejikọta gafee terminal, Neovim, btop, na shell kama ịma mma na screenshot.

Oche ikpeazụ bụ site na ọkpụkpọ. Mmemme na-amalite na October.
