Ya mere site taa, patronage ghe oghe nye mmadụ niile. Enwere tiers anọ: 16 USD, 256 USD, 2,048 USD, na 8,192 USD. Ike nke bits, naturally! Patron ọ bụla bụ akụkụ nke otu mission, USD ọ bụla na-azụta otu ihe: ezigbo ohere imezu amụma Linux on the Desktop!

Patron ọ bụla na-enweta digital rally credential iji gosipụta. Tier ọ bụla nwere badge, social card, na wallpapers nke ya, nke Artist in Residence anyị OldJobobo mere. Ego na-arụ ọrụ siri ike ugbu a. Foundation bụ exclusive sponsor nke Hyprland na premier sponsor nke Quickshell na mise, atọ n'ime pillars e wuru Omarchy na ha.
