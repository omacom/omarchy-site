Anyị na-ewere Emir Beganović dị ka Head of Infrastructure maka Omarchy! Ọ na-amalite November 1 dị ka Omacom Foundation full-time hire nke atọ, sonyere Krzysztof Wilczyński na Kernel na outfoxxed na Shell.

Nke a bụ otú Emir si akọwa njem nke ya na Omarchy:

Ịhụnanya m nwere maka Linux laghachiri ihe karịrị afọ iri abụọ. Aghọrọ m Ubuntu developer mgbe m ka bụ teenager ma tinye aka nke ukwuu na obodo mpaghara. Ọrụ m ewerela m site na mba atọ, na-arụ ọrụ na reliability na performance na startups na scaleups ma na-eduzi teams. M nọrọ ihe fọrọ nke nta ka ọ bụrụ afọ asaa na ngalaba SRE nke Booking.com, na-eduzi ọrụ na build systems na CI infrastructure. N'oge na-adịbeghị anya, m wuru Isorun, ebe m na-arụ ọrụ na microVMs, snapshots, isolation, scheduling, na disposable compute. Ugbu a m na-eweta experience ahụ laghachi na ịhụnanya mbụ m.

Ihe dọtara uche m n'ezie banyere Emir bụ TryOmarchy. O were desktọpụ Omarchy, tinye ya na browser (ọ bụghị reimplementation, kama ihe dị adị), ma mee ka ọ na-booting na sekọnd na ordinary CPU infrastructure na Cloudflare. Anyị malitere ikwu maka silly experiment ahụ, mkparịta ụka gbasara ngwa ngwa na laptọpụ, architectures ọhụrụ, OEMs, na infrastructure anyị ga-achọ ma Omarchy ga-agba ọsọ ebe niile anyị chọrọ. Anyị ghọtara na anyị kerịta otu vision.

Nke ahụ bụ ihe Emir na-abata iduzi.

Infrastructure maka Omarchy pụtara ihe karịrị ịgba servers ọsọ. Ọ bụ ihe anyị na-eji ewuwu, nwalee, na-ebuga Omarchy, na ime ka sistemụ na-arụ ọrụ reliably ka anyị na-agbasa na hardware ndị ọzọ na architectures ndị ọzọ. Ọ bụ ime releases dependable, ime failures reproducible, na inye mmadụ niile ọzọ na ọrụ solid foundation ka ha nwee ike ịga ngwa ngwa.

Enwere ọtụtụ ihe inyocha! Omarchy kwesịrị ịrụ ọrụ nke ọma na igwe dị na desk gị, mana enweghị ihe mere ọ ga-ejedebe ebe ahụ. TryOmarchy nyere anyị glimpse nke ihe na-eme mgbe ịmalite kọmputa Linux dị mfe dị ka imepe web page, na-etinye taste nke Linux naanị otu click pụọ. Anyị ga-ahụ ebe nke ahụ na-eduga.

Daalụ nye mmadụ niile na-akwado ọrụ a, anyị nwere ike inye ndị dị ka Emir oge ịbanye niile na Omarchy. Patrons anyị na-eme ka ọ kwe omume ịgbanwe ambitious experiments ka lasting work, na ndị oge niile raara onwe ha nye ime ka sistemụ niile ka mma. Anyị nwere ekele maka patronage ha.

Nnọọ n'ụgbọ, Emir! Ka anyị mee Omarchy na-agba ọsọ ebe niile.
