Ego Omacom Foundation eruola 10 million USD na Founding Patrons ọhụrụ abụọ sonyere mission: Drew Houston, cofounder na co-CEO nke Dropbox, na Peter Steinberger, onye kere OpenClaw.

Dị ka Founding Patrons asatọ mbụ, Drew na Peter na-enye 1 million USD ọ bụla. Nke ahụ na-enye foundation patrons iri na 10 MILLION USD dị ọcha iji kwalite audacious mission anyị ime ka Linux on the desktop mee n'ụzọ ka ukwuu.

Ọ dị ụtọ karị inwe Drew na Peter n'ụgbọ n'ihi na abụ m nnukwu onye na-akwado ha abụọ na ihe ha rụzuru. Abụ m onye ahịa Dropbox nwere ịnụ ọkụ n'obi ihe fọrọ nke nta ka ọ bụrụ afọ iri abụọ, ọ bụkwa etiti otú m si ebi na no backup, no cry ma na-agbanwe kọmputa mgbe niile.

Ya mere Omarchy na-abịa na nnukwu Dropbox integration site na igbe. Naanị gaa na Install > Service > Dropbox, ị ga-enwe file manager integration na bespoke menu panel maka controls.

OpenClaw enyela roadmap maka otú Omarchy kwesịrị isi dozie explosive growth ya. Olee otú ị si emeso PR backlogs na-aga parabolic na mberede? Gịnị maka security gburugburu plugins? Olee otú a ga-esi edozi meetups n'aha ọrụ? Enweela m obi ụtọ ịdọrọ na ndụmọdụ na experience Peter na ọtụtụ isiokwu ndị a.

Karịa nke ahụ, ha abụọ na-anọchite anya ụdị technical ambition m chọrọ gburugburu Omarchy. Drew gbanwere personal itch banyere ịkwaga faịlụ n'etiti kọmputa ka Dropbox. Peter gbanwere weekend project ka OpenClaw, na n'ụzọ chetara mmadụ niile ole appetite dị maka kọmputa na-adị personal, programmable, na fun.

Nke ahụ bụ spirit n'azụ Omacom Foundation. Anyị nọ ebe a ịkwado infrastructure, open-source projects, na developers achọrọ ime ka Linux desktop bụrụ delightful home maka next generation nke ndị ọrụ kọmputa.

Nnọọ, Drew na Peter. Founding Patrons iri. Nde USD iri. Amụma ahụ bịaruru 2 million USD nso ọzọ!
