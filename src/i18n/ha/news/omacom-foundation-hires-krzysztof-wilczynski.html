Omacom Foundation yanzu ta yi ɗaukar cikakken lokaci na farko! Krzysztof Wilczyński yana shigowa don jagorantar aikimmu a Omarchy Kernel. Wannan daidai shi ne abin da aka kafa gidauniyar don yi: biyan manyan mutane don yin aiki a matsaloli masu wuya don amfanin kowa.

Krzysztof tsohon mai ƙirƙirar kernel na Linux ne. Yana kula tare da PCI Endpoint subsystem tare da native PCI host bridge da endpoint drivers. Wannan bututun ne da ke haɗa Linux da GPU naka, NVMe drive naka, da katin WiFi naka. Shi kuma mai duba ne a sabon aikin Rust PCI na kernel. Kafin ya zurfafa a kernel, ya ɓata shekaru a injiniyan tsaruka, yana aiki a Kubernetes da container runtimes.

Yanzu zai kawo duk wannan ƙwarewa ga Omarchy Kernel. Mayar da hankali: aiki, jituwa, da tsaro. Muna son Linux ta tashi a sabbin laptops da tsofaffin ThinkPads iri ɗaya. Muna son ta yi aiki tun daga akwati a na'urori da yawa. Kuma muna son ta kulle sosai.

Krzysztof kuma yana shiga Omarchy Core, don haka zai taimaka sa alkibla ga distro baki ɗaya.

Maraba a jirgi, Krzysztof! Mu sa kernel ta yi ihu.
