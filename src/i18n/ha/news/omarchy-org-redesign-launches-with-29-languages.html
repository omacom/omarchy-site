Shafin rarraba Linux mai kyau, mai nishaɗi ya kamata ya yi kyau kuma ya yi nishaɗi. Don haka mun ba Omarchy.org cikakken sabon zane, kuma muna ƙaddamar da shi a harsuna 29 (saboda idan ka tara ~2m USD a tokens, me ya sa ba haka ba?). Ƙaramin sihirin retro, abin bincike mai yawa, da maraba mafi girma ga duniya.

Wannan shafi ne da ke son ka yi wasa da kwamfutarka! Pixels, launuka, da motsin rai suna ɗauke da ruhin Omarchy kai tsaye zuwa browser. Za ka iya jin wurin kafin ka zazzage ISO ɗaya. Har ila yau haɗina ne na sirri da waɗannan shekarun farko a duniyar online kafin intanet na ASCII art, Amiga demoscene, da wannan kyan gani mai cike da rai na 90s. Ƙarin launi, ƙaramin goge. Ƙarin pixels, ƙaramin gradients masu laushi.

Yanzu akwai wuri don nuna editors, terminals, wakilai, wasanni, Windows VM, da kayan aiki da ke sa Omarchy mai amfani kowace rana. Bincika jigogin al'umma, nemo plugin, ga ainihin workstations, ko gano taro kusa da kai. Haɗu da mutanen da ke gina shi da masu tallafa masa. A ƙarshe muna da ƙofar gaba mai isasshen wuri ga komai da ke faruwa a ciki.

Kuma wannan ƙofar gaba yanzu tana magana da harshenka. Larabci, Bengali, Catalan, Danish, Turanci, Filipino, Finnish, Faransanci, Girkanci, Hindi, Hungarian, Icelandic, Irish, Italiyanci, Japananci, Korean, Lithuanian, Polish, Fotugalanci, Sinanci Sauƙaƙa, Sinhala, Sifaniyanci, Swedish, Tamil, Thai, Turkanci, Urdu, Uzbek, da Vietnamese. Babban shafin da kowane labari an fassara su. Littafin jagora har yanzu yana cikin Turanci; za mu fuskanci shi gaba. Waɗannan fassarori ne na farko, kuma gyare-gyare daga masu magana da harshe ana maraba da su sosai ta repository na shafin.

Muna da yankuna na gida kuma: omarchy.ae, omarchy.dk, omarchy.fi, omarchy.fr, omarchy.gr, omarchy.hu, omarchy.in, omarchy.is, omarchy.jp, omarchy.kr, omarchy.mx, omarchy.ph, omarchy.pt, omarchy.se, da omarchy.tr.

Mambobin al'umma sun yi rijistar yankuna da karimci kuma sun ba da shawarar nuna su zuwa gare mu. Yayin da muke haɗa ragowar sunaye na gida, za ka iya amfani da bn.omarchy.org, ca.omarchy.org, ga.omarchy.org, it.omarchy.org, lt.omarchy.org, pl.omarchy.org, si.omarchy.org, ta.omarchy.org, th.omarchy.org, ur.omarchy.org, uz.omarchy.org, vi.omarchy.org, da zh.omarchy.org. Yi amfani da globe kusa da mai zaɓar jigo don tsallakewa tsakanin harsuna.

Wannan sabon zane ya fito daga babbar ɓulɓulawar aiki a rukunin Omarchy Design. Barış Girişmen ya ƙirƙiri zanen da ya zama ginshiƙin sabon shafin, ya gina pixel-grid shader, kuma ya jagoranci yawancin aiwatarwa da goge. Christoffer Hallas ya kawo prototypes, aikin motsin rai, sauyin jigo, da kwararar gyare-gyare. Daniel Schmier ya bayar da zane da aiwatarwa ga tambari, labarai, shigarwa, ƙungiya, gidauniya, da sassan shaida.

Godiya kuma ga Andrés Villagrán, Ryan Hughes, Yam Catzenelson, Eric Carmichael, Wes Oudshoorn, Taha, Aloïs Deniel, Adrian Rangel, Niklas Jul, HANCORE, OldJobobo, da Rick Blalock don ra'ayoyi, gwaje-gwaje, tunani, da martani da aka raba a hanya. Kuma ga kowa da ke taimaka wa tabbatar da yankuna na gida da maraba da mutane a harshensu. Abin farin ciki ne kallon duka wannan yana haɗuwa.
