Wannan babbar dama ce ga Linux. Tebur a buɗe yake ta hanyar da bai kasance ba tsawon shekaru. Wakilai sun sa operating system mai lanƙwasawa. Kayan aiki ba su taɓa yin kyau haka ba. Kuma gasa ba ta taɓa ƙwazo haka wajen fusata masu amfani da ita ba. Ba za mu bari wannan lokaci ya wuce ba!

Don haka Omacom Foundation tana hanzarta manufofinta na kashewa. Komai da aka tara a wannan shekara za a kashe a cikin uku masu zuwa. Ba a ajiye a endowment ba. Ba a digo digo a cikin shekara goma ba. A kashe!

Tare da 13 million USD da aka riga aka yi alƙawari, wannan yana nufin kasafin shekara fiye da 4 million USD ga 2027, 2028, da 2029. Kuma ƙari yayin da kuɗi ke shigowa daga masu tallafi na kowane iri. Masu Kafa, Manyan, Kamfanoni, da kowa da ya shiga manufa yau.

Wannan kuɗin yana zuwa ga ayyukan buɗaɗɗen tushe da aka gina Omarchy a kansu. Ga masu fasaha waɗanda ke sa ta kyau. Ga masu ƙirƙira waɗanda ke sa ta sauri, mai jituwa, kuma amintacce. Ga gasa, grants, da kayayyaki. Ga komai da ake buƙata don cika annabcin Linux a Tebur.

Kuɗin ba su ne maƙasudin ba. Manufa ce. Amma tabbas yana taimakawa samun duka!
