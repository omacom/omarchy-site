Girman Omarchy mai fashewa yana buƙatar ƙungiyoyi masu tsari a amsa, kuma za mu fara ta haɗa Ƙungiyar Tsakiyar Omarchy. Wannan rukuni zai raba alhakin jagorantar alkiblar distro namu, tabbatar da mu ci gaba da turawa gaba, da taimaka wa sauran masu bayarwa.

Bari in gabatar da Ƙungiyar Tsakiya ta farko:

Ryan Hughes ya kasance mataimakina a Omarchy tun rana ta farko. Mun yi faɗa tare da matsalar AUR na gaggawa na 3am a kwanakin farko, kuma mun shirya gina repository namu na package. Gudummawar Ryan ta shafi kowane layi na distro: shigarwa, packaging, goyon bayan hardware, wasanni, jigogi, kayan CLI, kuma yanzu Quattro. Ya taimaka jagorantar sauyawa zuwa Quickshell, tsarin plugin, da yawancin kayayyakin da suka juya Omarchy daga saitin Linux da aka haɗa a hankali zuwa cikakken operating system. Idan ba ya hacking a Omarchy, Ryan abokin tarayya ne a Oodle.

Tobi Lütke shi ne mutum ɗaya tilo da ya shiga duka ƙungiyoyina na tsakiya biyu! A matsayin memba na Ƙungiyar Tsakiyar Rails ta farko da aka fara a 2004, ya jagoranci ba kawai babban tarihin gudummawa ga framework ba, amma kuma ya rubuta Liquid, Delayed Job, da Active Merchant. Wannan aikin a ƙarshe ya zama ƙaramin kamfani mai suna Shopify, shin kun ji su? Yanzu shi mai bayarwa ne mai yawa ga Omarchy da ayyuka kamar try, Omasnap, Wi-Fi QR codes, da Quake-style agent console. Har ila yau ya zama direbana a mota #11 Shopify Racing LMP2 da ke tseren IMSA.

Bjarne Øverli shi ne CTO na Unite AS kuma mahalicin Aether, mai ginin jigo na gani da muke aika a Omarchy, da cliamp, mai kunna kiɗanmu na TUI. Ya taimaka juya tsarin jigoginmu daga tarin fayilolin config zuwa wani abu mai tsari sosai, tare da launuka masu ma'ana da jigogi da aka samar a Neovim, VS Code, btop, da sauran stack. Shi kuma ne ke da alhakin jigogi kamar Ethereal, Hackerman, da Lupine. Bjarne yana da wannan haɗin da ba kasafai na tsaurin shiri da ɗanɗanon gani da ya dace da Omarchy sosai.

Spencer Bull Distinguished Engineer ne a Dell yana aiki a kunna Linux client, AI, da software na platform. Shi ne gadar mu zuwa gaskiyar rikice-rikice na sabbin kayan aiki, yana taimaka wa Omarchy ta yi aiki da kyau a Panther Lake da sabbin inji XPS kafin jinkirin Linux na yau da kullum ya iya shigowa. Matsalolin kernel, direbobi, nuni, Wi-Fi, lasifika, kula da wuta: Spencer yana aiki a layin inda software ke haɗuwa da atoms, kuma samun wannan ƙwarewa a cikin Ƙungiyar Tsakiya yana da matuƙar amfani.
