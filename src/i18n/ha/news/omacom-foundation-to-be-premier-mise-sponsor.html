Tallafi na uku ya fita! Omacom Foundation tana zama premier mai tallafi ga mise, kuma ta haka ga jdx, wanda ya ɓata shekaru yana gina kayan da ke sa kula da sigogi daban-daban na Ruby, Node, Go farin ciki wajen kulawa a hankali, kuma yana ba wa duk kayan wakilan ka tashar haɓakawa da za ta iya tafiya da saurin fitowa.

Idan ka yi amfani da Omarchy, ka yi amfani da mise, ko ka lura ko ba ka lura ba. Shi ne ke kula da language runtimes, don haka mise use -g ruby yana ba ka Ruby ba tare da layin PATH archaeology ɗaya ba. Amma ɓangaren da na damu da shi yanzu shi ne wakilai.

Kowane babban coding-agent CLI a Omarchy yana zuwa a matsayin lazy-loading mise stub a ~/.local/bin/. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - duk suna zaune a can, ba sa kashe maka komai har sai karon farko da ka rubuta ɗaya da gaske. Sannan yana shigar da kansa kuma kana aiki. Babu package don farautawa, babu sigar don ɗaurewa, babu toolchain don tunani a kansa. omarchy-mise-install <package> yana naɗe duk wani CLI iri ɗaya, kuma omarchy update yana sa kowane ɗaya sabo.

Wannan ita ce hanya mai sauri. A The Age of Agents, gibin tsakanin jin labarin kayan aiki da gudanar da shi yana buƙatar zama kamar seconds huɗu, kuma mise tana bayar da hakan. Babu wanda ya buƙaci packaging wani abu ga distro tukuna.

Sha'awar tana tafiya ɓangarori biyu kuma: zirga-zirga zuwa mise ta hau 24% tun Omarchy ta fito! Mutane da yawa suna haɗuwa da wannan kayan a karon farko ta gare mu, wanda daidai irin abin da tallafi ya kamata ya gane maimakon ɗauka a banza.

Sake, godiya ta musamman ga Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, da Peter Steinberger don sa wannan ya yiwu. Idan ba don Oligarchy ba, ba za mu iya tallafa wa masu ƙirƙirar buɗaɗɗen tushe haka ba!
