Sabuntawa, September 9, 2026: Wannan tallafi an maye gurbinsa da outfoxxed ya shiga Omacom Foundation cikakken lokaci a matsayin Shugaban Omarchy Shell kuma memba na Omarchy Core.

Ƙarin kuɗi a ciki, ƙarin kuɗi a waje! Tallafinmu na biyu mai girma daga Omacom Foundation yana zuwa ga outfoxxed don kyakkyawan aikinsa a Quickshell. Makoo guda kawai a fitowar jama'a, amma tuni a bayyane yake cewa ita ce ɓangaren da ya ɓace, mai mahimmanci don mu cimma da gaske The Malleable Computer.

Hujja tana cikin plugins! Fiye da plugins dubu ɗaya an ƙirƙira a makon da Quattro ta fito. Wannan shaida ce mai ban mamaki ga sassaucin fasaha na Quickshell, da yadda wakilai ke ƙaunar rubuta lambar QML.

Wannan tallafi don haka yana sakamako ga outfoxxed don gudummawarsa mai ban mamaki ga Omarchy, kuma yana taimaka ƙarfafa haɗi zuwa tsare-tsarenmu na dogon lokaci ga The Age of Agents (kada ka wallafa hakan a Bluesky, za su yi hauka!). An tsara shi na shekaru uku tun daga ƙofar.

Godiya ta musamman ga Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, da Peter Steinberger don sa wannan ya yiwu. Idan ba don Oligarchy ba, ba za mu iya tallafa wa masu ƙirƙirar buɗaɗɗen tushe haka ba!
