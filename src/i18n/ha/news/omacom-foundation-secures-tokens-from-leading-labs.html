Omarchy ba rarraba Linux mai kyau, mai nishaɗi kuma mai wakilai ba ce kawai ga waɗanda suka ƙare suna amfani da ita ba. Haka aka gina ta! Da kyau, nishaɗi, da tokens masu yawa. A zahiri, Omarchy Quattro kusan wakilai ne suka gina ta kaɗai. Saboda suna da kyau sosai a abubuwa kamar bash, QML, da gyara matsalolin Linux.

Don ci gaba da sa Omarchy fi kyau yanzu da muka jawo ɗaruruwan dubban masu amfani da masu ƙirƙira tare, muna buƙatar ci gaba da gudanan tokens. Haka muke hanzartawa, ingantawa, kuma cika duk gudummawa masu ban mamaki da ke zubowa. Tuni muna da tarin PRs 1,600+ daga 'yan makonni kaɗan bayan ƙaddamarwa! Babu wanda zai ratsa ta ba tare da taimakon wakilai ba.

Saboda haka, ina farin cikin sanar da cewa mun tabbatar da tarin tokens daga yawancin manyan labs don wannan manufa! Na farko shi ne Meta Superintelligence Labs wanda ke shigowa a matsayin Mai Tallafi Mai Kafa na Token da 1.5m USD a tokens! Sabon model nasu na Meta Muse Spark yana sa lambobi masu kyau a farashi mai ban mamaki. Don haka 1.5m USD a zahiri tokens marasa iyaka ne! (Za mu yi ƙoƙarin ƙare su duk da haka!)

Amma muna kuma farin cikin tarbar biyu daga sauran manyan frontier labs, Anthropic da OpenAI, a matsayin Manyan Masu Tallafi na Token da 150,000 USD a gudummawar token kowannensu. Waɗannan za su zama masu amfani musamman idan aka haɗa su da izinin tsaro da ake buƙata don cire guardrails da muke buƙata don binciken tsaro da gyare-gyare.

Kuma ga open-weight models, ina da farin ciki daidai don sanar da Fireworks a matsayin Mai Tallafi Babba na Token kuma da 150,000 USD a tokens da aka yi alƙawari. Na kasance mai amfani da models na Kimi K musamman, kuma musamman idan suna gudana a fast mode. Za mu sa waɗannan tokens masu sauri a amfani mai kyau kuma!

Haɗe, wannan wani 1.95 million USD ne da aka yi alƙawari ga Omacom Foundation. Wanda sai ya sa jimillar alƙawuranmu zuwa 14.95 million USD. Wannan lamba ce mai hauka don rubutawa ga distro da masu suka na farko suka watsar a matsayin tarin dotfiles (wanda da gaskiya ba kuskure ba ne gaba ɗaya a lokacin, amma sai muka ci gaba da gyara komai!).

Tare da duk waɗannan tokens, za mu iya gyara komai. Kuma wannan shi ne abin da za mu yi!
