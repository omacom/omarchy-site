Tallafin Omacom Foundation yanzu ya kai 10 million USD tare da sabbin Masu Tallafi Masu Kafa biyu da suka shiga manufa: Drew Houston, wanda ya kafa kuma co-CEO na Dropbox, da Peter Steinberger, mahalicin OpenClaw.

Kamar Masu Tallafi Masu Kafa takwas na asali, Drew da Peter kowannensu yana bayar da 1 million USD. Wannan yana ba gidauniyar masu tallafi goma da DOLLARS MILIYAN GOMA mai tsabta don tura manufarmu mai ƙarfin hali don sa Linux a tebur ya faru ta hanya mafi girma.

Abin daɗi ne musamman samun Drew da Peter a jirgi saboda ni da kaina na kasance babban mai sonta duka da nasarorinsu. Na kasance abokin ciniki mai kwazo na Dropbox kusan shekaru ashirin, kuma tana da mahimmanci ga yadda nake iya rayuwa da no backup, no cry duk da canza kwamfutoci kullum.

Shi ya sa Omarchy ke zuwa da haɗin Dropbox mai kyau tun daga akwati. Kawai je zuwa Install > Service > Dropbox, kuma za ka sami haɗin mai sarrafa fayil da panel na menu na musamman don sarrafawa.

Kuma OpenClaw a zahiri ta samar da taswirar yadda Omarchy ya kamata ta fuskanci girma mai fashewa. Ta yaya ake mu'amala da tarin PR da suka tashi ba zato ba tsammani? Menene game da tsaro a kusa da plugins? Ta yaya ya kamata a mu'amala da tarurruka da sunan aikin? Tuni na sami damar amfani da shawarar Peter da ƙwarewarsa a da yawa daga waɗannan batutuwa.

Bayan haka, dukansu suna wakiltar daidai irin burin fasaha da nake so a kusa da Omarchy. Drew ya juya ƙaiƙayin kansa game da motsa fayiloli tsakanin kwamfutoci zuwa Dropbox. Peter ya juya aikin ƙarshen mako zuwa OpenClaw, kuma a hanya ya tunatar da kowa yawan buƙatar kwamfutoci da ke ji na sirri, masu shirye-shirye, da nishaɗi.

Wannan shi ne ruhin da ke bayan Omacom Foundation kuma. Muna nan don tallafa wa kayayyaki, ayyukan buɗaɗɗen tushe, da masu ƙirƙira da ake buƙata don sa tebur Linux gida mai daɗi ga ƙarni na gaba na masu amfani da kwamfuta.

Maraba, Drew da Peter. Masu Tallafi Masu Kafa goma. Dala miliyan goma. Annabcin yanzu ya ƙara kusantowa da wasu 2 million USD!
