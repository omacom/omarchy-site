Apple tana yin manyan kayan aiki. Kullum tana yi (ban da wancan lokacin da butterfly keyboard). Kuma mutane da yawa da ke zuwa Omarchy suna fitowa daga Mac. Yawancinsu za su so su ajiye na'urar su rasa operating system. Don haka za mu gama abin da Asahi ta fara: Omarchy tana gudana da kyau a Apple Silicon, da ƙaramin wahalar saiti.

Wannan aikin tuni yana da ƙungiya a bayansa. Yau muna sanya shi hukuma: Omarchy M, ƙungiyarmu ga Macs da Apple Silicon (da 'yan mambobi da ke ajiye tsofaffin Macs a ciki).

Bari in gabatar da mambobi.

Installer. Marcelo Alcantara yana gina native Mac installer. Ka zazzage app a macOS, tana jawo sakin Omarchy da aka sa wa hannu, tana sassaka partitions, kuma tana tayar da kai zuwa Omarchy kusa da macOS naka da ke nan. Babu USB stick, babu sihiri a terminal. Shi kuma ya yanke build ɗin da ya kunna monitors na waje ta USB-C.

Omarchy Mac. Naeem Malik ya kula da Omarchy Mac na shekara, yana sa Omarchy a inji M1 da M2 tun kafin duk wannan ya zama hukuma, kuma yana gina al'ummar Discord ta mutane fiye da 800 a kansa. Scott Jones shi ne rabin wannan aikin a ɓangaren Linux na shigarwa. Kwanan nan ya sa nunin M3 Air ya yi aiki a hardware display controller na Apple, wanda ya ɗauki wannan na'ura daga marar amfani zuwa mai amfani a dare ɗaya.

Gwada Omarchy. Eduardo Martinez ya gina Try Omarchy: ainihin tebur na Omarchy yana gudana a matsayin app na asali, mai saurin hardware a Mac naka, tare da kameranka, audionka, clipboard, da babban fayil ɗaya an haɗa su. Babu abin da za a raba. Kawai zazzage shi kuma ga abin da hayaniya ke kansa.

Packages. Shun Li yana tabbatar da komai da Omarchy ke buƙata yana nan a matsayin aarch64 package, tare da hotunan faifai na ARM64 na na'urori masu kama da jiki da kayan baƙo da ke sa su daɗi wajen amfani.

Direbobi da kernel. Ryan Murray yana tayar da Neo kuma yana gina ginshiƙan da sauran ƙungiya ke aiki daga kansa. Yana haɓaka SPTM emulator wanda ke sa ya yiwu a bi macOS a chips na M4 da sabbi. Waɗannan traces suna da mahimmanci don sa nuni, GPUs, audio, wireless, da Bluetooth su yi aiki. Dj yana rubuta direbobin GPU, kuma ya sa Touch ID ya yi aiki ta magana kai tsaye da Secure Enclave. Miguel Cruz ya ƙware a aikin M5: ya reverse-engineer chip N1 na Apple kuma ya sa Wi-Fi da Bluetooth nasa su yi aiki a Linux. Har ila yau ya bayar da hardware video encoding da decoding, Bluetooth, da ingantawar tebur a Neo. Liam yana tayar da trackpad na Neo, kula da wuta, lasifika, da fitar headphone, tare da traces na macOS na Ryan suna samar da ginshiƙi mai mahimmanci. Eryk Wieliczko yana kula da goyon bayan M4 da aiwatarwa clean-room, kuma yana aiki a haɗawa da packaging. Jaidip Subedi ya kunna nuni na waje ta USB-C a M2 MacBook Air.

MLX. Joshua Warren yana gina mlx-omarchy, wanda ke kawo tsarin koyon inji MLX na Apple zuwa Linux a GPUs na Apple ta Vulkan. Lambarka har yanzu tana cewa import mlx.core as mx, amma yanzu tana gudana a Omarchy.

Tsofaffin Macs. Shawn Yeager yana da tarin pull requests ga 2016 da 2017 Touch Bar MacBook Pros: lasifika, Wi-Fi, firikwensin hasken yanayi, Touch Bar kanta. Randy yana reverse-engineering chip T2 don kawo Touch ID ga Intel Macs, kuma yana rubuta kowane gyara ga 2019 MacBook Pro a hanya. Jon Kinney ya kasance yana aiki a goyon bayan ARM ga Omarchy shekara guda da ta wuce, kuma yanzu yana sa dukan ƙungiya ta daidaitu a kan wanda ke yin menene.

Tare, ƙungiya ce mai ban tsoro. Mutane goma sha biyar waɗanda ba su jira izini ba, kuma waɗanda ke amfani da wakilai don tafiya cikin saurin da da ya zama kamar wauta shekara guda da ta wuce.

Manufa ga fitarwa ta farko ita ce cikakkiyar jituwa da M1 da M2, ciki har da Pro da Max:

  • Installer mai santsi, tare da zaɓin shigarwa da gwadawa
  • Cikakken goyon bayan GPU
  • Monitors na waje ta USB-C
  • Touch ID
  • MLX
  • Rufin faifai

Sabbin na'urori ana aiki a kansu a layi ɗaya, amma M1 da M2 ne inda muka dasa tuta tukuna. Baya ga inji M, akwai miliyoyin tsofaffin laptops a waje, yawancinsu tuni sun yi ritaya daga sabbin burin macOS na Apple, kuma kowannensu kwamfutar Omarchy ce mai ban mamaki da ke jiran faruwa.

Ba sai ka jira fitowar da aka goge don farawa ba. Try Omarchy zai sa ka kallon ainihin tebur a Mac naka a minti kaɗan. Kuma idan kana shirye ka jajirce, installer zai sa Omarchy kusa da macOS a M1 ko M2 yau.

A ƙarshe, roƙo. Idan kana aiki a Linux don kayan Apple, ko direbobin kernel ne, firmware, packaging, installers, ko koyon inji, za mu so mu ji daga gare ka. Akwai kyakkyawan zaton ka warware wani abu da muke nema, ko akasin haka. Tuntuɓe mu a apple@omarchy.org.
