Nzela malamu koleka ya kobanda kosalela mwa trésor oyo tosili kolever mpo na Omacom Foundation koleka na gamin Linux cracké koleka na Pologne: Vaxry!

Lokola mokeli ya Hyprland, azali responsable ya moko ya ba technologies misato ya core na Omarchy (Arch Linux/Hyprland/Quickshell), mpe nazali ravi kosakola sponsorship exclusif oyo etiki ye afocus mobimba na kodevelopper yango liboso sans besoin ya kokanisa développement commercial to fundraising.

Tozali koengager long terme na Vaxry mpo kosunga avenir long terme ya Omarchy. Deal ya sponsorship exclusif na biso etiami mpo na mibu misato, na option ya mibale mosusu.

Deal epesi mpe nzela na programme Hyprperks ya Hyprland ezalaki ebima ofele, mpo na bato nionso, lokola ba abonnements payants ekosila. Kasi Hyprland ekocontinuer kozwa ba dons personnels mpo kotika bato basomba développement ya projet mpe kosunga Vaxry mpe équipe.

Merci mingi na 37signals (mbote ngai moko! 😂), Butterfly, mpe Framework mpo kosalisa Hyprland ekoma na point oyo. Ravi que Omacom Foundation ekoka kozwa kobima na 10 octobre, mpe kopesa moko ya ba développeurs malamu koleka na moment Linux ya sika oyo chance ya kokota mobimba.

(* Deal oyo ezali na aide directe te mpo kozwela Vaxry copine, kasi nakozala disponible personnellement lokola référence mpo na mituna nionso na likambo wana.)
