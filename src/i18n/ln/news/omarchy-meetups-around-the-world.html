Omarchy ekoli na communauté mondiale, na ~ba membres 35,000 na Discord, mpe sikoyo ezali tango tobanda kokutana elongi na elongi. Mpo tobandi Ba Meetups ya Omarchy na calendrier partagé mpo na ba rassemblements na mokili mobimba.

Oyo ezali ba événements etambolisami na communauté. Osengeli na permission te mpo na koorganiser moko, moto te azali na ville, mpe moto nionso akoki kosangisa moko bisika nionso. Solola na tina na Omarchy, Linux, open source, programmation, personnalisation, to culture hacker adjacente nionso oyo esalaka bato basepela kosala ba ordinaters amusants lisusu.

Batela yango ouvert na bato nionso, divulguer ba sponsorships nionso, mpe kobongola kombo ya Omarchy na business ya événements to pitch ya vente te. Au-delà, sala lokola ba adultes civilisés (na émerveillement ya bana), mpe zwa temps malamu.

Mema laptop na yo. Lakisa montage na yo. Echange ba themes mpe ba plugins. Salisa moto na installation na ye ya liboso. Likanisi ezali kosangisa bato oyo basepelaka na ba ordinaters personnels, programmables, kitoko na chambre moko.

Okoki kozwa meetup ekoya to kotinda ya yo moko na nzela ya calendrier mondial ya Omarchy na Luma. Tosala yango mouvement mondial!
