Omarchy elobaka que ordinater esengeli ezala kitoko ndenge ezali productif. Likanisi wana ememami na ba salisi ya themes mpe ba artistes mosusu ya Omarchy kobima na mokolo ya liboso. Kotonga ba palettes mobimba, na temps na bango moko, ofele, mpo balingaki machine na bango emonana lokola eloko.

Mpo tobandi Omarchy AIR - Ba Artistes na Bovandi - mpo kosunga ba malamu koleka kati na bango. Bovandi bwa sanza motoba mpo na ba artistes basalaka librement na ba themes, ba plugins, mpe nionso mosusu bakanisi Omarchy esengeli sima. Roadmap te, ba tickets te. Likanisi ezali kotika créativité etambola na lisungi ya Omacom Foundation.

Bovandi nionso eyaka na 2,500 USD na sanza mpo na sanza motoba mobimba, compte token mpo ba agents basenga kotika te, mpe boyebi ya libela lokola ancien AIR tango sanza motoba esili.

Ezali na ba esika kino misato na tango nionso, mpe mibale kati na yango ezwami deja mpo na saison oyo ya liboso.

Azali ingénieur mécanicien oyo akomaki moko ya ba forces esthétiques ya communauté oyo, avandaka na Omarchy Core, mpe asalisaka kobatela écosystème ya plugins.
