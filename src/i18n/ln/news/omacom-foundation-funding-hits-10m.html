Bofundi ya Omacom Foundation esili kokoma na 10 million USD na Ba Patrons Fondateurs mibale ya sika bakoti na mission: Drew Houston, cofondateur mpe co-CEO ya Dropbox, mpe Peter Steinberger, mokeli ya OpenClaw.

Lokola Ba Patrons Fondateurs mwambe ya liboso, Drew mpe Peter bazali kopesa 1 million USD moko na moko. Yango epesaka fondation ba patrons zomi mpe MILLION ZOMI YA DOLLARS ya propre mpo kopusa mission na biso audacieuse ya kosala Linux na bureau esalema na nzela monene koleka.

Ezali sukali extra kozala na Drew mpe Peter na bateau mpo nazalaki fan monene ya mibale na bango mpe ba accomplissements na bango. Nazalaki client enthousiaste ya Dropbox pene décennies mibale, mpe ezali central na ndenge nakoki kovivre na no backup, no cry kasi kobongwana ba ordinaters constamment.

Yango nde mpo Omarchy eyaka na intégration Dropbox malamu kobima na boite. Kende kaka na Install > Service > Dropbox, mpe okozala na intégration ya gestionnaire ya fichiers mpe panneau ya menu sur mesure mpo na ba contrôles.

Mpe OpenClaw epesaki essentiellement feuille de route ya ndenge Omarchy esengeli kotraiter croissance explosive na yango. Ndenge nini na ba backlogs PR oyo ekomi parabolique mbala moko? Nini na tina na sécurité ya ba plugins? Ndenge nini ba meetups na kombo ya projet esengeli kotratama? Nasilaki kozwa plaisir ya kopuiser na conseil mpe expérience ya Peter na ba sujets ebele kati na yango.

Au-delà, mibale bazali koreprésenter exactement mitindo ya ambition technique oyo nalingi na nzela ya Omarchy. Drew abongolaki démangeaison personnelle na tina na kotambolisa ba fichiers kati na ba ordinaters na Dropbox. Peter abongolaki projet ya weekend na OpenClaw, mpe na processus a remindaki bato nionso appétit boni ezali mpo na ba ordinaters oyo emiyokaka personnel, programmable, mpe amusant.

Yango ezali molimo na sima ya Omacom Foundation mpe. Tozali awa mpo kofunda infrastructure, ba projets open-source, mpe ba développeurs esengeli mpo kosala bureau Linux ndako agréable mpo na génération ekoya ya ba utilisateurs ya ordinaters.

Boyei malamu, Drew mpe Peter. Ba Patrons Fondateurs zomi. Million zomi ya dollars. Prophétie ekomi pene na 2 million USD mosusu!
