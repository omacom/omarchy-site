Omacom Foundation esili kosala embauche na yango ya liboso plein temps! Krzysztof Wilczyński azali kokota mpo kotambolisa mosala na biso na Omarchy Kernel. Oyo ezali exactement oyo fondation etiami mpo kosala: kofuta bato malamu basalaka na ba problèmes durs mpo na litomba ya bato nionso.

Krzysztof azali développeur ya kernel Linux ya kala. Abatelaka elongo sous-système PCI Endpoint elongo na ba drivers natifs PCI host bridge mpe endpoint. Yango ezali plomberie oyo econnectaka Linux na GPU na yo, disque NVMe na yo, mpe carte WiFi na yo. Azali mpe relecteur na mosala Rust PCI ya sika ya kernel. Liboso ya kokende profond na kernel, apesaki mibu na ingénierie ya systèmes, basalaka na Kubernetes mpe ba runtimes ya conteneurs.

Sikoyo akomema expérience wana nionso na Omarchy Kernel. Focus: performance, compatibilité, mpe sécurité. Tolingi Linux ekima na ba laptops ya sika mpe ba ThinkPads ya kala ndenge moko. Tolingi esala kobima na boite na ba machines ebele. Mpe tolingi ekangama makasi.

Krzysztof azali mpe kokota na Omarchy Core, mpo akosalisa kopesa direction mpo na distro mobimba.
