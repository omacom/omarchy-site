DigitalOcean ezali kokota na Omacom Foundation lokola Patron Corporatif Fondateur, kopesa 1 million USD na mbula mpo na mibu misato! Yango ezali engagement ya 3 million USD mpo na développement, entretien, mpe bopanzani ya Omarchy. Vote ya confiance incroyable na OS kitoko, ya esengo mpe ya ba agents oyo tozali kotonga.

Nasalelaki DigitalOcean Droplets koleka décennie moko. Ezali nzela ya simple mpo kotia machine Linux na internet mpe kobanda kotonga eloko. Tosalelaka yango kotambolisa contributors.rubyonrails.org, site oyo ezali kofêter ba milliers ya bato oyo basalisi Rails ezala oyo ezali. Infrastructure oyo nasengelaki na yango mibu ebele, mpo kampani na sima na yango ekota na biso esalaka esengo spécial.

Mpe bazali patron parfait mpo na OS ya ba agents. DigitalOcean epesaka deja ba Droplets one-click mpo na Hermes mpe OpenClaw. Okoki kozala na agent na ndako na bureau Omarchy na yo mpe mosusu basalaka jour et nuit na Droplet.

Kasi eteni ya patronage oyo esekisaki ngai penza ezali mbangu oyo nionso esanganaki. Nabengaki Paddy Srinivasan, CEO ya DigitalOcean, na X na mercredi. Tozwaki appel nuit moko wana. Natindaki proposition na samedi. Na dimanche, tosilisaki nionso.

Yango ezali vitesse ya collaboration oyo ezali possible na X, ba agents, mpe ambition sans limites! Tango bato bamonaka opportunité moko mpe balingi kosala eloko esalema, raison te ya kopesa sanza mpo kokoma na oui. Tokoki kotambola na vitesse ya ba possibilités liboso na biso.

DigitalOcean ekoti na Meta Superintelligence Labs lokola Patron Corporatif Fondateur, pembeni ya bato mpe ba kampanis nionso oyo basili kotomboka mpo kofunda mission oyo. Ndayi na bango ememi lisungi na biso mobimba na pene 18.5 million USD. Esika ebele mpo koinvestir na bato, ba projets, mpe infrastructure oyo esalaka Omarchy malamu koleka mpo na bato nionso.

Lokola kopesa bato ya kokamwa ebele mosala! Tosili kobenga Krzysztof Wilczyński lokola Mokonzi ya Omarchy Kernel mpe outfoxxed, mokeli ya Quickshell, lokola Mokonzi ya Omarchy Shell. Kopesa bato lokola bango chance ya kosala plein temps kotonga Omarchy mbangu, fiable, mpe capable ezali possible kaka na ba patrons lokola DigitalOcean. Lisungi na bango ebongolaka ba ambitions na biso na ba emplois ya solo mpo na bato oyo bakoki kosala yango esalema.

Merci, Paddy mpe bato nionso na DigitalOcean. Tia lisungi oyo na mosala mpe sala avenir ya Linux kitoko, ya esengo, ya ba agents esalema!
