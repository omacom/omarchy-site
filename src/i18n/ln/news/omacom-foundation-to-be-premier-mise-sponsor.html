Sponsorship ya misato ebimi! Omacom Foundation ezali kokoma sponsor premier ya mise, mpe par conséquent ya jdx, oyo apesaki mibu kotonga outil oyo tranquillement esalaka kogérer ba versions différentes ya Ruby, Node, Go esengo ya kogérer, mpe epesaka ba outils agents na yo canal ya mise à jour oyo ekoki kolanda vitesse ya livraison.

Soki osalelaki Omarchy, osalelaki mise, que omonaki to te. Ezali oyo ebongisaka ba runtimes ya langues, mpo mise use -g ruby epesaka yo Ruby sans ligne moko ya archéologie PATH. Kasi eteni oyo nasepela na yango mingi sikoyo ezali ba agents.

CLI nionso majeur ya coding-agent na Omarchy etindamaka lokola stub mise lazy-loading na ~/.local/bin/. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Antigravity, Copilot, Crush, Grok, Pi, Oh My Pi - nionso evandaka wana, ezofuta yo eloko te kino mbala ya liboso ovandi otapa moko solo. Mpe einstallaka moko mpe osalaka. Paquet te ya koluka, version te ya koépingler, toolchain te ya koraisonner na tina na yango. omarchy-mise-install <package> e-enveloppe CLI mosusu nionso ndenge moko, mpe omarchy update ebatelaka nionso kino ya suka à jour.

Yango ezali voie rapide. Na L'Ère ya ba Agents, écart kati na koyoka outil mpe kotambolisa yango esengeli ezala pene secondes minei, mpe mise epesaka yango. Moto te asengelaki kopackager eloko mpo na distro nanu.

Intérêt etambolaka na ngambo mibale, mpe: trafic na mise emati 24% kobima Omarchy ebandaki! Bato ebele bazali koyeba outil oyo mbala ya liboso na nzela na biso, oyo ezali exactement mitindo ya eloko sponsorship esengeli koreconnaître na esika ya kozwa comme acquis.

Lisusu, merci spécial na Tobi Lütke, Patrick Collison, Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Matthew Prince, Brendan Iribe, Jason Fried, Drew Houston, mpe Peter Steinberger mpo kosala yango possible. Soki ezalaki te mpo na Oligarchy, tolingaki kokoka kofunda ba développeurs open-source boye te!
