Tango ya kolota minene. Omarchy Quattro epesaki bato chance ya koyoka oyo ordinater malléable ya lobi emonanaka, mpe basepeli na yango (mingi!). Esalaka sikoyo lokola obligation morale kosala avenir oyo ezala disponible mingi mpe kobongola fondamentalement ndenge bato bazalaka na ba ordinaters na bango mbala ya liboso na oyo emonanaka lokola libela.

Mpo kosala bongo, nazali koincorporer Omacom Foundation mpo kosala que mission oyo ezala financée mobimba, durable, mpe prêt ya koaccélérer.

Fondation sans but lucratif oyo ekobatela ba marques, ekofunda infrastructure, ekopusa mosala, mpe ekosunga ba projets open-source mpe ba développeurs oyo Omarchy edépendre na yango.
