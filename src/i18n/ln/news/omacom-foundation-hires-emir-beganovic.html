Tozali kobenga Emir Beganović lokola Mokonzi ya Infrastructure mpo na Omarchy! Abandaka 1 novembre lokola Omacom Foundation ya misato embauche plein temps, kokota na Krzysztof Wilczyński na Kernel mpe outfoxxed na Shell.

Tala ndenge Emir alimbolaka voyage na ye moko na Omarchy:

Bolingo na ngai ya Linux ezongaka sima koleka mibu tuku mibale. Nakomaki développeur Ubuntu nazalaki nanu adolescent mpe nazalaki profondément na kati ya communauté locale. Carrière na ngai ememaki ngai na ba pays misato, basalaka na fiabilité mpe performance na ba startups mpe ba scaleups mpe kotambolisa ba équipes. Napesaki pene mibu sambo na département SRE ya Booking.com, kotambolisa mosala na ba systèmes ya build mpe infrastructure CI. Récemment, natongaki Isorun, esika nazalaki kosala na ba microVMs, ba snapshots, isolation, planification, mpe calcul jetable. Sikoyo namemi expérience wana na bolingo na ngai ya liboso.

Oyo ekataki attention na ngai penza na tina na Emir ezalaki TryOmarchy. Azwaki bureau Omarchy, atieki yango na navigateur (te réimplémentation, kasi eloko ya solo), mpe asalaki yango ebotama na secondes na infrastructure CPU ordinaire na Cloudflare. Tobandaki kosolola na tina na expérience stupide wana, mpe conversation epanzanaki mbangu na ba laptops, ba architectures ya sika, ba OEMs, mpe infrastructure tokosenga soki Omarchy eketambola bisika nionso tolingi. Tomonaki tozali na vision moko.

Yango nde Emir azali kokota na bateau mpo kotambolisa.

Infrastructure mpo na Omarchy elingi koloba mingi koleka kotambolisa ba serveurs. Ezali oyo tosalelaka kotonga, komeka, mpe kotinda Omarchy, mpe kobatela système esalaka na fiabilité ndenge topanzanaka na hardware ebele mpe ba architectures ebele. Ezali kosala ba versions fiables, kosala ba échecs reproductibles, mpe kopesa bato nionso mosusu na projet base solide mpo bakoka kotambola mbangu.

Mpe ezali na ebele ya koluka! Omarchy esengeli esala kitoko na machine evandaka na bureau na yo, kasi raison te esengeli etikite wana. TryOmarchy epesaki biso aperçu ya oyo esalemaka tango kobanda ordinater Linux ekomi pete lokola kofungola page web, kotia goût ya Linux moke na clic moko. Tokomona esika yango ememaka.

Merci na bato nionso oyo efundaka mosala oyo, tokoki kopesa bato lokola Emir temps ya kokota mobimba na Omarchy. Ba patrons na biso esalaka possible kobongola ba expériences ambitieuses na mosala durable, na bato plein temps dédiés kosala système mobimba malamu. Tozali reconnaissants mpo na patronage na bango.

Boyei malamu na bateau, Emir! Tosala Omarchy etambola bisika nionso.
