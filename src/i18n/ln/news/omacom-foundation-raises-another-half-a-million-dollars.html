Omacom Foundation ezwaki demi-million ya dollars mosusu! Kati na stock ya sika ya ba tokens, ndayi corporatif ya mibu misato, mpe ba milliers ya dollars kobima na patronage ouvert, yango ezali pene 510,000 USD mosusu elakami mpo na Linux kitoko, ya esengo mpe ya ba agents. Yango ememi total na biso ya ba ndayi mpe ba dons na pene 15.5 million USD!

Ya liboso, tozali koyamba OpenRouter lokola Patron Corporatif Distingué na 150,000 USD na tokens. Tango allocation wana esili, tokoki korenouveler arrangement na accord mutuel. Ba tokens oyo ekopesa makasi na ba agents oyo batongaka, bamekaka, mpe babetelaka Omarchy. Mingi tokoki kotia ba agents na mosala kobongisa ba bugs, korevoir ba contributions, mpe kobongisa distro, mbangu tokoki kosala ordinater ya bato nionso malamu. Carburant ebele mpo na mission!

Tokosala mpe ba millions ya dollars na tokens oyo epesami ekende na nzela ya plateforme OpenRouter excellent. Mpo tokoka kokabola ba tokens malamu na ba équipes malamu kobima na ba modèles malamu.

Mpe ezali na Four Technologies, ekoti lokola Patron Corporatif Distingué na ndayi ya 100,000 USD na mbula mpo na mibu misato. Yango ezali 300,000 USD mpo kosunga ba projets ya communauté oyo Omarchy edépendre na yango, infrastructure oyo ebatelaka yango etambola, mpe mosala ya komema Linux kitoko na bato ebele. Kozala na ba kampanis koengager mpo na mibu epesaka biso confiance ya kosala ndenge moko.

Tala ndenge équipe ya Four Technologies elobaka:

Tozali kampani fintech AI-forward, mpe ba kampanis lokola Four Technologies ekoki kotonga ndenge tozali kotonga kaka mpo na infrastructure open-source bato mosusu batongaki liboso. Kosunga Omacom Foundation nde ndenge totiaka mbongo na sima na yango. Tokanisi que étape ekoya mpo na AI ezali appli mosusu likolo ya système d'exploitation te, ezali AI etongami directement na kati ya système d'exploitation, mpe yango ezali pari Omarchy azali kosala. Tolingaki kozala partie ya communauté oyo ezali kosala pari wana, te kaka koprofiter na yango sima.

Mpe kobima kofungola patronage na bato nionso, tozwaki pene 60,000 USD kobima na pene ba donateurs 500 na nzela ya patronage ouvert. Yango ezali réponse incroyable kobima na bato oyo balingi kosalisa kosala yango esalema. Contribution nionso ebakisaka na oyo tokoki kosala elongo.

Ba tokens mpo kotonga na yango. Mbongo mpo kosunga ba projets tozali kodépendre na yango. Bato mpe ba kampanis oyo balingi kotia eloko na sima ya kondima na bango na Linux na bureau. Ndenge tocontinuaka!
