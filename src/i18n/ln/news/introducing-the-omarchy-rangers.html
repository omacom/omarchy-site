Omarchy ekoli mbangu que ba milliers ya bato bazali kokota mbala moko, mpe mingi kati na bango bayaka kobima na macOS to Windows. Yango ezali ba installations ya liboso ebele, "wapi na banda" ebele, mpe ba mituna ebele oyo ezuaka litomba na réponse ya solo kobima na moto oyo amesana kosalisa basusu.
