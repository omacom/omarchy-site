The full-time employees of the Omacom Foundation, paid for by our patrons to make Omarchy better for everyone.

Krzysztof Wilczyński

Head of Omarchy Kernel

Krzysztof co-maintains key parts of Linux’s PCI subsystem. His work helps the kernel communicate with modern hardware, from graphics cards to fast storage. He leads Omarchy Kernel performance, compatibility, and security, and serves on Omarchy Core.

outfoxxed

Head of Omarchy Shell

outfoxxed created Quickshell, the toolkit behind Omarchy Shell. He makes the desktop faster, more reliable, and easier for people and agents to extend through plugins. He also serves on Omarchy Core.
