Omarchy has grown into a global community, with ~35,000 members on [the Discord](https://discord.com/invite/tXFUdasqhY), and now it's time we started meeting face to face. So we're launching [Omarchy Meetups](/meetups/) with a shared calendar for gatherings around the world.

These are community-run events. You don't need permission to organize one, nobody owns a city, and anyone can put one together anywhere. Talk about Omarchy, Linux, open source, programming, customization, or whatever adjacent hacker culture gets people excited about making computers fun again.

Keep it open to everyone, disclose any sponsorships, and don't turn the Omarchy name into an events business or a sales pitch. Beyond that, behave like civilized adults (with child-like wonder), and have a great time.

Bring your laptop. Show off your setup. Swap themes and plugins. Help someone through their first installation. The point is to get the people who care about personal, programmable, beautiful computers together in the same room.

You can [find an upcoming meetup or submit your own](/meetups/) through the global Omarchy calendar on Luma. Let's make this a worldwide movement!
