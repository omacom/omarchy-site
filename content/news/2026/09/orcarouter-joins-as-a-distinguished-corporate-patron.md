I'm delighted to welcome [OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/) to the [Omacom Foundation](/foundation/) as a [Distinguished Corporate Patron](/patrons/#distinguished-corporate-patrons)! They're donating $150,000 in tokens to our Strategic Token Reserves (very big now, much intelligence).

We'll primarily be using these tokens in OrcaRouter for their security-tuned models, helping our security and kernel teams in particular. First up is [OrcaCyber Zero 1.0](https://www.orcarouter.ai/models/orca/orcacyber-zero-1.0), a coding model post-trained for vulnerability analysis, reproduction, and security research.

OrcaRouter reports that Zero 1.0 reproduces 1,478 of 1,507 real-world vulnerabilities on the first attempt in CyberGym Level 1, a benchmark drawn from 188 open source projects. That's a 98.07% pass rate. We're looking forward to seeing how it performs on the security and kernel problems in front of our teams.

Here's how the OrcaRouter team describes their work:

> OrcaRouter is a security-first AI gateway and execution layer for AI agents, using adaptive routing to select the right model for each workload while providing guardrails, an agent firewall for tool/MCP calls and egress, ZDR/privacy controls, and end-to-end observability across cloud and local agent execution.
>
> OrcaRouter is also a major publisher of open-weight cybersecurity models, with 1.5M+ downloads on Hugging Face in the past 30 days. Its flagship cyber model, OrcaCyber Zero 1.0, is currently in closed beta for trusted security research, vulnerability analysis and reproduction, and red-team workflows.

Thank you to the OrcaRouter team for putting these tools behind our mission. We're looking forward to putting their models to work finding and fixing the problems that matter to everyone running Omarchy.
