The Omacom Foundation

The nonprofit behind Omarchy. It holds the trademarks, funds the infrastructure, promotes the work, and supports the open-source projects and developers the distro depends on.

Patrons

The foundation is funded by its patrons: twelve Founding Patrons contributing $1,000,000 each, Distinguished Patrons at $100,000, Distinguished Corporate Patrons at $100,000 a year — and soon a tier open to everyone.

The Patrons

Sponsorships

Multi-year sponsorships for the projects Omarchy is built on — Hyprland, Quickshell, and mise — so the people behind them can work without thinking about fundraising.

The Sponsorships

Artists in Residence

Omarchy AIR pays artists a six-month stipend to work freely on themes, plugins, and whatever else makes the system beautiful.
