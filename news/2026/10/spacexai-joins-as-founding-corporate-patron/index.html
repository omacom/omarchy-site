SpaceXAI joins as a Founding Corporate Patron with $1.5 million in Grok tokens

SpaceXAI is joining the Omacom Foundation as a Founding Corporate Patron, donating $1.5 million in Grok tokens! That’s a serious stack of tokens to further accelerate the agents and teams that build, review, and fix Omarchy. And we’re going to put them to great use.

Grok 4.7 and beyond will be helping power our automated agents, like Omabot, who’s been reviewing the flood of PRs that keep pouring in. And it has its work cut out. We’re closing in on 7,000 pull requests from more than 550 contributors, and every one of them deserves a proper look. That’s not a job for mere humans!

But it’s not just the bots: every Omarchy team is going to get to tap into these tokens, whether they’re hunting bugs, polishing themes, porting to new hardware, or tackling whatever crazy idea comes next. More tokens means more of the backlog getting cleared, faster.

What an honor it is to have SpaceXAI on board! I mean, what’s cooler than partnering with a literal rocket-science space company? These are the folks catching boosters with chopsticks. And given where they’re headed, there’s a real chance we’ll soon be consuming tokens beamed down from data centers in space. “Omarchy, now powered by intelligence from orbit” — I can’t think of a more fitting future for the Year of Linux on the Desktop.

SpaceXAI joins Meta Superintelligence Labs, DigitalOcean, and Alibaba Cloud as a Founding Corporate Patron. Their pledge brings our total backing to approximately $23.2 million. Which means even more room to invest in the people, infrastructure, and open-source projects that make Omarchy possible. Not bad for a project that started as a pile of dotfiles.

Thank you to the whole Grok team at SpaceXAI for making this happen.
