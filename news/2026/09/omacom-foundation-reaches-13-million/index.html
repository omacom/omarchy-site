Omacom Foundation reaches $13M with four Distinguished Patrons

The Omacom Foundation just crossed $13 million! Our first four Distinguished Patrons have pledged $100,000 each to the mission:

Ryan R. Hughes needs no introduction around here. He’s a member of Omarchy Core, my wingman on the distro since day one, and a partner at Oodle. Now he’s backing the foundation with his own money too. That’s conviction!

Ed Huang is the cofounder and CTO of PingCAP, the company behind TiDB, the distributed SQL database. He’s spent a decade proving that serious open-source infrastructure can also be a serious business.

Adrien Treccani founded Metaco, the Swiss digital-asset custody platform that Ripple acquired. Another crypto pioneer joining the mission of making Omarchy a great place for crypto.

Max Schoening is Head of Product at Notion, and before that he ran design at Heroku and was VP of Design at GitHub. He’s shaped more of the tools developers love than most people will ever know.

Together with the twelve Founding Patrons and our two Distinguished Corporate Patrons, that’s eighteen backers and $13 million committed in under two weeks.

Welcome aboard, all four. The mission grows!
