The sponsorship lets Vaxry work on it without having to think about commercial development or fundraising — and it freed the existing Hyprperks program to be released for everyone, for free. Quickshell

Full-time Omacom Foundation employee

Quickshell is the desktop construction kit behind Omarchy Shell. It provides the building blocks for bars, menus, notifications, widgets, and lock screens, using QML to turn ideas into interfaces that update as you work. That flexibility lets people and their agents build plugins and shape the desktop around their needs. Its creator, outfoxxed, is a full-time Omacom Foundation employee as Head of Omarchy Shell and a member of Omarchy Core, working to make the shell and its foundations faster, more reliable, and easier to extend.
